This marketing technique manipulates you every day. Imagine a vending machine on your campus, assuming it sells 100 cans a day. And if we put a Pepsi machine next to the Coke machine, you would believe it would be selling 50-50 or 60-40 cans a day. Wrong. Chances are it's gonna be 200 cans each. But why? Because when there's only one machine, the question you ask yourself is, "Am I going to drink a Coke or not?" But when there are two machines, you ask yourself, "Do I want a Coke or Pepsi?" It's counterintuitive, and it can increase each competitor's sales.
