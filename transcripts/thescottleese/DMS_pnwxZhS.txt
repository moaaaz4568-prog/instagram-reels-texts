The most frequently asked questions that I get from founders and CROs are: Why isn't my sales team hitting quota? It's not because your reps are lazy or your product isn't sexy enough. Let's get this straight. Reps are out here guessing, using spreadsheets from 2012, and closing deals with vibes instead of data. How long do you think it will take before you understand that this doesn't work anymore? Stop blaming ghosted leads. Track where deals die. It's probably in your discovery call. Run a process audit. If your onboarding calls are longer than The Godfather and your onboarding doc is longer than War and Peace, you've failed. Simplify it now. Ask your reps what's broken. Ask them what's a blocker. Shocking, I know, but they know the answer. So stop blaming your team. Start fixing the machine. Or you can keep yelling at the Zoom walls. Your call. Quotas are not hit with pep talks. They're built with process.
