You can hustle your way and improvise your way to five million bucks without documenting things. You cannot just do that to twenty five and fifty. It breaks down. In order to go from five to twenty five, you most likely have to bring in new people, and in order to transfer that knowledge, stuff has to be documented. I don't care if you put it in Notion or Google Docs or whatever the heck tool you like. Somebody coming on board needs to be able to go somewhere and say, "Where is the emails? Where is the cold call pitch? Where is the demo pitch? Where is the objections? Where is the ICP? Where is the buyer personas? Where is the competitive landscape?" And like seventy five other things, they have all these materials, right? So they know exactly what to do.
