If you're building out an affiliate program or referral program, you've got to find a way to incentivize the people to come work for you. One reason your referral program sucks is you're just simply not paying people enough. If you sell a $500 product and you're paying people 10%, they're making 50 bucks. Nobody cares about 50 bucks. You've got to have a much larger payout to get the attention of somebody. Sell a $500,000 product, you pay 10%, that's 50 grand. Now you got my attention.
