Most executives know everything there is about negotiation, right? Wrong. I can't even begin to tell you how many executives I've talked to who left hundreds of thousands, if not millions, of dollars on the table because they didn't know the right questions to ask prior to taking the role on. You got to ask questions about equity ownership, vesting versus milestone bonuses, preferred versus common shares, actual value of the options today, strike price, total number of outstanding shares. Do you know how many VPs don't know anything about this stuff and they just sign paperwork coming in from founders that say, hey, you have this number of shares, congratulations. Do yourself a favor, talk to somebody who's got experience with this stuff, study up, know which questions to ask.
