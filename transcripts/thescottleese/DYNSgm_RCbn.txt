The best skill you can develop is acting on an idea the second that it hits you, even when every part of your brain is telling you to wait. Because it's not about motivation; it's about not giving your brain enough time to talk you out of doing something about it. So you're going to feel the resistance, the doubt, the thousand reasons why now isn't the right time, but you're not going to sit down and negotiate with any of that. When the idea hits, you let it be the starting point, not a someday. That's when everything should start to change for you, because instead of having a list of things you're going to do one day, some fancy honey-do list, you start having a list of things that you already did.
