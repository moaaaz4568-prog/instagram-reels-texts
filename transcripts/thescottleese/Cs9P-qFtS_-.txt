My favorite thing about sales is that you can come from nothing and build yourself into something. I grew up in the poorest county in all of California. I did not have a business degree. I never had a sales job, or in fact, I never even had any semblance of a career until I was 27 years old. You can start from anywhere at any time in your life and build yourself into a high earner and take some of those skills that you've learned along the way in sales and apply it to entrepreneurship. I started off selling couple hundred dollar deals. I ended up raising hundreds of millions of dollars in venture capital funding. I've exited companies worth billions of dollars before. And if you asked me a long time ago, would I ever do any of those things, the answer would be no. But sales provided me the vehicle to be able to do that stuff.
