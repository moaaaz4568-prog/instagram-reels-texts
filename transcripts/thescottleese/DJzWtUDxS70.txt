Pro tip out there for anybody in Rev Ops, VP of Sales, CRO kind of roles: add a CRM stage called "In Legal Review," in which you monitor time in stage and price amount and have a weekly review to prioritize. You're going to quantitatively report monthly to management things like sales cycle extensions, deals lost in stage reasons, total in cycle right now. This is a pro tip for those who are looking to sync up the CRO and legal team and get some of those deals across the line a little smoother.
