Ten out of ten uncomfortable things that build extreme confidence, and they get progressively more niche. Go twenty four hours with zero entertainment no music, no phone, no scrolling, just you and your thoughts. Fast for a full day. Talk to one new stranger doesn't have to be anything deep, just engage in a conversation. Record yourself talking for ten minutes and actually watch it back. Sit alone in a restaurant with no phone and just observe and be present. Go to the gym and complete a workout with no headphones, just you and the weights. Ask for that discount, the upgrade, the favor, and let people say no. Give your real opinion about things before you checked if it's socially acceptable to do so. And get so comfortable being uncomfortable that fear stops being a reason not to do the things you want to do.
