What makes a top of funnel content? A hook built for strangers. The first line, frame, or visual has to work for someone with zero context about me. No inside references. No, as I mentioned in the last week, you need to assume that the person who is watching this has not seen your face ever. They don't know anything about me. Number two, a broad searchable topic. For example, fitness, losing weight, top market. Number three, a reason to follow. Why would a stranger follow you in the first place? Top of funnel content should end with a reason for a stranger to want more from you. A tease of what's coming, promise of value, a personality that makes them curious.
