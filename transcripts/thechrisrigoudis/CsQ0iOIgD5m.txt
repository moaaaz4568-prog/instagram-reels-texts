Easiest way to increase your agency's revenue plus get more yeses: add performance bonuses in your next meeting, in your next deal. Instead of just pitching, "Hey, I want a two thousand retainer every single month," it's two thousand a month plus five percent off of the revenue that we generate. Why this works so well is you are not just getting the increase of the revenue off of the sales that you produce, but you also show them that your incentives are aligned. They're very clear on why you are both on the same side of the table, and they will work with you. You will get more yeses, plus you'll get more revenue out of the work that you do.
