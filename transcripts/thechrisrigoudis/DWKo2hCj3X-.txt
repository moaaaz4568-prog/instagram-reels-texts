The easiest way to get disappointed in life is expectation. It might sound like a wild statement, but I have zero expectations from everyone. And that means sometimes you can be overly paranoid. It can feel maybe that you are alone or you have immense responsibility for everything. But I found like to be a very peaceful way of living life. I expect people to fuck me over.
