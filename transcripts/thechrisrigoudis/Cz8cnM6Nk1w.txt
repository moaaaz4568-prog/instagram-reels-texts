Whoever is asking the questions controls the conversation. For example, if you receive an email or a DM and someone asks you, "Tell me more about your services, I'm interested," instead of sending them everything, say something like, "Yeah, happy to send you details. Before I do, our packages differ depending on client needs, so if it's appropriate, I'd have to know a little bit more about your current situation." And then you can ask a present reality question. For example, "What do you have in place now when it comes to solving X and Y and Z problems?" What you've done is you've reversed the flow from being the one who pitches to being the one asking questions, in control as a trusted advisor. That's how you build trust in their mind, position yourself as a trusted advisor, and also put yourself in the best possible position to get the sale. Make sure you follow for more tips.
