If you're in sales, this might be a typical scenario and tell me if this is you. You send the prospect a few time slots to book with you and they ghost you and now you follow up with, "Hey Chris, did you have a chance to book a time?" Don't be a sales nerd. They obviously didn't book, so why ask that question? Then you're dragged into desperation mode and you have to constantly keep pinging them for them to book a call. Your attempt is futile because you're even more needy now and they will never book with you. Instead, take off the salesperson mask and instead of trying to push them towards what you want to happen, push them away from their goal. So ask them this: "Hey John, I saw you didn't book yet. Is this still of interest to you?" Or "Hey John, are you still looking to meet to grow the company?" Or "Are you still looking to meet for you to lose weight?" By pushing them away and using this reverse psychology trick, you will actually get them to book much faster so you can lock in the sale.
