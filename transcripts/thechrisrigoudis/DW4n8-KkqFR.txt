When you want to close a prospect, you need to figure out what their real goal is. And the first time you ask them that question, you'll get a surface-level answer. Instead, always go two layers deeper. So you can ask him, "What would achieving that goal actually do for you?" And, "How would your life tangibly change once you get there?" And once he describes his goal as if it's a scene in a movie, that's when I know I got his real goal.
