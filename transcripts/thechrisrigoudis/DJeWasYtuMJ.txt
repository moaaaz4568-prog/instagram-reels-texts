Rejection is simply redirection. No means next. Every no is getting you closer to the person who desperately needs what you're offering. These nos aren't about you or your ego. They're slowly guiding you to the right buyer. So instead of taking rejection personally, just get curious. Ask yourself, what can you learn from it? Oh, clarify. Was it your timing, your tonality, your approach, or was it the wrong fit? Use data to improve. I'd love you to embrace rejection as the necessary path to yes, because rejection reveals the direction you should take.
