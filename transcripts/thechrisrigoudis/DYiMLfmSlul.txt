Words that kill deals before they even start. Don't say, "Tell me a little bit about yourself." Instead say, "What's the biggest problem that you're trying to fix right now?" Don't say, "I'd love to learn more about your business." Instead say, "Walk me through what's not working right now." Don't say, "How did you hear about us?" Instead say, "What made you book this call today?" Don't say, "Let me tell you what we do." Instead say, "To make this call worth your time, what's the main problem we're trying to fix?"
