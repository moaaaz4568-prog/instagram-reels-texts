When you start a sales call, "How's your day?" is a bad move. "What were you hoping to get out of the meeting?" is an excellent move. When you're closing the sale, "Which card would you like to put this on?" is a bad move. "What are the best next steps?" is an excellent move. When you're following up on a sale, "Just checking in" is a bad move. "Have you given up on your goals?" is an excellent move. When you're asking questions in sales, "What are you struggling with?" is a bad move. "What's slowing you down?" is an excellent move. When you're about to lose the sale, "Let's book a follow-up call" is a bad move. "What's holding you back from getting started?" is an excellent move.
