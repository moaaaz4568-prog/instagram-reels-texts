What your prospects say versus what they mean. When your prospect says, "Send me a breakdown of what twenty five k gets me," what they really mean is, "Convince me this isn't a scam." When your prospect says, "Do you have references?" what they mean is, "I need permission to spend this much." When your prospect says, "Can you send me some case studies?" what they really mean is, "I am not sure this will help me yet." When your prospect says, "I want a guarantee," what they really mean is, "I don't trust myself to do the work." Understanding your prospect's trust objections is just good communication.
