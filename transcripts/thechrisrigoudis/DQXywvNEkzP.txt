Never end the call without sending an email with the info. That line's the digital version of handing someone a brochure and praying they call you back. It feels polite, but that's how momentum dies. You just took a fire that was burning in front of you and tossed it into their cold inbox. A real closer doesn't hand off the moment. They lock it in then and there. They pull the info up, walk them through it, point to the screen and say, "Let's look at it together right now and lock it in so we can get you started." And when they buy, then send them, "Cool, I'll send you a quick recap with all your onboarding info." That's leadership. Amateurs send info, pros close the deal. Because deals don't close on emails, they close in the moment.
