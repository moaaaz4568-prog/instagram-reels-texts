If someone asks you how much does it cost, don't give them your price. They're in cost-based thinking. You have to reframe them into outcome, results-based thinking. Say this: Well, what's needed for you to fix X and Y and Z problems? The investment would be ten thousand, and most of my clients see results in the first ninety days. Where would you like to go from here? Now you've tied them making the investment with fixing their problems, and they're much more likely to say yes.
