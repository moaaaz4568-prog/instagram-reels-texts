Three years ago, I was trying to buy a business mentorship program to take me from thirty thousand to one hundred thousand a month. There was one thing that completely shifted my mindset. It's when the salesperson compared where I need to be to where I am today. In that moment, I felt incredibly disgusted with myself, but I was also incredibly motivated to step up. It was never about what I'm buying. It was more about what I'm leaving on the table every single month and where I could be compared to what I was doing at that time. So if you're selling, the takeaway for you here is always challenge your prospects based on their current reality compared to where they could be.
