When your prospect says, "I need time to think," it doesn't mean that they're not interested. It really means, "I can't explain this decision to myself or to someone else yet." When your prospect says, "It's too expensive," it doesn't mean that it's about the price. It really means, "I don't see how this will make or save me more money compared to how much it costs." When your prospect says, "I'm not ready," it does not mean, "Maybe I'll do this later." It really means, "I don't know what will get worse if I don't do this right now." When your prospect says that they're broke, it doesn't mean that they're scared to spend money. It really means they don't believe it will work for someone like them. When your prospect says, "I need to sleep on it," it doesn't mean they need to wait a day. It really means there are questions you haven't answered yet.
