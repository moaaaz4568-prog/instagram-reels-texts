You'll get ghosted after perfect meetings. The ones that felt magical, those prospects vanish into the sales Bermuda Triangle. The second thing they don't tell you is that your real enemy isn't other companies, it's do nothing. Status quo is a serial killer of sales. The third thing is emotional math. Rejection hits more than regret. The deal that you didn't chase haunts you more than the one they said no to. Also remember, people lie. Prospects lie more than politicians. "So as it approved" means I haven't asked yet. "Next week" means never. And remember, it's a mental warfare game. You will question everything after three rejections in a row. Your confidence is made of glass and hope.
