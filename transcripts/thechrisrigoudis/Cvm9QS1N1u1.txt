If you want to make the perfect sales presentation, you need to take notes. But most people, they take 5011 paragraphs that lead nowhere. Instead, focus on these five points. Number one, hell, their current situation. Number two, heaven, what do they want to achieve? Three, the bridge, what are they currently using to go from hell to heaven? Four, what don't they like about their current bridge? Obviously, they're talking to you, so they don't like something. Five, what are the consequences of staying on that bridge? What is it costing them staying on that bridge and not buy your thing? This will stretch the gap, and once you mention those points in your sales presentation, it will be a no-brainer for them to buy.
