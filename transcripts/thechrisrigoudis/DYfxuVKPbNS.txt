Overcoming your prospect's money objections. When your prospect says, "I need to look at my finances," they really mean, "I can afford it, I just want to make sure you're worth it." When your prospect says, "What's the ROI?" what they really mean is, "Justify this purchase so I can justify it to myself." When your prospect says, "It's a lot of money," what they really mean is, "I need you to make me feel okay about spending this much." Overcoming your prospect's money objections is just good communication. And if you want to know how to overcome all of your prospect's objections, follow for more sales tips.
