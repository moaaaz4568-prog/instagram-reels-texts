Do you know why people don't buy your stuff? It's because you're telling them your prices, which is the cost of action, but you're not showing them the cost of inaction. And the challenge with it is your cost is visible. They can hear it and they can see it. It's the money that they have to invest. But the cost of inaction is invisible. So we have to show them, to get them to realize what are they missing out in terms of time, in terms of money, in terms of energy by not investing, by not making a change. So how you're able to do that is you have to ask them, well, this is too expensive. If they say this is too expensive, you have to say, well, that's the problem. But have you considered what happens if you don't make a change and things stay exactly the same, status quo? You have to remind them that action is expensive, but inaction costs a fortune.
