When the buyer says just tell me the price, don't reply with that depends. We have multiple options. Instead, say let's dig in to make sure we can help you first. I would hate to sell you something that doesn't benefit you. Because when you send them, we have multiple options. The buyer's brain flags you as a sales professional. They're thinking this guy is trying to get the highest amount of money possible out of me. Instead of seeing you as a trusted advisor who's there to help them.
