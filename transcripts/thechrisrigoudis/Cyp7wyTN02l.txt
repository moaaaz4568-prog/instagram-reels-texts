Big misconception in sales is trying to sell anything to anyone. Hey, I can sell ice to Eskimos. It's not about that at all, right? Because you can spend 20 hours selling to one person and convincing them and discounting and doing everything you can to get the sale, or you can spend that same amount of time selling to five people who actually want what you sell. So always remember, it's not about selling anything to anyone or hard closing everyone. It's about seeing if there is a sale in the first place. You tell me which one is a better use of your time.
