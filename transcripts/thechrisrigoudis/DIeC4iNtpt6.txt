How you pre-handle money is super simple. You say something like this: "Chris, as you might understand, it takes an investment when it comes to installing a new sales process in your business. So what have you set aside so you can attract those 15 leads that we talked about earlier on?" That's how you get an idea around what they have set aside. Never use the word budget because it immediately triggers them to think about cost, and instead ask them what have they set aside, or how much of a percentage of the money that you will make are you willing to invest so you can see that increase in sales. So always position it as a percentage of the money that they're going to invest to get the result, instead of the predetermined budget that they have set aside.
