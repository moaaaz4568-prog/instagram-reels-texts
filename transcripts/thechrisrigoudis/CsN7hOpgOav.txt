The fundamental error in objection handling is focusing on the objections. Here is what I mean: Your goal isn't to help your clients overcome their objections. Your goal is to help them overcome themselves, their mindset, their beliefs, their false assumptions. Especially when it comes to B two B services, a lot of people have bad experience with previous vendors or have a specific mindset and beliefs that that's holding them back from getting their business to the next level. Your stance and your certainty during that conversation should challenge their mindset, the person who they are, instead of their words. When you do this properly, you'll stop hearing "you are right," which means nothing. It doesn't move the conversation forward. You'll start hearing, "you know, actually that's a good point," which it makes them realize that the objection was silly in the first place. So buy less into what they say, buy more into what they mean.
