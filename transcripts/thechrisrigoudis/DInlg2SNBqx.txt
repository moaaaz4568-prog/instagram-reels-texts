Confused minds don't buy. The big mistake that people are making here is they're just being a tour guide. They're just answering every single question the prospect has and they're showing every single thing that they're doing. Which does the prospect really care about every single detail, every single button, every single service and task you do for them? All they care about is, am I getting the end result? Am I getting my problems fixed? So the more irrelevant technical points you throw at them during your presentation, the more confused your prospect becomes. Remember.
