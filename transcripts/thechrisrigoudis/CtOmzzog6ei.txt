Getting clients can be the easiest thing. The key to scale your business, your agency, is enrolling clients in a container where they don't suck you dry. Have a situation where instead of one boss in your previous job, you have fifty bosses that pay you little but ask for a lot and they drag you around. That's where you have to come in, strike the right deal with the right offers. You can have few clients, small team that keeps you highly profitable, still have a business that fuels your lifestyle.
