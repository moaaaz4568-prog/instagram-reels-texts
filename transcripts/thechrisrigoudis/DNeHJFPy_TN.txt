A sad fact about human nature is most people don't love you, they love what you give them: attention, validation, access, benefits. In psychology, this is called reciprocal liking. People attach to the source of their reward, not the person themselves. So stop giving once in a while and see what happens. When someone values you, being you, more than you giving them something, you know that person is loyal. Everyone else that steps away was just renting you.
