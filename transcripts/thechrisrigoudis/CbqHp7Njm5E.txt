If you ever send a marketing email out and you got replies back with, "Wow, amazing! This is the best thing ever! Great tips! Love the email!" You should print out that email and burn it. Because the goal isn't to get you compliments. The goal is to guide your audience to the best next step. Whether that's making a purchase, whether that's driving them to a call, whether it's jumping on the chat with them. Whatever message was that piece that you sent out, it never really resonated. Whether that was the pain point or the story that you used or the insight that you wanted to communicate, it went over their head. So next time be on the lookout for those responses so you know what to.
