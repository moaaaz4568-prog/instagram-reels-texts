What are the formulas for ridge regression? Ridge regression adds a penalty term to the cost function of linear regression in order to shrink the coefficients of the predictors towards zero. The penalty term is proportional to the squared magnitude of the coefficients. Cost function equals SSE plus lambda sigma beta squared. SSE is the sum of squared errors between the predicted and actual values of the dependent variable. Lambda is the tuning parameter that controls the strength of the penalty, also known as the regularization parameter. Beta is the coefficient of each predictor.
