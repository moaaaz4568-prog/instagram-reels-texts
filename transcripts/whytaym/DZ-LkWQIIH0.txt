I am twenty years old and I have the belief that whoever I call, I can convince them into meeting me. This is not because I've mastered my script. This is not because I have remembered every word my script has to offer. This is because I understand that everyone is different. Everyone likes me talking in different tonalities. Everyone likes different compliments. Everyone has different personalities. So when I call that certain person or when I call different people, I don't do the same thing over and over again. Depends on how they respond to me, I talk. Do this if you want to convert every single cold call into a meeting.
