To all the salespeople out there, it's okay to say no to your clients. It's okay to reject them sometimes. When they come to you with an idea in mind, if you do not think it is best as an expert to do so, reject them. Suggest what you think is best instead of just agreeing with what they have in mind for the sake of closing the deal. You rejecting them builds that trust. So if you think what your client is requesting is not so suitable for him, suggest what's better.
