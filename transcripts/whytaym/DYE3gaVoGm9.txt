One of the most common mistakes I see a lot of sales people do is they walk into the meeting and immediately they start pitching, talking about their service, their product, and how they can make the prospect more money. This isn't how it works. What you need to take into consideration is the eighty twenty rule. You need to let the prospect speak eighty percent of the time and you only speak twenty percent. That way they're gonna indirectly tell you what they need and how you can help them and their pain points, and then you go ahead and pitch how you can help them.
