You've done your cold call, you've booked the meeting, now you're meeting with the person. The most common thing I see salespeople do and waste their time on is meet with the wrong person. A person who isn't able to make a decision. What you need to do in order to be more efficient and save time for yourself, make sure the person you're meeting with is able to take a decision so that when you pitch them, you know whether it's a yes or a no.
