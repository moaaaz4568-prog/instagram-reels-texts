I've came across a lot of salespeople in my life, and one thing that makes high performers stand out is the habit of blocking two things on their calendar on a daily basis. No matter how busy they get, how packed their day is, they will always do this. Number one, outreach. They will always look for leads. They will always outreach. They will always do anything and everything to keep their pipeline full because a full pipeline prevents panic. Number two, industry research. They target one to two industries and study it like they want to open a business in that industry. That way, when they sit with a prospect, they're considered an expert.
