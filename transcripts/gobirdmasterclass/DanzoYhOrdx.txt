Here are five design trends you need to know right now. First, bold minimalism: clean layouts, strong typography, and less noise, but more impact. Second, AI-powered creativity: designers are now blending human taste with AI tools to create faster and more experimental visuals. Third, chrome and metallic textures: shiny, reflective styles are coming back, giving designs a futuristic, high-end feel. Fourth, retro futurism: a mix of old-school aesthetics with modern digital elements, inspired by early internet and '80s sci-fi.
