How to price my service? It's simple, and I personally use this formula to calculate the exact price that won't scare the client away and won't leave me underpaid. And it's called the SCOPE formula, and it's a simple framework to price your work. So let's break it down. S means skills, your experience, editing ability, results, and reputation. C means complexity. Is it a simple talking head video, a faceless edit, or advanced motion graphics? O means outcome. Is it a regular social post or an ad designed to generate sales? P means project size. How many videos and how long they are? Is it a monthly package or a one-time project? E means extras, rush delivery, extra revisions, multiple formats, or delivering all the project files.
