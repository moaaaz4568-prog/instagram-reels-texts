The only difference between great days and bad days is whether or not I follow these four things directly in my control. So I have a sticky note on my computer that has four simple commandments that lock me into focus before interacting with my team, prospects, partners, clients, friends, or enemies. I call this Stoic Stack: Neutral, Prepared, Clear, Concise. Neutral to everything. Good news or bad. Never too high, never too low. Be prepared. Preparation unlocks everything else. If you show up prepared, you're dangerous with a perceived IQ that jumps 50%. Clear. Because I'm prepared, I'm clear on what I want, how I want it, why I want it, and how to execute it. And last is concise. Communicate that clarity in as few words as possible. Because brevity is the stuff of savages. And I work on these every day because they are the traits I admire most in others.
