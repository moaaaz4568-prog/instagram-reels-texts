Doing hard things voluntarily is cool. Had been since back before Marcus Aurelius started writing about. But I also recommend not shying away from the hard things you didn't choose. The real hard stuff thrown at you by life. Face it in the same spirit. This will make me better. This is shaping who I am to become. True loss, grief, pain, failure, and betrayal are not as sexy as an ice bath, yet doubly as effective at building character. Real growth often comes from how we handle ourselves in the battles that we didn't choose. This is a reminder to self, so tag anybody who might need a reminder too.
