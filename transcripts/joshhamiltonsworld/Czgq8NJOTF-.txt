A lot of people have died on me, which is probably why I talk about mortality a lot. I've developed a way of thinking about grief that's become pretty useful to me. See, for me, grief is not an emotion to move away from. It's a reality to move forward with. Coping is not about forgetting those we love. It's about finding ways to keep their memory alive through the act of living. They only live on through us by choice. We pay our respects by leading a life that honors the best of who they were, by carrying it forward within you.
