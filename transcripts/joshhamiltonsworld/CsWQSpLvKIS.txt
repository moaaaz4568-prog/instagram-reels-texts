Business rebellion in two steps. Step number one, build a business that helps people by solving a real problem in a better way. Step number two, do so profitably. Don't overcomplicate it. Solving a problem is all the purpose you need, and the profits will allow you to grow, both rewarding you for and accelerating the core function of the business, solving the problem. This is a rebellious act in the face of the current norm, which seems to be building businesses focused solely on top line revenue, like a lot of these money at all cost Instagram morons selling air that we all put on a pedestal.
