Are you struggling to outsource your service delivery and build a good team? Well, I kinda had the same problem, and here's how I solved it. There was no mysterious switch I hit to magically make incredible but still affordable talent appear in front of me. I sat down and spent a whole day building a course for my service delivery, super detailed, super in-depth. It took time, but once it was done, pretty much everybody that watched that course was able to deliver my service with very minimal margins for errors or mistakes. Combine that with some intensive feedback and daily calls for the first few days, and you're set.
