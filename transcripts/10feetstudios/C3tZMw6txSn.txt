This is a huge mistake that I didn't even realize people are still making on sales calls, and if you're doing this, it is costing you thousands of dollars. I'm talking about not collecting the cash or any kind of commitment on the sales call. I guess you might think it's too pushy to collect the payment on the call, but if you think about it, there really shouldn't be any reason for them not to pay as long as they're convinced with your offer and their finances are good. If any of these are the case, then you should go straight into objection handling because sending an invoice later won't solve any of these issues and chances are that invoice won't ever get paid. And I'm talking from experience because I know I sent way too many invoices that never got paid.
