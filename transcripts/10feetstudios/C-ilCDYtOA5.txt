How do we as content agency owners actually choose a good niche? There's so many different opinions on this. Here's what I think you need to know. Look, the goal is to stick to your niche, obsess over your clients, and ultimately get to the point where you possibly know more about your clients than they know about themselves. And if you want to get to that point, then you simply cannot work with people that you don't like. So the most important thing is to go into a niche that you're actually interested in and ideally had some past experiences in. I'll give you an example. One of my clients went into the niche of online business coaches and he hated them. He thought a lot of them were super scammy and he didn't even want to work with them. He himself though was a sports content creator and also coaches other people on sports and now he's working with sports coaches and he's loving it because he actually has a genuine interest in these people. And I love working with content agencies because I've been in their same shoes, I've had the same problems and I know how life changing it can be to grow and scale your agency. Now obviously make sure that your niche isn't broke and that you actually solve a real problem, but I think the most important thing is to go into a niche that you actually love.
