A lot of people make this one huge mistake when building an agency. You're building a business that you don't really want to run. If you could make 20k a month profit every single month starting tomorrow, but you would have to work 12 hours a day, all day, every day, seven days a week. Would you still do it? Probably not, but most of you guys are building a business like that. I know that because I was doing the same thing and I was working eight hours plus a day just editing videos all day long. Look, I was already making really decent money at that point, but I realized that I didn't come onto this world to edit videos all the time. So I decided to build out systems, hire people, and I started to build my business in a way where it actually supports my lifestyle. I recommend you to do the same and start with that as soon as you can, so you don't build a business and then look at it and say, what the fuck did I just build? I don't even want this.
