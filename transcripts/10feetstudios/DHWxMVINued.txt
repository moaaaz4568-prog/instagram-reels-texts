Here's why you're working 10 hours a day but are still stuck, overworked, and underpaid. See, there's a big difference between being busy and being productive. Being busy is stuff like editing videos all day long, scripting videos, and just servicing your clients in general. Being productive, on the other hand, is determining the actual bottlenecks within your business and solving them. Stuff like learning how to book more calls, optimizing your offer to get better results for your clients, or building a workflow systems and hiring the right team to take care of your clients. The truth is you can fill a full day of work without ever attempting to solve any of these problems. But if you never solve them, there is always going to be this ceiling that you can't go beyond. So if you're stuck, it simply means that you're working on the wrong things.
