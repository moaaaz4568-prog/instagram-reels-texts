There are these two huge mistakes that agency owners make when starting a personal brand, and you need to avoid them. First off, if you are not at at least five k a month, don't put too much time into creating content. I see people starting out and putting all of their efforts into creating content, thinking they're kind of magically gonna start getting clients from it. Guys, most people that you see having a personal brand run ads towards it to get clients from it, and for that you need to have a budget and cash flow. So if you're still below five k a month, spend most of your time on outreach to actually get those first initial clients and get some cash flow in. And then the other thing I see is people making personal brands that are not actually their personal brands. They're acting like somebody they're not.
