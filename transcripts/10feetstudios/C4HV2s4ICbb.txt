I hate sales, but luckily I don't really have to sell anymore. And when I say I hate sales, I mean getting on calls with prospects who are cold or not qualified. If you keep doing that, you'll get objections all day long. It's not the right time for me, or it's too expensive and not really within my budget. I have to think about it. Let me let me sleep on it, and then you book a follow up call and they no show anyway. You can try to handle all those objections, but to be honest, it's just exhausting. So what you should do instead, and what's been working for me, is just creating content around the common objections that my prospects have and kind of just pre-selling with my content. That way, pretty much only qualified prospects come onto the sales call, and the sales call kind of just turns into an onboarding call.
