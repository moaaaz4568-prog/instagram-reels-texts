I took five hundred and ninety one sales calls in the last year, and here are three things I learned that helped me achieve a seventy seven percent close rate. First, stop thinking about sales as a way to just make more money. Sales isn't about pushing people to buy; it's about helping people make the right decisions. Next time you are on a sales call, actually listen to your prospect's problems, and if you truly believe that your offer can solve them, then help them see why. And if you can't help them, don't sell. That alone will take so much pressure off of your calls. Second, believe in what you are selling. If you don't have conviction, why would somebody trust you with their hard earned dollars and give you thousands of them? If your offer isn't great, just go and fix it. Because if you genuinely believe in what you are selling, then sales becomes ten times easier. And third, my hard rule never schedule any follow ups. Actually learn how to handle objections, and if you come in sales, then I will send you a full breakdown of how to handle the most common objections on calls.
