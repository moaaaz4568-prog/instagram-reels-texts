I got broken up with five days into college. Here's how it landed me an internship that pays almost $1,000 a week. After crying for hours on end and then trying to lock in and then repeating the cycle, I went feral. Because I realized something. I had gone from an inexperienced 18-year-old to a person who had gone through something incredibly relatable. I went to every networking event I could find, I messaged every single person I knew on LinkedIn, and I started conversations by talking about my breakup. And after just a few days of networking, just by being my authentic self, I landed my first tech internship that paid.
