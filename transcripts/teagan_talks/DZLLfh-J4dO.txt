I genuinely used to be one of the most deeply insecure people I know, and the only reason why I was able to make it out the other side months later is because I began working in startup growth and marketing. Early stage growth is all about speed of trial and error. How fast can you execute on a crazy idea? How fast can you recognize if it failed, learn from it, and then try again? Being able to quickly rotate through that cycle is what makes somebody good at growth. Now conversely, being bad at growth is not being able to rotate through that cycle or making assumptions before you execute. In my time working in startup growth and marketing, I've practiced the art of not making assumptions and I've applied this mindset to my life. Insecurity is really all about untested assumptions. You assume that you're gonna get judged for something, you assume you aren't capable of doing something, you assume that people have malintent towards you. And confidence comes from execution. So I urge you, if you're somebody who struggles with not feeling self-assured or not feeling confident, be an executor first, be action-oriented, be somebody who tests out the opinions that you have about yourself before you develop them.
