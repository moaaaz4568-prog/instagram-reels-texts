Remember, Twitch began as a camera strapped to a founder's head. Spanx began as Sara Blakely cutting up pantyhose. Nike's first store was the trunk of a car. Reddit began as a single page full of links submitted by their founders. Zappos was a website full of pictures of shoes that weren't even in stock. And Groupon began as a scrappy WordPress blog. Your MVP doesn't need to be anywhere near perfect. You just need to get it out there.
