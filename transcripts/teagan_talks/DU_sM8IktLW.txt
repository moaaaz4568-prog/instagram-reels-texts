Since getting into startups in college, I feel like I've become a much weirder person. I don't mean that in a bad way, but I do feel like in high school, most of my decisions were optimized for fitting in. I dressed the same as everybody else, I had normal goals, I wanted normal things. And now I feel like in the startup industry, I'm intentionally doing things that help me stand out. And I think that's a good thing because I'm no longer allowing other people's expectations to dictate how I live my life. And it's also lonelier. There are times when I'm just such a different person now than I was before that I don't even feel like I know myself. But ultimately, doing things that surprise you and living unpredictably are such valuable traits for an entrepreneur to have. I'm trying to develop gratitude for feeling a little bit confused about who I am because I think that actually is a sign of progress. That's my "yap" for today.
