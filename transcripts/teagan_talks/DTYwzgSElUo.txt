If you want to win at startups, you need to begin practicing delusion. And I don't mean delusion in the sense that you lie to yourself, but I do mean being audacious enough to believe in a future that other people don't see yet. The tech industry is so unstable and very speculative. Nobody really knows for sure what's going to explode next. And on top of that, the market is completely oversaturated with ideas that are basically carbon copies of each other. Even the world's fastest-growing startups right now are very easily duplicated. Which is why I think that more than ever, people aren't just betting on ideas alone. They're betting on founder-first ideology. Because in such an unstable environment, if you're able to provide a sort of delusional confidence in your ideas and your ability to execute, you're already one step ahead of your competitors.
