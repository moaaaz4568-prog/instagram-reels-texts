Pretty clear that everybody's just sick of seeing AI taking over everything on social media. But it can be really useful for you. So this is what I recommend if you want to still leverage AI to make your life easier with content, while at the same time not falling into the AI trap. So you want to use it for things like initial research or helping you build out a structure or helping you get your ideas fleshed out. But what AI really can't replace for you is things like coming up with the ideas or giving personal examples, writing out your script word for word, or even writing your captions. Because first of all, everybody can tell the AI voice. Marketing is all about getting people to feel something so strongly enough that they take an action. And AI can never do that for you.
