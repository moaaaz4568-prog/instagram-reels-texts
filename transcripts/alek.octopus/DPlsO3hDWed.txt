Alex, what's the most important part of sales? Is it closing? No, the most important part is making people feel understood. How to do it? One of the best techniques that I can teach you today is simply adding "it seems like," "it sounds like," and "from what I'm hearing" in front of the sentence and repeating exactly what they said. It looks like this: "It sounds like from what you're telling me, you're struggling with closing." "From what I've heard, it sounds like you're struggling to get people to buy." And then you add, "Is that correct?" This makes people feel understood, that increases the trust in you, and it helps you to close more deals.
