What are three hypnotic words I should always use in sales? I'll give you my best ones, but you need to promise me that you will use them wisely. I promise. The most powerful word that you can use in business is imagine. It helps your clients to picture the ideal scenario for them and to relate it to the emotion that they would feel by achieving it. Number two is because. Because appeals to the logical side of the brain, justifying the transaction that they're making and the emotion that they're feeling. And finally, what if is a psychological word that relates to the cognitive bias of loss aversion, which states that people are willing to value more things that they're about to lose rather than things that they already own. Use those three words to make more sales.
