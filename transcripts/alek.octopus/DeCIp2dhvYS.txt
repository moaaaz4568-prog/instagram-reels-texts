Ten out of ten habits that make you dangerously persuasive, and the last one it's kind of crazy, but it works. Number one, sell this mug in ten seconds. The only twist is you can't actually say the word mug. You have to explain what it does and how it helps the person. Number two, put up an interview, mute it, and try to find out the mood. Then unmute it, replay the part, and see how wrong were you. Number three, explain your job with words that a six-year-old can understand. No job title allowed. Number four, tell the same story as good news and then bad news, and see how your tone changes every time you say. Number five, ask a friend to stop you each time you say "um," "uh," "um." Try to do one clean minute without stuttering. You see, you don't need more fancy words. You just need to actually use the right tone and the right sentences at the right time, and this is exactly how you become dangerously persuasive.
