The problem that most people when they're building their businesses don't realize is that oftentimes the change isn't in building a whole new process. It's mostly about working on the basics so hard until you've mastered them. And so they're thinking about this big change and the big change never happens because they haven't even mastered how to do the little changes that they've implemented along the way. And so probably if you're looking to scale your business, easiest thing to look at is how do I improve just a couple of percentages of close rate? How to actually get an extra 10 leads a week? How to get an extra 20 leads? And so if you figure out the basics, most times it will change the entire business.
