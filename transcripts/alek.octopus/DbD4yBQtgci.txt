My favorite sales trick to teach people is to actually give a fuck. I mean, a lot of people care about selling, but they don't necessarily care genuinely about the client. Instead of thinking about converting the sale, think about how you can add more health, more wealth, more happiness to your clients. Think about their transformation and what they stand to gain. And if you do that, by nature, you also make more money. So yeah, I guess actually give a fuck.
