Honestly, I don't take business advice anymore from anybody that doesn't have children. Some twenty-something year old telling you to just work sixteen hours a day or something. These people are completely out of touch with reality. First of all, most people have nine to five jobs, so they don't have sixteen hours a day. They're lucky if they can fit in like two hours a day to work on their business. And then full time entrepreneurs like myself who have children definitely don't have sixteen hours a day. Even me, I'm lucky to get in like four to five quality hours of work a day. So I would say be careful listening to anybody who's giving you advice who's not in a similar situation as you or who hasn't been there at least.
