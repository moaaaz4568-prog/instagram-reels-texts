You could have the most amazing product in the world. It could change people's lives. It could be the best of the best. But if no one knows that product exists, or no one knows you exist, or no one understands the value of that product because you weren't able to communicate it properly, then it doesn't matter how good the product is. It doesn't matter how good you are at what you do. What matters is your ability to communicate your value to other people. That is the most important skill that you could master in business.
