Afraid to share your message with the world because of what people might think? Here's a perspective that's helped me overcome imposter syndrome. When I publish anything online, a part of me really wishes that everyone would like it. But here's the truth. When you create something, 99% of people will either not like it, not be impacted by it, or simply not care that much about it. But that's not who you're doing it for. When you put something out there, it's for the 1%, not the 99. So adjust your expectations. Most people will get little to no value from what you offer, and that's normal. Your message is not meant for everyone, but a small percentage of people absolutely will get value from what you have to say. Keep those people in mind when you're creating, not the others.
