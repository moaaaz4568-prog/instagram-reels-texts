If you get zero replies on your outreach, watch this video. Most of you guys open with the pitch. The first message is basically, "Here is what I do. Want to call?" Called prospects see that, and they block you. So I stopped pitching in the first message. Instead, I lead with something valuable, something free—a video, a quick insight, something that's actually useful to the prospect. Plus a soft CTA without any pressure. And reply rates went up. Not because I changed the offer, but because I started genuinely connecting with each prospect.
