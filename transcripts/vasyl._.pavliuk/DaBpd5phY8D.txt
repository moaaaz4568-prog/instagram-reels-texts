Your subject line is your open rate. Keep it under four words. Plain text emails always outperform designed ones. A bad list will kill a good copy every time. Never open with "I." Make the first word about them. Make the ask so small they feel silly saying no. Lead with their pain, not your pitch. Never attach files to cold emails. Test variables one at a time, never all of them at once. Warm up every domain for at least three to four weeks.
