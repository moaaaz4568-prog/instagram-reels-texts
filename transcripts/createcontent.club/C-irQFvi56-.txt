You will never go viral on Instagram if you don't know about the secret marketing strategy that uses psychology to get people to watch all of your videos. This principle is called loss aversion, and in marketing it's often used to push people to buy anything. But you can apply it to content as well to get people to watch all of your videos and get millions of views. Here's how it works: People pay more attention when they know they are going to miss out on something than they do when they know that they are going to gain something. So whenever you create a new video, make sure that your hook and the video's heart are more focused on making people feel like they're missing out on something if they don't watch the entire video rather than just giving value.
