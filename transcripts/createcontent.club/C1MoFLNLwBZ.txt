This is how to reprogram your brain. Think of your brain like a body. If you only eat highly processed and unhealthy food for an entire year, your body will suffer. But if you choose organic and healthy food, your body becomes healthier and stronger. It's the same thing with your mind. The food for our brain is information we consume. By carefully selecting what we feed it, we can reprogram our brain, making it stronger and healthier. I've noticed that when I'm in a highly productive and healthy state, I tend to consume content and knowledge from people who represent the version of myself I aspire to be. On the other hand, during those times when I feel weaker and less motivated, my content choices reflect that. So pay close attention to what you consume, because in many ways, that defines who you become.
