Balance is a myth. If you want to achieve great things in life, if you want to do anything that is above average, you can't take the average person balance and hope it will work for you. You need focus, or at the very least, you need phases in your life in which you are willing to truly focus and obsess on one single thing. This is what I realized both when building my dream physique and now when building my first profitable business. In both of these experiences... The first step that allowed me to have that breakthrough was pushing to the extremes. Going in depth and obsessing over one single thing. Don't get me wrong, this is not a sustainable strategy. It's not something that you will want to do for the rest of your life, but it's what you need to break through in the first place.
