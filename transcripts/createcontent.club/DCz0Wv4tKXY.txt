Have you ever seen one of those videos in which you keep hearing the person talking even if the clip already changed? This makes the video feel smoother and keeps people's attention way more than simple cuts, and it's actually really simple to do. The principle is called L-cut, and the name comes from the shape that the clips make when seen on your timeline. To recreate it, all you need to do is open your editing software of choice, place your two clips, and then take the audio of the first one and expand it to... continue while the second clip is playing, just like this.
