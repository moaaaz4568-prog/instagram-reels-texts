You're trying to get more clients and you run more ads or you do more outreach but you don't realize that you're literally trying to fill a bucket with a hole in it. Even if you manage to get your costs down on ads or you improve your reply rate or your outreach by 5%, you know that it's not scalable. And the reason why is very simple: People don't trust you. When they see your ads or when they see you in their inbox, what do you think is the first thing that they will do? They search you up. They look at your website. In a world where we have a massive trust recession, the first thing that goes through their mind will be: Is this person even legit? And guess what will make them trust you? Correct. Your personal brand. Does your content scream, 'I can trust this guy'? Or does it say, 'I'm just another coach trying to sell you something'?
