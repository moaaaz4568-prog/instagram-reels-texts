I own a content agency, and creating content is actually one of the smallest parts of my job. Here's what I actually do every day as the owner of a content agency. A lot of people probably assume that I spend all day editing videos and posting them on Instagram, but that's only a small part of it. I'm reaching out to potential clients, taking sales calls, building strategies, and figuring out what every client should actually be posting. I'm also directing my content creators, managing feedback, and making sure that everything stays on schedule. And then there's the whole other business side to it: improving our offers, managing expenses, refining our systems, hiring, and finding ways to deliver better results. So running a content agency is really a mix of creativity, sales, strategy, managing teams, and occasionally putting out fires. And somehow all of that still has to happen while I create my own content.
