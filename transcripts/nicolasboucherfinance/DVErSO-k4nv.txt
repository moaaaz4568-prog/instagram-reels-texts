If you think you are a smart CFO because you have tons of AI tools, you are not a smart CFO. You actually don't need a lot of AI tools. You need the right one for you. What is the right one? The one that connects to your work data. You are in a Microsoft environment? Take Copilot. You use Google everywhere? Good news, in your Google Workspace license you already have Gemini. You are a fractional CFO, a consultant or a small team? You can choose ChatGPT or Claude, but then you need to connect your working document yourself.
