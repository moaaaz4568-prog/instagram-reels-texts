How you can do this slide animation in Premiere Pro. Start off by positioning the text where you want it to be and add the transform effect to it. Add three keyframes positioning one from the start, middle, and end. That way you can get this nice movement effect in the middle. Crank the first keyframes a crap ton, same with the last one, don't touch the middle one. Scroll down, uncheck use composition shutter and crank shutter angle all the way for a nice movement blur.
