After flying out to Tampa Bay, Florida, and meeting my millionaire coach, here is the biggest lesson I learned from him that made him wildly successful. He doesn't copy anybody. He figured out what works for him, how he wants to work, live, and grow as a human being. And anytime anyone says, "But this works better," he's like, "Cool, that's great for you." The mindset just hit me really hard. Most people are so busy following what's working for others, they never figure out actually what works for them. So if you want to succeed long term, stop copying, start paying attention to what actually works for you, what you like, and just stick to it and build from that.
