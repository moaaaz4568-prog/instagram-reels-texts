If your hook doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters. Hooks are the most important for short-form content, and you have only three to five seconds to grab someone's attention. Here's an example of a bad hook: Hey guys, so I wanted to talk about. . . That's so boring. You're already losing viewers. Now here's an example of a good hook: Want to know how I doubled my followers in 30 days? That's a good hook. It makes you curious and want to stick around.
