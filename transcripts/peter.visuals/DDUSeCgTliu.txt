Four mistakes to avoid when pricing your products. Number one, ignoring psychology. Ever noticed how $99 feels cheaper than $100? That's psychological pricing. Use small tweaks like charm pricing to make your product irresistible. Number two, underpricing. Setting prices too low can make your product seem cheap and hurt profits. Price based on value, not just cost. Number three, not testing pricing. Never assume your price is perfect. Test, tweak, and find what works best for your audience. Number four, too many options. Confusing your customers with endless tiers leads to no sales. Keep it simple with two to three clear choices.
