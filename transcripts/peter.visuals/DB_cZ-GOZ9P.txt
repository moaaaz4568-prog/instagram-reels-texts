Two quick Instagram tips for content creators. Tip one, don't get stuck on making everything look perfect. Content that provides real value often beats a polished video without any value. So, stop obsessing over little details. If your video has value, that's good enough. Tip two, stop wasting your time curating the best hashtag list. Honestly, hashtags don't really matter. Even the Instagram CEO has confirmed that hashtags don't magically make you go viral. Instead, focus on making good content that adds value and optimize your posts for SEO.
