There are three types of videos that are blowing up right now, and they work in every niche. Number one is pointing reels. This is where you compare different things or scenarios in your niche by pointing at pop-ups on the screen. Number two, rating reels. Pit different stuff in your niche against each other using a tier list, a scale of one to ten, or a blind ranking format. And number three, clone conversations. Have two versions of you, the expert and the beginner, and have the expert teach something to the beginner.
