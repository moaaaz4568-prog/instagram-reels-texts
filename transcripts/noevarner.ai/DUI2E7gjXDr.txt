Everybody's talking about ClaudeBot right now, but nobody's talking about how dangerous it is. Hundreds of ClaudeBot instances are getting hacked and thousands of IP addresses are getting leaked onto the internet, which is a crazy security risk because now all of your personal information is on the internet for people to hack. So if you want to avoid this, do two things. One is buy a separate computer and put it locally on that device. Make sure that device is totally wiped so there's no personal information on it. Two is connect it to a cloud service so nobody can hack into it.
