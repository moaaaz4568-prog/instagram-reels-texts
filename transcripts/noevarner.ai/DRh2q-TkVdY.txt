No one talks about the learning curve that happens with N8N. People just see these fancy builds on Twitter and TikTok and think that if they just copy the JSON, they'll be able to use it too. But in reality, if you have no idea how to do the basics, you are so screwed. An example of this is an HTTP request. You don't even know what the fuck that is and you're still trying to put N8N builds together, then you got problems, buddy. What I did in the beginning to help the learning curve is I literally would just take each node that's installed in N8N and I would try on a some form of flow. I would fuck up over and over again until it made sense and I got it and then I learned. You downloading a bunch of JSONs from somebody is not gonna help you. Sorry.
