This is a hot take, but we are not in an AI bubble. And what I mean is regular businesses aren't in an AI bubble. AI businesses are in an AI bubble. With these regular businesses, they're just adopting AI technology. That doesn't put us in a bubble. That just advances the tech faster. But with all these AI companies, there are literally AI companies started every day. And just like any space, if there is a large supply of businesses but the demand stays the same, it's going to pop. So all of these AI startups and companies you see now, they're not going to exist in a year or two. But all the regular businesses, they'll be just fine.
