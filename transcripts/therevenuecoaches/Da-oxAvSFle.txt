The moment you say your price out loud, most founders panic. They keep talking, and that's the mistake. The first person to speak after the price is stated usually loses. They start justifying, discounting, negotiating against themselves before anyone reacts. So what do you do instead? You say the number and you say nothing else. That silence feels unbearable the first time. It's not your job to fill it. Half the time they'll fill it and they'll talk themselves into a yes.
