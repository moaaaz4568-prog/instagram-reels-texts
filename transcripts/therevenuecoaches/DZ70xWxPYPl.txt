42% of startups failed because nobody wanted what they built. And every single one of those founders thought they had a good product. Most of them did have a good product. But they spent two years building before they had a single real conversation with a paying customer. They did maybe surveys, they did beta groups, they did friends and family feedback. None of these people were ever going to tell them the truth. Nobody wants to crush someone's dream. So they launched, and the market told them what their network never would. By the time they figured out what the market actually needed, the runway was gone. The product was never the problem. Talking to real customers before building was the part they skipped.
