When you look at the top one percent of salespeople, they do not tell stories because when you're telling stories, the buyer is just listening. Top sellers will flip it. They'll get the buyer to talk. They'll ask, they'll probe, and they'll slow it down to let the other person go much deeper into their problems. People do not believe your story as much as they believe their own words. Ask more questions. Try and talk less. That will win you more deals.
