When a prospect tells you, "I need to think about it," don't book that follow-up. That's how a deal dies. Do this instead. Instead, say this word for word: "What is the one thing you're still deciding on right now?" Then stop talking. Let it hang. If they say nothing specific, just need time, say this: "That usually means it's a trade-off. Is it cost? Is it timing? Is it whether this solution solves your problem?" And if they still dodge, say this: "If we don't clarify now, a later call won't change anything." That's how you stay in control without adding pressure.
