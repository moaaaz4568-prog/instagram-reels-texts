Stop asking, "It's now a good time" on cold calls. That is killing your conversations. That question is giving prospects an easy way out before you say anything that matters. Say this instead: "This is a cold call. I'll be brief. The reason for my call is..." You didn't ask for permission. You framed the call, respected the time, and gave context. If it's relevant, they stay. If it's not, they stop you. Either way, you stay in control.
