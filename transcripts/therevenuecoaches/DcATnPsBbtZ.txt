Stop trying to get 100 customers. Get to 50 first. The rest gets easier. Three phases to get there. Phase one, your first 10. Founder-led, one conversation at a time, no automation. If you automate this phase, you're automating a process you haven't proven works yet. Phase two, your next 25. This is where you build your first repeatable process. Phase three, your final 15. This is where you test if it works without you on every call. Skip a phase and the whole thing collapses at 51.
