If a prospect says, "I need to think about it. Can you send me an email?" and you immediately agree, you probably just lost that deal. That moment is not about missed information. It's about something that's unresolved. If they're missing details, they would ask. So slow it down and say this instead: "When you say you need to think about it, what is exactly holding you back? Is it the investment? Is it the timing? Is it working together?" Then stop talking. Email reinforces decisions. It does not create them. If it does not get resolved on the call, it rarely gets resolved later.
