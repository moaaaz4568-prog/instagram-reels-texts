Prospect says, "I'm already working with someone." Here's exactly what not to say: "I'm better than them." Don't say that, you sound defensive. Do these three steps instead. Step one: Acknowledge. Totally fair, that's not an issue. Step two: Clarify. Are you 100% satisfied with how it's working right now? No one is ever 100% satisfied. Step three: Guide. If there was one thing you could change to move faster, what would that be? By following these three simple steps, now they're telling you the gaps they have with who they're currently working with. That's how a deal moves.
