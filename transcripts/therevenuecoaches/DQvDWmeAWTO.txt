Stop overcomplicating sales. You only have to ask three questions and three questions only. What's your challenge right now? How does that make you feel? How much longer? These three questions do all the work for you. Ask them in this order and watch what happens next. The first question gets them to admit the problem. The second question gets them to feel the problem. The third question, this is where everything changes. When you ask them, how long are you willing to live with this problem? And they tell you, not another day? Boom, you close that deal.
