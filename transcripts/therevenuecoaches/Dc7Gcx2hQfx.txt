Every startup goes through five stages from prototype to scale-up. Prototype: pre-revenue and no paying clients yet. MVP: first clients but at a high cost to acquire them. Product market fit: selling in a repeatable, sustainable way. Go to market: repeatability growing at a pace and scaling what's already working. And scale-up: everything is firing on all cylinders.
