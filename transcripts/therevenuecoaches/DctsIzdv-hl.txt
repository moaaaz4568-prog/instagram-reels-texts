We keep seeing the same three things stall founders out right around one to two million in revenue. Mistake one, still running the business like you're pre-one million, still founder-led on every sale, still making every decision yourself when the business has already outgrown that. Mistake two, not hiring the right people for this stage. You need a head of sales, marketing lead, people who can own the function, not more generalists doing a little bit of everything. And mistake three, no proven repeatable go-to-market built for the next stage of your business. And posting once a week on LinkedIn, no, isn't a strategy. That's an activity, not a system. You didn't get stuck because the market changed. You got stuck because you didn't. Comment growth below if you're still running your business the way you did at $500, 000 in revenue.
