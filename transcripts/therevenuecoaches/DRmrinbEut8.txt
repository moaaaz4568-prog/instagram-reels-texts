Every company sets quarterly rocks, but most spend their weeks buried in pebbles and sand. Rocks are the priorities that actually drive revenue for your business, increasing your pipeline, fixing your conversion. Pebble is small and necessary for the business, internal updates, reporting, administrative tasks that keeps the business moving along. Sand is what is killing your whole growth. Lots of meetings, urgent fires, ad hoc requests, that is not driving any form of revenue. If you let sand and pebbles fill your week, there's no room left for the rocks. And this is why a lot of companies miss their targets, not from lack of effort, but from lack of focus.
