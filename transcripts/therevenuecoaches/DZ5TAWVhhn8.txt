There is a version of the founder-led sales problem everyone talks about—the founder who cannot let go, who inserts themselves in every deal, who can't trust the team to close without them. That founder exists, but the more common version is a founder who desperately wants to step back but cannot—not because of ego, because there's nothing to step back to. Nobody documented what they actually do in a sales conversation. Nobody built the sequence. Nobody figured out which objections come up and what resolves them. So the founder keeps selling it, not because they have a control problem, because handing over a process requires a process to exist first. The work is not letting go. The work is building what you need so someone else can sell it for you.
