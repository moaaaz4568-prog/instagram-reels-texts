If you keep hearing "I need to think about it," you probably pitched too early. Most founders present the solution before the buyer has actually felt the cost of the problem. If they say to you, "Yeah, sales have been slow lately," and you just jump into your pitch, you just skipped the most important part. Ask this instead: How long has it been like this? What has the cost been to you so far? And what happens if it stays like this another quarter? Now they're not describing the issue, now they're feeling it. People do not buy because your solution sounds good. They buy because staying the same feels worse. If you pitch before they feel the real pain, selling becomes uphill.
