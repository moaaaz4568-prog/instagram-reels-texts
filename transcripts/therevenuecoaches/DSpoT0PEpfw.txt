When you don't have a sales process, you run into one major problem: you experience low conversions. When you don't have a sales process, you don't know where you're leading the prospect to, and you tend to second guess yourself. And the moment you second guess yourself, the prospect feels it. In a world full of uncertainty, people crave certainty. Sales is the transfer of certainty. If you're unsure, they hesitate. If you're clear, they buy.
