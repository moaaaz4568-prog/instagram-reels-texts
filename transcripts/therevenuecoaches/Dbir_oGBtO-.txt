A lot of people think fractional means a discount version of a full-time hire. It's not that at all. Fractional means you get someone who's already solved this exact problem before, without paying full-time for a role you don't need full-time yet. The real comparison isn't fractional versus a full-time salary. It's fractional versus getting it wrong for six months with a full-timer who's still figuring out your market. One costs a fraction of a salary, the other costs a fraction of a salary plus six months you don't get back. It's not the cheap option, it's the right timing option.
