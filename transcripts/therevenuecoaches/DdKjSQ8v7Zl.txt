The second you discount, you've told them your price wasn't real. Discounting doesn't solve a budget objection; it confirms the price was never the real issue. Ask instead, if price weren't a factor, would this be a yes? If they hesitate, price was never the objection. Find the real one. A discount buys you a close, but it doesn't buy you a customer who believes in the value. Comment "growth below" for a pricing objection handling playbook.
