Social media is like a video game. There are certain skills and levels required to make over $100,000 a month as a content creator or a brand. You need to level up your writing skills, acquire copywriting and storytelling tactics to understand how to grab people's attention. Stack your content like you stack your in-game currency. Be consistent. Unlock new relationships by networking with people who rank higher on the social scale. Up your in-game hours. Build content systems to have three tweets a day, three short-form videos a week, and one long-form newsletter and video a week. Finally, understand how to harness your power and become a master to move your audience to purchase your offer.
