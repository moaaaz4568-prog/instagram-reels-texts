Three web design trends you need to know for 2025. Engage users with interactivity. Elements like hover effects, scroll-triggered animations, and interactive objects encourage users to spend more time on websites, which can lead to higher conversions. Incorporate nostalgic retro elements. The retro design movement is one way for brands to stand out in a sea of modern and minimalist sites. It can also tap into user nostalgia, creating powerful emotional connections. Delight users with micro-interactions. It enhances your users' website experience by adding small animations that guide user actions. But don't go too wild on this; you don't want to distract users too much.
