How to animate your Figma designs in seconds. First, search for this plugin, select your design and click copy. Open it in Jitter and hit the AI button. Choose the animation style you want and the AI will animate your design instantly. Boom! Your first version is ready. Now you can refine it in the timeline, make sequences longer or shorter, change the easing, or try different effects. Once you're happy, export it in your preferred format.
