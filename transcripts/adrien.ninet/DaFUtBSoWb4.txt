Cloud Design hit a million users in its first week, but everyone was burning their credits in fifteen minutes. They just fixed it and added five new features designers were waiting for. The last one is my favorite. First, the usage limits. It's now pooled with Cloud Chat, Code, and Co:here, so it lasts way longer. Next, design system imports. Run the 'design sync' command and Cloud pulls your real design system from a repo, a Figma file, or your codebase. So every screen it builds uses your actual components. Then they rebuilt the editor. You get real drag, resize, and align controls plus a full layer tree like Figma. So you tweak your designs manually without burning tokens on every change. There are also new export options—send your design to a zip, standalone HTML, or straight into other tools. And the big one, the Cloud Code handoff now goes both ways.
