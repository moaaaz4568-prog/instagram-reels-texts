Man-to-man, get out of your own damn way. I think one of the biggest things holding most people back is it's not money or connections or opportunity or whatever they're blaming the things on. It's overthinking everything. And I used to do this constantly. I'd think about an idea forever, I'd wonder if I was ready, I'd worry about what could go wrong, I'd basically talk myself out of doing things before I even tried them. But once I stopped... doing that, things started moving way, way faster and I started to make decisions quicker and started trying stuff sooner. Some worked, a lot of it transparently didn't, but at least I was moving forward. And you want to get an answer and you want to be able to move on to the next thing because you don't want to waste any more time.
