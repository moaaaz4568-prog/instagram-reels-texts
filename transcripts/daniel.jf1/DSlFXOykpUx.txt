I can't think of a worse opportunity than going and finding a small business and getting an SBA loan from the government and then purchasing that small business with this SBA loan because you are personally liable for the loan. Say you default on the loan and the business is terrible and the business falls apart. Not only are they going to take all the assets of the business, but they're going to take all of your personal assets as well and you're going to need to declare bankruptcy and then you are completely screwed. Now compare this to just keeping things extremely simple and starting a business that doesn't require you to get a $2 million loan that you're personally liable for. Instead, you could just go find a simple service business, sell that as a service to other companies for three grand a month, a simple marketing service, a lot of it can be fulfilled with AI, and then you just do some cold emails to get the clients and your total investment to be able to do this is maybe 300 bucks instead of $2 million.
