One time I saw a YouTube video and it was titled something like pretty interesting. It said, "What to say when a client asks for a guarantee." It was some younger dude who probably has never run an agency before ever in his life, and he was just selling a course on how to do it. And it said something along the lines of like, "Well, here's what you tell the prospect. Does anything in life have a guarantee?" Now this is just another version of you treating the prospect like an idiot. You managed to get a prospect on a sales call, you have zero case study, zero authority, and you tell this person, "Does anything in life have a guarantee?" And it's like you think they're like an idiot. Stop treating them like an idiot.
