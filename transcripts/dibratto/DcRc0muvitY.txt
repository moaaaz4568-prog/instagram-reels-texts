The art of the B2B customer testimonial. Ask your happiest customer for a quote about your product. They say, "Yeah, we like it, saves us some time." You send that to marketing. Marketing sends it back to you after workshopping it, where it says, "Product name has fundamentally transformed the way our business operates and unlocked tremendous efficiency gains across our workflows." You send it back to the customer. They say, "Yeah, looks good." Now add their headshot and company logo, add it to your homepage, and boom, you have social proof. Follow for more.
