What's a random forest algorithm in machine learning? Remember how I've talked about decision trees in my last video? Now imagine not just one, but a whole forest full of them. That's random forest. Let's say you want to know if a fruit is an apple. So you ask one friend like a decision tree and they say yes. But what if you ask ten friends and seven say yes and three say no? You'll probably believe the majority and that's exactly how random forest works. Each tree in the forest gives its answer, and the final result is based on voting. The most common answer wins. And which one is better? Which one should I trust? Of course random forest is better because if one tree makes a mistake, the others can help fix it. Just like how asking more friends can give you a better idea. So you're saying one tree can be wrong, but a forest of trees can make a smarter decision. Exactly, that's random forest.
