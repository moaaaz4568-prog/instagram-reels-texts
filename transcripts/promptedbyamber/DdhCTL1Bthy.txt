The number one mistake that I see when people are cold calling is how they go for the close. Never will I ever ask them if they want to jump on a call. If I've gotten to the part of the close, that means I've already established that you have a problem, I have a solution. So obviously, we're gonna jump on a call. I'm not asking for the call, I'm assuming the call and giving them two options. Would you prefer to jump on a weekend or a weekday? Wednesday or Monday? Tuesday or Thursday? Evening or morning? 9am or 1pm? That's their options, not yes or no. I wouldn't even be asking for the meeting if it didn't make sense, but obviously it makes sense, so let's move forward.
