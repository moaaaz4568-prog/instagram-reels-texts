Matt Drance shipped his first live-coded app, Rock Planner, for iPad with just fun. Rock Planner is a daily planner app built for Apple Pencil that integrates with your calendar and makes taking daily notes effortless. Matt didn't know Swift or SwiftUI when he started, but that didn't stop him. He used Cursor to experiment and soon enough, he was shipping the app. Matt has experience with Objective-C and UIKit, but after years of development, Cursor turned him into a superpower. This is what happens when AI meets a skilled developer. A few prompts, a little live coding, and Rock Planner went from idea to ship product.
