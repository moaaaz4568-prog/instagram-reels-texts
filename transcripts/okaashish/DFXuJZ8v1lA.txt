Let's talk about an interesting new domain extension .new. It's crazy how companies are using this new domain extension to save a lot of your time. .new is used for anything new. Want to open a new Google Sheet? Type in sheets.new in the browser. New Gmail account? email.new. New Google meeting? meeting.new. And a new playlist on Spotify? playlist.new.
