So the other day I was late to go somewhere and I took a wrong turn. I'm driving down and I remembered I left something at home. And for a moment I was frustrated and worked up, but what helps me get through this is knowing that every delay and obstacle is a way of Allah removing hardship from your life and answering your dua if you're a sincere believer. Because had I not been diverted or delayed, I may have crashed or died, or some misfortune would have struck me. And the way Allah answers your duas is sometimes by protecting you from things that you don't even know about. This is why it's so important to be sincere and to always make dua, even if it seems like nothing is happening. It could be that Allah is diverting a hardship from you or answering your dua in a different way every time you face an obstacle or something like this happens.
