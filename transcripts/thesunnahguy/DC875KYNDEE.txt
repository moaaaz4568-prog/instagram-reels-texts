You must visit three places to truly understand life: the hospital, the prison, and the graveyard. At the hospital, you will understand that nothing is more beautiful than health. In prison, you'll see that freedom is the most precious thing in life. And at the graveyard, you will realize that life is worth nothing. The ground we walk on today will be our roof tomorrow. So whenever you visit these three places, pay very close attention to the lessons they teach you.
