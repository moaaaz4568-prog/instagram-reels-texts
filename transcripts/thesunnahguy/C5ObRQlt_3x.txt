I would break down in sajdah and say, "Oh Allah, please keep my heart steadfast. Ya Muqallibal Quloob, sabbit Qalbi Ala Dineek. " This one dua has kept me upon Islam. Ya Muqallibal Quloob, sabbit Qalbi Ala Dineek. I make this dua in every single sajdah without fail because I can't lose my deen. Wallahi, you can't lose Islam. You can lose everything. Wallahi, my wife, my kids, everything can go except this iman in our hearts. I ask Allah to keep that iman firm in our hearts and to allow this iman to permeate throughout our lives and never ever to leave us even on our deathbed. Oh Allah, make the best of our days the last of them. Oh Allah, only take us when You are satisfied with our iman, Ya Rabb. Ameen.
