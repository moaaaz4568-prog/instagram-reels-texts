Someone once asked, "What does it mean to be British?" I want Britain to be about British. And I heard someone once say, "Being British is about driving around in a German car, going to an Irish pub to drink a Belgian beer, and then going home to grab an Indian curry or a Turkish kebab, on the way to sit on a Swedish sofa and watch American telly on a Japanese TV, and in the end of all of this, British people are suspicious of anything foreign."
