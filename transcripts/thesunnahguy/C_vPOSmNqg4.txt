The traditional dad needs to make a comeback. Not the psychopathic dad from the 1900s who never said 'I love you' and hit you with a belt for no reason. No. But just back to the dad who believes that 'because I said so' is a perfectly legitimate answer to any question a child may have. Just back to the dad who would never say anything as stupid as 'my kid is my hero' or 'where do you want to go and eat dinner? ' or 'one more story and then we really have to start thinking about going to bed, okay? ' Dads don't negotiate. They say 'you will apologise to your mother', 'don't make me turn this car around', 'some things just happen because life is unfair', 'clean your room', 'be quiet', 'the adults are talking and it's not all about you'.
