The Prophet Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam said, "No one will enter Paradise through their good deeds." He said, "Even me, except if Allah envelops me in His mercy." So know that you will never do enough good deeds to enter Paradise through your actions. The only way you will enter Paradise is by receiving the mercy of Allah Subhanahu Wa Ta'Aala. So never ever lose hope in His mercy. Be humble and live your life knowing that you can only be successful if Allah forgives you on that day.
