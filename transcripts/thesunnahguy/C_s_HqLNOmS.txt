This generation doesn't know how to build healthy relationships. We end up saying things like, "I don't owe anyone anything," but we do owe people something. You owe an apology to those you have offended. You owe gratitude to those who have supported you, and you owe respect and kindness to those around you. When you recognize that it's not all about you, that's when you'll be able to build healthy and fulfilling relationships. But sadly, this generation doesn't want to hear that.
