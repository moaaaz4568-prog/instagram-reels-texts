If you give something to your children once, they will appreciate it. If you give it twice, they will anticipate it. If you give it a third time, they will expect it. If you give it a fourth time, they will feel entitled to it. Worst of all, if you give it a fifth time, they will become dependent on it. To raise children who understand the value of rewards, it's crucial to teach them that effort and sacrifices are required to earn anything. Otherwise, you are creating entitled and ungrateful children. And mark my words, they will cause you problems and misery when they're older.
