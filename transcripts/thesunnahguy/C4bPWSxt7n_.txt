People think that repentance is complicated. You've sinned, perhaps you're drowning in sin, and the thing stopping you from repenting is that you think that it's some long-winded, complicated process. Let me show you how simple it is. All you need to do is from your heart feel sorry for what you've done, make the intention never to do it again, and ask Allah to forgive you. You don't need complicated words or a sophisticated dua. Just speak from your heart, close your eyes, and whisper. O Allah, forgive me.
