Keep this in mind: if you have food in your fridge, clothes on your body, a roof over your head, and a place to sleep, you are richer than seventy-five percent of people in the world. If you have money to spend as you wish and the freedom to go anywhere that you want, you are among the top eighteen percent of the world's wealthiest people. Furthermore, if you are alive today with more health than illness, you are more blessed than the millions of people who will not survive this week. Lastly, if you can read and understand this message, you are more fortunate than the three billion people who cannot see, cannot read, and cannot access knowledge and information that surrounds us. Life is not for complaining about pain and sorrows, but rather, life is about prioritizing the countless other reasons to be grateful and happy.
