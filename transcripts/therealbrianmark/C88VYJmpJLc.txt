The worst way to start a sales call is to pick up and say, "How are you?" and then talk for three minutes about the f***ing weather. You don't actually give a shit. They know that you don't give a shit, so it's a complete waste of time. Instead, you want to spend about 30 seconds building rapport and then jump straight into it. Say this: "Arnel, what's going on, brother? It's Brian Mark from PT Domination. How are you?" "Yeah, I'm great as well. Listen, I've got back-to-back calls booked all day. Are you cool if we just jump straight in?" Boom, follow for more value.
