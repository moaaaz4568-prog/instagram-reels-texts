Here's the hard part about being an entrepreneur is we need to decipher the difference between stuff that is ours and stuff that isn't ours. Because as entrepreneurs, we accept full responsibility for our entire business. That's always how we operate. And so sometimes you're trying to coach somebody to do something or trying to coach them into a certain role, and you're absolutely doing everything in your power and they're still not getting it. And you still are like, how can I do better? What can I do to improve? But like, go look at the other individual and ask yourself, are they carrying their 50 bricks? Not only are they carrying their 50 bricks, are they getting the outcome that we committed to together? Because they might be carrying their 50 bricks, but they might not also be good fits for the position.
