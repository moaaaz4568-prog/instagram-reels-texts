Here's the difference between a 10k coach and a 50k coach. A 10k coach thinks, how can I get more clients this week? A 50k coach thinks, how can I increase my profit margins? A 10k coach thinks, I'm overwhelmed and I don't have enough hours in a day. A 50k coach thinks, I need to hire three extra people so that we have 30 total hours of work time per day. A 10k coach thinks I can build this entire business by myself. And a 50k coach thinks I need to build a vision for this business so big that other people's visions fit inside of my vision. Follow for more value.
