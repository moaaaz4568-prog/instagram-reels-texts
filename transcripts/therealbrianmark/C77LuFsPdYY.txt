Never ever ever say these three words on sales calls. Number one, never say expensive, say premium. What sounds better? We have an expensive program or we have a premium program. Number two, never say cheap, say affordable. People associate the word cheap with low quality, but when you say affordable, they feel like they're getting a deal. Number three, don't say payment, say investment. When I say payment, it means I'm losing money, but when I say investment, it implies that I'm gonna get something back.
