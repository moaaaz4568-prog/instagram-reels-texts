Stop posting twice a day on social media if you want to be consistent. And I know you're thinking, "But Brian, you post three times a day." You're right. Posting multiple times per day does work if you do it consistently. If you try to post twice a day and you're not used to it, you'll do it consistently for a couple weeks and then you'll drop off and burn out. Instead, you need to post once a day, seven days a week, build that consistency, and then add more volume. Follow for more value.
