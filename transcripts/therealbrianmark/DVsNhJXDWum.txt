Three of the worst words to never say on sales calls. Number one, price. It makes your product sound like a purchase. Instead, you want to say the word investment. Number two, contract. The word contract scares people. Instead, say agreement. And number three, sorry. When a prospect opens up and tells you something that they're struggling with, as soon as you say sorry, you sound like a friend. Instead, say thank you for opening up and sharing that.
