Step number two, one hour after that, go into your messaging sequence and like one of the messages that they've sent. This will bump your message up to the top of their inbox. Step number three, after 12 hours, send a funny GIF. I like to send Forrest Gump waving. Step number four, the next day, if they still haven't responded, send them a video.
