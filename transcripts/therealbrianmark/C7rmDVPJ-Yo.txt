Three words that make you look weak on sales calls. Number one, I think. When you say I think, you sound unconfident and uncertain. Instead, say I'm confident. Number two, I'm following up. Those words imply that you're chasing the prospect and that you need them. Instead, say I just had a second to get back to you. Number three, filler words like um and uh. When you use words like this, it makes you sound unprofessional. Instead, when you're on a sales call, pause. And then continue on with the next sentence. Follow for more value.
