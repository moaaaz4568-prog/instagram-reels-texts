Here's the number one reason online fitness coaches can't get their business off the ground when they're first getting started. They're seeking the easy way out without understanding the basics. Running an online coaching business requires four things: expanding your network, attracting more clients, converting more clients, and delivering an amazing service. Expanding your network looks like posting TikToks and Reels on a daily basis. Attracting more clients means that you're putting out content that solves problems for your ideal client. Converting more clients means you're taking interested people that are engaging with your content and turning them into paying clients. And delivering an amazing service requires Trainerize, Evolution Nutrition, and a Facebook group for support. If you understand all four of these things, you will build a successful online fitness business.
