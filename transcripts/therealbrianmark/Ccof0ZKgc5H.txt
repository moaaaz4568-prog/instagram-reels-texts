Here's the number one piece of advice I'd give to myself if I had to start my online business over: slow down. When I first started my online coaching business, I was in a rush. I wanted more likes, more comments, more followers, more engagement, and because I wanted these things so fast, I was taking shortcuts. Instead of studying organic content and learning what was working, I was trying to cut corners, which sabotaged long-term growth. And so if I could go back and I could start it all over, I would tell myself to slow down.
