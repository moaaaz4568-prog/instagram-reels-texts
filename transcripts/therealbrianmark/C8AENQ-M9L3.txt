When a client says, "I'd love to get started if you can offer me a discount," here's what you say. First, don't just cave and offer a discount. Instantly, you lower your status in the prospect's eyes. Instead, reply with this word for word: "Listen, John, my man, I am stoked to help you lose 20 pounds, but I cannot offer you a discount. I would never decrease the value that I will give you as your coach, therefore I cannot decrease the price of the program. Now, if cash flow is an issue for you, I'm happy to offer you some sort of installment plan, but a discount is off the table because my results are guaranteed. Fair enough? Boom, follow for more value."
