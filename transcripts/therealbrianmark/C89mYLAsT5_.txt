Stop being so f***ing addicted to the views in your reels. Because the reality is the videos that get the views on my page are not the ones that drive the sales. Videos that I can articulate a specific problem that my audience member has and then give them a tangible solution, those are the ones that drive the sales. Example, four words to never say in marketing is one of my most viral pieces of content, but it doesn't make me money. The video that does drive the sales is how to overcome the wife objection on a phone call. Views are important, but so is content that converts. So 50% of your content should be created with exposure in mind, and 50% of your content should be created with conversion in mind. Follow for more value.
