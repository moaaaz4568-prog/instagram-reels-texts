Is it better to sell ten clients at fifty dollars a month or one client at five hundred dollars a month? Clients that pay fifty dollars a month are needy, demanding, and they expect a high ticket service for a low ticket price. You'll also have to handle failed payments, canceled contracts, and an overwhelming amount of objections like, "I need to talk to my spouse about this." It will take ten times the amount of effort to sell and ten times the amount of effort to deliver on a low ticket package. Or you could just sell one client at five hundred dollars a month, make the exact same amount of money, and have a lot less workload. Follow for more value.
