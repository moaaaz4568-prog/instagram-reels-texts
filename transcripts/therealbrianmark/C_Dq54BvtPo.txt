Never ever ever give your prices away in the DMs. Even if your prospect asks, if you do, they'll judge your program based on the price, not on the value you can provide for them. So the next time a prospect asks you what you charge, here is what you say: I've got programs that fit all budgets, but first, mind if I ask a couple questions to see if I can help or not? Kaboom! You are welcome. Fall for my value.
