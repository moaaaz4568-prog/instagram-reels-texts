Let's play a game. Take out your phone and make a list of the top ten people that you talk to the most. Organize them into three different categories. Category number one: people that you help lift up. Category number two: people that are on the same level as you. Category number three: people that you aspire to be like. Now, if you want to be successful, the people that you aspire to be like need to outnumber the people that you're trying to help up.
