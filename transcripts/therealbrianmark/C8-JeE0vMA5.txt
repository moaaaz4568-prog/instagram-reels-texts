There are only three things that you need to create content as a beginner. Number one, a phone. No shit, dumbass. What I'm saying to you is that you don't need a fancy camera or setup to get started. Number two, a tripod. You can buy one off Amazon for literally $30. Number three, great lighting. Ideally, you buy a ring light so that you have a tripod and nice lighting, but you could also film in front of a window. Follow for more value.
