You're on a sales call with a potential client. Instead of trying to sell your product and telling them how great your product is, you should spend 80% of the call figuring out what their pain points are, what their goals are, what their struggle is, how long they've been struggling with, what the consequences of not taking action are. All the time on the prospect, and then spend 15% of the time talking about how your product is going to get them from their pain point to their goal.
