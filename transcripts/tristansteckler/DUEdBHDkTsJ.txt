You can only sell belief if you have that yourself. If you struggle with closing, it has nothing to do with your script; it has everything to do with who you are as a person. Your prospect's not the problem, the offer's not the problem, your comp structure's not the problem, the setters are not the problem. Your own self-belief is the problem. You can only hold your prospects to a standard that you hold yourself to.

If you don't believe in yourself, how are you going to convince your prospects to believe in themselves? It's like the blind leading the blind—it doesn't make any sense. Most closers think they need better war tracks or they need to give seven or eight different analogies or they need to be like Shakespeare and perform on the calls to get people to trust them. But the simple truth is the reason that you're doing all that is because you fear that if you don't, you're going to lose the person. They're not going to trust you, and they're not...

You don't have enough conviction and belief in yourself. That's why you're losing your prospects. Follow for more value.
