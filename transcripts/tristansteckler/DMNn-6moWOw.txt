One of the simplest ways—not easiest, but simplest ways you can stand out in a crowd in your corporate job or everyday life—is to learn how to speak properly. So many people don't learn public speaking skills and it has a drastic negative impact on their careers, how they're perceived, their trajectories. In my opinion, it's one of the most important things you can do. I was listening to a guy on a call today give a demo presentation to a potential client and he said 'um' or 'like' forty times...
