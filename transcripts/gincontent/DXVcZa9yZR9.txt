Do I need to decorate my Instagram feed? No, people don't really care about that. But you do need to separate your work and your content. Huh? How are they different? Anything you create as part of your job is work. If you're a designer, your design projects. If you're an engineer, the products or systems you built. Everything else is content—how you made it, what tools you used to make it, basically behind the scenes. Oh, now I get it. But how do I separate them? It's easy. Go to your video and click 'Remove from main grid.' Keep your work on your main profile and your content in the Reels tab. That way, clients can instantly check out your work the moment they visit your page. God bless you.
