Did you know you can get free RPG content at your local shop? Last weekend on June 27th, some of the top RPG publishers created exclusive previews, kits, and accessories for attendees. This was all a part of Free RPG Day. I went to visit the local card shop to see what was available and there were so many to choose from. This was such a great event to celebrate role playing and the power of storytelling. I ended up picking Dungeons & Kittens and already started planning and feeling inspired.
