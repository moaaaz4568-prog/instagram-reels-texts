I have always lost people. I lost people to drugs, cancer, tragedies. I lost my first child to miscarriage. I lost my ex-husband to overworking and disconnection. I lost family members to drama. I lost friends too. I've also lost hope at many moments of my life. Why can't I be more normal? Why can't I fit in? Why can't I just want the same as everyone else? What I realized this week and came in is that I can't save everyone in the world just with my heart and my micromanaging of all the things. The formula teaches us how to receive love, accept it, and validate it. Life-changing. All of you in the circle have touched my heart in a special way this week, and I'm eternally grateful for all of you. I love Unblinded because it is not a commercialized, plasticky, branded monster. I love Unblinded because it feels like a real, caring family in a very safe home. You all became my home, and I'm committed to never losing you. Thank you.
