Something that sits on my heart constantly is the disbelief of people, which is totally understandable. A lot of people, when they share personal development information, put people down. They're like, "Yeah, they don't understand." There's no "they." We're the "they," and every single one of us have a fear of failure and rejection. Bringing that present, constantly evaluating, because every new mountaintop we get to, the next new mountaintop is stopped by that fear of rejection and failure. Once you have that self-awareness, everything frees.
