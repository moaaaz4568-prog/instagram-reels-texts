I am certain that everything is named unto its purpose, and if something is named outside of its purpose, it causes it to be misunderstood and therefore misused. Unblinded is fulfilling a purpose, and we are all part of that. I am unblinded. You are unblinded. I am certain of that, and that certainty grounds me. I am certain that unblinded is the way. It is the path for truth, for enlightenment, for action, for power.
