This was a dream I had since I was eight years old, to one day write Batman comics. When my first Batman comic book came out, through the tears, admittedly, I panicked. I said, "Oh my God, this dream I had since I was eight has come true. I don't have a dream anymore. I need a new dream." And that's when I remembered back that cold night in January '66. I said, "Okay, now's the moment. I got to go make dark and serious Batman movies. I have to show the whole world the true Batman." And that was the next step forward.
