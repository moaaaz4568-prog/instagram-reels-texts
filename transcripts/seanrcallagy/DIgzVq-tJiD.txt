Relationship capital is when people can say something to people and they believe them because they know, like, and trust them. So when they say this is a good thing, people go, "Okay, yep, it's a good thing," and they listen. That's relationship capital. And they have the people organized in a way that they can disseminate that information rapidly because they're in an email list that they've opted into, or because they're at somebody's live event, virtual or in person, or because they're in somebody's social media.
