What I wish someone would have told me sooner is this: that influence is the only human attainable superpower, and people do ridiculous, crazy things in business building, but it could be as process-driven as wrestling, baseball, football, whatever sport you play, musical instrument you go after. It could be that. I wish someone told me that sooner. I went to Columbia University as an undergrad, Ivy League. I went to law school, graduated. Nobody told me how to succeed in the capitalist structure, and I wish someone would have told me that all that education, all that money had nothing to do with me making money and being free in my life. But that's my work in the world. That's what I wish someone would have told me sooner.
