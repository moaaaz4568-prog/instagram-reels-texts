Here's how to break your shitty habits. Clean your environment. You're not addicted because you lack discipline. It's because you're constantly leaving whatever you're addicted to right in your peripheral vision. Whether it's vaping or jacking off, stop making it an option for yourself. If you're struggling to quit something, it's probably not because you lack willpower, because most humans' willpower is pretty similar. It's probably just because you don't know the method.
