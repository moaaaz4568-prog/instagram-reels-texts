The line when somebody says, "Oh, you're a good salesman, aren't you?" I just want you to say, "I appreciate it, but this is nothing to do with me and everything to do with you." What's coming up for you that made you say that is typically they'll go, "Well, you know, you're good at persuading," and then I want you to push back on that by saying, "Well, is it me that's good at persuading, or am I just asking you questions and you're persuading yourself?" It's actually them wanting to do it. They perceive that as you being a good salesman. Most of the time, if you've asked them the right questions, it's just the fact that those questions are getting them to the point where they know they need to do something. And it's easier to shift focus to you being a good salesman than it is for them to internally admit that they need to get off their ass and make a change.
