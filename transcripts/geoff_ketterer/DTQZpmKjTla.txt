Last week I had a conversation with a business owner who said all of his top reps keep leaving because they're getting burnt out. And that wasn't the case at all. They were leaving because they were getting pissed off. For the most part, good closers don't stop wanting to do the job. What they do want to get away from is chaos, shifting targets, and leaders who check out when the going gets tough. They get tired of carrying the quota for the rest of the team and watching all the underperformers get a free pass. The reason that happens is because nobody wants to have the difficult conversation. Sales is really a game of clarity, clear expectations, clear direction, clear leadership. And when your top performers don't get that, they're going to opt out. And not because the targets are too high, because the standards are getting negotiated down.
