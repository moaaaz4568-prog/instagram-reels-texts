I researched over 100 hours of cold call openers so that you don't have to, and these are the three best that I found. Number three is my favourite, but let me know what you think. Number one is, hi John, I'll be totally upfront with you. This is a sales call. You can either give me 30 seconds or put the phone down, totally up to you. Number two is, hi John, would I completely ruin your Wednesday if I told you this was a sales call? Number three is, hi John, would you reward my honesty if I told you this was a sales call?
