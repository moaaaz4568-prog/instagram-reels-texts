What happens when you mortgage your house to buy a random chemical plant? Well, you make $80 billion. This is Jim Ratcliffe. At 39, he was working as a dealmaker at a private equity firm, but he hated it. So when he saw an overlooked BP chemicals unit for sale that everyone else ignored, he mortgaged his house, put in $140, 000 of his own savings, and bought it for $37 million. And within two years, he took Inspec public for a staggering $164 million. But his blueprint was simple: acquire undervalued, boring assets, strip out inefficiencies, and scale them by prioritizing profitability. In 1998, he left Inspec and went on a rampage. He kept buying companies and completed over 20 acquisitions using the exact same playbook every time, proving that buying businesses is one of the most reliable paths to massive wealth.
