Money doesn't buy happiness. You ever heard that statement? Have you ever heard a rich person say that? Never. The only people who say shit like money doesn't buy happiness are broke people. Because here's the truth. Broke people have no right to say money doesn't buy happiness. Why? Because they haven't made enough money to qualify themselves enough to make that statement. And if they did make enough money, they probably wouldn't be making that statement in the first place. So get these negative beliefs around money out of your head, because chances are it's the very thing that's keeping you broke.
