Every guru online is selling you on the seven figure a year dream like it's some kind of finishing line. And what you'll notice is that they'll share a lot of revenue screenshots, but they won't share their P&L. Funny that. You know what it actually looks like to build a high ticket coaching business that does seven figures a year? Well, after closer commissions, setter commissions, software expenses, client success managers, and all the other bullshit that comes along with it, you'll be lucky to keep 20 to 30 percent profit. Then there's coaches out there that are building $40, 000 a month subscription businesses with 70 percent profit margins and making just about as much profit as these seven figure scale bros. If you're not lucky, you're going to be building a business that ends up turning out like a prison. You better wake up.
