If you're trying to work with everybody, you're going to end up working with nobody. Because the reality is is that most online fitness coaches they think to themselves, "Well, if I just cast this wide net and I work with mums, dads, men, women, school teachers, bodybuilders, chefs, athletes, etc., that they're going to get more of the market share." When in reality, what a prospect cares about is being understood on a deeper level. So your ability to actually niche down, focus on one person, one problem, and then articulate that problem in detail to them inside of a marketing message is going to be your ability to position yourself as an authority, charge higher prices, and actually get more clients than you can handle.
