When I first got started as an online fitness coach, I thought that all I needed was a mentorship program and it would automatically make me successful. What I realized was that working with mentors was only half the equation. The other half of the equation was having the commitment to doing the work consistently so that eventually I would master the skill sets required to be successful. A mentorship program is not going to save you. Developing the skill sets over a long enough time horizon will save you.
