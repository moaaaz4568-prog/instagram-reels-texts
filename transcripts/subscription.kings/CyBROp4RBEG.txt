Every time I hit a new milestone in my business, the biggest thing I'm looking for is for shit to start breaking. And when this happens, instead of just continuing to scale through the shit, I take a few weeks to clean up the mess, refine our client delivery systems, and then continue scaling further. A lot of coaches in the space, they fall into the trap of sacrificing client results for more revenue, and it only comes back to bite them afterwards. So if you're scaling your business right now and shit's starting to break, make sure you take some time to clean up the mess before continuing to scale further.
