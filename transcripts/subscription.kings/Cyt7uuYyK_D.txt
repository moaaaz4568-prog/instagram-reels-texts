The biggest hack in business is to learn from somebody who's been where you are and actually understands the nuances inside of the five core functions, which are lead generation, lead nurture, lead conversion, client delivery, and retention. The idea of mentorship is to speed up your process and to collapse time. So you can either do it on your own and learn from your own failures, or you can learn from somebody who's already made every mistake in the book, so you don't have to go through the same thing.
