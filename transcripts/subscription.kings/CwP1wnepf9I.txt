When it comes to marketing and sales, I want you to think about every single part of the process like a seesaw. Where on one side of the seesaw you've got the benefit and the value, and on the other side of the seesaw you've got the cost and the risk. So when you're running ads, when you're putting out content, when you're trying to get prospects to show up to the call, when you're trying to get people to buy, you need to think about, am I stacking up the benefit and the value so much so that it far outweighs the cost and the risk? And if you can do that, then you won't have a problem with lead generation or conversion again.
