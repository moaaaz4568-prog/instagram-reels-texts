Never apologize for being late to a meeting. If you show up five minutes late to a meeting, the worst thing to say is "I'm sorry for being late." Instead, when you show up late, say this: "Hey, thanks so much for waiting for me. Let's get into it." This positions you in a power frame and shows your appreciation for the other person, so you don't look like a d***. So the next time you're running a little bit late.
