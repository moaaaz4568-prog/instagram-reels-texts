The easiest way to sell a service is the service sells itself. What does that mean? I am a cold email agency. I acquire my clients through cold email. So when I hit a client up through cold email and I get them to hop on a call with me, the skepticism is killed immediately because they know cold email works because I just acquired them through cold email. So if you are a service-based business, the best way to acquire clients is through that service. You have to eat your own cookie.
