Maturing is realizing that smoking will keep you from your goals. If you continue to smoke, your goals will get farther and further away. I know because I smoked for over a year and I stayed in the exact same position. I was just going through life. I felt like I was in a simulation. As soon as I stopped smoking, my life literally 10x. I got into my dream apartment. I got in the best shape of my life. I made the most money I ever made. Smoking will continue to push your goals back if you do not quit.
