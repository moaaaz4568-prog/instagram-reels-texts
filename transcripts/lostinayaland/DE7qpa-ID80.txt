There is one hill I'll die on: your network is everything. At nineteen, I was stuck in a nightclub photography gig I despised, so I invested every dollar into courses and events, knowing it would pay off. I booked tickets to events in Bali, Canada, Miami, with one goal: to learn and network. Those genuine connections became my lifeline. Most of my work now comes through the people that I've met over time. So remember, relationships can unlock opportunities that talent alone can't.
