If you run Facebook or Instagram ads, I found a way to make a really cool ad creative. This is a DIY 3D image creative. Check it out. All right, first I took a picture of myself and then cropped out the bottom. Then I went to Canva, took the same picture before I cropped out the bottom, and auto-magically removed the background. Then I went to this website, zeeub.com, to create a fake Facebook post using the cropped out image. Then I used a free photo manipulation software called GIMP, and I layered that image without the background over the top of the fake Facebook post. I did have to erase some of my body, and this is how it turned out. I don't know, I think that looks pretty cool. And I'm not a designer, so even though it took me a little bit of time, it really wasn't hard.
