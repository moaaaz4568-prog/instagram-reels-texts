Neuroscience has shown that people are more likely to believe those that are confident than even experts that are correct. Multiple studies showed that people believed that those with the highest levels of confidence were right, even after proving to them multiple times that they were in fact wrong. So in your marketing and sales messages, make sure that you are confident. Make sure you are unwavering and don't offer too many alternative possibilities because that signals uncertainty.
