Hey, all right, today I want to talk about probably the most important psychological theory for marketers, the self-perception theory. This theory basically states that actions are not informed by attitudes, but the other way around. Our attitudes are shaped by our actions. Basically, that means that we humans don't know ourselves, so we look at our actions to figure out what kind of person we are. Why does that matter to marketers? Because so much marketing is directed towards changing attitudes, but really it should be focused on changing the action. How do you do that? One way is to do it yourself. Due to mirror neurons and a billion other things happening in the brain, we humans like to mimic. Another way is just to make sure that your copy is focused towards taking the action rather than changing the attitude. If you sell an eco-friendly product, rather than trying to convince people they should care about the environment, just focus your copy on click the buy button, buy one, feel convenience, feel better about yourself. Click the follow button, check out the link in the bio, that kind of thing.
