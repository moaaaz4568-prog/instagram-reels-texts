Marketing tip: Neuroscience has uncovered that print marketing can actually outperform digital, and the reason is because the human mind is able to create a stronger emotional connection to something physical. Something that all of us in the e-commerce space can take advantage of is inserts. So we talk about product inserts a lot, but we're always focused on the QR code or the NFC tag and all the cool things that can get people into your funnel. But we tend to ignore simple design elements: heavy card stock and raised lettering to improve the tactile experience. That will create a more memorable experience and a deeper emotional connection. You can take that to the bank.
