So in the book The Persuasion Code, it talks about the importance of cognitive ease in your marketing message. Basically, the easier the message is to decode cognitively, the more successful your marketing will be. And this is the reason why video outperforms text so much. So in order to make your marketing message as cognitively easy to decode as possible, you want to use comparisons wherever possible. You want to show proof, so this would be a demonstration or customer testimonials. And you want the representation of the product to be as three dimensional as possible.
