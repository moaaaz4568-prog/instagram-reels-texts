How I became an international speaker completely by accident. After some success in Amazon FBA, I used to answer questions for people online and a guy saw that and asked me to come speak in an event in Hong Kong. A guy in the audience at that event then asked me to speak at his event in Romania. Then that guy's friend needed a speaker for his event in Kiev. Then I got invited to Hamburg, then China, London, Vancouver and Taipei. Public speaking is not something I ever thought I'd do but it has been an awesome ride.
