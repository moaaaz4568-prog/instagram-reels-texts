Did you know that Blockbuster could have bought Netflix for a paltry $50 million? Netflix founders Mark Randolph and Reed Hastings recall the story of back when Netflix was a small startup and they were still mailing out DVDs before streaming even existed. They talk about how they were so intimidated standing in Blockbuster's corporate office. They even recall Blockbuster's CEO struggling not to laugh at them when they threw out $50 million. So of course the offer was rejected. Now Netflix has a valuation of $232 billion.
