Reciprocity is a psychological principle that basically states that we humans feel obligated to give back once we've received, and it's a powerful tool in marketing. But I want to talk to you about reverse reciprocity. In the book Brainfluence, a small business conducted a test where they gave a lead magnet, which is what most businesses use as a bribe to get you to opt into their list. They gave their lead magnet first and then asked for an opt-in, and they claimed that their opt-in rates were dramatically higher. I have a personal anecdote about this too. I have a friend who is running rebates to rank his products on Amazon, but he was giving the rebate money first before the purchase. As a result, he grew a huge fan base of loyal customers, and I'm not kidding, the guy's doing like a million dollars a month in sales on Amazon now. So I feel like it's worth at least testing.
