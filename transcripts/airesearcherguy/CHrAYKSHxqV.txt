Every consumer before they make a purchase calculates a mental equation that looks like this: the benefits they'll receive minus the cost equals what's called gain. It's an instantaneous calculation, but if the benefit to their life exceeds the cost, the gain will be positive. If the perceived benefit to their life doesn't exceed the cost, it'll be a negative gain, and then the purchase won't happen. So how you convey more benefit and ensure that your product or service has a positive gain is through what's called a USP or unique selling proposition. So make sure whatever you're selling always has a USP.
