Great question. If I was launching a product on Amazon today and just started today, this is exactly what I'd do. Sign up as a seller on Amazon, Pro account. Then I'd go to Signal Analytics AI, sign up to get my first free PFR report. That report shows you the purchase frequency of certain keywords. I would then optimize my listing, focusing on all these keywords, with a heavy focus on the top 20. Then I would run Facebook ads for a free product to a ManyChat flow that offers a 100% rebate for my product. Then when my inventory limit is raised to 500, I would go to TikTok and Instagram and look up coupon influencers and pay them for posts offering my product with a 40% off coupon. That exact sequence is how I've ranked hundreds of products for important keywords and got a steady stream of sales in through social media.
