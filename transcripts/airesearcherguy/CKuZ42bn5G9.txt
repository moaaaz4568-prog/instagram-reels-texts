How I set up my print-on-demand. So I've used Printify, BU, Printful, and Gooten. Printify and BU products are rather cheap, and I've gotten returns on them. So my default is always Printful, except for when things are just exorbitantly too expensive, then I'll use Gooten. Both Printful and Gooten have integrations with Etsy, so you just integrate your Etsy store. You create a product design under a template, and then when you're ready, you just click add to store. It'll add to your Etsy store as a draft, where you can go in and change the title and the tags and make sure everything's optimized. I use Placeit to create my mockups, and for designs, I either hire somebody on Fiverr or I use Canva to create a T-shirt design myself. Then I upload it directly to Etsy, and I also upload the same design onto Merch by Amazon.
