Now, when it gets to the point where it's time to start talking about price, do not ever let on that you're the sole decision maker, because you always want to be able to confer with your partners. Now, there are two lines that work really well in this situation. After you get your samples, you go back to the factories and you say, "Look, I really liked the quality of your product. However, I got a quote for a better price from a competitor, and my business partners are inclined to go in that direction. I would love to make the case to work with you because I really do enjoy the product, but is there any way that we can work on this price so that I have more of an argument to make on your behalf?" The other way you can do it is, "Hey, I really like the quality of your product. We're currently talking to multiple factories and a few of them have even given us better pricing. We are still strongly considering your product, but if you could give us this product at X price per unit, I can assure you that we will cut off communication with all other factories and we will make you the sole provider of this product to us."
