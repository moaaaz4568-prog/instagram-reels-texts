Is Amazon cheating? On a lot of search pages, you'll see the very first organic listing will have "Featured from our brands." That's an Amazon brand. That does not seem fair. I wanted to see if this was a really big problem, so I took a thousand keywords from Brand Analytics. Then I used Octoparse to create a scraper. There's my scraper running the search. And every time it hits a page that says "Featured from our brands," it records the keyword. And looking at the final list, a full 43% of keywords, Amazon is placing their product in the top organic search result.
