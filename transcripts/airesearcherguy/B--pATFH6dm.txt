I'm not saying you shouldn't source from the US. If you are looking to source a consumable product, for example, absolutely do it from the United States. I'm just saying it's important to understand the pros and the cons of each, and also realize that the US and China are not the only two places that you can source from. At the end of the day, however you can communicate with a supplier, as long as they can give you reasonable lead times and a fair price that leaves you with enough margin to actually make a profit, I don't think it should matter where in the world they're from.
