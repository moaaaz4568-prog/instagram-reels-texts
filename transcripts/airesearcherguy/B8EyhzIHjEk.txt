Now I want to talk about the principle of consistency, but in order to do that, first I need to tell you a story about Korean War POWs taken prisoner by Chinese soldiers. It was discovered that Chinese soldiers during the Korean War employed psychological tactics against American soldiers. Essentially what they did, among many tactics, was have American soldiers copy down pro-communist statements and then have them read those statements aloud in front of their peers. What this does, when you write something down we've discovered, is it essentially reinforces it in the brain. And then when you state things in front of people, that forces you to hold yourself personally accountable.
