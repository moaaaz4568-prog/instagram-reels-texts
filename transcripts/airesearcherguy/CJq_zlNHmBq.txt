My struggles as an entrepreneur with self-doubt. So when I started on Amazon, I crushed it so fast, so much that I got a reputation for it. So I leveraged that to get high-paying consulting gigs and a high-paying job. So I didn't have to take any money out of the business. I reinvested every dime. Then a year and a half later, due to some events that I had no control over and a lot of rash and inexperienced decisions, I lost my brands and every dime that they had generated. This completely destroyed my confidence. So after that, every business endeavor I took on was either mediocre or fizzled out. So when we started a new brand on Amazon a few months ago, I honestly thought it wouldn't do well, especially when my business partner told me we don't have a budget for rebate promotions. Despite that, I invented a way to make it work, and we sold out of our entire first inventory run with a 50% ROI. I tell that story as a reminder that your failures do not dictate your worth. And just because you don't have the receipts showing that you're at 100% success rate right now doesn't mean that the knowledge that you've worked for doesn't have value.
