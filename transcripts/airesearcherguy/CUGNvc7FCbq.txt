For all you algorithm geeks out there, if you want to learn more about how TikTok's algorithm works, I recommend checking out this piece from the Wall Street Journal about it. It's really interesting. They created hundreds of fake bot accounts just to figure out how TikTok ranks and categorizes videos that it serves accounts in their For You page. But basically, they discovered that TikTok really niches each account down and will go down these rabbit holes of content, which may very well explain why certain categories, like the political category, can get kind of fringe and wild sometimes. The way their analysis mapped also confirmed that while hashtags may not be necessary, they definitely do play a part. But I recommend watching the video. It's really interesting.
