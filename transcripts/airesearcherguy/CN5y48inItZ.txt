Here's another neuromarketing copywriting tip. Behavioral science research shows that people assign a higher value to items that they own. This means that if you can get a prospective buyer to picture themselves owning your product, they are significantly more likely to purchase it. So how can you do that? Using something called ownership prompts in your copy. The easiest prompt is to just tell people to picture themselves owning the product. But you gotta do it in a way that doesn't sound weird. Here's an illustration. Imagine how easy it'll be to slice through vegetables because of the way the ergonomic handle perfectly fits in your hand. If that person does imagine that, they're probably gonna buy your knife. Boom.
