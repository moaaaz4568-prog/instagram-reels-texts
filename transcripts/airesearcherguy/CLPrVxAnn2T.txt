You know Tim Ferriss, the author of The 4-Hour Workweek? Did you ever hear about the time that he won a Chinese kickboxing tournament? Took home the gold. Anybody that's a fan of his probably knows that he is into martial arts. But did you know that he had only been introduced to Chinese kickboxing four weeks prior to winning that medal? How did he do it? Mostly by exploiting a loophole in the rules. He still managed an amazing feat and he did it by being resourceful. But Tim's all about hacking. Hacking the mind, hacking experience. I mean, this guy boasts learning languages inside of three months. He's not special. Any of us can do this. Any of us can hack our own experiences. We just gotta figure out how to be resourceful.
