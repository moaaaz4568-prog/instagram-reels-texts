According to internal documents from Amazon, Amazon has a number of scoring mechanisms for ranking and badges. These are all connected to a keyword search, so the algorithm follows the customer journey from the search query all the way to the add to cart, which signals the most buying intent. This is the reason why add to cart holds so much weight in the ranking algorithm. However, a separate algorithm tracks expected add to cart conversions, so if your product falls short of that, then any benefit from add to carts will be nullified.
