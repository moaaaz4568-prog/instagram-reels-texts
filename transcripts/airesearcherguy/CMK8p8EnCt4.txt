What is a print-on-demand product? Excellent question. First, you take a graphic design that you own, either you made it yourself or you bought it. Then you go to a platform like Merch by Amazon, Redbubble, Printful, and you upload that design on t-shirts, phone cases, bags, and pillows. Then you can sell those products on Etsy, on Amazon, on your own website, and anytime somebody orders it, the print company will make it on demand and send it out for you.
