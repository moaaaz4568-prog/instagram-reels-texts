When you go to Alibaba's website, you'll see a ton of different filters and things on the left-hand side. I'm just gonna show you real quick the most important things to pay attention to. First of all, I just go ahead and never work with any supplier that has been on the platform for less than three years. The reason is because I figure if they're a fly-by-night institution, Alibaba will catch up with them within either a year or two. So by year three, the chances that they're legit go up a lot more. The other thing I do, I only work with suppliers that will take trade assurance. Now I don't like to use trade assurance because the fees are pretty high, but if a factory is willing to use trade assurance, that tells me they're probably legit.
