If you're selling on Amazon, the best way to build an audience is to create a community around a market that your brand creates products to serve. How you build that community is by building attention. Attention is a commodity, so you have to provide value and be everywhere that your customers are. So for me, that's Clubhouse rooms, that's TikTok profiles, that's Facebook groups, that's Instagram pages, everywhere that your customer might be. Now here's the key. In those spaces, you want to provide valuable content in an attempt to help your audience get to their desired state without having to actually buy your products. In doing that, you will build a community of people that want to spend money with you.
