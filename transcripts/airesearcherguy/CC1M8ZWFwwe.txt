Here are some alternatives to Alibaba for finding suppliers to buy private label products from. The only other website I've used and can recommend is Global Sources, which has actually been around longer than Alibaba. They have a great supplier vetting process. Most of their suppliers are very quality. The only problem is they have way less to choose from, a lot smaller selection than Alibaba does. Aside from that though, in truth, the best alternative to Alibaba is actually trade shows. I know right now most places are not doing in-person trade shows, but when they do open back up, the reason why it's such a good alternative is because you actually get to make deals and shake hands with your suppliers in person. And a lot of people think the only trade shows are overseas, but actually there are plenty that take place in Vegas. Yes, that still requires an investment of flying there, but Vegas is a lot closer to US-based people, and there are a lot of quality factories and suppliers that go there, even from overseas.
