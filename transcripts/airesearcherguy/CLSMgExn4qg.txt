Merch by Amazon versus Printful, got it, let's go. So Merch by Amazon does give you access to their marketplace, including their search browse pages, which could potentially lead to a lot of exposure. One downside though is that they keep a very big chunk of the money. See, on a $20 shirt, you only make about $5.23. Printful on the other hand is not a marketplace. It just gives you a place to design your shirts and then it fulfills them for you. However, you can integrate them with your Etsy store, with your own website, and even Amazon. I like to list on Etsy because the fees are a lot lower. Problem is the traffic is nowhere near the same.
