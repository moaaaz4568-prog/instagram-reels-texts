Once upon a time, Kodak, Polaroid, and Fujifilm were slaying it in the traditional film market. However, since the emergence of digital film technologies, both Polaroid and Kodak have filed for bankruptcy. So how did Fujifilm survive? See, whenever these new technologies entered the marketplace, competition became fierce. Polaroid was trying desperately to stay relevant. Kodak was attempting to sell digital cameras when there were other companies selling better cameras for cheaper. Fujifilm was smart though. They diversified. Rather than go deep in the same niche and attempt to assert their dominance, they saw that they could use their existing technology to support new industries, things like cosmetics, medical imaging, and LCDs. Within that process of diversifying, they reduced their dependence on film altogether, were able to prevail. So sometimes the answer isn't just adapting to new technologies. It can also be about diversifying and getting creative with where you can apply your skills and your current capabilities.
