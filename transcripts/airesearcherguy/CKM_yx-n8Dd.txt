You want to do print on demand, but you don't have any design skills? Let me show you my process for putting together a design team within 48 hours. Step one, go to fiverr. Com. Step two, search for the type of design you're looking for. Now, rather than go through each and every one of these, looking through all of their portfolios, choosing one and hoping that it works out, pick five gigs. Get the most basic package that you can that gets you what you need. This is usually going to be between five and ten dollars. Give all five of them the same design to do, and then pick the best one. As your needs and your design types change, you just repeat the process over and over again, and that's how you build a design team.
