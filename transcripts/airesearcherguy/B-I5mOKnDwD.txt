With regards to creativity, during this time, I will use the travel niche as an example. I know a lot of people who are in the travel niche and they're freaking out right now because they say the niche is dead. If I were in the travel niche, here's some examples of what I would do to remain creative and give customers what they are looking for right now. Start print-on-demand T-shirts that say, "I would have got this in Bali, but dot dot dot coronavirus, " or something to that effect, right? Sell postcards from the exotic destinations people wish they were at and call them, "Physically from my living room, mentally from Greece. " Make some kind of social media challenge where people take pictures with their postcards or something. Sell wall art where people can temporarily decorate a room to look like it's a tropical destination. Sell indoor hammocks. If you got the money, create virtual tours of vacation spots. Point is, you just got to be creative. People still want stuff, they're just shifting their focus and their trends right now.
