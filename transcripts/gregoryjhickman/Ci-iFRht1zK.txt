You need to go from hiring desperately to hiring logically. Unfortunately, a lot of service providers, agency owners continue to take on any type of client, which then leads them to having to hire often expensive experts. So they're kind of always trying to manage this stable of other contractors and freelancers just to get the work done. We need to shift to hiring logically. When you can solve a specific problem for your client, now let me go hire those specific types of people that only need to do a specific checklist set of items to move our client through our process.
