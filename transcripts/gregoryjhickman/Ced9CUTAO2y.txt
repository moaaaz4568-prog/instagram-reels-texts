You can probably get to seven figures selling just one thing, one service. If your service is ten thousand dollars, you get ten clients a month and you have a seven figure business just selling that one thing. Obviously, that one thing needs to be desired by the market that you serve, and you need to be good at getting those results, ideally profitably. So, I recommend one core service that gets you to at least fifty to sixty k a month, and maybe add on a continuity agreement or service where you are getting paid for your advisory and your thinking that can help you kind of cross that one hundred thousand dollar a month mark.
