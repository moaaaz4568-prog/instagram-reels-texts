Productization is about systemizing processes, not compromising on quality or creativity. In fact, it can actually free up your time from the administrative tasks and give you more time to focus on the creative elements of your work. And most importantly, it always results in more consistency, or at least it should, from a quality of client perspective, happier clients, sustainability in your lead generation, and most importantly, sustainability in your revenue and profit. Now look, there's always going to be a little bit of commoditization when the word productize comes up, just because there are examples of people that have chosen to go down that path.
