Why you're not closing your clients and your sales calls has nothing to do with your pitch. It's got everything to do with your authority and whether they believe that you can actually get them results. There are people charging five, ten, fifteen k for the exact same service as you, and you're charging one or two k. Same service, it's just a completely different price. The only difference is that their prospects are fully convinced that they can get them results. People don't pay for a service, they pay for certainty that they know that they can get to where they want to go. And the way that you build that is through social proof. Not just a screenshot of somebody showing that they had a good experience. Real people with real situations showing exactly where they started and where they ended up.
