A candy shop in Southern California did an experiment where they gave some people who were walking into the store a free sample of candy and all the other people they didn't give a free sample to. The people who received a free sample when walking into the store were 42% more likely to buy candy at the store. But why? The law of reciprocity, of course. When you receive a positive action from someone you're way more likely to return that positive action to them. How can you implement this into your business?
