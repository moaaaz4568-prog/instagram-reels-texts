"Don't make this mistake in business. When Betty Crocker originally came out with the box cake mix, all you had to do was add water and milk, and these didn't sell very well. But when they added the egg as well, sales massively increased.

But why? By adding the water and the egg, not just water, the amount of effort to make the cake increased. When less effort is needed, the value of something is perceived as less.

So having a product where the customer needs to do a little some-some is not always a bad idea."
