The best advice I can give you about LinkedIn is be very, very, very careful about who you take advice from. There's a mass of people out there reposting whatever they saw on their feed from people who took it from their feed. So my two cents on this, verify their profile first. See if they have case studies, see if they have a following, and most importantly, use your common sense. If it looks like it was copied off of ChatGPT, it probably is.
