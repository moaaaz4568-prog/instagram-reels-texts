Never complain and blame anyone for anything. Every time you point fingers at someone, you're basically telling yourself that you are powerless, and that certain something or someone has far more power over you. Think about it. We only complain about things we can't control. But the moment you stop the blame game and start owning everything is the exact moment your transformation begins and you become unstoppable. I learned this the hard way. Broke college student blaming the system, the economy, everything but myself. Know where that got me? Nowhere. The day I started taking responsibility for everything, my failures, my broke ass situation, that's when things changed. I built my business on this principle. If my client's funnel not converting well, it's my fault. I need to make it better. Traffic cost too high? My problem to solve, not Google or Facebook. Complaining is comfortable.
