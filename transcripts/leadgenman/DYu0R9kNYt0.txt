Claude just dropped Routines, which is basically their take on Any .Net and or Zapier, except you are not writing a single line of code, nor you are connecting any nodes manually. With Claude Routines, you just type what you want to automate in plain English, and it builds the entire automation for you. This is one example where it is fetching me top ten AI news and sending me inside my Gmail, and it hardly took around two to three minutes to build this entire automation. Whereas in the same example of other low code no code automation tools like Any .Net and or Zapier, this will take me around six hours to build this entire automation. But with Claude Routines, you just describe what you want to happen, and it builds the entire automation for you without having any technical knowledge at all.
