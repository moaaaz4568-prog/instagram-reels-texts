Here's a tip to help you with your content creation: batch shooting. I used to think of an idea, script the idea, film the idea, and edit the idea in one day. That wasn't sustainable. The structure now looks like this: I come up with ideas and scripts daily. I batch shoot eight to ten videos on Thursdays and I edit them on Fridays. I'm trying to post ten reels every week.
