Understand that what you have been doing for the last five years is the reason you are where you are today. Insanity is to continue taking the same actions and expecting different results. Your life is not a slot machine that you can just crank every now and then and expect new opportunities and adventure. If you change nothing and continue what you have already been doing, in five years time you'll be exactly where you are today. In order for things to change, you need to change. Follow for more.
