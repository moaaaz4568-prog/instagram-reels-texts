Anything worthwhile in this life requires consistency and effort over a long period of time. Most rely on motivation and sparks of intense work to get to that next level, which is a crucial mistake since you let your success be dependent on your emotions. If you fail to plan, you plan to fail. You can stack the odds to your favor if you allow success to be a byproduct of the processes and systems you use. Systems run the business and people run the systems. That's the change of perspective you need to make success a matter of when and not a matter of how.
