Lesson three out of six from twenty twenty three is focus. In a world full of distractions, your ability to stay focused is a superpower. What I neglected to realize for a long time was that being truly focused during a task and tapping into that flow state is actually a muscle you can build through repetitions, almost like a game you get better at the more you play. Psychic entropy and flow state were two of the main concepts I personally looked a lot more into this past year, and I would highly encourage you to do so too. Whether you use the Pomodoro technique, Hans Zimmer soundtracks, kitchen timers, there's nothing more rewarding than actually getting more shit done in less time while enjoying the process.
