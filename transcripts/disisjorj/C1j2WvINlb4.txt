Lesson six out of six from 2023 is leverage. Every single tweet costs nothing and has the potential to reach the entire world. The best way I can explain it is like a business that spends the entire marketing budget on billboards rather than utilizing the internet, social media, forums, email marketing. We all are in luck because access to the internet is pretty much free. But 99% of people consume content, they don't create content. And I would argue it's because they haven't realized the potential and the opportunities that come with creating content. Remember to live by design and not by default. I hope this finds you well.
