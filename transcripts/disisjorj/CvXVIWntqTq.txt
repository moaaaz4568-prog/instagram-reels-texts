I noticed that whenever I started something new and especially something I was scared of, in a matter of months I would find myself asking, "Why didn't you start this earlier?" It happened with this account, my jiu jitsu training, the live cohort I launched. I understood that fear can stop you from doing so many things in life, but at the same time, it can be the single best indicator you need to go after that. Rather than avoiding it, use it as a guidance because I guarantee you nothing will change unless you change. And what better way to do that than doing what scares you the most? Break from that comfort zone, take the leap, and become more than what you already are. Follow for more.
