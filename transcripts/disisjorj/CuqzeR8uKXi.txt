Stop for a moment and consider this. We use the word mindset very often, I know I do, but what does it actually mean? How I perceive it is that you set your mind to something. You program your mind to achieve something. And in order to do that, you need to act as if you're already there. If you truthfully stick to a plan of a healthy guy who has a clean diet, doesn't drink every other night, and walks for half an hour every day, you will eventually reach that healthy guy physique and mindset. To be it, you need to start living like it. That's all for now, full of aur.
