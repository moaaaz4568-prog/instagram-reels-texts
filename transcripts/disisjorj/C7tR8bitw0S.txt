I don't think many people realize the mistake that lies underneath goal setting. Hear me out. When you set out a goal to reach your ideal body, your ideal bank account balance, or even following online, you're saying, "This is where I am and this is where I want to end up." Aside from all the hours spent on dwelling, since every day that passes is just a reminder that you're not there yet, most fail to understand that the actual goal is worthless. Flash news: everyone has one, and more often than not, the same one. Your habits, your routine, the choices you make daily, that is what determines where you're gonna be in a year's time. Walk the walk to talk the talk, not the other way around.
