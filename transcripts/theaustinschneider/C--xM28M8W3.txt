I keep watching YouTube videos and I can't seem to scale my agency. It's something I hear a ton from agencies; they're overwhelmed by the amount of information, what you do with the information, what order to actually use the information in, and they have no idea where to start. And they're missing one key part. Accountability. Actually, research by the American Society of Training Development found that individuals have a 95% probability of completing their goals when they have accountability. The reality is is you probably don't need more information, you probably just need more focus. Information with accountability. Some of the best coaches I work for do just that. It's something that I obsess over at AgencyU.
