Most agencies start as a one-person show where the founder does absolutely everything but to scale, you need structure because without structure there is literally no growth. The hustle and grind culture is outdated and unsustainable to truly scale in your content agency. You need to stop working like a freelancer and start thinking like an actual CEO. And one of the best ways to do this is to have an agency operating system. And what this does is it allows you to delegate, automate, and remove yourself from the day-to-day operations so you can finally scale beyond your personal capacity. Think of it as almost a central nervous system for your agency allowing you to focus on growth activities rather than just getting buried in the daily operations. This leads to more profit in your agency and consistent client satisfaction.
