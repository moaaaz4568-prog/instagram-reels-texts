Five words agency owners should never say on a sales call. Don't say, "Yeah, we can do it all." Say, "We only work with clients who need specific thing that you can offer." Don't say, "We can work within your budget." Say, "Our typical clients see X result with X price." Don't say, "We'll run your ads." Say, "We'll help you get 10 qualified appointments every single month." Don't say, "Let me know if you're interested." Say, "When would you like to start?"
