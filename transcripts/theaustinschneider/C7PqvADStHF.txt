If you're sick and tired of operational bottlenecks in your agency, here's how to build a system that works. Look, the real problem in an agency is likely fulfillment. It is the silent killer. You're bogged down in tasks that should be streamlined. You're working in the business and not on the business, which literally causes you to run out of hours and feel overwhelmed. I literally used to work from 4 AM to 8 PM doing everything for our clients. It affected not only the business but also my personal life.

Here's what I did. I figured out what I could eliminate, automate, and delegate. First, I eliminated what is not needed. I trimmed the fat and let go of any tasks that did not contribute to what I should be doing as an owner. Second, I invested in automations. For repetitive tasks, there is always a tech solution that can free up your time. Finally, I delegated. I established a well-structured system, a well-documented process that could easily be followed by...

When you do this, you not only free up your time, but also mental space, allowing you to focus on scaling your agency.
