I ran forty-eight miles through the Grand Canyon. Why? To become a better business owner. I realized my biggest obstacle as a business owner wasn't strategy or skills; it was my mental capacity to handle difficult challenges. But there was a problem: I was well undertrained, had limited supplies, was dehydrated, literally had to bring everything inside of one backpack, had extreme pain and complete exhaustion. It felt impossible, but I just kept going forward. Completing that run for me transformed my business mindset by a long shot. Whenever a sales call feels hard or a client challenge feels overwhelming, I remember pushing through fifty miles of suffering in the middle of the freaking Grand Canyon. Physical challenges to me build the mental toughness that entrepreneurship demands.
