There's a lot of people who come to us and they say, "Oh, you know, I tried paid ads before. I spent $2,000 and I just got clicks and nobody actually bought." They're probably missing out that the paid ads didn't work because they did work. The problem is everything outside of that: your offer, your creative, your funnel. So, for most of the people, it's not that ads didn't work; their offers just suck, their funnel just sucks, their messaging just sucks, their sales framework sucks. So what we need to do is we need to actually optimize things outside of just the ads to make the ads actually work.
