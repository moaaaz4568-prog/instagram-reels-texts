People actually like this? Mediocre in taste but strong in branding. Mike Cessario started Liquid Death to mostly cater towards the grungier metal crowd, but now it's literally taking Gen Z by storm as their favorite canned water. Coming from an advertising background, Mike knew the way to get in front of people was to stand out and think outside of the box. Which is exactly what he did with Liquid Death in a catchy tagline that says "Murder Your Thirst." This just goes to show that branding can make any product look cool... and taste better too.
