Educational reels get trust, storytelling reels get followers, and authority reels get leads. Educational reels teach a valuable tip, process, or lesson. Storytelling reels share a personal experience, win, loss, or founder story. Authority reels show a client or personal before and after. Educational reels post three to five times per week. Storytelling reels post once a week. Authority reels post once a week. Educational reels use a lead magnet or follow CTA. Storytelling reels use a follow CTA.
