Here's how to come up with a year's worth of content for your business in under an hour. Let's say I was in the fitness industry. Come up with the 12 most talked about topics. Every topic is going to be for a different month. Then go onto Google and search each topic as a question. Scroll to the questions and hit the down arrow and back up and do this about 30 times. These are the most asked questions and use each one of these as a real idea that you can answer. Do this for all 12 topics and you have 365 days worth of content ideas. For more social media tips.
