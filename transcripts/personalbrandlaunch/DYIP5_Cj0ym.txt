Everybody has a niche on social media. But did you know your niche falls into one of three categories? And based on that category, there are different structures and formats that are going to work better for you than others. Category number one is visual niches. These are niches that you see in order to learn. Category number two, verbal niches. These are niches you need to hear in order to learn. And category number three, product niches. These are niches where you need to see, verbally, and hear visually in order to learn. The video structures that work well for these niches are tutorials, testimonials, and tips/slash hacks.
