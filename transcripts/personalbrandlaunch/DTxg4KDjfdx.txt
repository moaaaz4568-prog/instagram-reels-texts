Which video do you think got more views? Video A or Video B? If you chose Video B, you would be correct. What about these two? A or B? The correct answer is A. Alright, one more. A or B? The answer is B. But why? The reason is something called CPS—cuts per second. All the high-view videos that I showed had three to six shots or cuts within the hook. All the low-view videos I showed had one shot in the hook. Basically, for a hook to visually grab attention, one simple thing you can do is add multiple cuts or shots within the hook of the video.
