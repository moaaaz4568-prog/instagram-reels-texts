My client month one through three got up to 200 followers, but by month four he had over 100k. Most people can't deal with slow growth so they'll never make it to the actual big gains. You don't need thousands of followers to get sales on social media. If you're not getting sales, you're either not presenting it correctly or you're not in front of the right audience.
