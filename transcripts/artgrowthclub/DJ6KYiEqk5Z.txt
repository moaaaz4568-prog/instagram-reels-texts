Please never post stories to grow on Instagram unless you turn on these three settings to allow people to interact with your stories and increase your views. First, before posting any art reel, add the notify me sticker in the morning. This is a very good way to get initial engagement on your reels. Second, go to your profile, click here in settings, scroll to messages, and in story replies, make sure to turn on this option. Go back to settings and tap comments. Click stories and enable this option. And finally, return to settings, scroll to media quality, and make sure to enable this option. Follow for more Instagram tips for artists.
