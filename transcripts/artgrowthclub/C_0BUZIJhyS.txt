It's day one of building our Instagram art account from zero. Let's decide the posting schedule. If you're a beginner with less amount of time, on Mondays you can post one art post, on Tuesday two to three stories, on Wednesday one reel, on Thursday either one story or one reel, on Friday two to three stories along with a reel. If you're a beginner who has some time to dedicate for content creation, then on Monday you can post one reel of your art, on Tuesday you can post one art post or carousel, on Wednesday you can post one reel, on Thursday one art post or carousel, and on Friday, Saturday, Sunday you can post another reel.
