Here are three reasons why artists don't like the art community. One: you either get a ton of encouragement if you're great or just starting, but then it dries out. Two: the pressure and commodify your art really early in people's art journey. This can be heartbreaking to base your talent upon your number of art commissions. That is why we created the Artistry Community Discord. You can now find a peaceful and loving group of artists to sell your art and promote your art without stress for completely free. How to join? Follow and comment down the word "art".
