Instagram hack for artists you didn't know existed. If you want to grow your art account but you are having trouble getting views, likes, and follows, it might be because Instagram isn't recommending your content. To check this, follow these steps: One, click on the three dots in your profile; two, go to Settings and Privacy; three, click on What content can't be recommended? By clicking on this, you can check whether your content is eligible for recommendation or not. If you don't see a green checkmark in the recommendation guidelines, then it might be the problem.
