Be careful with this Instagram feature. This is how you stop Instagram from taking your data. Pay attention and save this video. Go to your profile and click on the three lines on the right. Go to Account Center. Here, go to your Information and Permissions. Then go to your Activity outside of Instagram, and finally Recent Activity. Now look at the number of sites that Instagram is sharing your information with. To avoid this, just click on the site you want to remove, disconnect, and confirm.
