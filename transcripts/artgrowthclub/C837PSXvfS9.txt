Here's how to prevent Instagram from banning your art account by turning on these three settings. Save this Reel to not forget. Setting number one: go to Settings, click on Website Permission, then click on Message Links and turn off this option. This saves your art account from spammy websites and anonymous logins and helps Instagram maximize your reach to art lovers. Setting number two: go to Account Center in Settings, click on your information and permissions, then click on Update Contact and turn off Connect Contact. This setting will secure your contact information if anyone hacks your Instagram account. Setting number three: go to Account Status in Settings and check if your content is eligible for recommendations. This setting helps reduce the chances of banning your Instagram account and allows you to reach more non-followers.
