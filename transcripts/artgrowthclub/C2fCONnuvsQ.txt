Social media is ruining artists' lives. Social media makes beginner artists insecure about their art, which is so horrible and disgusting. Also being attached to posting art can be harmful; it can go to obsession with your art account and can damage your mental health. That is why we created the Art Lovers Club. As artists need a place to share their art with love without stress for completely free. How to join? Follow and comment down the word 'art'.
