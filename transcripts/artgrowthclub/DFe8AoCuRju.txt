Three things every Instagram artist should do in 2025. First, go to edit profile, click on category and select artist. Make sure to turn off display on profile to target your art community. Second, to make your art posts attract art lovers and rank higher in search, go to account status and make sure these two options have a green checkmark. Third, but before I say share this with as many artists who need to know about this too, go to settings, click on creator tools and controls and switch your account type to professional and as always follow for daily art growth tips.
