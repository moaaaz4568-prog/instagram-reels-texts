The art community is full of gatekeepers. Did you know why it is so hard for you to grow your art on Instagram? When there are so many people to show your art to, it is simply because Instagram is oversaturated with art content. So many artists are trying to show their art to the same audience, and this creates competition between artists and makes Instagram toxic. If you want to be free of this pressure while still growing your art account, I suggest you join the Art Growth Club, where you can meet like-minded artists and show your art in a safe place.
