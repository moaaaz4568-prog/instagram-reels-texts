Never say these words if you want to sell your art on Instagram. Don't say 100, say 99. This technique is called enchanting pricing. Using odd numbers makes the price feel more affordable. Don't say buy now, instead say there are only three pieces left. Creating urgency and scarcity encourages people to act faster. Don't say discount, say limited time offer. Limited makes your art feel more exclusive and valuable. Never say cheap, say accessible. Cheap sounds like low quality while accessible implies great value. Finally avoid saying friend or comrade. Use your client's name when speaking directly, building a personal connection that makes them feel special. Now share this with your favorite artist and follow me for more tips.
