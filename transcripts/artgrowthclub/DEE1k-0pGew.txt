Never take photos of your art without turning on these eight settings. Go to your phone settings, go to camera, go to formats and select High Efficiency. Click on record video and select 4K at 60fps, but make sure to turn off HDR. Next, go in record slow-mo and select this option. Go back and in Preserve Settings, turn on Creative Controls and Night mode. Finally, go back and turn on Composition Grid and View Outside the Frame. Save this video and follow for daily art growth tips.
