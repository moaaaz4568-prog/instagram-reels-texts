Breaking news! Instagram is removing the highlight bubbles. And the crazy thing is, I know what you're thinking: I've worked so hard on my highlight bubbles and built that brick by brick. Now they're just going to take it away from me? But this crazy move is intended to simplify your profile display. Instagram is preparing to move your story highlight bubbles from above the profile grid into their own section within your profile tabs called POGs. Follow to learn how to use them.
