Artists are quitting Instagram after this new Meta policy. Instagram updated their privacy policy for 2025. They will start using your photos and videos without your consent to train the artificial intelligence of Instagram and Facebook. How to avoid this? Go to Instagram settings, scroll down and click on the option About. Select Privacy Policy, click on the three lines. Scroll down and under Other Policies select How does Meta use your information for AI? Now a hidden link will appear. This link is your right to say no to this policy. Click on it, fill out the form and send it so Meta doesn't collect your data. Share this video with as many people as possible who need to know about this too.
