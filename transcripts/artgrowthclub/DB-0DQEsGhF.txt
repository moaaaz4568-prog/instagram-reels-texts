The truth about the art industry is that your clients will become your friends quicker than your friends will become your clients. Your clients will tip you; your friends will ask for a discount. Your clients will book in advance; your friends will ask to be squeezed in last minute. Your clients will share your artwork, while your friends will pretend they didn't even see the post. It's sad, but in many cases... it's the truth. Share this with someone who needs to know this too.
