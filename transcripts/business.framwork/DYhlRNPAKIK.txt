This is how top brands sell out products in minutes without offering a single discount. Phase one is the early access. 24 to 48 hours before the launch goes public, email your past buyers first. Give them exclusive access to the drop before anyone else. This rewards loyal customers and lets you capture demand before the public launch even starts. A large percentage of the inventory can sell here alone. Phase two is the public launch. Now the product goes live to everyone else. But because early buyers already claimed a portion of the inventory, stock is already limited. Phase three is the close. Once the edition sells out, send a follow up to non buyers. Let them know the drop is officially gone and won't come back. This reinforces that future drops are worth paying attention to. Each launch trains your audience. Customers learn that if they wait, they risk missing out. So over time, more buyers purchase earlier and at full price.
