The one thing I love most about my marketing framework is how modular it is. I can add in details to my strategy and it will adapt to give it space. I can add in or remove apps depending on my current needs for them. I can add in parts like a new offer if I have a new product. Literally all I have to do is duplicate and move it to the right place. Having this flexibility is how I was able to map my entire operation in a single place and make the necessary adjustments to make it better.
