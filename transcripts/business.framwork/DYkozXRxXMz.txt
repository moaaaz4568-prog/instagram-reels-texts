Instead of standard promotional copy, send an email curated by your team. Choose one lead pick where a team member shares a personal anecdote and exactly how they use that product in their daily routine. Then you include two to three secondary picks featuring quick, honest quotes from other employees. It has to feel like it was written by someone with an actual opinion and read like a note from a friend. You want to send these every two to four weeks and make sure you rotate writers every time. Bring in someone from customer service, then operations, then product development. Different departments have different voices and will naturally provide unique perspectives. Try this and watch how many of your repeat buyers finally try a new product.
