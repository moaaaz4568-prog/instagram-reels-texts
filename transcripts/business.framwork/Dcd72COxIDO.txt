If I had to describe my job as a business owner, I'd compare it to conducting my own orchestra. I'm essentially building the symphony from scratch. Take my marketing for example. I know who we're targeting, I know how we position ourselves and the tone of voice we use, I know how we use paid ads, organic channels and email to bring people in and generate sales, I know our sales process and what happens after someone becomes a customer. Basically, I know how every part of my marketing works and how they all connect together. And that's really my job, to look at the whole thing and figure out what's working, what isn't, and what I need to change to get the business to where I want it to go.
