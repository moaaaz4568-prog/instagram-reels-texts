There is literally only four tonalities that you need to know to dominate sales. I call them the four Cs: curious, concerned, certain, confused. We use the curious tone to show we're genuinely interested in someone's situation. We use the concerned tone when we want the prospect to become concerned about their own situation. And it also shows them that we're empathizing with what they're going through. The third is certain. It's our job as closers, as salespeople, to transfer certainty. When people come into sales scenarios, they're usually confused and uncertain about something, and we need to transfer it. And then finally, it's the confused tonality. I like to use this to subtly challenge someone. So if they're saying something and it's not really adding up, it's not really making sense, then we might want to lay that one on. The trick behind mastering tonalities is almost like being a method actor. If you can genuinely feel that emotion, be genuinely curious, be genuinely concerned, be confused if someone's talking shit, and have absolute conviction around the product you're selling, you've already done 90% of the work.
