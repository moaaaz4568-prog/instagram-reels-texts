This effect is called the match cut and here's how to do it. All you need is a few frames where the person is walking in the same direction but in different locations. Step one, head out and bring your tripod and camera. Make sure that the focal length and distance from the camera is the same in each shot. The easiest way to measure distance is to just count your steps away from the camera. Step two, film yourself walking from one side of the frame to the other. Repeat it for as many times as you need to nail that shot. Step three, it's editing time. Bring everything over to your editing software. Overlap your clips. Select the upper clip and bring the opacity down to 50%. Adjust the size and position to match the clips perfectly. And then trim the clips so you can get that effect of the background changing.
