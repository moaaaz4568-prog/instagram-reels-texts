Imagine this is your video. You spend loads of time and money making the editing the best it can be. Use every hack and trick that you know, like SEO and hashtags. Then you just take a tiny half-baked idea and plop it on top. And you completely skip on the personality. But the truth is no one wants a burger without a patty, no matter how good the bun, the lettuce or the tomato. You can pretty much skip out on everything else and still have something people want. But you need a big idea and you need some personality. Yet this is where people spend the least amount of time, and it's why no one wants to eat what they cook up.
