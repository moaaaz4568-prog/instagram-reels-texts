So Morning Brew used one particularly interesting tactic to grow their newsletter, using our psychological bias against us, the curiosity gap. So the curiosity gap is the gap between what people know and what they want or need to know. These open loops create pain that people will try and close with information. So Morning Brew uses these at the start of their newsletter, asking you a question you definitely won't know the answer to, and then not revealing the answer until the end of the newsletter. This means you have to stick through the whole thing to close the loop, which increases consumption. But this sort of open loop thing is also used in a great deal of your favorite TV show. And if you're a content creator, consider using them in your videos or articles to keep people around for longer.
