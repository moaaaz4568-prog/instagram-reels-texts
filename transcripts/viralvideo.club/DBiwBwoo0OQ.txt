I know what it feels like to think you're too late to make something of yourself. My dream of being a big time musician was squashed when I got nodules. My dream of being a pro runner was destroyed when I got hit by a car. I spent my twenties completely adrift, and by twenty seven I was homeless. Friends were winning awards and making money, but I was struggling to eat. And I just couldn't shake this feeling that it was just too late for me to do anything of worth. The funny thing is, the pain that I felt at rock bottom is ultimately what drove me to success. Working so many random jobs from fun park rider to private driver has allowed me to see life differently from others, and this difference has become my edge. So no fucking way are you too late. You're right on time, baby.
