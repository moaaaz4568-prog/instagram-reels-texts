Too many people think you can just start coaching people or that you can start a social media marketing agency when they don't know much about life and they don't know how to grow social media channels. How does this happen? There's too many gurus online telling us that we have a teachable skill. Well, if we're young and we haven't really learned any valuable skills, then that's just not the case. And that's okay. When we're young, we're supposed to go from not knowing, and as we age, we learn more and we begin to know and then we pass on that learning to the future generations. But we can't start out at 16 or 18 and say, yeah, well, if you want to learn how to grow your business, which I have zero experience in, then give me 9.99. Spend time learning the skill first and then you'll become valuable and then you deserve the money.
