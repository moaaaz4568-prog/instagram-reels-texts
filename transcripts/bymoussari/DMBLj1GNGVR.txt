Man, don't ever start your videos like "Hi guys, I'm a content creator" or "In today's video, I want to teach you about how to." Nobody cares. Do this instead: I made $50k this month selling this simple PDF. This hook got me 20,000 followers in the last 10 days. See the difference? Every story has five elements: Who, What, When, Where, How. Traditional storytelling starts with when, where, and who, but on social media our brains are fried. People on here don't care who, when, where, or how you did something unless you give them a reason to care first.

So in your next video, start with either the result they desire or the moment they relate to. This alone will massively improve retention. But if you want a deeper understanding of how to structure your entire story, comment "Please hook me" and I'll send you my entire storytelling structure.
