4. "I hear you. And I want you to know I am taking everything you just said seriously."

Winston Wolf never dismissed the emotion in a room he was trying to fix. He absorbed it, acknowledged it, and used it as the foundation for the solution.

This phrase does not solve the problem. It earns the right to solve it.
A buyer who feels genuinely heard lowers the temperature enough for reason to re-enter the room.
You cannot close a deal in a room that is still on fire. This phrase puts the fire out first.

Acknowledge before you fix. The sequence is everything.
