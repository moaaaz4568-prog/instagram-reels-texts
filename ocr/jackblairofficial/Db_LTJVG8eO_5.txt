WHAT WOULD HAPPEN TO HER NEXT TUESDAY?
Savage made the abstract concrete by putting a real
person and a real date into the room.
A policy is a concept nobody feels. A named person
on a named day is something the buyer can picture,
and pictures move people.
Move the buyer from the idea to the day it happens
and the decision makes itself.
