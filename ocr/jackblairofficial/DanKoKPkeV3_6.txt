6. "Let's skip whose fault this is and go straight to the fix."

Geniuses are trained never to trash the product to bond with an upset customer. Reps break this rule daily, badmouthing their own company to seem honest, and every one of those confessions gets replayed to the buyer's boss later.

Bond over the fix, not the fault.

Every flaw you confess for rapport gets quoted in their next meeting.
