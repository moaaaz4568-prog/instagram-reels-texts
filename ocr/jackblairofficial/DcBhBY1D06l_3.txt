SHE SHOWED UP IN PERSON
She asked Fidel Castro for years. When the yes
finally came in 1977, she flew to Cuba and he drove
the jeep himself.
A letter can be ignored and a call can be ended. A
person standing in front of you has to be answered.
Show up where the decision lives. Everything moves
faster in the room.
