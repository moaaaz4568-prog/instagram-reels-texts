5. The breakthrough is one person remembering one moment.

Sarah Paulson recorded Pedro's audition tape in 2013. She showed it to Amanda Peet. Amanda's husband was David Benioff — the GoT showrunner.

The chain: Pedro → Sarah → Amanda → David → cast.

None of it happened without one friend with one tape.

The breakthrough comes from one person walking into one moment to the right room.
