6. "WHY ARE WE DOING THE SMALL VERSION OF THIS?"
Feldman's rule was that a large case takes the same hours as a small one, so he stopped taking small ones. He once wrote twenty million dollars of coverage in a single day.
When a buyer scopes themselves down, say so out loud. The work ahead is identical either way, and only one version is worth your week.
The small deal costs what the big one costs.
