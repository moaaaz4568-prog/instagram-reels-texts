6. "I know this is not comfortable to hear. I would rather have this conversation now than watch you deal with the consequence of not having it."

Baum's greatest quality was not his intelligence. It was his genuine care about the outcome for the people he was trying to protect.
This phrase delivers the difficult truth with the warmth that makes it land as concern rather than criticism.

The buyer who was defensive about their current position suddenly feels genuinely looked after.
You are not telling them they are wrong. You are telling them you care enough to say what is true.
That combination closes the most resistant buyer in every room.

Deliver the hard truth with genuine care behind it. The buyer who feels both will trust everything you say next.
