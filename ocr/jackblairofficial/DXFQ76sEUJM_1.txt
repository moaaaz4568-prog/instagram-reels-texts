1. "Can I ask you something that might be a little direct?"

McNulty never waited for permission to go where the conversation needed to go. But this phrase earns it anyway. It signals that something real is coming and makes the buyer lean in instead of shut down. You get to ask the hard question and the buyer is already primed to answer it honestly. Permission granted means truth delivered.
