SMALL REASONS, THEIR MOUTH

The effect runs in your favour when the reasons are
the buyer's own and the outside push stays light.

Before the close, have them state what this fixes, in
their words, and write exactly that into the recap.
You are banking their justification, not yours.

The reason they said out loud is the one that
survives.
