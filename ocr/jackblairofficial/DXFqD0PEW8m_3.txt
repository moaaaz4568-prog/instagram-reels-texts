3. "I know there have been reasons to hesitate. Here's why now is different."

Every rep carries a losing streak into their next call whether they admit it or not. Kendall Roy walked into boardrooms after public failure and still made his case. This phrase acknowledges the hesitation in the room without surrendering to it. You name the doubt before they can use it against you. Then you walk straight through it.
