1. "I'D RATHER TELL YOU THREE WEEKS AND BEAT IT THAN TWO AND BLOW IT."
Magic Kingdom's posted waits routinely run above the real line. Independent timers clock actual waits at roughly two thirds to three quarters of the number on the board.
You quote the number that wins the meeting, then spend three weeks losing to it. Quote the number you can beat. The deal is graded on the gap.
The verdict is written the moment the promise is.
