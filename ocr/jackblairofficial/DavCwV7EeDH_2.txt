2. "You said no pitch. Fine. Ten minutes, and I won't pitch. I promise."
Zenith's chairman took the meeting on one condition. It would not be about advertising in Ebony. Johnson agreed, walked in, and never broke the rule. He let the room do the selling instead.
A buyer who's written you off will still give you ten minutes if the ten minutes cost them nothing.
Take the meeting on their terms. Win it on yours.
