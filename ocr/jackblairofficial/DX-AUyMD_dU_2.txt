2. October 19th, 1987.

The Dow fell 22% in a single day. Black Monday. The biggest one-day percentage drop in stock market history.

Belfort had just landed his first job at a real brokerage. By the end of the week he was unemployed.

The lesson: the room you're in doesn't protect you when the room itself collapses. The skill is what you carry between rooms.
