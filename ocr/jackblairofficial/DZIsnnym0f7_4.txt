4. He refuses to let comfort make the sale soft.

"Put that coffee down. Coffee is for closers only." He strips away every little crutch the room hides behind.

The breaks, the chatter, the waiting around. He knows comfort is where deals go to die. The closer who stays slightly hungry, slightly on edge, outworks the one who got cozy every single time.

Comfort is the enemy of the close.
