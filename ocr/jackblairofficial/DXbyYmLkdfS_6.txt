6. "I want to give you everything you need to make this decision today. What is the last piece of information you need from me?"

Bauer always identified the final obstacle and removed it before it had the chance to extend the mission.
This phrase positions you as the person solving the last remaining barrier rather than adding to it.

The buyer who answers gives you the final objection in the most solvable form. You solve it. The deal closes.
The buyer who cannot answer has just discovered there was nothing left to wait for.

Ask for the last piece. The buyer who finds it almost always finds the close right behind it.
