I'M NOT SURE THAT'S RIGHT.
Wintour does not argue, raise her voice, or explain herself at length when she disagrees. She says one calm, quiet sentence.
The softness is the power. A quiet "I'm not sure that's right" lands harder than a loud objection ever could.
Stay calm under disagreement and the room is yours. Get loud and you hand it away.
