4. "I want to be straight with you. The guilt trip is not going to change the number. But I am happy to talk about what will."

Vic always named the tactic before the person using it could weaponise it further.
This phrase does the same thing with enough directness to stop the tactic cold and enough openness to keep the conversation alive.

The buyer who was applying emotional leverage suddenly finds it named and neutralised in one sentence.
They either pivot to a real conversation or reveal that the guilt was the only move they had.
Either way you are now in control of where the deal goes next.

Name the tactic. The buyer who hears it named almost never uses it again in the same conversation.
