1. The industry writes you off before the world does.

Robert Downey Jr. had five drug arrests between 1996 and 2001. A year in Corcoran prison. Fired from Ally McBeal in 2001.

By 2003 every studio in Hollywood had decided he was uninsurable.

Audiences still loved him. The people writing the checks didn't.

The people deciding your future stop seeing you long before the people who'd pay for you do.
