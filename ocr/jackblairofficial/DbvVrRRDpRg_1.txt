Dennis Mileti spent 50 years studying warnings at the Natural Hazards Center. The first warning element in his model is the source. People don't act on information. They act on it from someone they believe would know.
Earn the right to be heard, then hand back the edge of what you know. A rep who names the limits gets believed inside them.
People act on the messenger before the message.
