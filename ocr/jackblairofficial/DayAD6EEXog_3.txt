3. "Let me show you something. Might be nothing for you. Might be exactly the thing."

When Roma finally reveals what he's selling, he hands it over like a curiosity, not a quota. No urgency. No countdown. The buyer gets to discover it instead of being charged by it, and a discovery is something people defend. A pitch is something they resist.
