3. "I know it's wider than what you carry.
That's why it'll sell."

Going wide was the whole idea. Ties in
1967 were narrow, his were not, and he
walked them into stores himself, one
buyer at a time, until the width was the
reason people asked for him. Whatever
your buyer flags as different, don't sand it
down. Say why the difference is the point.

The objection is usually the feature.
