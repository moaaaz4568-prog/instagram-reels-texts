7. No pressure. I just don't want you deciding on autopilot.

The core of Langer's work is that most bad decisions are made mindlessly, not on purpose.

Naming the autopilot is disarming. It signals you want a real decision, not a reflex, and that is exactly what a confident closer wants.

Invite them to actually think. The rep who is not afraid of a considered no closes more real yeses.
