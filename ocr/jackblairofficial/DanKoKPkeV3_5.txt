5. "That's a rough spot. Let's get you out of it."

The workbook makes a bold-type distinction: empathy, never sympathy. Feeling sorry for someone leaves you both standing in the hole. Feeling it with them and moving is the job.

When your buyer vents, give the pain one real sentence, then put the first step on the table.

Sympathy sits in the hole with them. Empathy brings the ladder.
