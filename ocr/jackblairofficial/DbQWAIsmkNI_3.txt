3. "WHAT USUALLY HAPPENS RIGHT BEFORE
YOU SAY YES TO SOMETHING LIKE THIS?"
A clean question about process, in their language,
not yours. Grove's rule was to explore someone's
world without installing his own map.
So ask how decisions like this actually get made.
They'll name the partner they check with, the night
they need to sleep on it, the person whose blessing
counts, and now you have the map nobody
volunteers.
Their process beats your playbook. Ask for theirs.
