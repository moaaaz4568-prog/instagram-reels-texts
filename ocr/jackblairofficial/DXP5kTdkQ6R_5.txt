5. "What would make this decision easier to defend internally once it is made?"

Varys understood that every decision has an audience beyond the room that it is made in.
Your buyer has a board, a manager, a committee, or a partner they answer to.

This phrase finds the internal case your buyer needs to make after you hang up.
They tell you the language, the metrics, and the framing that wins their room.
Now you build your close around the argument they need to make, not the one you want to deliver.

Arm the buyer for the conversation after yours. That conversation is always the real close.
