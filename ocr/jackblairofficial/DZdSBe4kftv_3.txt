3. He made himself the prize.

Most reps act like the buyer is the prize
they're chasing.

Klaff flips it.
He behaves like the scarce
one the other side has to qualify for, which
makes them lean in and start selling
themselves to him. Chasing lowers your
value; being chased raises it.

Position the deal as something they earn,
not something you beg for.
