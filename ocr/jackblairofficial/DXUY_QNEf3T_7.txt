7. "I said I would get you an answer by end of day. Here it is."

This phrase is not about the answer. It is about the timestamp.
Chigurh's entire reputation was built on the gap between what most people he said and what he actually did.

When a buyer receives exactly what was promised at exactly the time it was promised, without having to ask, without a reminder, without a nudge, with something so rare in the relationship with vendors that it registers not as good service but as something closer to shock.

Do what you said. When you said. That is the close that never needs to be made twice.
