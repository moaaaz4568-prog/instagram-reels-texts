7. "FORGET THE DECK. ONE SENTENCE. THIS TAKES THE MONDAY CALL OFF YOUR CALENDAR FOR GOOD."
The biggest disruption available to a rep is abandoning the pitch mid-pitch. It resets the buyer's attention completely, and whatever comes next becomes the frame.
Use it once, late in the pitch, when the room has gone flat. Drop the presentation, say one plain sentence about the buyer's week, and stop talking.
The reset is the disruption. The next sentence is the whole sale.
