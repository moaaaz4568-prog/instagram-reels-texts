4. “What you do today pays you in 90 days.”

Blount calls it the 30-Day Rule.

The prospecting you do this month does not pay this month. It lands 60 to 90 days out. Reps who only prospect when they feel desperate are always working a pipeline that is already too late.

Prospect when you are busy, so you are never broke when you are not.
