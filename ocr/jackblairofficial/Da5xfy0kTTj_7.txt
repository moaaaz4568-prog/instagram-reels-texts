7. "Worst case you laugh at me. Here it is anyway."

On day three Jiang asked a doughnut shop for Olympic rings made of doughnuts, an absurd ask, and they arrived free in fifteen minutes. Timid requests collect polite refusals. Timid ones get taken seriously. Audacious ones get taken seriously. If the ask is big, make it bigger and say it with a straight face.

Timid asks get polite nos.
