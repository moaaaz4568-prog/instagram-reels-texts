Finding 1 | The Ceiling
Cost: $0

I uploaded my comp plan, 12 months of
commission statements, and my close rate.

Claude found the cap my company had
buried in the accelerator language. Showed
me the exact month I'd hit it. November.

Then it pulled the math on every month after.
The harder I worked, the smaller my
percentage got. By Q4 I was earning 3 cents
on the dollar I generated.

The ceiling wasn't a feeling. It was a clause.
