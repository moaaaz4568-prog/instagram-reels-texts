2. “IT’S HANDLED.”
The catchphrase works on one condition, and the
show is honest about it: she only says it when she is
already moving. The words are a receipt, not a wish.
Earn the phrase, name the
fix, start the fix, then say it. Said early it is a lie. Said
in motion it is the most calming sentence a buyer
can hear.
Certainty is a claim. Handled is a status.
