4. "I HAVE PUT A LOT INTO BEING SURE ABOUT THIS."
In Moscovici's model the minority that visibly pays a price for its view is the one that gets believed. Cost signals sincerity in a way that fluency never can.
Let the buyer see the work behind the claim. Name the calls you made, the numbers you pulled, the hours it took. A position that cost you something cannot be dismissed for free.
What you visibly paid for your view is what they quietly credit it.
