3. "I hear you. Before we go there, I need you to have this piece of context or the rest will not land."

This phrase stops a buyer mid-reditect and reinserts your process without conflict.
It frames your agenda as a service to their understanding, not a power move.

The buyer who was about to take the wheel suddenly needs what you go anywhere.
You have not argued. You have not pushed back.
You have made your sequence feel essential to their outcome.

Make your process their advantage. They will stop trying to skip it.
