1. He treated door to door as his school.

Newly married and nearly broke, Hays borrowed forty dollars to get to Nashville and learn to sell books door to door for Southwestern.

Most people would have seen a summer job. He saw a training ground, and the rejection, the scripts, and the long days taught him everything after. Where others see grunt work, the pro sees rep and the skill is actually built.

The hard sales job is where the skill is actually built.
