4. "Let me stop talking. What did I get wrong?"

Grant's explanation for the result:
extraverts talk too much, listen too little
and read as too excited. Ambiverts switch.
Every ninety seconds, hand the call back
with that line. It's not modesty, it's a check.
If they say nothing, you're fine. If they
correct you, you just found the objection
two slides early, while it's still cheap to
answer.

The loud rep sells the pitch. The quiet rep
sells the buyer's words.
