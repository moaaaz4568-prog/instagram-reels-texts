I’D RATHER LOSE THIS DEAL BEING STRAIGHT
THAN WIN IT ON A PROMISE I CAN’T BACK.
Tommy walked into the final meeting expecting to
lose, and said it all anyway.
Saying you would rather lose than overpromise is
the one assurance that cannot be faked, because it
costs you something in the room while you say it.
Buyers price everything, and they price that highest.
The truth that risks the deal is what closes it.
