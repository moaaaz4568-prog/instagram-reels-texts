5. The faith you don't talk about isn't a disqualifier.

Mahershala converted to Islam in 1999, before becoming famous. He's said publicly that his faith shapes his work.

In Hollywood, a black Muslim man wasn't supposed to win Best Supporting Actor twice.

The identity you have that doesn't fit the standard mold is rarely a disqualifier. More often, it's the texture that makes your work yours.
