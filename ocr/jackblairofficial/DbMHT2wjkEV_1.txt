1. "LET'S DO THE SMALL VERSION. YOU PICK WHAT IT HAS TO PROVE."

Maria Konnikova got con artists to explain themselves. Step five is the convincer, a small win that is completely real.

The con always picks that win. Do the opposite. Let the buyer set what to prove, then make it small enough that the trial has to finish inside a week.

A small proof beats a big promise.
