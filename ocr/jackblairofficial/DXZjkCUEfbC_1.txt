01. Cold Call Opener

"Write me 5 cold call opening lines for [job title]
at [company type] all in 2026,
reference a specific problem this buyer faces in the [industry] space.

Each opener must:
Grab attention in under 8 Seconds,
and end with one question that makes them want to keep
talking.

No introducing myself first.
No asking if it is a good time.
Under 15 words per opener.

Tone: direct, confident, immediately relevant.
No fluff. No scripts.

You stop losing the call in the first ten seconds.
Every opener you have now earns the next thirty.
