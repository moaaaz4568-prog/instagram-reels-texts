1. PROMISE THE WORK, NOT THE PRIZE.
1
Rivals promised freshmen the starting job. Saban told recruits exactly how hard Tuscaloosa would be and what nobody would be handed.
Overpromising wins signatures and loses clients. Tell the buyer the true cost of the first ninety days and watch trust arrive early.
The honest pitch survives delivery.
