1. "My name is Dana. I've been a nurse on this floor for eleven years, and I'll be with you until seven."

Hospitals teach it as AIDET, a Studer Group script used across American hospitals: acknowledge the person, introduce yourself with your name, your skill and your experience, then say how long you'll be there. The credential comes before the care. On a call, your years and your lane are the first thing out of your mouth, not the last.

Trust starts with who you are and how long you'll be around.
