4. When they blow up, go silent.
Noesner's pause. When emotion spikes,
most reps rush to defend, which feeds the
fire. Say nothing and something strange
happens: nobody can sustain a one-sided
argument. They burn out, calm down, and
come back to the table on their own.
An outburst can't survive your silence.
Wait it out.
