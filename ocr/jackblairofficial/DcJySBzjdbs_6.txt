AFTER THE SIGNATURE, FEED THE BELIEF
Post decision doubt is real. Every buyer wakes at
2am asking whether they were fooled and whatever
they find next decides the renewal.
Inside week one, send their own stated reasons
back, plus the first small win. Give the belief
something to eat.
The sale ends the doubt or the doubt ends the sale.
