1. "THE PRICE IS THE PRICE, FOR YOU AND FOR EVERYONE."
Saturn banned haggling outright and sat at the top of the sales satisfaction rankings almost every year through 2001, beating brands that cost three times more.
The line works whether you set your prices or defend ones handed to you. Say it early, say it level, and every negotiation tactic aimed at you loses its target.
A price that moved was never real.
