1. "Let me ask you something. What does another ninety days of this actually cost you?"

Most reps create urgency by talking about what the buyer will gain.
Roma knew the sharper edge is always what they continue to lose.

This phrase does not push. It invites the buyer to do the maths themselves.
By the time they finish answering, the cost of waiting is their number, not yours.
You never applies the pressure. They felt it entirely on their own.

The close that lands hardest is always the one the buyer builds themselves.
