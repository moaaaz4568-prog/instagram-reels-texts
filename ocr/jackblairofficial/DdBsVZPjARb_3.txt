3. "LAST TIME WE SPOKE THIS WAS URGENT.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE TUESDAY?"
Excited Tuesday, vague Friday, gone by Monday.
Every pipeline has one of these right now, and it is
rarely about the product.
Quote their own urgency back with the date
attached. Nobody likes contradicting their own
Tuesday self. They either recommit or they tell you
the real blocker.
Urgency that cooled has a reason. Find it.
