6. "If that went away, what would it be worth to you?"

Need-payoff questions. Rackham found the buyers who described the benefit themselves were the ones who bought. Their own words did what the rep's brochure couldn't. Ask it and go quiet. Write down the exact phrases they use. Those phrases are your proposal, your follow-up email and your close, in the order they said them.

The buyer's benefit list beats your brochure every time.
