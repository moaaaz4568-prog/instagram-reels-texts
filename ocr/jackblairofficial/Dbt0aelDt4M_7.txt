"DID I GET THAT RIGHT, OR DID I ADD SOMETHING?"
Perspective getting has two halves: ask, then verify what you heard. In the studies, accuracy only moved when the answer came from the other side.
End discovery by replaying their words and inviting the edit. What survives the check is real. Build only on that.
Verified beats intuited, every single time.
