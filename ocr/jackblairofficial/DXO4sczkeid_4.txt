04. Stall Diagnosis

"This deal has been stuck at [stage name] for [number] weeks.
Here is everything I know about the prospect and the deal so far: [paste details].
Diagnose the most likely reason it has stalled and give me
3 different approaches to unblock it this week.
Each approach must be specific, actionable, and
executable in one conversation.
Tone: analytical, honest, no false optimism.
Under 50 words per approach.
Zero guesswork.

You stop guessing why the deal went quiet. You know
exactly how to move it."
