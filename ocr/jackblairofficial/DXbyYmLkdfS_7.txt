7. "I am going to call you back in two hours. I want you to have a decision ready and I will have whatever you need waiting."

This is Bauer's signature move and the most powerful urgency close available on any stalled deal.
It sets a specific timeline, signals complete preparation and creates a commitment on both sides simultaneously.

The buyer who agrees to the two hour callback has already made a psychological commitment to deciding.
The rep who calls back exactly when they said they would demonstrates the reliability that makes the yes feel safe.
Two hours. Exact. Every time. The close lives in the follow-through.

Set the specific time. Call at the exact moment. The deal closes in the gap between the two.
