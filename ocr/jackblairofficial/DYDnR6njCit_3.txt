3. The skill that lit him up wasn't on the time card.

Jim worked 8-hour janitor shifts at the Titan Wheels factory after school. Mopped floors his mother had walked all day.

The 5 minutes of mirror-practice on his break was the only part of the day that felt like him.

The hours that pay you and the hours that wake you up are rarely the same hours.
