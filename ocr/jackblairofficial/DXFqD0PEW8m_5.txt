5. "I've thought about every reason this might not work for you. None of them outweigh this."

Preparation is conviction made visible. Kendall's strongest pitches came when he had already answered every objection in his own head before anyone asked. This phrase tells the buyer you have done the work, considered the risks, and still arrived at the same conclusion. Certainty that has been tested is impossible to argue with.
