02. Not Interested Handler

"My prospect replied to my cold outreach with some version of not interested.
Their exact words were: [paste their reply].
My offer is: [one sentence].

Write me 3 responses that:
Acknowledge their position without arguing with it,
reframe the conversation around a different angle they have not considered,
and end with one question so low friction it is easier to answer than ignore.

Under 40 words each.

Tone: calm, confident, completely unbothered by the no.

Zero guesswork."

The reply you used to archive now becomes the conversation that books the call.
Every not interested has a move. Now you have three.
