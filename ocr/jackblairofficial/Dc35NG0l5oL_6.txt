6. "I AM NOT HERE TO TELL YOU. I AM HERE TO RUN THE CONVERSATION."
The discussion leaders in Lewin's study were not lecturers. They asked, they listened, and they kept the room on the question until the room answered it.
Swap the presenter role for the chair. Ask the question, hold the silence, and let the buyers do the persuading of each other. A room trusts its own voices more than it trusts yours.
The rep who runs the conversation wins it. The rep who dominates it loses it.
