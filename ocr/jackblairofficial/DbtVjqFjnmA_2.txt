AI TAKES THE PREDICTABLE JOBS FIRST.
Anything repeatable, rules-based, and done at a desk is the easiest thing to automate.
Data entry. Reporting. Support tickets. Admin. Basic code.
If a task can be written down as steps, a machine can learn to do it. That describes most of the white-collar world.
