1. "HERE'S THE ONE THING I WANT YOU TO GET FROM THE NEXT TWENTY MINUTES."
In 1993 Nalini Ambady showed strangers thirty silent seconds of thirteen teacher evaluations. Their snap ratings matched the real end-of-semester evaluations at a .76 correlation. No sound. The content never got a vote.
Your first half minute works the same. Open with one declared destination and the silent read becomes certainty instead of nerves.
The grade lands before the content loads.
