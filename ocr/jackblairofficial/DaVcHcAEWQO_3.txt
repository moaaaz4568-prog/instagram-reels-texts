3. This might not land, but here's what I see.

Grant's work on powerless communication found something backwards. With a wary audience, tentative talk beats the confident hard pitch.

A hedge lowers their guard. Certainty from a stranger triggers resistance. A question from a peer gets weighed.

When they are defensive, drop the armor first. It is the fastest way to get theirs off.
