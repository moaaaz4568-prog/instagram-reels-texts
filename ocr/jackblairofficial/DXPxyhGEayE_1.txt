1. "I want to be clear about something before we go any further. This is what we are working with."

Cersei never let anyone else define the terms of a room she was standing in.
She set the parameters early and made everyone else negotiate within them.

This phrase reestablishes your position at the exact moment a buyer starts rewriting the deal.
It is not aggressive. It is definitive.
The buyer who was reaching for control suddenly finds the boundary they did not see coming.

Set the terms before they do. Every negotiation belongs to whoever frames it first.
