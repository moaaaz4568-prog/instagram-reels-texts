1. "Help me understand what is driving that concern because I want to make sure I am solving the right problem."

Most reps hear an objection and answer over the objection. Littlefinger always answered the person, not the words they chose to say out loud.

This phrase buys you the intelligence every close actually requires. The buyer stops performing their hesitation and starts explaining their real position. Now you are not pitching against a decoy. You are closing against the truth. This phrase finds the rest of the story.

The objection is never the whole story.
