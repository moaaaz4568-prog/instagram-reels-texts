6. "The price isn't mine to move. The market set it, and I showed you the market."
Boras is famous for never cutting his number to make a deal easier. The price isn't personal, so it isn't negotiable by mood.
Anchor your price to the evidence you already showed, and a discount request becomes an argument with the data, not with you.
A price with evidence behind it doesn't need defending.
