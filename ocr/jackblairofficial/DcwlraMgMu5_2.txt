2. "HERE'S THE WORST THING THEY'LL SAY
ABOUT US. SOME OF IT IS FAIR."
McGuire's lab used truisms nobody had ever
questioned, like brush your teeth twice a day.
Unchallenged beliefs collapsed fastest under first
attack. Untested confidence is the weakest kind.
Give the objection in weakened form and concede
the fair sliver. The buyer's belief in you grows scar
tissue exactly where the rival will cut.
Untested belief folds at first contact.
