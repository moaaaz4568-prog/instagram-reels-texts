6. "I am not asking you to move forward. I am asking for one honest conversation."

When a deal has gone cold, asking for the close again kills it completely. This phrase asks for something smaller and gets something bigger.

One honest conversation is easy to say yes to. Inside that conversation, you find the real objection you never had the chance to solve. That is the only objection that was ever standing between you and the close.

Stop closing on the wrong thing. Open the door to the real one.
