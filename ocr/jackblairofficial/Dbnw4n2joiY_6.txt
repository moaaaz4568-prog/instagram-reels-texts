6. "YOU KNOW BOTH SIDES OF THIS NOW. WHAT WOULD YOU PROPOSE?"
Mediation runs on one bet: terms people author, people honor. When a room locks up, Piazza flips authorship and makes the parties write what ends it.
Hand the buyer the gavel before the call dies. Whatever they propose is a map of what they'll actually sign, drawn by the only hand that matters.
Authored terms don't get appealed.
