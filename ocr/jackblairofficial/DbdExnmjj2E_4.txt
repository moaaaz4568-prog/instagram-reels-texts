4. "WHAT SHOULD I HAVE ASKED YOU THAT I HAVEN'T?"
Diamond's thesis is that people don't want to be argued with, they want to be valued. One question does it wholesale, by making the buyer the expert on your blind spots.
It reopens every door at once and hands over the microphone. Then go quiet and write down every word they say.
The unasked question is the most expensive one.
