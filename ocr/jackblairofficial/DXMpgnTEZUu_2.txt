2. "I am not going to tell you what to do. But I can tell you what people in your position have found works."

Nucky never issued orders to people who needed to feel autonomous.
He offered information and let them reach the conclusion he had already prepared for them.

This phrase removes every trace of pressure while steering the conversation with precision.
The buyer stops feeling managed and starts feeling advised.
Advice from someone who has seen the pattern is the only thing a smart buyer acts on willingly.

Give them the map. Let them think they chose the destination.
