7. "Here's exactly what I think you should do, and I won't hedge it."

When the moment came, Johnson made the ask directly, no apology, no softening. The clarity of the ask was its own kind of pressure.

A confident, specific ask is easy to answer. You stop hinting and state the next step in one plain sentence. Buyers state next step in call on vague asks and move on clear ones.

Clear asks get answered. Vague ones get dodged.
