6. "What would have to be true for this to be an easy no?"

Edmondson's whole insight was that people need it to feel safe to say the negative thing. So ask for the no on purpose. It's lower stakes than the yes, so they answer honestly, and their easy-no reasons are your real objections, finally in the open.

Ask for the easy no and the real objections show up.
