5. "WANT THE DEAL I'D DEFEND IF I SAT ON
YOUR SIDE OF THE TABLE?"
Piazza's signature device is the mediator's proposal.
When two sides deadlock, he writes the terms he'd
defend as fair to both, and each side answers him
privately.
Late in a stuck deal, stop negotiating and author the
package you'd buy from their chair. Sign your name
to it first and hand it over finished.
Verdicts get answered. Offers get argued.
