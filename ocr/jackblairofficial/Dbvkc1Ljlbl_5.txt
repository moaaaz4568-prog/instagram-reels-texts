"I'D RATHER BE USEFUL THAN LIKED."
Owens has said plainly that referees who try to be best mates with the players damage their own officiating and damage the game.
Warmth that's angling for approval reads as need, and need reads as pressure. Be the calm one who's useful, not the friendly one who's fishing.
Likeable is a byproduct. Chase it and you lose it.
