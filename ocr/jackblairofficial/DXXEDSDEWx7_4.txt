4. "I have looked at what you are dealing with and I have to tell you, this is very solvable."

Burgundy always made the people around them feel that whatever situation they were in was manageable because he was there and he had seen worse and he had always prevailed.

This phrase does the same thing for a buyer who has been carrying a problem they are not sure can be fixed. It signals calm expertise and the buyer stops feeling stuck and starts feeling possible. A buyer who feels possible is a buyer who is already moving toward yes.

Make the problem feel solvable by sounding like someone who has solved it before without breaking a sweat.
