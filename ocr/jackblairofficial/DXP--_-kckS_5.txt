5. "What I am about to show you is not what you expected when you booked this call. I think that is going to work in your favour."

Reddington always reframed the terms of every engagement before the other side had finished setting them. This phrase signals that what is coming is different without telling them how, and different is the one thing every saturated buyer wants.

The buyer who was braced for another standard pitch relaxes their defence. They become genuinely curious about what you brought instead.

And curiosity is the most receptive state any buyer can be in when your close arrives.

Surprise them before they finish expecting the ordinary. The close lands in the gap.
