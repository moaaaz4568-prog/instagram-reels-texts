01. Buyer Blueprint

"Build me a discovery call preparation brief for a [job title] at a [company type] in the [industry] space. Include their most likely priorities in 2026, the pressures their role is under, and 3 business problems my [product or service] directly solves for someone in their position. Format as 3 sections, bullet points, under 20 words per point. Tone: strategic, specific, no assumptions. Zero guesswork."

You stop walking into discovery blind. You start walking in already closing conversations.
