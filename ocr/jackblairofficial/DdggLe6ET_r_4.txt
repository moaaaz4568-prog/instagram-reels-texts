4. "Who else feels it when that goes wrong?"

Big deals, Rackham found, are decided by more than one person over more than one call. An implication that stays at the buyer's desk stays small. Ask who else pays for the problem, then ask them what they'd say about it. Now the problem has witnesses, and a problem with witnesses gets discussed at the meeting you're not in.

A problem with witnesses gets a budget.
