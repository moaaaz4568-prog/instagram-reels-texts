6. The version of you you wanted to be
hasn't shown up yet.

10 years ago you had a picture of who
you'd be by now. Sharper. Healthier. Closer
to the life you wanted.

That version hasn't arrived. The job kept
the appointment for them.

A job that's prevented your better self
from showing up isn't a job. It's an
obstacle in a costume.
