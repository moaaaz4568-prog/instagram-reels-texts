1. He never lets a buyer see a flicker of doubt.

Altman says luxury buyers can smell hesitation, and the second they do, they take over and start looking for a reason to fire you.

So he walks in as the most certain person in the room, dead sure of the property and the price. High-stakes buyers follow conviction. The moment you waver, you hand them the wheel.

Certainty closes. Doubt gets you fired.
