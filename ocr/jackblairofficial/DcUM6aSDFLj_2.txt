2. “IF THIS CALL GOES PERFECTLY, WHAT DID THEY AGREE TO OUT LOUD?”
A call without a named finish line drifts into a conversation. Conversations feel good and close nothing. The difference is decided before the phone rings, not during.
Write the yes down in one sentence. Not “build rapport.” A date, a number, a name, a next step someone can put on a calendar.
Nobody closes by accident.
