4. "Set realistic expectations, then beat them in ways they don't expect."

Branson built Virgin's whole reputation on surprising and delighting customers across every business he touched.

He promised honestly and then over-delivered, so customers felt they got more than they paid for. Under-promise, over-deliver, and people sell you to everyone they know. The pleasant surprise turns a buyer into a fan and a referral.

Beat the expectation and they bring you the next sale.
