5. “WHAT WOULD YOU TELL THEM I DID, IN ONE SENTENCE?”
Referred customers arrived already valuable in the data, not just over time. The sentence the referrer says is the first pitch, and you don’t get to write it.
So rehearse it with them. Whatever they answer becomes the cleanest pitch you own, in the only voice the next buyer already trusts. Steal it word for word.
Your best pitch is a customer quoting you.
