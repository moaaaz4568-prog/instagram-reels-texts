4. June 1996. Off the coast of Sardinia.

The yacht was originally built for Coco Chanel in 1961. Belfort had renamed it after his second wife.

He insisted on sailing into a storm with 50-foot waves. The captain refused. Belfort pulled rank. The waves smashed the hatch and the yacht sank.

The Italian Navy pulled everyone out of the water. The lesson: the only person on the boat who can't see the storm is the one who paid for the boat.
