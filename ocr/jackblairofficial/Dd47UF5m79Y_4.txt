4. The nibble.

Right after the yes, the buyer asks for one more small thing, and reps give it away because the deal feels done. The fourth prompt lists the eight nibbles your buyer will try after the yes. Here's what it does:

It writes the trade for each nibble, in the words you'd say on the call, so nothing is given, only exchanged. Then it marks the two you should simply say no to, and writes the no so the deal stays warm.
