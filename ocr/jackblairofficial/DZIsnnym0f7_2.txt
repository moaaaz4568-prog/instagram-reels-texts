2. He puts the blame where it belongs.
On the closer.
The room whines that the leads are weak.
Blake holds up the good leads and tells
them flat out, these are for closers.
His point lands like a slap. A real closer
turns a bad lead into a deal. The second
you blame the list or the market, you have
handed away the one thing you control,
which is you.
Good closers do not get bad leads.
