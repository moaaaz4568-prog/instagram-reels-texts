4. "I want to understand specifically what would need to change for this to move forward."

Wick always assessed the obstacle before he committed his approach to removing it.
This phrase does the same thing on a stalled deal with complete precision and zero wasted energy.

The buyer who answers gives you the exact conditions of yes.
The buyer who cannot answer reveals that the obstacle was never concrete enough to be the real reason.
Either way you now have something real to work with instead of something vague to manage.

Find the specific obstacle. Vague obstacles protect comfortable inaction. Specific ones have comfortable solutions.
