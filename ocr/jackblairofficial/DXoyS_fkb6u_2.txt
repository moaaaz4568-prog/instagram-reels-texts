2. "If I could show you exactly how this works, would you at least be open to hearing it?"

Belfort never asked for the close cold.
He built a chain of small yeses first, one micro-agreement at a time.

By the time the real ask came, the buyer had already said yes five times in their head.

He didn't sell the close.
He sold the conversation that led to it.
