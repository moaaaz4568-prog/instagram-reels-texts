7. "I'm not going anywhere."

He sold for Converse from the early 1920s until he retired in 1968. Coaches trusted him because he kept coming back, season after season. Say it when a buyer is scared of being left alone after they sign.

Now look at what he built. Teach to you. Show up after the sale. It's the same playbook online companies run today.

He just had to drive to every buyer. You don't.
