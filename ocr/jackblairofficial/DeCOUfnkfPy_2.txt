2. The pattern he's seen 200 times.

His move on a rival is a pattern, not an argument: I've seen this play out 200 times, here's what happens next. I gave Claude my last twenty deals, won and lost, and asked for the three patterns I can say out loud the moment a buyer names a competitor. What came back:

Three patterns from twenty roofs, one sentence each. The one that lands: "Every bid that came in $9,000 under ours carried a one-year workmanship warranty. All three of those owners were calling a different roofer by year three." Claude's rule: say the number, say what happened, stop talking.
