1. "One loyal customer is worth $332,000."

Sewell's famous calculation, the one that reframed his whole business.

He did the math on a car buyer who stays loyal for life: the cars, the service, the referrals. It came to $332,000. After that, no single sale felt small, and no customer felt disposable.

Price one customer over a lifetime and you stop treating any of them cheap.
