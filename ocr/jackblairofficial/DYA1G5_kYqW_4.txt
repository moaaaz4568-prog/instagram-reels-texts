4. Art's leadership philosophy was three words: Push Up People.

He didn't hire executives from outside. He pulled them from his field force.

A schoolteacher who hit her sales numbers became a regional manager. A regional manager who hit hers became a vice president.

By the time A.L. Williams was the largest insurance company in America, every senior leader had started part-time at her own kitchen table.
