4. "We're fine either way. This only makes sense if it makes sense for you."

Here's Mamet's buried joke. Blake, the ABC guy, appears in one scene, dangles the Cadillac and the steak knives and drives off. Roma, who isn't even in the room for the speech, tops the board doing the exact opposite of it. Pressure telegraphs need, and need is the one smell no buyer has ever paid for.

Always-be-closing is how you never do.
