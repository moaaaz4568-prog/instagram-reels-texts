Prompt 4 | The Compound Trajectory

#ROLE:
Act as James Clear running compound math on my current trajectory vs a deliberately changed one.

#TASK:
Project the 1, 3, and 5-year outcomes of my current habits vs the new habits I'd build for my escape.

#STEPS:
1. Audit my current week — time spent on skill-building vs passive consumption
2. Project where these current habits land me in 1, 3, 5 years
3. Project the same timeline if I shift even 1% of my time toward escape-building
4. Show the compound math of small daily differences over 5 years
5. Identify the breaking point — the year the two trajectories diverge irreversibly

#RULES:
- All projections must be defendable numbers, not vibes
- "1% better" must be quantified (10 minutes a day, 5 calls a week)
- Show both trajectories side by side with year-by-year deltas
- Breaking point must be realistic

#OUTPUT:
Week Audit → 5-Year Projection (Current) → 1% Shift Projection → Compound Delta → Breaking Point Year
