"I COULD BE WRONG ABOUT THE STAKES HERE. WHAT BREAKS ON YOUR SIDE IF THIS DOESN'T WORK?"

Supply risk is one of the two axes, and it is measured by what happens to the buyer when a supplier fails. Not by how much they like the product.

Get the consequence said out loud, in their words, before anyone opens a spreadsheet.

Your price is set by what your failure would cost them.
