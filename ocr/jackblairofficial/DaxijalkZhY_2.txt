2. "Who else got left out of this decision? I should be talking to them."

To get in the door, Talleyrand rallied the other excluded parties, Spain and the smaller treaty signatories, and argued the Congress had no legitimacy without all of them. The embarrassed victors backed down and even burned the minutes of their earlier meetings. Then he took his seat at the big table.

When you're locked out of a deal, find who else is locked out, and arrive representing more than yourself.

Locked out alone is weak. Locked out together is leverage.
