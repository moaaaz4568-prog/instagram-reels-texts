2. "I get the sense there is something sitting underneath this that we have not talked about yet. Am I reading that right?"

This is the most disarming question available on any stalled call.
It signals that you see more than you are being shown, without accusation, without pressure.

The buyer who hears this almost always confirms it.
And what they say next is the most valuable information on the call,
the real reason the deal has not moved and the only thing that will move it.

Read between the lines out loud. The buyer will fill in what they find there.
