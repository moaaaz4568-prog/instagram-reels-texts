3. "IF THIS WERE YOUR IDEA INSTEAD OF MINE, WOULD IT WORK?"
In Ross's survey the terms never changed. Only the name on the proposal did, and that alone moved the vote by half.
Take the concession you planned to make anyway and hand it over to them as a hypothetical of theirs. Let them say yes to it as their own thought before you confirm it.
