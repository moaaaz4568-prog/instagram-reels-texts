5. "Give me thirty seconds to think about that properly. You'll get a better answer."

Logue's whole method ran on pace. He coached the King's broadcasts to a slow, measured delivery, and his last whispered words before a major one were about taking it quietly. Rushed reads as rattled.

When a heavyweight buyer fires the hard question, most reps blurt. Claiming the pause out loud reads as weight, not weakness.

A blurted answer sounds junior. A taken pause sounds senior.
