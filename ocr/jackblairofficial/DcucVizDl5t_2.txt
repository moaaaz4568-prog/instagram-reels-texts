2. "YOU CAN BUY JUST THE TOOL. IT DOES HALF THE JOB ALONE."
Studios could hire any single actor anywhere. CAA offered the actor with the director and the five writer already attached. Within four years the five were billing $90.2 million.
Concede the partial option out loud, then price what it leaves broken. Buyers hear honesty first, then start adding the missing pieces themselves.
Sold alone is sold short.
