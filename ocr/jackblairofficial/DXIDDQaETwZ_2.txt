2. "I'm not here to convince you. I'm here to show you what you're walking away from."

Pressure closes weak buyers and destroys strong ones.
This phrase removes pressure and replaces it with consequence.

The buyer stops feeling sold to.
They start doing the math themselves.
That is the shift every rep needs and almost none can create.

The best closer in the room never sounds like they're closing.
