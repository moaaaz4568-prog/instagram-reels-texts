4. "Tell me what the last rep who sat in this seat promised you and did not deliver."

This phrase is for the buyer who has been burned before and is using that burn as a shield. Tommy Egan always wanted to know what he was walking into before he made his move.

It surfaces the real objection hiding behind the polished scepticism.
The buyer tells you the truth and you get the only information that you actually matters.

Now you are not closing against a competitor. You are closing against a disappointment you can specifically fix.

Find the wound. Be the answer to it. The close follows.
