THE QUIET HALF IS A WEAPON
The introvert half of the curve carries the part
extraverts skip: hearing what the buyer actually
wants before responding to it.
Deploy silence as a tool you chose, in discovery and
after questions, not as a personality you apologise
for.
The notes you take outsell the lines you deliver.
