05. Voicemail Script

"Write me 3 voicemail scripts for a cold call to [job title] at [company type] in [industry].

Each voicemail must:
Be under 25 seconds when spoken aloud,
open with a specific reason for calling related to their world,
create enough curiosity that they want to call back or reply,
and end with one clear and easy next step.

Tone: calm, specific, not salesy.

No pitching. No fluff."

You stop leaving voicemails that never get returned.
Every message you leave now gives them a reason to call back.
