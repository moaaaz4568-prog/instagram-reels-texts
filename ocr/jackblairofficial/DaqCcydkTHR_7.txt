7. "Here's the short list. Everything on it works. Now it's just fit."

One more finding from the study. People who chose from the small set were happier with what they picked afterward.

So pre-vet the finalists. When every remaining option is one you'd stand behind, choosing wrong is off the table. The fear dies, the yes lands, and they like the deal more later.

Make wrong impossible and the yes gets easy.
