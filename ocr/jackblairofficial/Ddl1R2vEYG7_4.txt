4. "You're the one who has to live with this. Not me."

At the Leopold and Loeb sentencing Darrow spoke for roughly twelve hours to a single judge, John Caverly, and told him the responsibility was his alone. He never begged. He handed the weight over and let it sit there. When your buyer hesitates, stop pushing. Hand them the decision out loud, then stop talking.

Pressure makes people stall. Ownership makes them decide.
