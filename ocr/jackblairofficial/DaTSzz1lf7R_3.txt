3. "It sounds like you've been burned before."

His finding from decades of standoffs:
behind nearly every aggressive person is a
loss. A hostile prospect usually isn't hostile
at you, they're guarding an old wound
from the last guy. Speak to the loss and
the hostility drains out of the room.

Anger is a scar. Address the wound, not
the volume.
