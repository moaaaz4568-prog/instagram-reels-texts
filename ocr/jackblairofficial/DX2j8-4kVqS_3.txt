3. THE EVENING OUTREACH SCRIPT

What it does: builds 5 cold DMs that get coaches and agency owners to take a 15 minute call this week.

"Write 5 cold DMs I can send tonight to high ticket coaches, agencies, and offer owners hiring remote closers. Each DM under 60 words. Open with a specific reason I'm reaching out (reference their offer, their last launch, their recent post). No fake compliments. End with a direct ask for a 15 minute call this week. Vary the angles. Make it sound like a peer reaching out, not a job applicant."
