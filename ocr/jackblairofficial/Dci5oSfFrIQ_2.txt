2. HE SOLD THE PROBLEM BEFORE THE PRODUCT.
Feldman rarely opened with insurance. He opened with the hole a death would blow in the business.
He called it selling the problem, because a buyer who owns the problem goes looking for the product.
Reps do it backwards and wonder why nobody leans in.
No problem, no sale. Small problem, small sale.
