TAKE AS LONG AS YOU NEED. I’D RATHER YOU BE SURE.
Sugarman wrote long copy on purpose. A reader still reading is a reader still buying.
He never cut a page to save someone time. He cut anything that was not carrying its weight. A buyer asking more questions is not stalling, they are closing themselves.
Length never lost a deal. Boredom did.
