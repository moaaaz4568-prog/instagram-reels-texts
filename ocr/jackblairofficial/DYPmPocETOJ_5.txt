5. The role that picks you isn't the role you sought.

Daniel wasn't chasing Bond. Barbara Broccoli, the franchise producer, watched Layer Cake (2004) and decided he was 007.

The biggest role of his career found him during a low-budget British crime film, not during a Hollywood audition.

The right opening finds you when you're doing the work in front of you.
