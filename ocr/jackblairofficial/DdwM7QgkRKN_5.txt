5. "Cut the words I'm not sure. Keep everything else."

In the same experiments, one thing did cost the advisor, and it replicated the earlier research exactly. Advisors who said they were not sure were judged worse than confident ones. Same doubt as the number, the range and the lean. Different words. That phrase is the entire bill.

You are not paying for the doubt. You are paying for the phrasing.
