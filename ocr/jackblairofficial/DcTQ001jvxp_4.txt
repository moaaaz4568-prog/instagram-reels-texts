4. “YOU DON’T HAVE TO LEAVE THEM. SPLIT THE ORDER.”
He rarely asked an airline to divorce Boeing. He asked for a corner of the fleet, and the corner grew.
The flip is not a swap, it is a wedge: the pilot project, the second-vendor slot, the one region. Incumbents defend the whole account and lose the corner.
Nobody flips whole. Everybody flips a slice.
