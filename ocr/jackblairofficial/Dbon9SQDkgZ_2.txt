2. "I DON'T SELL THE PRODUCT. I SELL WHAT SHOWS UP ON THE DAY YOU NEED IT."
Feldman's most repeated reframe was that he did not sell life insurance, he sold money. He never described the mechanism, only what it turned into.
Stop naming your deliverable. Name what it becomes in their business on the worst week of their year.
Sell what it turns into, never what it is.
