6. "I'm genuinely good at this part. I'm honestly not the right fit for that part."

The effect only works from competence, so lead with what you're excellent at, then admit where you're not. Confidence plus one honest limit reads as an expert. Confidence with no limits reads as a salesman. Pair your real strength with a real boundary and buyers relax.

An expert names a limit. A salesman pretends there isn't one.
