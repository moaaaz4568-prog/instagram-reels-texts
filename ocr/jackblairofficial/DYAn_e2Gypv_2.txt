2. Bill memorized the shopping habits of
every customer on his route.

500 of them. By name. By family. By what
they bought last time.

He'd knock and open with: "Say, aren't you
about out of soap? That's what you
bought last time."

The buyer didn't have to explain
themselves. Bill already knew them.

Most salespeople try to be remembered.
The best ones remember first.
