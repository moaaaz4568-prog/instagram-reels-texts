2. "I want to be honest with you. I believe in this more than anything I have brought to a client in years."

Shelley Levene's greatest close was not technique. It was belief so raw it became contagious.

Buyers do not buy the product. They buy the person presenting them. When that conviction is real, unscripted, and slightly vulnerable, it lands in a place no polished pitch ever reaches.
You do not need a perfect month behind you. You need to mean what you say.

Authentic belief is the one thing no competitor can replicate.
