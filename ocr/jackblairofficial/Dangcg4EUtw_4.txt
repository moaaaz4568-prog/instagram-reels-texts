4. "We're not touching the hard one yet. Bank the easy three first."

His other documented method was step-by-step: small concessions agreed early, each one a stepping stone laid before the brutal issues. Momentum is a currency.

Stack the easy agreements out loud so that by the time you reach the deal-breaker, walking away means demolishing a staircase they helped build.
