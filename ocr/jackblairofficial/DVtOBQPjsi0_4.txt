Prompt 4 — The Objection Deepdive

"My buyers keep saying [specific objection]. Write me 5 different ways to respond depending on whether it's a price objection, a timing objection, or a trust objection. Make each response feel natural and confident, not rehearsed."

Different buyers mean the same objection for different reasons.

This prompt makes sure you have the right answer no matter which version shows up.
