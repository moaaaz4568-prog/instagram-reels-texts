3. "Before we get into the deal, tell me about you."

Government taught her this one. On Carter's White House staff, a colleague she thought did nothing but wander the halls talking to people got her a meeting with the chief of staff that she couldn't get herself. "Personal relationships trump everything else," she says, and the first five minutes of her calls are about the person, not the space. Ask about the buyer before you ask about the budget, and moves at the speed of you.

People sign with the person who knew them before the ask.
