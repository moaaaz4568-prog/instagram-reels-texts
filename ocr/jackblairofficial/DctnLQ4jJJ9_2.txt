2. "FORGET THE PRICE. WHAT WOULD YOU
RATHER HAVE THAN THE MONEY?"
A generator became an "instant party," a keg and a
lit Budweiser sign. The party became a snowmobile.
Both sides grinned in every photo, and money never
touched the chain.
When the number stalls, widen the trade: terms,
timing, access, the pilot, the intro. Hunt for the thing
they want more than they want to keep the money.
It's rarely about the money. It's about the swap.
