3. "WALK ME THROUGH HOW YOU DO IT TODAY.

at the top-grossing Woolworth's in America and out-
earned the store manager doing it. It won by
standing next to the slow manual way, staged long enough to hurt.

while you time it. Don't interrupt. The old way indicts
itself if you give it the floor.
