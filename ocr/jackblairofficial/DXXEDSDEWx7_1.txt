1. "I want to be upfront with you. I have done this enough times to know exactly how this conversation should go."

Ron Burgundy never walked into a room with doubt visible anywhere on his person.
He projected a history of success so completely that the room accepted it before asking for evidence.

This phrase signals experience and authority in the opening seconds before the buyer has formed a full opinion.
The buyer stops evaluating whether you belong in the conversation and starts listening to what you have to say.
Confidence announced early enough becomes the frame everything else is measured against.

Set the authority frame in the first sentence. The buyer will spend the rest of the call inside it.
