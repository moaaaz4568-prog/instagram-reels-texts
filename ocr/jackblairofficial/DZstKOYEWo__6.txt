6. "I do not compete on price. I compete
on me."

Umansky refused to win business by
cutting his fee.

When someone pushed on commission,
he did not flinch. He showed them the
network, the marketing, and the result
only he could deliver. Discounting your
price quietly tells people your value was
never real.

Never cut your price. Raise your proof.
