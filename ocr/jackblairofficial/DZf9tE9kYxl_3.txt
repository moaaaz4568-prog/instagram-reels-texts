3. "How long has this been going on, and what have you tried?"

Sandler built the pain funnel because people buy emotionally and justify it later with logic.

These questions walk the buyer down from a surface complaint to the real cost of doing nothing, in their own words. People are moved by the pain they say out loud, not the one you point at.

Let them talk their way into why they need you.
