5. "It's not the price. It's that you can't picture it yet. So come and look."

Downtown after 9/11, nobody would take an office, and no argument moved them. She asked a psychiatrist why. People fear the unknown, he said. So she stopped arguing and made the site familiar: fashion shows, art exhibits, anything that got people to come and stand there. Condé Nast later signed 1.1 million square feet at One World Trade Center. When a buyer stalls, the fix is rarely another point. It's a demo, a walkthrough, a call with a customer who already lives there.

A buyer can't say yes to something they can't see.
