05. Next Step Script

"Write me a closing line and a next step proposal for a prospect who said [paste what they said at the end of your last call].
The closing line must assume forward momentum without pressure. forward
The next step must be specific, time-bound, and easy to say yes to.
Under 60 words total.
Tone: confident, natural, frictionless.
No pitching. No fluff."

You never end a call without a real next step again.
Every conversation moves forward.
