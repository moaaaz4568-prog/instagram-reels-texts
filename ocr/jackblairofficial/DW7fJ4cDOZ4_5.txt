5. The Pre-empt Play
"Write a 150-word message I can send
to [prospect name] before I can send
our next call that frames 'checking with the
team' as a risk rather than a safety
net. Reference [their stated goal or
pain point]. Confident tone. No
pressure. No pitching."
They walk into the next call ready to
decide. Not stall.
