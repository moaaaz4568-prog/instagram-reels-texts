2. "All you needed was a dream and the willingness to take a risk."

Ulukaya's documented description of why he came to America.

He arrived in New York in 1994 with $3,000, no English, and no connections. He worked for a rug merchant and pumped gas at a Brooklyn filling station to pay for college.

The risk was the only thing he could afford.
