4. "I've heard that concern before. The reps who said that cost them the most."

Objections are not the problem.
The pattern behind the objection is.

This phrase reframes their hesitation as a familiar mistake, not a unique and valid reason to walk. It lands without arrogance because it use it sounds like experience, not ego.

Every objection has a graveyard. Put theirs in it before they commit.
