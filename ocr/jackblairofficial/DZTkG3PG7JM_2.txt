2. He set goals far bigger than his circumstances.

Hays repeated one phrase for the rest of his life, set high goals.

A broke kid from small-town Texas had no business aiming where he aimed, and he aimed there anyway. The target you set quietly decides how hard you work and who you become. Most people miss because they aimed too low to begin with.

Small goals are the most expensive thing you can set.
