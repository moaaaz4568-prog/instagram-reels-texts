3. He saw every client as a thirty year
relationship, not a signature.

While other agents chased the close and
moved on, Fakharzadeh kept showing up
long after the policy was signed.

He won more MetLife honors for keeping
clients than almost anyone alive, because
the families he served sent their
children, their colleagues, their neighbors.
One real relationship quietly became a
hundred introductions.

Sell once and you eat once, serve for life
and you never prospect cold again.
