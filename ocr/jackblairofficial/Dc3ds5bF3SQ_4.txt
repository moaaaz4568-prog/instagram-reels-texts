4. "IF IT FAILS, IT COMES BACK TO ME. NOT TO
THEM. TO ME."
It was her patent, her company and her name on the
box. When a mop failed it did not come back to a
network. It came back to her, and the audience
could hear that.
Put your own name on the promise, out loud, before
the buyer asks who is accountable. A promise with a
person attached is a different product from the same
promise on a page.
The channel sells the product. The name sells the
promise.
