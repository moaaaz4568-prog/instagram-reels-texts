THEY BUILT A FACTORY BEFORE THEY BROKE IT

When their supplier raised prices in 2008, Costco did not pass it on. It built its own hot dog plant to keep the number alive.

That is what figure it out means in a deal: absorb pain invisibly before you ever renegotiate a promise the buyer can see.

Buyers forgive your costs. They never forget your promises.
