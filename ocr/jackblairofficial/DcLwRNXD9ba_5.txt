4,000 A YEAR SAID IT WORKED
By 1856 more than four thousand reapers a year
were selling on the plan. The structure was not a
concession. It was the growth engine.
When a payment structure closes one impossible
deal, productize it. The second buyer should hear
policy, not favour.
A favour convinces one buyer. A policy convinces a
market.
