7. "Is there anything else I can do for you before I go? I have the time. I'll be back at three."

The scripted close of hourly rounding. In a six week study across 27 units in 14 hospitals, that visit, ending with an open question and a stated return time, cut call lights, cut falls and raised satisfaction. The open question says you're not rushing out. The time says you're coming back.

End every call with the open question and the hour you'll call again.

Now be honest with yourself. You already run half of these on every call. What is the room paying you for it?
