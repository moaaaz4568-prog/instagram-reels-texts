5. He knew the people around you
decide who you become.

Jones lived by a line he repeated for fifty
years, you'll be the same person in five
years except for the people you meet and
the books you read.

So he deliberately surrounded himself
with people better than him and let them
raise his standard. Your network isn't who
you know, it's who's quietly pulling you up.

Stand next to people you have to stretch
to keep up with.
