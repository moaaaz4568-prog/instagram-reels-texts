"SO DO YOU WANT IT?"
The manual is blunt about the ask. More sales are lost by the man who never plainly asked than by the man who was refused.
Say the short version and then stop talking. The silence after a plain ask does work that no additional sentence can do.
The shortest question in selling is the one most reps never say.
