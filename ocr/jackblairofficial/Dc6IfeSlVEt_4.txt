4. SELL THE RECEIPT, NOT THE MACHINE.
The script never led with brass and gears. It led with
what the register ended: missing cash, arguments,
messy books.
The product is never the purchase. What it makes
stop, and what it makes possible, is. Lead there and
the specs can wait.
They pay for outcomes. The product tags along.
