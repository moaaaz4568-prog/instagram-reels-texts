1. "I am not going to take you through a presentation. I am going to tell you three things that matter for your business right now."

Tywin Lannister never performed for a room. He informed it.
Senior buyers have survived a thousand presentations and they are exhausted by all of them.

This phrase signals immediately that this conversation operates at a different level.
No deck. No features. No theatre.
Just three things that matter, delivered by someone who did the work to know what they are.

Strip the performance. The executive who stops waiting for the pitch starts listening for the insight.
