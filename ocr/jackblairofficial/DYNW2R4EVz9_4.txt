4. The years that feel wasted are building craft.

Between 1995 and 2013, Pascal did dozens of guest spots: Buffy, Touched by an Angel, Law & Order, The Good Wife, NCIS.

He did more professional acting in those 18 years than most actors do in entire careers.

He didn't get more skilled at 38. He got seen at 38.

The skill compounds. The visibility is what eventually arrives.
