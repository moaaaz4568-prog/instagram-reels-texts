4. "WHAT'S YOUR GUT TELLING YOU YOU'LL DO?"
The boldest question in the set. Ask it while the stakes still feel low.
Across 116 experiments the finding repeats: people act consistent with predictions they hear themselves make. A buyer who says he thinks he is in just closed himself a week early.
A prediction out loud is a promise in public.
