02. Intent Signal Message
"A prospect who fits my ideal customer profile
just [describe the intent signal: visited my pricing page, watched a
demo video, opened my email 3 times, clicked a specific link].
My offer is: [one sentence].
Their role is: [job title] at [company type].
Write me 3 outreach messages that:
Reference the signal to start a conversation naturally without making
them feel tracked,
connect the signal to a relevant conversation about their situation,
and end with one low friction question.
Under 60 words each.
Tone: observational, warm, completely human.
Zero guesswork."
You stop ignoring the signals your prospects are already sending.
Every intent signal now becomes the opening line of a conversation
worth having.
