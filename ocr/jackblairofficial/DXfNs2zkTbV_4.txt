4. "I am not here to impress you with what I have done. I am here to show you what I am going to do."

Tommy always spoke in futures because his past was not yet the asset his vision was.
This phrase pulls the entire conversation forward into the territory where you are strong rather than your own history.

The buyer who was about to ask for case studies suddenly finds themselves evaluating a plan instead.
Plans are harder to dismiss than credentials because they speak directly to the buyer's future rather than your past.
The rep who sells the past never reaches.
Sell the future when the past is thin. The buyer who can see the plan buys the plan.
