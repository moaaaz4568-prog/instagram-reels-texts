4. "Let's not ask if it's expensive. Let's ask what it's worth if it hits."

Duke's whole game is expected value. You never judge a bet by what it costs, you judge it by the size of the pot against the odds. A buyer stuck on price is staring at the ante and ignoring the pot. Move the math to the upside and the number starts to shrink on its own.

Price is the ante. The pot is what you're playing for.
