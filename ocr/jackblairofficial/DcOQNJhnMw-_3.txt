ASK THE QUESTION THEY ARE HIDING
Underneath every stall on a big ticket is a private calculation: what happens to me if this fails. It never gets said out loud, so it never gets handled.
Ask it straight: if this went sideways in a year, what would it mean for you? The relief you hear is the real objection surfacing at last.
The unspoken risk kills more deals than price.
