2. "I don't have the biggest name in the room. What I do have is this."
Jimmy McGill never had the corner office. He had preparation, hunger, and the ability to make one thing land harder than everything else. This phrase frames the conversation before the buyer can dismiss you. You take the objection off the table yourself and replace it with proof. Own the room you are actually in.
