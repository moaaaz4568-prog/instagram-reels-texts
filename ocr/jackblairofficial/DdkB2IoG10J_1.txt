1. "I might be wrong about this, so tell me where."

In Give and Take, Adam Grant shows that people who talk with less certainty get more agreement, not less, because the listener stops defending and starts thinking. He calls it powerless communication, and it works hardest on a buyer who expects a pitch. Say it right after your first read of their situation, then stop talking. Whatever they correct is the real reason they took the call.

Certainty makes them defend. Doubt makes them talk.
