7. "DIG YOUR WELL BEFORE YOU'RE THIRSTY."

It is the title of his networking book and the whole method in six words. The 66 questions get asked while things are friendly, so the file already exists on the day the deal turns hard.

Start files on accounts you have not opened yet. The worst time to learn a buyer is while you need their signature.

Wells dug in a drought come up sand.
