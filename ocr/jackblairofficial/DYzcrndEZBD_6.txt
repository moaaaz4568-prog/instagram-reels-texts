6. "Objections are not rejection."

Blount on the moment most reps fold.

A rep hears no and feels personally rejected, so they retreat. Blount separates the two. An objection is just a question or a reflex, not a verdict on you. You answer it calmly and keep going.

They are not rejecting you. They are reacting to an interruption.
