7. "You are going to hear from someone else about this in the next thirty days. I would rather it be me."

Peggy Olson knew her ideas were right before the room agreed. She never waited for permission to believe that.

This phrase plants a flag without arrogance. It tells the buyer the decision is coming whether they like it or not, and positions you as the person they want to have that conversation with.

Confidence in your timing is the close most reps never make.
