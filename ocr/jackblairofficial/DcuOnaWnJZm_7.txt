7. "BEFORE YOU ANSWER, CAN I WATCH HOW
YOU DO IT TODAY?"

Every signal Ford needed was on the street, free,
two years early. Watching beats asking, and it was
the one method the 250 million dollars never bought.

The ride along, the shadow session, the screen
share of the current mess. Ten minutes of watching
beats an hour of asking. Then quote what you saw.

Watch the feet.
Quote the feet.
