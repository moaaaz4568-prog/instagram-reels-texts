2. "I WROTE DOWN WHAT WE FOUND. DOES THIS MATCH WHAT YOU REMEMBER?"
Loftus and Pickrell handed 24 adults a short written story about being lost in a mall at five. It never happened. A quarter of them adopted it as memory.
Buyers trust a written page more than their own recall. So send your recap within the hour, every line true, and have them confirm it. The memory they keep is now the accurate one, written in your words.
Whoever writes it down owns the record.
