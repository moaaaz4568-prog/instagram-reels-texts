3. "At least get a piece of the piece."

Master P's documented rule on partnership.

He never took a flat fee for his music. He never took a one-time license. He branded every album with the No Limit logo, cross-promoted every artist inside every album cover, and turned the label into a brand bigger than any single rapper on it.

He owned the franchise, not just the franchise player.
