1. "PICTURE THIS ALREADY FAILED. WHAT WENT WRONG FIRST?"
Gary Klein built the premortem on a strange finding.
Tell people a plan might fail and they shrug.
Tell them it already failed and ask why, and they get
30% sharper at naming causes. Run it mid-deal.
Past tense, straight face. The objections they've
been sitting on come out as history.
"Might fail" gets a shrug. "Did fail" gets the truth.
