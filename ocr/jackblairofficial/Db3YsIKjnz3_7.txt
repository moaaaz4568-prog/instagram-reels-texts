7. COUNT YOUR PICKS.
Five for five: you close under pressure for a living.
Three or four: your instincts beat your training. Two
or less: you answer the wrong letter every week.
Most reps do. It is what they were taught.
The five rules: contain, do not attack. State the line
once. Answer the buyer who wants the deal. Never
trade the deal for the last word. Build the bridge they
retreat across.
