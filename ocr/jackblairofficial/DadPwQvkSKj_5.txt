5. Objections are self-inflicted.
You pride yourself on handling objections.
The research called that the tell.
The gap between top and average was
never how they handled objections. It was
how few they got. Objections spike when
you pitch before the need is explicit.
Say: "Before I show you anything, tell me
what's broken about how you do it now."
