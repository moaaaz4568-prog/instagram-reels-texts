01. Objection Library

"Build me a complete objection handling library for a [job title] selling [product or service] to [ICP] in the [industry] space. List the 10 most common objections I will face in 2026. For each one write the real fear driving it underneath the surface, and one response that validates the concern, reframes the risk, and moves the conversation forward. Format as a numbered list, objection then fear then response. Under 50 words per response. Tone: confident, human, zero defensiveness. No fluff. No scripts."

You walk into every call knowing exactly what is coming. Nothing catches you off guard again.
