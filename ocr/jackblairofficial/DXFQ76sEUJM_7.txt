7. "I'd rather you tell me no today than a maybe that goes nowhere. Where do we actually stand?"

McNulty hated wasted time more than a bad outcome. A clean answer was always better than a slow fade. This phrase respects the buyer's time and yours, removes all social pressure, and almost always produces a real answer. Buyers respect the rep who asks for the truth. And the truth is always easier to close than a stall.
