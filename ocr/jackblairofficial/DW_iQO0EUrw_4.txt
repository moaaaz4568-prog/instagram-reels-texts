5. The Price Shield

"Write me a 3 part response I can use when a buyer says my price is too high. Part 1 acknowledges their concern. Part 2 reframes the cost against the cost of inaction. Part 3 closes with a question that moves the conversation forward. Under 100 words. No apologising. No pitching."

The price objection lands. You don't move. The deal does.
