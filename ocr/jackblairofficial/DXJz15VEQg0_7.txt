7. "I think you already know what you want to do. I think you have known for a while."

This is Roma at his most dangerous.
It is the phrase that names what the buyer has been avoiding and makes avoidance feel exhausting.

It does not close the deal. It removes the last reason not to.
The buyer who hears this and feels it is true has nowhere left to hide.
They do not feel sold to. They feel seen. And that is the close no objection can survive.

See the buyer clearly enough and they will close themselves every single time.
