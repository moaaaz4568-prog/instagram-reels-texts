4. "WHO OWNS THE GAP BETWEEN YOUR VENDORS WHEN SOMETHING FALLS THROUGH IT?"
A CAA package had no seams. Director, writer, star arrived aligned, one call when anything slipped. Studios weren't paying for talent. They were paying for no gaps.
Make the seams visible. Ask who owns each handoff today. Every gap they can't name is a cost you can, and you sell the only version without them.
Buyers forget parts. They remember gaps.
