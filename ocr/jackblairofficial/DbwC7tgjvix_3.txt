TAKE ME BACK. WHAT WAS GOING ON WHEN
THIS BECAME A PROBLEM?
Context reinstatement. Put a witness mentally back
at the scene, the sights and sounds and what they
felt, and recall jumps. Memory is filed by moment,
not by topic.
Stop asking what their problem is. Ask when it
became one. The story of the moment carries the
stakeholder and the urgency inside it,
unprompted.
Nobody remembers a pain point. Everybody
remembers a bad day.
