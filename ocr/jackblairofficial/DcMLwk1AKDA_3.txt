THE REFLEX HAS A NAME
The pattern got named after him: the Semmelweis reflex, the automatic rejection of evidence that convicts the person hearing it.
Expect it on every call where your product implies the old way was expensive. The pushback is not about your data. It is about their face.
Buyers reject the mirror, not the math.
