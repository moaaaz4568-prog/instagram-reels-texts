02. Question Stack

"Write me 10 discovery questions for a [job title] who is considering [product or service]. Each question must uncover pain, create self-awareness, or surface a consequence they have not considered. No yes or no questions. No surface level questions.
Each question under 20 words.
Tone: consultative, curious, peer to peer.
No fluff. No textbook sales language."

The rep with the best questions owns the call. Now yours are built before you dial.
