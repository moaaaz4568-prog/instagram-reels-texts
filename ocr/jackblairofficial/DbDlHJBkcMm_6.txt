6. "You told me a minute ago what actually matters to you. Let's hold everything to that."

Cicero listened for the admission and built his whole case on the opponent's own words. A point the listener conceded themselves can't be argued away later.

You catch the buyer's own stated priority and measure the decision against it. Their words close harder than yours ever will.

Their own words are the strongest thing you own.
