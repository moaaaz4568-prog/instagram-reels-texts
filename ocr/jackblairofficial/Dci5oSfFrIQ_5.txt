5. HE MADE THE PRICE FEEL LIKE PENNIES.
He broke premiums into pennies a day against the
size of the hole, and the giant number got small.
The skill was not discounting. The price never
moved. He changed the denominator, not the
number. Reframed the cost against the cost of doing
nothing.
Price is a fraction. Control the bottom half.
