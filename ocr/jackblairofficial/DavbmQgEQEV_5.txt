5. "Before we sign anything, what do your people need today?"

Lee's army was starving. Grant sent 25,000 rations across the lines before the ink had dried. The moment after yes is when generosity counts double, because nobody expects it from the winner.

Solve their urgent problem first and the contract stops being a defeat and starts being a rescue.

Feed them first. Sign after. They never forget it.
