6. "I did my homework, so I'll skip the basics. Here's the one thing I couldn't figure out on my own."

Rackham found fact-gathering questions bore buyers and quietly lower your status. The best reps ask fewer, sharper ones and walk in already knowing the easy answers. Never spend a live call collecting what a search bar could have told you.

Every question you could've googled costs you rank.
