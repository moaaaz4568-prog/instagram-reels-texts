Tracy proved selling is not about pressure,
it is about psychology.

Ask the right questions, build real value,
and guide a human calmly to yes. That is
closing, and right now it is the most
valuable skill in sales.

AI is wiping out every sales job that runs
on a script. The one thing it cannot do is
earn a real buyer's trust and walk them to
a decision. That part runs remote: you take them
from a laptop, book the calls, you keep a cut of every
deal you close. No floor, no commute, no
manager deciding your worth. If you have
ever asked the right question and
watched someone talk themselves into
yes, you already do this.
