2. The people around you aren't going where you're going.

The friends you grew up with. The coworkers you've been around for years. The family that loves you.

They have their lives. Their lives are not yours. Their next 10 years won't look like the next 10 years you want.

A future you can't share with the people closest to you is a future that will eventually ask you to leave.
