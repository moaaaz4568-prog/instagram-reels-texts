5. He asked for the close 7 times per call before walking away.

Most reps ask once. If the buyer says no, they back off and call it polite.

Hopkins reframed the no as one piece of data, not a verdict. He asked again 6 different ways before he accepted it as final.

The deal that closes on the 7th ask is the same deal that died on the 1st ask for the rep who asked once.
