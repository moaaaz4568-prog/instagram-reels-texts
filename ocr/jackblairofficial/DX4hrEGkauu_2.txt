2. THE ROLE CEILING

What it does: explains why your current job
mathematically can't pay more no matter
how hard you work.

"Now look at my job title and current
employer. Pull the standard comp band for
this role at this company size in my market.
Tell me the realistic ceiling, the average time
to hit it, and the percentage of people in this
role who break through it without changing
employers or roles. Show your math."
