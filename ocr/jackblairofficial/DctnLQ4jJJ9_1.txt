1. "YOU'VE GOT SOMETHING BETTER THAN BUDGET FOR THIS."
July 2005. Kyle MacDonald puts one red paperclip on the table and starts trading. The game: find the person for whom the thing is worth more. Fourteen trades later it's a house in Kipling, Saskatchewan.
Open your deals the same way. Inventory what the buyer already has: the unused licenses, the dying vendor contract, their story, their logo. Rooms run on more than money. Rooms run on several.
Budget is one currency. Rooms run on several.
