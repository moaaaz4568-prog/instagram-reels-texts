6. "I am not going to pretend I am not invested in this outcome. I am. Here is why that works in your favour."

Wick's commitment was never hidden and it was never a weakness.
It was the thing that made everyone who encountered him take the situation seriously.

This phrase converts visible investment from a desperation signal into a value signal.
The buyer who was reading your follow-through as neediness suddenly reads it as dedication.
A rep who is dedicated to the outcome is a partner worth having after the signature.

Own the investment. Then reframe it as the reason they should want you in their corner.
