6. "YOU DON'T HAVE TO BE FINE WITH THIS YET."

Of all six components, requesting forgiveness scored last. It asks the wounded party to do the work.

This line does the opposite, it hands them the timeline, and anger with nowhere to push against runs out of fuel fast. Give the room. You'll usually get the pardon you never asked for.

Forgiveness shows up faster when nobody asks for it.
