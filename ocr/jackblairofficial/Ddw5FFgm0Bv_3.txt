3. The town of 13,000.

"Who do you know that I should know?"
Feldman never left East Liverpool, Ohio,
population 13,000. From that desk he sold
more life insurance than anyone in history,
because he knew every business owner in
town and every one of them knew
someone else. I gave Claude my closed
deals and asked for my version of the
town. What came back:

It ranked fourteen accounts by how many owners each one can
seat me with, named the ten to see this month, and wrote every
ask. "One text with my name in it, and I will do the rest."
