BE THE INCUMBENT BEFORE YOU ARE ONE
The line aged out only when challengers stopped
fighting the fear and started borrowing safety: pilots,
guarantees, logos, one small safe yes at a time.
If you are the unsafe choice, shrink the bet until
saying yes to you is safer than ignoring you. Then
grow inside the door you opened.
You cannot argue away fear. You can shrink it.
