7. "You've told me a lot. Want me to show you what I'd do with it?"

The turn. The winners asked few questions, then used the answers. The losers kept asking, because they hadn't listened well enough to know when to stop. Recap three things they said, in their words. Then present only the part of your offer that touches those three. Everything else can wait for the second call.

Discovery ends when the buyer hears himself in your pitch.

Now look at where all seven started. 326,000 calls, and the reps who won asked less and heard more.

Sound familiar?
