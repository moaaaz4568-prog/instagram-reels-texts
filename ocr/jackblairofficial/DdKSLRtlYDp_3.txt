3. "How many times has the pay plan changed in the last 24 months?"

Once is a business decision. Three times is a strategy. Every rewrite lands after the reps have figured out how to win on the old one. Ask for the dates. If the plan changes more often than the model year, the store is managing you, not paying you.

A plan that keeps moving was never built for you to win.
