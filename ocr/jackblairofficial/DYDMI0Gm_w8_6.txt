6. The skill he was using was the wrong skill.

Rock could outwork, outlift, and outhit almost anyone. The NFL didn’t pay for that skill. It paid for being faster than 250 other guys with the same one.

His real skill was the room he walked into. He was just using it on the wrong product.

The skill that gets you noticed for free is usually the one you’re not getting paid for.
