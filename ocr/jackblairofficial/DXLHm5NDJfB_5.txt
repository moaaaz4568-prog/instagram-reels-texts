5. "Help me understand how decisions like this typically get made in your organisation."

Every company has a decision-making process that nobody puts in the proposal request.
This phrase finds it before you build your entire close around the wrong person.

Tom Hagen never assumed authority. He confirmed it quietly before he moved a single piece.
The rep who knows the real process owns the timeline.
The rep who guesses it loses two weeks and a deal to someone they never knew existed.

Process is power. Find it early. Use it throughout.
