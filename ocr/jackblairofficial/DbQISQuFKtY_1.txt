1. "LET'S NOT START THIS ON A RANDOM TUESDAY."
Hengchen Dai and Katy Milkman tracked gym visits, goal commitments and diet searches, and found them all spiking after temporal landmarks. New weeks, new months, birthdays.
People are 33% likelier to chase a goal at a week's start. Your deal is someone's goal. Stop launching it into the deadest part of their calendar where calendars start.
Deals start best where calendars start.
