1. "Test the headline before anything else."

From his 1932 book. The first rule he never broke.

He'd run two headlines against the same offer, same list, same week. Whichever pulled more orders won. The loser got killed. He was doing this in the 1920s, decades before anyone called it A/B testing.

He killed thousands of headlines this way. The ones that lived made BBDO clients millions.
