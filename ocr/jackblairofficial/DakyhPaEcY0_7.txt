7. "You'll never have every answer before you decide. Nobody at the table does."

Every hand Duke ever played was decided with cards still face down. Waiting for complete information isn't caution, it's how you lose to the person willing to act on good odds. For the buyer stuck in "let me think," name the fantasy of certainty and hand them the real choice.

Waiting for certainty is just folding slowly.
