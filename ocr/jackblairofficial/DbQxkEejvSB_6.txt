6. "DO YOU WANT THE NUMBER NOW, OR AFTER YOU'VE SEEN HOW THIS WORKS?"
Price is the loudest query in any deal, and whoever schedules it controls the sequence.
So hand the scheduling to them, with both options placed after value. Either answer keeps the order intact. Johnson's finding, applied: the number lands differently depending on everything retrieved before it. Same price. Different deal.
The same number weighs less after value.
