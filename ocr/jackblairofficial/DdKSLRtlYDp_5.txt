5. "What happens to my commission on
an unwind, a refinance, or a
chargeback?"

You closed it. The bank did not fund it. The
customer came back. Which of those
come out of your check, and how long
after the sale can they? Get it
in writing. The store already has the answer in a
document you have never been
shown.

A deal is not yours until you know what
can take it back.
