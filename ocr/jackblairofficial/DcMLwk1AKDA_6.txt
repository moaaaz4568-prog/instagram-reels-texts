ANGER READS AS RISK, NOT CONVICTION
As the rejections piled up, he wrote open letters
calling Europe's professors murderers. It ended his
influence entirely.
The moment you argue with heat, the buyer stops
weighing your claim and starts weighing you.
Conviction persuades. Fury disqualifies.
The louder the certainty, the smaller the audience.
