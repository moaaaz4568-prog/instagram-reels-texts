4. "I have heard that objection before. Here is what I found when I looked into it properly."

When a buyer dismisses you with a surface-level objection they expect you to fold. Most reps do. They apologise, they soften, they start over from a weaker position.

This phrase signals that you have seen this play before and you came prepared for it. It does not argue. It does not push back. It simply shows the buyer that you are several moves ahead.

Preparation is the only authority nobody can take from you.
