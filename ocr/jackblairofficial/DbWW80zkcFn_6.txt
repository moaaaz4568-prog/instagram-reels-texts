6. "I'D RATHER BOTH OF US WIN SMALL THAN BEAT YOU BIG."
Here's the tournament's strangest fact: tit for tat never once beat its direct partner in a single match, not one.
It took the top spot anyway, because both sides kept scoring the whole way. Say the line above to a buyer squaring up like it's a boxing match, and mean it. Deals are rounds. A book of business is the standings.
Lose the round, win the standings.
