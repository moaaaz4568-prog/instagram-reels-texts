"HERE'S WHERE I THINK WE ALREADY AGREE."
The recipe ends on common ground, and the study found receptiveness early in a disagreement forestalls escalation at the end of it.
Before the number talk, list out loud what is already settled: the problem is real, the timeline is real, who it is for. Shrink the disagreement to its actual size.
Most deals are ninety percent agreed. Argue only the last ten.
