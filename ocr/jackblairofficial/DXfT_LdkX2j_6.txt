6. "I am not going to make a decision under pressure that I am going to regret when the pressure is gone."

This is Vic Mackey's operating philosophy translated into a sales boundary and it is the most honest close on this list. It signals self-respect and strategic thinking in a single sentence.

The buyer who was applying pressure suddenly understands that the pressure is not working and never will. They either move to a genuine conversation about what works for both parties or they confirm that pressure was their only play. A buyer with no other play is a buyer who is about to accept the terms you already set.

Signal that pressure does not move you. The buyer who discovers this early stops wasting time and starts negotiating honestly.
