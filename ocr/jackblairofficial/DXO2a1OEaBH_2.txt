2. "You clearly know your industry well. That is exactly why I want to show you what most people in it are missing."

A hostile buyer arms themselves with their own expertise.
Tyron always honoured the intelligence of his opponents before he outmanoeuvred them.

This phrase validates their knowledge and immediately converts it into telling the reason to keep listening:
The buyer stops defending their position because you just put them on the same side as you.
Now you are not the rep with a pitch. You are the person who sees what their expertise almost missed.

Respect the intelligence in the room. Use it to open the door they were guarding.
