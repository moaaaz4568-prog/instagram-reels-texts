4. The first thirty seconds decided every call.

Most agents in 1955 opened with their company name. Their credentials. Their pitch.

Mehdi opened with a question about the buyer's family. About their kids. About what kept them up at night.

By the time he mentioned insurance, the buyer had already decided he was different.

The first thirty seconds is where the close lives. Most agents waste them.
