4. He sold premium in the customer's own space.

When Hays built Tom James, he did not open a store and wait.

He sent sellers to fit custom suits in the client's home or office, the same door to door logic aimed at high ticket. Meeting the buyer on their turf removes friction and signals you are worth their time. Bring the offer to them and the close gets easier.

Go to the buyer, do not wait for the buyer to come to you.
