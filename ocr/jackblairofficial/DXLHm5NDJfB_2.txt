2. "I want to make sure that when this goes to your team, they have everything they need to feel confident."

This phrase does two things simultaneously. It acknowledges that the decision is bigger than one person without making that person feel powerless.

It also positions you as someone who is thinking about their internal sale, not just yours. The buyer suddenly has an ally in the process instead of a rep at the door. Tom Hagen never left a meeting with him he met could win their own room.

Arm your champion. Their sale is your sale.
