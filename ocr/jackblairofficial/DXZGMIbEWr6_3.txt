03. Angle Rotator

"I am reaching out to [job title] at [company type]
in the [industry] space about [one sentence on your offer].

Write me 5 completely different outreach angles
I can use across different touches in my sequence.

Each angle must approach the same offer from a different
direction
reference a different pain point, outcome or trigger
and feel like a standalone message not a follow-up.

Under 40 words per angle summary.

Tone: varied, fresh, never repetitive.

No fluff. Zero guesswork."

You stop running out of things to say after the second touch.
Every message in your sequence hits a completely different
nerve.
