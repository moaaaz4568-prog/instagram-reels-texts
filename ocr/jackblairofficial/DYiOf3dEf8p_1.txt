1. “Deliver WOW through service.”

Core value #1 at Zappos, painted on the wall in the call center.

Every Zappos call had one goal: make the customer’s day. Not move them through fast. Not upsell. Just create one moment they’d tell other people about. That was the entire pitch.

Zappos didn’t sell shoes. They sold a moment. The shoes were the souvenir.
