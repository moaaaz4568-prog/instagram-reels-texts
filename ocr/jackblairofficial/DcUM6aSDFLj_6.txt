6. "WHAT AM I BRINGING THAT THEY HAVEN'T SEEN BEFORE?"
The just-checking-in call is the most hated call in sales. It asks the buyer to do your job: find a reason this conversation should exist.
No new number, no new proof, no new idea? Don't dial yet. One fresh piece of value turns a nag into a visit worth taking.
"Just checking in" means "I brought nothing."
