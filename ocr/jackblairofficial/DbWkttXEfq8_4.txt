4. "IF YOU'RE JUST AFTER THE CHEAPEST
OPTION, I'M HONESTLY NOT IT."
De Beers never defended the price. Its ads told
buyers a diamond should cost two months' salary,
and the market moved up to meet it.
Saying it out loud costs you the buyers you were
never going to close and buys conviction with
everyone else. A seller who can name who they're
not for gets believed about who they are for.
"For everyone" is another way to say cheap.
