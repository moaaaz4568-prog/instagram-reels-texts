3. "Act as if you are already the closer you want to be."
Maltz taught that you do not wait to feel confident, you behave as the person first.
He found that acting the part reshapes the self-image that drives behavior. Carry yourself, speak, and prepare like the top closer on the floor today, not someday. The feeling catches up to the action, never the other way around.
Behave like it now and you become it.
