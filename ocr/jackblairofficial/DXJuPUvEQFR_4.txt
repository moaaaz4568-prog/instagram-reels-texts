4. "I am not here to tell you we are better. I am here to show you where we are different."

Better is a claim every rep makes and every buyer discounts immediately.
Different is a conversation. And conversations close deals.

This phrase removes you from the feature war entirely.
It positions you instead of the competitor's.
The buyer stops scoring you against the other option and starts evaluating you on your own merits.

Change the comparison. Change the outcome.
