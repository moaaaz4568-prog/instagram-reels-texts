1. The Objection Map

"I sell [product or service] to [target buyer] at a price point of [price]. Write me a list of the 10 most likely objections I will face on a sales call, ranked from most to least common. For each one include why the buyer is really saying it underneath the words. Under 300 words. No fluff."
