4. "When the price comes up, here's the sentence that goes with it."
Sharpening was their name for it: one detail outlives the rest and becomes the whole story. In your deal, that surviving detail is always the number. Never let the price travel alone. Weld it to the one line that justifies it, or the partner hears a cost with no case.
A price that travels alone arrives twice as heavy.
