4. "You know what, I think we are actually going to get along well. Here is why I say that."

Neal Caffrey never waited for rapport to develop organically when he could accelerate it deliberately.
This phrase names the connection before it has fully formed and makes it real by naming it.

The buyer who hears this smiles before they know why.
What follows must be specific, observational, and true, not flattery, not performance, real.
A genuine observation about the buyer lands like a gift no other rep thought to bring.

Name the connection. Then make it real with something only you noticed.
