7. END WARM, WHATEVER THE GAVEL SAYS.
Nineteen minutes of war ended with a smile and a
joke. The most expensive sale in history closed like
a dinner among friends.
End warm even when it dies. The buyer who walked
today refers you tomorrow only if the last minute felt
clean.
Every ending is the next deal's opening.
