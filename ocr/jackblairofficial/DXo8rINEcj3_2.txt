02. Research Compensator

"I am new to selling [product or service] to [ICP]
and do not yet have a track record to reference in my outreach.

Write me a cold outreach message for [job title]
at [company type] in [the industry] space
where the credibility comes entirely from the depth of my research
rather than from results I have not yet produced.

The research I have done is: [paste what you know about their
industry, challenges or trends].

Under 80 words.

Tone: informed, specific, completely confident despite the lack of
history.

No pitching. No fluff."

You stop waiting until you have results before you reach out.
Deep research replaces the track record you have not built yet and the
buyer cannot tell the difference.
