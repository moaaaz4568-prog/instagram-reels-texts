1. "BEFORE WE TALK YOUR PRICE, HERE'S WHAT THIS USUALLY RUNS."
Kahneman proved the first number a person hears quietly sets the frame for every number after it. He called it anchoring.
Drop a real, higher reference point before the discussion and your actual price lands as a relief, not a shock. The first number wins.
Set the anchor before they set it for you.
