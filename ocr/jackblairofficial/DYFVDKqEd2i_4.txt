4. The coach was the first adult who saw him.

Bobby Stewart was a counselor for Boys, the juvenile detention facility Mike was in at 13. Stewart noticed how hard Mike hit a heavy bag and called Cus D'Amato.

Cus drove up from the Catskills and watched Mike spar for 10 minutes.

Some lives change because one person finally looked at you instead of through you.
