3. "You're probably right that now isn't the time."

The extraverts in Grant's 340 lost because they pushed through the stall. The ambiverts agreed with it, then asked a question. Say the sentence flat, like you mean it, then ask what would have to change for it to be the time. A buyer who was braced for pressure has nothing to push against, so they answer honestly, and the honest answer is your next call.

Agree with the no. Then ask what a yes needs.
