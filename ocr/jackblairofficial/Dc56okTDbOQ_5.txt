5. KEEP THE LOSING BIDDER ALIVE.
His small nods kept hesitating paddles in the game.
One more, his eyes said, and one more was often
the whole difference.
A quiet buyer is not a dead buyer. A light check in
that asks for nothing keeps them at the table until
they are ready to move.
Deals die from neglect, not from no.
