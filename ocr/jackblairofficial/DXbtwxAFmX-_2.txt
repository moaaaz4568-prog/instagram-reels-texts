2. "The organisations that move on this in the next six months are going to look back on it as the decision that changed everything."

Fury always made the stakes of inaction feel historically significant.
He never let anyone in his orbit believe that the choice in front of them was a small one.

This phrase elevates the decision from a budget conversation to a strategic one.
The buyer who was treating this as a routine vendor evaluation suddenly feels the weight of a real inflection point.
And buyers who feel the weight of an inflection point make decisions at a completely different speed.

Make the moment feel significant. Because the buyer who moves it almost always is.
