4. The buyer who thinks you're too small.

The last prompt makes Claude the buyer who has been pitched by the big firms and wants to know why you. It presses on size, track record and price, then grades whether you sounded like the authority or asked to be one. Here's what it does:

A restaurant owner holding two quotes from bigger payroll companies. Grade: 7/10. The line that worked: "Of the nine restaurants on our report, seven were paying late fees before. Which are you?" The line that lost a point: "we're small but we care." Nobody buys from small.
