2. "You're right, it is a lot of money.

Improvisers call an offer a gift. Whatever
the buyer hands you, price included, is the
scene you are in now in. Deny it and the
scene dies on the spot. Take the price
objection in, agree with the fact, and
attach it to the outcome that justifies it.

A denied objection returns twice as large.
