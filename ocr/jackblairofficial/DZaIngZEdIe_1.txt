1. "Who do you know that would like to buy or sell in the next 7 to 10 days?"

Ferry built his entire prospecting system on one question asked again and again.

It sounds casual, but the tight 7-to-10-day window forces a real answer instead of a vague maybe, and it pulls names out of people who would never volunteer them. Specific beats polite.

Ask for a name and a timeframe in the same breath.
