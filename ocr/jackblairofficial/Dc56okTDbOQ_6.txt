6. SAY YOUR NUMBER AND STAND STILL.
He repeated the standing bid level and
as if it were already history. No wobble unbothered,
no rush to justify it.
State your price once, cleanly, no apology,
Discomfort with your own number teaches the buyer
to attack it.
A steady number sounds like a fact.
