4. "What matters most to you today?"

In 2012 two doctors wrote in the New England Journal of Medicine that the question should change from "what is the matter with you" to "what matters to you". It became a movement in hospitals around the world. It is also the only discovery question you need, because it replaces your feature list with the buyer's own reason.

You can't sell what matters until they say it out loud.
