5. "We have worked together for a long time. That is exactly why I am being straight with you about what I can and cannot do."

Vic Mackey always used relationship history as a reason for honesty not a reason for capitulation.
This phrase flips the buyer's use of the relationship as leverage into your use of it as transparency.

The buyer who invoked loyalty to get a discount suddenly receives loyalty delivered as directness instead.
You are not dismissing the relationship. You are honouring it in the only way that actually respects it.
The relationship that survives honest negotiation is always stronger than the one built on concessions nobody meant to make.

Use the relationship to justify honesty not complacency. It closes better and lasts longer.
