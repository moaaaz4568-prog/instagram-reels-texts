SHE STARTED YEARS BEFORE THE ASK
Walters wrote notes constantly. Congratulations,
condolences, thank yous, in her own hand, to
newsmakers, their lawyers and their mothers.
By the time a story broke, she was not a stranger
asking for a favor. She was a name the family
already knew.
The pursuit starts long before the opportunity does.
