1. "BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE, HOW ARE YOU FEELING ABOUT HOW THIS HAS GONE?"
For two decades, Wharton MBAs spent more auction points on Stuart Diamond's negotiation course than on any other. He built it on one uncomfortable finding: the moment someone turns emotional, arguments stop landing.
Open every tense call right there, before the recap, before the agenda. You can't bargain with a feeling you haven't found, and no buyer volunteers one while you're selling.
Moods set the exchange rate on reason.
