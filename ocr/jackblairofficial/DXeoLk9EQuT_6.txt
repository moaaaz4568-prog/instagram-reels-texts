6.

"If money wasn't the issue at all, would there be anything else holding you back?"

Surface objections are smokescreens.
The real objection lives one layer deeper, and ask the question that uncovers it.

Answer what they said and you lose.
Find what they meant and you win.

The first objection is rarely the last one.
