6. "This is Maria. She's got you tonight. I've told her everything, so you won't have to repeat yourself."

The warm handoff, done at the bedside, in front of the patient. The next person is introduced by name, the story travels with them, and the patient never starts over. Reps lose more deals in the handoff to onboarding, finance or the manager than they lose in the close. Do it live, by name, with the story intact.

A handoff made in front of the buyer is a promise kept in public.
