03. Content Engager

"A prospect just [liked, commented on or shared] this piece of content I created:
[paste or describe your content].

Their role is: [job title] at [company type] in the [industry] space.
My offer is: [one sentence].

Write me 3 outreach messages that:
Reference the specific content they engaged with as the reason for reaching out,
add one piece of value or insight that extends the content naturally,
and end with one question connected to their world.

Under 60 words each.

Tone: genuine, content-led, zero sales pressure.
No pitching. No fluff."

You stop letting content engagement go unacknowledged.
Every like, share and comment now becomes the warmest outreach conversation you have all week.
