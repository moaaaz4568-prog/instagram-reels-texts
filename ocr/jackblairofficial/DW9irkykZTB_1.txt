1. "I've said what I needed to say. The next move is yours."

Rhades never repeated himself in a courtroom. He made his case and let the weight of it sit.

Most reps panic the second a buyer goes quiet. They start talking again and undo everything they just built.

Stop rescuing them from the silence. That silence is doing your job.
