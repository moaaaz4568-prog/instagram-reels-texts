2. "Who actually gives the yes on this? If it isn't you, let's get them on the call."

Her rule, in her own words: "Who is it that can actually say yes? If you're not talking to that person, you're not going to get a yes." A 1.1 million square foot lease has a dozen people who can say no and one who can say yes. Most reps pitch call and find out on the follow-up that the decision lives somewhere else. Ask inside the first ten minutes. If the answer is a name you haven't met, your next job is that meeting, not a proposal.

A yes from the wrong person is a no you haven't heard yet.
