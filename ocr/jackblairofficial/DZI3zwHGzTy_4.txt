4. "Everybody has a number. We just have to find it."

Goodman assumes a deal exists and treats his job as locating the terms.

He approaches every standoff believing there is a version both sides can accept. That confidence keeps him probing for the real motivator instead of giving up. Stay curious about what would make them say yes and you usually find it.

There is always a deal. Go find it.
