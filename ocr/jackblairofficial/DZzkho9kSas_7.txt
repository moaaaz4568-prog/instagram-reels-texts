7. He reframed what everyone thought
they were buying.

Beane changed the question from "who's
the star" to "who helps us win."

When you can't win on the old terms,
change the terms. Shift the buyer's frame
to the ground where your offer is clearly
the best choice, and the decision makes
itself.

Change the question and you change the
answer.
