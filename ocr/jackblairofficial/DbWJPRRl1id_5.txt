5. "WHICH PARTS OF THIS SHOULD WE JUDGE

small ones scored separately. That's the book's core
prescription, structure before verdict.

each scored on its own before anyone says overall.
Structure is a cage for the gut, and the gut escapes
any verdict it can reach whole.
Nothing hides in a line item.
