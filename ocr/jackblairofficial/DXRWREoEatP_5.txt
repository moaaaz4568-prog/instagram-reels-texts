5. "I want to make sure this conversation is actually useful for you, not just for me. What would make this call worth your time?"

Neal Caffrey always positioned himself as someone who was there for the other person first.
That posture disarmed rooms that were armed against him before he walked through the door.

This phrase converts a sales call into a service conversation in a single sentence.
The guarded buyer stops waiting for the pitch and starts thinking about what they actually need.
Now you are not a rep on a call. You are the person who asked the question on nobody else asked.

Serve before you sell. The buyer who feels served never feels sold to.
