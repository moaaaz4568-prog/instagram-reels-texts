4. "This isn't about me needing the deal. This is about what happens to your business if you don't move."

The moment Kendall stopped making it about himself was the moment he became dangerous. This phrase removes you from the equation entirely and puts the buyer's own consequences at the centre. They stop evaluating you and start evaluating their own decision. The best close is the one where the buyer closes themselves.
