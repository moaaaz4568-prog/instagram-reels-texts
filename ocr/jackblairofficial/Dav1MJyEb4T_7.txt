7. "People sitting exactly where you are finished this. The pattern is on my side."

The record built itself into proof. Two passers in 1978. Seven in 1980. Fourteen in 1981. Eighteen in 1982. By 1991, six hundred Garfield students were sitting AP exams. Nobody had to believe Escalante anymore, they just had to look.

When belief is the objection, stop arguing the offer and show the pattern of people who started in their seat.

Precedent closes what pitches can't.
