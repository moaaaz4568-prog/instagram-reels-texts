6. "Every month this waits is about $2,400 you don't get back. Want me to show you where that comes from?"

His rule: dramatize your ideas. Truth alone isn't enough, it has to be made vivid. Don't say the cost of waiting. Show it as a number they can picture and offer to prove it.

A number they can picture beats an argument they can debate.
