2. "I have been thinking about your business and I want to share something I noticed."

This phrase has nothing to do with your product and everything to do with their world. It signals that the relationship did not end when the contract was signed.

Buyers who feel genuinely considered do not take competitor calls seriously. They already have someone who thinks about their business when they are not in the room. That is the most defensible position in any account and it costs nothing but attention.

Think about their business more than they expect. They will never look for a replacement.
