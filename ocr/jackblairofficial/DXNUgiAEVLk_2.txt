2. "You are not just buying what I am selling. You are buying the fact that I will be here when it matters."

Tommy Egan's greatest currency was reliability, never money. It was reliability when everything else was chaos.
That is the one thing every buyer secretly needs and almost no rep ever offers explicitly.

This phrase separates you from every competitor who disappears after the signature.
It makes the relationship part of the value proposition, not an afterthought.
The buyer stops comparing features and starts comparing what it feels like to have someone in their corner.

Be the rep they call when something goes wrong. Win the deal before it even starts.
