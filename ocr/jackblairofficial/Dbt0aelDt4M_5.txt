"HOW DID THAT LAND ON YOUR SIDE?"
The method that actually worked in the experiments
was almost insulting in its simplicity: getting the
other person's perspective directly, by asking.
Price lands, and most reps scan the face for the
verdict. Skip the scan. Request the verdict out loud
while it is still forming.
Reactions are told, not read.
