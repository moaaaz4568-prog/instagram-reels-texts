5. "I want to be direct with you because I think you can handle it."

This phrase is a velvet scalpel.
It signals respect and cuts through stall tactics in the same breath.

Buyers who are used to being coddled by reps do not expect directness.
It resets the dynamic and puts you back in control of the room.
What follows it lands ten times harder because you earned the right to say it.

Respect is a frame. Set it early and never apologise for it.
