3. "The lost deals aren't the real problem. It's what they're doing to your confidence."

People can name their external problem, but they buy the solution to the internal one. Miller's point is that the surface issue is never what actually moves them. Name the feeling underneath the fact, and the buyer feels understood in a way no competitor bothered to try.

People buy the fix for the feeling, not the fact.
