6. "You went quiet and I get it. But I'd rather ask than assume."
This phrase turns the awkward follow-up into a human moment.
The buyer stops feeling hunted and starts feeling respected.
Most reps assume silence means no and disappear.
Mike assumed nothing. He asked. Then he listened.
The rep who asks outlasts the rep who assumes every time.
