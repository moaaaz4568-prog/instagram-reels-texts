6. "I do not work with everyone. I want to make sure this is the right fit before we go any further."

Omar chose who he worked with because the code meant the work had to be worth doing.
This phrase deploys a sales context and the effect on the buyer is immediate.

The buyer who was used to being pursued suddenly finds themselves being evaluated.
That reversal of dynamic changes the entire energy of the conversation before a single feature has been mentioned.
The rep who can walk away from the wrong fit is always the rep the right fits choose first.

Signal the selectivity. The buyer who feels evaluated almost always wants to qualify.
