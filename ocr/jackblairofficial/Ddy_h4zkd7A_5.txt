5. "Given the chance, would you start now or wait for the next opening?"
"Given the chance" and "option is" both predicted funding. The pitches that framed the offer as one option among a few, open now, got backed. The researchers called it scarcity, and they found it in pitches that never used the word urgent. So make the choice real and make it a question. Most offers have an actual start date, a cohort or a calendar, and asking about it lets the buyer feel the window without you inventing one.
Scarcity you have to invent is scarcity you don't have.
