3. “Make the calls on the days you do
not feel like it.”

Ferry on the difference between pros and
amateurs.

Anyone can prospect on a good day. He
taught that the money is made on the
days you do not feel like picking up the
phone and do it anyway. Consistency, not
mood, builds the business.

The calls you make on the bad days are
the ones that pay.
