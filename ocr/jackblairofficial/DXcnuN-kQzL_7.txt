7. "I could tell you what you want to hear. That is not why you should work with me."

This is Baum's philosophy in a single sentence and the most powerful positioning statement available on any call. It signals that your value is not in your agreeableness. It is in your accuracy.

The buyer who has been surrounded by people telling them comfortable things suddenly wants to know what accurate feels like.

This phrase does not close the deal. It makes the buyer decide they want to be closed by someone like you. And that decision is always worth more than any technique that follows it.

Position yourself as the truth in a market full of comfortable lies. The buyer always remembers who told them.
