4. HE LET THE SILENCE DO THE INTERROGATING.
Carter answered honestly that he had not always
done his best. Rickover paused, then asked, why
not.
Two words, and a future president carried them for
the rest of his life. The most powerful follow up is
short, quiet and impossible to dodge.
Why not is a complete sentence.
