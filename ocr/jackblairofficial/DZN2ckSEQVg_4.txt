4. He made himself impossible to ignore.

As Madman Muntz he wore wild costumes and ran outrageous stunts on TV.

He turned a used-car lot into a show, and people tuned in just to see what he'd do next. Boring gets skipped. A character gets watched, and watched is the first step to sold.

Be unforgettable and they keep watching.
