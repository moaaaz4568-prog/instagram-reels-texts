5. "You won't be the first. Here's who already carries it."

Deliveries from that drawer went to Paul Stuart, Neiman Marcus and Louis of Boston, the best men's stores in the country. After that no buyer was gambling on a kid from the Bronx with a box of ties. They were catching up to the stores they already copied. Before your next call, write down the two names your buyer respects most who already said yes. Lead with them.

Nobody wants to be first. Everybody wants to be next.
