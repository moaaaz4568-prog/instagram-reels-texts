PRICE THE FAILURE, NOT THE PRODUCT

Safe never means cheap. IBM carried premium prices for decades because the alternative being weighed was not a rival system, it was a career.

When they push the math: what does the wrong choice cost in rework, downtime and explanations. Your premium is the insurance line.

Expensive and safe beats cheap and explained.
