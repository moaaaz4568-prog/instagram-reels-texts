5. "In every adversity is the seed of an equal benefit."

Stone refused to treat a setback as the end of the story, and built that into the way he sold.

A rejection became information, a lost deal became a lesson, and the reframe kept him moving when other reps folded. The story you tell about the no decides what you do next.

Find the opening inside the setback and keep going.
