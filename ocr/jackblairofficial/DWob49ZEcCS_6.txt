6. "People tell you who they are.
But we ignore it because we want them
to be who we want them to be."

The most expensive mistake
in sales.

The rep who hears "I need to think
about it" and keeps pitching
isn't listening.

The buyer already told you
what they need.

Listen to what they're actually saying.
Then close them on that.
Not on what you wish they'd said.
