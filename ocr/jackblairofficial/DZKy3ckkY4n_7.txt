7. He kept aiming higher when he had every reason to coast.

After building a property empire, McGrath still says his goal is to build the greatest real estate company in history.

He never let early success cap his ambition. A failed student who lost his sporting dream simply refused to stop. Whatever you have built, set the target past it. The size of your goal sets the size of your effort.

Aim past what you have already done.
