This is the part that should scare you,
and it is not a layoff.

Nobody got walked out for this. There was
no announcement, no severance, no 6am
email. The jobs just stopped being
created. A manager needed a person,
could not prove the machine would fail,
and the request died quietly in a meeting
you were never in. That is not
watching the news for layoffs. That is not
what this looks like when it reaches you. It
looks like nothing happening at all.
