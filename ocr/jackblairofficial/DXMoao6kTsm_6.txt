6. "You clearly know your space well. That is exactly why I want to show you something you probably have not seen."

Dominant buyers are often highly experienced and proud of it.
Fighting that pride is the fastest way to lose the room permanently.

This phrase honours their expertise and converts it into the reason to keep listening.
The buyer stops defending their position and starts wondering what they missed.
Al Swearengen never beat a strong opponent by going at them directly.
He made them move themselves.

Validate the strength. Then use it to open the door they were guarding.
