1. "Before we go any further, help me understand who else this decision affects inside your organisation."

Most reps close the person in front of them and lose the deal to someone they never met. Tom Hagen always knew every player before the meeting started.

This phrase maps the room early while it still costs you nothing to ask.
You find the blocker, the champion, the budget holder, and the silent veto in one conversation.
The deal you thought was a straight line becomes a map, and maps win deals that guesswork loses.

Know the room before you pitch. Every name is a door.
