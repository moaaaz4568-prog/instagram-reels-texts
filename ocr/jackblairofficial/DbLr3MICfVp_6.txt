6. "IF THE ANSWER IS NO, NOTHING CHANGES
BETWEEN US."
A pressured recruit was a dangerous recruit, so Hall
made refusal safe.
The safety itself pulled people in, because a free
choice is a real one. Make no genuinely safe and
say it out loud. Then stop selling.
A safe no today beats a resentful yes.
