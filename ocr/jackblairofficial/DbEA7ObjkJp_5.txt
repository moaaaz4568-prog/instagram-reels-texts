5. "Let me give you a reason to move on this that has nothing to do with me selling it."

Bernays never pushed the product. He created an occasion, a change in the world that made the product the natural response, and the pitch rode in on something that wasn't a pitch.

You attach the decision to a real trigger in the buyer's situation, a deadline, a change, a cost that just appeared. Now moving is a response to their world, not to your ask.

Give the yes a reason that isn't you.
