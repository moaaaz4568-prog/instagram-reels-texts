GIVE THE COMMITTEE ITS ONE SENTENCE
A committee does not approve the best option. It
approves the most explainable one, the choice that
sounds responsible, title retold in one sentence.
Write the sentence for them: we went with the team
that has done this forty times at our size. If your
champion cannot say it in one breath, tighten it.
The most repeatable reason wins the room.
