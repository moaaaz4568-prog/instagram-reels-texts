03. Job Change Opener

"A prospect just started a new role as [job title] at [company name or type].
My offer is [one sentence on what you sell].

Write me 3 outreach messages that:
Reference the new role as the reason for reaching out,
connect the challenges of their first 90 days to the problem I solve,
and position my offer as something that helps them win early in the role.

Under 70 words each.
No generic new role pitch.

Tone: timely, specific, peer to peer.

No pitching. No fluff. Zero guesswork."

You stop missing the most receptive window in any buyer's career.
A new role means new problems and you now show up with the answer before anyone else does.
