3. "WHEN'S THE LAST TIME YOU MADE A
CHANGE THIS SIZE?"
The method's core move. Canvassers didn't argue
their case, they asked people for a memory of their
own that carried the same weight.
On a deal, that's the last big change your buyer
made that paid off. Get them telling that story and
their own proof starts arguing for you.
People are persuaded by their own memories, not
your examples.
