4. "I am going to walk you through the deal so you have everything you need to support it internally."

Gerri always made sure every person whose support she needed had the information to give it confidently. She never assumed that good deals sold themselves inside a company.

This phrase arms your internal champion with the exact language, data, and framing they need to win the room you are not in.

The deal that depends on a champion who was never properly briefed almost always loses to a stakeholder who was.

Your champion is only as strong as the case you built for them to carry.

Brief your champion completely. The deal without you is the deal that survives every internal meeting.
