4. The buyer who lowballs everything.

The fourth prompt makes Claude that buyer. It opens at half, calls an expert, threatens to walk, and grades every spot where your price moved. Here's what it does:

Five rounds with an owner who opened at $3,600 and said he'd "call my guy at ADP." My price moved in round three, on "let me see what I can do." Grade: C. The fix: the trade in round two, then silence, plus the line it handed me: "I don't have a cheaper version. I have a smaller one. Want to see it?"
