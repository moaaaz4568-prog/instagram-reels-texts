1. "I am going to be direct with you because I think directness is the only thing worth your time and mine."

Logan Roy Year One had no inherited credibility and no established name. What he had was the willingness to say the thing directly when everyone else was circling it.

This phrase cuts through every layer of corporate politeness that normally separates a cold rep from a serious decision-maker. The buyer who was expecting a warm-up gets a signal that this conversation is going to operate differently. Directness in the first sentence is the fastest credibility available to a rep who has not yet earned any other kind.

Open with the directness most reps save for the close. The buyer who feels it immediately respects everything that follows.
