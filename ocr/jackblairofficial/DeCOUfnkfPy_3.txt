3. Price is the wrong variable.

They'll match my price. They won't match my outcome. O'Leary never defends a number, he changes the variable the buyer is judging. I gave Claude my price and the result a customer actually gets and asked for the math that makes the cheaper quote the expensive one. What came back:

The math on a $38,000 roof against a $29,000 quote: a 25-year system is $1,520 a year, a 12-year one is $2,416, before the second tear-off. The line: "The cheaper roof costs you $900 a year more. You just pay it later, with a crane on site." Price never came up again.
