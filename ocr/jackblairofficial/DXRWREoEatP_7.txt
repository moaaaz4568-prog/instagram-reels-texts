7. "I want to leave this call having actually helped you with something, whether we work together or not."

This is Neal Caffrey's philosophy in a single sentence. Give first, completely and without conditions, and the room will follow you anywhere.

This phrase removes every transactional feeling from the call and replaces it with genuine generosity. The buyer who hears this stops calculating what you want from them and starts thinking about what you might give. That mental shift is the close that happens before the close, the moment the buyer decides you are different. Give before you ask. The buyer who receives first closes fastest every time.
