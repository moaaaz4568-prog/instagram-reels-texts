3. "I am not going to tell you everything is fine with your current approach because I do not think it is."

Baum never validated a position he believed was wrong just to keep the room comfortable. He called it and took the consequences and the people who listened to him made money.

This phrase is the most disarming thing a buyer hears from a rep who is supposed to be selling. The buyer who gets a challenge instead and challenges from credible sources land like nothing else. The rep who tells the truth when it is inconvenient is always the rep they remember.

Challenge the current approach when you genuinely see a better one. The room that argues is the room that is listening.
