4. He bet everything on serving instead
of selling.

Savage built his whole career on a
backwards idea, stop trying to sell and
start trying to serve.

He gave advice freely and sent business
he couldn't handle to people who could,
trusting it would circle back. It did, in
referrals no cold call could ever buy.

Give value before you ask for the signature
and the signature comes easy.
