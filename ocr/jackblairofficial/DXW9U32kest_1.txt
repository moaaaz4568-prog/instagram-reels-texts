1. "Here is what I am offering. Here is what it does. Here is what happens next."

Ron Swanson never dressed up a simple thing in complicated language to make it sound more impressive. He understood that clarity is the most impressive thing available in any room full of noise.

This phrase strips the entire sales conversation down to three sentences and closes in all three. The buyer who has been waiting for the pitch to end suddenly realises it already has. There is nothing to object to because there is nothing extra to pick apart.

Remove the excess. The close hiding underneath it is always cleaner than the one you were performing.
