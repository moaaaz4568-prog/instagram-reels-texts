6. What you broke can still be rebuilt.

Downey had spent 5 years on drugs, in courts, and out of work. His first marriage had ended.

By 2024 he'd won an Academy Award for Oppenheimer. Married to producer Susan Levin for 19 years.

Nothing was unfixable.

The relationships and reputations you've damaged aren't permanently lost. They're just paused.
