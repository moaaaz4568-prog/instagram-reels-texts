3. Buyers with less financial confidence
raise more objections.

"I need to think about it."
"Now's just not a great time."
"Things are tight right now."

These aren't new objections.
They're the same ones — arriving more often,
with more conviction behind them.

The rep who trained for this environment
closes. The one who didn't gets stuck on every
call.
