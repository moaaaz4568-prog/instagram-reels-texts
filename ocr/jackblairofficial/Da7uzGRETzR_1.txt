1. "What if it starts next month instead of today? Does the answer change?"

Richard Thaler won the Nobel Prize for proving people accept tomorrow what they refuse today. His Save More Tomorrow plan asked workers to commit future raises instead of current pay. Most signed. Their savings quadrupled.

When a buyer stalls, move the start date before you ever touch the price.

Today's no is usually just today talking.
