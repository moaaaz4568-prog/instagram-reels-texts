2. Ribbons on the pigtails.

Her mother's rule, and her first book's title:
if you don't have big breasts, put ribbons
on your pigtails. Sell the one thing you
have that they don't. I gave Claude my
offer and the three things the big firms
have that I don't. What came back:

Three gaps, one ribbon. The big firms have the brand, the client
list and the lower price. The ribbon: I answer the phone. The
opener Claude wrote: "You'll never get a ticket." Then the proof, a text answer number from
me. You'll get me answered in four
minutes, shown on the call.
