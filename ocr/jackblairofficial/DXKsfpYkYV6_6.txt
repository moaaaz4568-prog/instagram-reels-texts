6. "The only thing standing between you and the outcome you told me you wanted is this conversation."

Blake understood that most buyers already know what they need.
They are just waiting for someone with enough conviction to confirm it.

This phrase connects their own stated desire to the decision in front of them.
You are not introducing a new idea. You are closing a loop they opened.
The sale was made when they told you what they wanted. This phrase collects it.

A buyer who told you their goal has already told you how to close them.
