5. "Be obsessed with the product. Let the customers tell the story."

Ulukaya spent 2 years with Turkish yogurt master Mustafa Dogan perfecting the recipe before he shipped a single case.

When customers started calling in to say they loved it, he had his small team reach out to bloggers, Facebook, and Twitter.

In 5 years Chobani became a $1 billion brand. With no advertising.
