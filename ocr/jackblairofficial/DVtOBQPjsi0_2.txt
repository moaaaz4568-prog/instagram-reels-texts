Prompt 2 — The Discovery Machine

"I'm about to get on a discovery call with [buyer] in [industry]. Write me 10 questions that uncover budget, urgency, and decision process without making it feel like an interrogation. Each question should flow naturally."

The best reps don't wing discovery.

They walk in knowing exactly what they need to find out and exactly how to ask it.
