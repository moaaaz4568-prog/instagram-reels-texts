SHE PAID TO UNDERSTAND THE OBJECTION
Mattel brought in Ernest Dichter, the father of
motivational research, to find out what mothers
actually felt about the doll.
The discomfort was real, but underneath it sat a
want: mothers wanted daughters who knew how to
present themselves. The pitch became with the objection.
She did not argue with the objection. She found the
want living underneath it and sold to that instead.
