6. Art's most famous speech was delivered in 1987 to a room of religious broadcasters.

The title: "Just Do It."

A man with a pacemaker telling 800 strangers that the only difference between the people who make it and the people who don't is the willingness to start before they feel ready.

He repeated the line throughout: "Just do it. Just do it. Just do it."
