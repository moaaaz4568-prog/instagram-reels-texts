3. "I could make this easier. I'd also be making it worth less."

Colleagues told Escalante the kids couldn't handle the prerequisites, so teach down to them. He refused, because the value was the difficulty. Passing AP Calculus meant something precisely because nobody handed it to you.

Buyers will ask you to strip out the demanding parts of your offer. Every piece you strip discounts the exact outcome they came for.

What costs nothing to start is worth nothing to keep.
