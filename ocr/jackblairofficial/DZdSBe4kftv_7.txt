7. He stacked it all in the last three minutes.

Klaff saves his strongest move for the end.

He stacks intrigue, the prize, the time limit, and his authority together right before the decision, so the buyer feels the pull all at once and acts on it. Hot decisions are made fast, not slept on.

Build to one emotional peak instead of leaking your best cards early.
