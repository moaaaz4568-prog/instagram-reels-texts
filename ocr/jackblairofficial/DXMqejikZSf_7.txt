7. "I think the timing on this is actually better than it looks. Let me show you why."

This phrase reframes a buyer who thinks they need more time.
It does not pressure. It recontextualises.

Jimmy Darmody always saw the opportunity before the roomy did.
This phrase channels that clarity without the impatience that made him dangerous to himself.
The buyer stops seeing delay as safety and starts seeing it as cost.

Reframe the timeline. The close was always closer than they thought.
