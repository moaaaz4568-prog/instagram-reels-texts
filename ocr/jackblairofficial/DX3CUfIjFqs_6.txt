6. Best is the enemy of better.

The rep who waits to perfect the pitch never runs it. The closer who runs the 70% pitch 100 times more than the rep running the 95% pitch 10 times closes more.

Volume of attempts beats elegance of approach.

Horowitz rebuilt Opsware in public, on a public company filing, with a 35-cent stock price. Imperfect at scale beat perfect in theory.
