5. MAKE THE MEETING EFFORTLESS FOR THEM.
The president expected to interrogate a salesman.
Instead he got to sit back while his own concerns
were handled one by one, in his own order of
importance.
The easiest yes is the one that requires no work
from the buyer.
Remove their labor, earn their trust.
