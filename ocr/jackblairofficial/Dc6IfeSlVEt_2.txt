2. MEMORIZE IT UNTIL IT DISAPPEARS.
Every NCR man learned the script cold before
meeting a merchant. Not to sound canned. The
opposite. Known words free the eyes.
Reps who wing it spend the call composing
sentences. Reps who drilled spend it reading the
buyer. The buyer who drilled feels the difference.
Drilled lines make room for listening.
