5. He made a promise he had to keep on camera.

He showed a clunker as the special and swore he'd smash it with a sledgehammer if it didn't sell. It didn't, and he kept his word on camera.

The stunt proved he meant what he said, and people trust a seller who follows through. Make a bold promise, then visibly keep it.

Keep a loud promise and they believe the next one.
