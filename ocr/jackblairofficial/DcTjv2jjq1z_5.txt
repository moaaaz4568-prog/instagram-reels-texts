5. "THAT'S TOMORROW'S PROBLEM. TODAY WE FIX THIS."
In every episode the client wants to litigate how it happened while the fire is still burning. She refuses the autopsy until the patient is stable.
Buyers do it too: mid-crisis they want to renegotiate the whole relationship. Park it, out loud, with a date. Triage first, retrospective second.
You cannot run the post-mortem and the surgery at the same time.
