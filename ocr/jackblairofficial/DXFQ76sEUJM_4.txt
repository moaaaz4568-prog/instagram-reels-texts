4. "If this doesn't get solved in the next 30 days, what happens on your end?"

McNulty always made the cost of inaction visible before anyone wanted to look at it. This phrase forces the buyer to calculate their own consequence out loud. Their answer becomes your most powerful closing argument. You did not create the urgency. They did. You just asked the right question at the right time.
