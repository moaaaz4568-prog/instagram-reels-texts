7. "Say it back to me. If it's not clear, that's on my explaining."

If you confuse, you lose. Miller's test is simple: a stranger should grasp what you do and what to do next in seconds. When a buyer can repeat your offer back in one plain sentence, they can sell it to themselves, and to whoever else has to sign off.

A confused buyer never buys. Clarity closes.
