6. "I want to earn this relationship back. Tell me where to start."

This phrase is Jerry Maguire in one sentence.
Not a defence. Not a pitch. A direct request to be shown the path back to the trust that was lost.

The client who hears a rep ask where to start instead of what to defend responds completely different.
They open up. They give you the one thing that actually needed fixing.
And the rep who fixes the one thing always keeps the account.

Ask where to start.
The client who tells you has already decided to give you the chance.
