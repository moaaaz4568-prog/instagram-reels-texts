2. The first career isn't the real career.

Mahershala was a college basketball player. Then he a sports data guy. Then he decided, at 27, he wanted to act.

Two careers worth of skills already built. None of them his real career.

The first career you build isn't a waste. It's the prerequisite that taught you what discipline, repetition, and showing up actually look like.
