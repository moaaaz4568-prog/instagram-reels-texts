3. "If something changes on my end, you will hear from me before you have to ask."

Chigurch never surprised anyone with an outcome they were not prepared for.
That predictability was not a weakness. It was the source of every room's respect for him.

This phrase signals proactive transparency in a market full of reps who go quiet when things get complicated.
The buyer who hears this stops bracing for the unexpected and starts relaxing into the relationship.
A buyer who is relaxed does not shop for alternatives.

Be the rep who communicates first. That habit closes more deals than any pitch.
