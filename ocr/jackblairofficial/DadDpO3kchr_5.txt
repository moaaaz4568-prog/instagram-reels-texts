5. "Forget what I want. Does this feel fair to you?"

His verdicts didn't come from legal technicalities. They came from a jury's gut sense of what justice looked like.

People will fight to make a situation fair, even against their own hesitation. Fairness is the oldest close in the human brain.

Frame your deal as the fair outcome and the buyer starts arguing your side for you.
