3. "Sorry, one last thing before I go."
The signature move. The guard drops the
second the threat looks like it's leaving.
Doctors call it the doorknob moment: the
real problem comes out at the door.
Save your most important question for
after the meeting officially ends. Bag
packed, hand on the door, then ask it.
The truth comes out at the door.
