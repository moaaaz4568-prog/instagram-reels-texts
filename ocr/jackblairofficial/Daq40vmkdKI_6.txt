6. "If today's a no, here's the smaller step that keeps it moving."
The framework gives you two asks: the direct one and the transitional one. When the big yes isn't there yet, offer the small commitment that keeps them walking forward instead of away. A resource, a trial, a next call. Never end a conversation on nothing.
When the big yes isn't ready, offer the small one.
