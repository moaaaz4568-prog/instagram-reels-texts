1. "Can I get ninety seconds to tell you why I called? Then you decide if we keep talking."

In the Freedman and Fraser study, one tiny yes changed what people agreed to weeks later. Homeowners who accepted a small sign took the monster billboard at four times the rate.

Never open on the big ask. Open with a request so small that refusing it feels absurd.

Small doors open big rooms.
