4. "It stopped responding. Here's what we do."

The manual carries an actual banned-words list. Never "crash," say "stops responding." Never "bug," say "condition." The facts stay identical; the temperature drops.

Audit your own deal vocabulary the same way. "Problem" becomes "situation." "Cancel" becomes "pause." Words set the room before the facts arrive.

Same facts, calmer words, warmer deal.
