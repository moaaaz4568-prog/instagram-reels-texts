Here's the part nobody retracted.

AI takes the predictable work first.
Anything repeatable, rules-based, done at a desk. The follow-up email. The CRM note. The research. The first qualifying question. The report your manager reads on Monday. If a task can be written down as a series of steps, a machine can learn to run it. That describes most of the white-collar world, and a large part of most sales jobs.
