3. "I'm not sure that's right."

Wintour does not argue, raise her voice, or over-explain when she disagrees. She says one calm, quiet sentence.

The softness is the power. A quiet "I'm not sure that's right" lands harder than a loud objection ever could.

The closer who stays calm under disagreement controls the room. The one who gets loud loses it.
