6. "This deal is the floor, not the ceiling."

He was the first agent to build his athletes into brands in their own right.

He pushed his clients into the communities and into business, partly because it was right and partly because it was shrewd. When kicker Rolf Benirschke gave to charity for every field goal, the city loved him, and a beloved player keeps his job far longer than a merely good one.

Sell the bigger future and one signature stops being the whole point.
