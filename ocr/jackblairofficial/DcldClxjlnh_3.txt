3. "CAN I ASK A DUMB QUESTION?"

A smart question gets the rehearsed answer. A dumb one gets the truth, because nobody rehearses for the rep they are not worried about.

Ask the obvious thing everyone else was too impressive to ask. Budget. Timeline. Who really signs. Watch how much comes back unguarded.

Dumb questions collect what smart questions scare away.
