They changed their story in the same week.

In May the CEO of OpenAI said he was "delighted to be wrong" about AI eliminating entry-level white-collar jobs. Days later the CEO of Anthropic, who had said AI could wipe out half of all white-collar work, reframed it. Automate ninety percent of a job and the last ten percent expands to fill the day. Altman and Amodei spent a year telling you the water was rising. Then both said it wasn't, the same year both of their companies go public at around a trillion dollars each.
