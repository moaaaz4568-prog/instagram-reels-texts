1. Sell the vision, not the specs.

Iacocca didn’t sell a car with a list of features. He sold young, fast, and free, and let the Mustang become the thing you felt about yourself.

Nobody buys specs. They buy who they get to be. Paint the picture of their life after the yes, not the parts list.

Sell the feeling. The features just back it up.
