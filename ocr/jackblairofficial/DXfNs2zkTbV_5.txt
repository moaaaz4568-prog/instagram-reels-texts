5. "I want to be straight with you. I am building something here and I want you to be part of it from the beginning."

Tommy Shelby made the ground floor of something real.
This phrase converts the absence of an established brand into an invitation that feels more exclusive than any established name could offer.

The buyer who was evaluating you against better-known competitors suddenly feels the pull of being first.
Being first is always more exciting than being safe and excitement closes deals that safety comparisons cannot.

The unknown rep who makes the buyer feel like an early partner always beats the known rep who makes them feel like a transaction.

Make them feel first. The buyer who accepts that position almost never goes to the established alternative.
