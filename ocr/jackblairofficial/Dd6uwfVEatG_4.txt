4. The buyer who has heard every pitch.

Five rounds with a skeptical dad: where he felt something, where he checked out, then his hardest question. "Does your battery run my central air for thirty hours, or am I buying a very expensive fan?" Grade: B, plus the line that lost him rewritten.
