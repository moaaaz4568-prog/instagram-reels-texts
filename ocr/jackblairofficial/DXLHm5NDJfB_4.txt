4. "Is there anyone in this process whose concerns we have not fully addressed yet?"

This is the question most reps are afraid to ask because they do not want to find new problems. Tom Hagen understood that hidden problems do not disappear. They decide.

This phrase surfaces the blocker while you still have time and position to handle them. The objection you find in this conversation is the one you can solve. The one you do not find closes the deal for your competitor. It is always better to know.

Ask for the problem. It is always better to know.
