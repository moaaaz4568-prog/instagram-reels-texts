5. "Picture yourself a month from now,
having already said yes. How does that
feel?"

Wendy moved people by making the
decision feel like it had already happened,
somewhere they could see it.

People don't move toward features, they
move toward a version of themselves they
want to be. Put them inside the after, and
the choice becomes obvious.

Sell the person they get to become, not
the thing they have to buy.
