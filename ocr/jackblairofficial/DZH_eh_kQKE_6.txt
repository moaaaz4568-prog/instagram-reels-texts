6. "I take care of my people, and they never forget it."
Underneath the swagger, Harvey is fiercely loyal to the people around him.
That loyalty buys him allies who go to war for him for life. Treat a client like someone you will still know in twenty years. One person who trusts you completely is worth a hundred quick, forgettable deals.
Loyalty is leverage that never expires.
