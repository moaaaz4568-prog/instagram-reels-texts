2. "I am going to be direct with you because that is what this conversation deserves."

Blake never eased into a room.

This phrase sets the tone in the first ten seconds.
It tells the buyer that this call is different from every other call they took this week.
Directness at the start earns trust faster than warmth ever will.

Set the standard for the conversation before the buyer can set it for you.
