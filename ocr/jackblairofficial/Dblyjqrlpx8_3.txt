3. "HERE'S WHAT THIS PILOT WON'T DO, SO NOTHING SURPRISES YOU IN WEEK TWO."
Before you board, Disney tells you the ride is dark, loud, and drops fast. The warnings stand before the queue, not after the scream.
Draw the line while the buyer can still say no. Name what the pilot excludes, out loud, on the call. Buyers forgive limits they agreed to and punish the ones they find.
The fence you build today is the fight you skip later.
