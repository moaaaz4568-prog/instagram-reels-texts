3. "WHAT WOULD MAKE YOU WALK AWAY
FROM THIS?"
No con ever shows the mark an exit. The whole
architecture is built to keep one out of view.
So show them yours. Ask what would disqualify you,
then respect the answer when it comes. A buyer
holding their own exit stops hunting for one.
The exit you name is the one they stop looking for.
