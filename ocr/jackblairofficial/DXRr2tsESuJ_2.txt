02. Live Reframe Tool

"I am on a sales call and my prospect just said: [paste exact objection].
My offer is [one sentence description].
Write me 3 different ways to respond in the next 30 seconds.
Each response must validate their concern without agreeing with it,
shift the frame from risk to opportunity,
and end with a question that keeps the conversation moving.
Under 35 words each.
Tone: calm, direct, peer to peer.
Zero guesswork."

You stop freezing when the hard objection lands. You have three moves ready before they finish the sentence.
