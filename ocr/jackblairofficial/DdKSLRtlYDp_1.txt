1. "How many sales consultants left this store last year?" (كم مستشار مبيعات غادر هذا المتجر العام الماضي؟)

NADA's number is the industry. Yours is the store. A desk that knows its own turnover and says it out loud is running a seat. A desk that shrugs is running a door. Ask for the count, not the vibe. The store already reports it.

Every store knows its number. Ask for it.
