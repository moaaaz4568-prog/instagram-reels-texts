4. "I have been in this business long enough to know when something is worth fighting for. This is worth fighting for."

Bombay came back not because the circumstances improved but because he remembered what was worth the effort.

This phrase brings that same energy into a sales conversation with a buyer who is as on the fence.

It signals conviction without desperation and experience without arrogance.
The buyer who hears a rep willing to fight for the outcome rather than the commission

stops evaluating the product and starts trusting the person presenting it.

Fight for the outcome not the commission. The buyer always knows which one you are doing.
