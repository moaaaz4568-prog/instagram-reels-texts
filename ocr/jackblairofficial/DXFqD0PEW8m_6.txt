6. "I'm not asking you to take a risk. I'm asking you to make a decision you already know is right."

Fear of commitment kills more deals than price ever will. Kendall Roy reframed every high stakes moment as a choice between staying stuck and moving forward. This phrase removes the word risk from the buyer's mind and replaces it with clarity. They already know the answer. You are just giving them permission to say it.
