2. "Don't pee on my leg and tell me it's raining."

Her most famous line, aimed at anyone dressing up an obvious dodge.

Judy refuses to pretend a weak excuse is a real one. In a close, name the stall politely but plainly instead of playing along. People respect the person who calls the bluff with a smile.

Name the dodge and the truth comes out.
