2. "I need to know today whether this is moving forward. Not because of my timeline but because of yours."

Bauer always reframed urgency around the mission not around himself.
This phrase does the same thing and the effect on a stalling buyer is immediate and significant.

The moment urgency belongs to the buyer's situation rather than the rep's quota it becomes impossible to dismiss.
The buyer stops feeling sold to and starts feeling advised by someone who is thinking about their outcome.
That shift from rep's urgency to buyer's urgency is the only close that holds under scrutiny.

Make the urgency theirs. It lands harder and holds longer than any deadline you could manufacture.
