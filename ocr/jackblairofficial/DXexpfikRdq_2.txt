2. "I think I have not been showing up for you the way I should have been. I want to change that starting today."

Maguire won Rod Tidwell back not with a better pitch but with a more honest one.
This phrase deploys that same honesty at the exact moment a client needs to hear it most.

The client who was compiling reasons to leave suddenly has one significant reason to reconsider.
A rep who can name their own shortfall without being prompted is a rep worth keeping.
That level of accountability is rarer than any feature your competitor is offering them right now.

Own the shortfall before they raise it. The client who hears you name it first almost never leaves.
