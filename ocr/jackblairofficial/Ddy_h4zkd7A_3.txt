3. "Two owners your size started in September. Do you want their names?"

"Has pledged" predicted funding.
Someone visible had already said yes, and the next stranger followed. That is social proof, and the study caught it working on people with nothing but a web page to go on. On a call it has to be true and it has to be specific. Two named companies beat "hundreds of clients", and offering the names is the part that makes it believable, whether or not they take you up on it.

A crowd nobody can name is not a crowd.
