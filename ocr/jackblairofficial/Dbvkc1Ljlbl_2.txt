"YOU DON'T HAVE TO AGREE WITH ME ON THIS."
A referee cannot make anyone agree. Thirty
professionals can think he's wrong all afternoon and
it changes nothing. What he needs isn't agreement,
it's acceptance, and those are different things.
Stop hunting for approval of your price or your
timeline. Ask them to accept it as the terms, not to
love it.
You need the deal accepted, not applauded.
