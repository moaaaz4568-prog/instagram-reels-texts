"WOULD TUESDAY OR THURSDAY WORK
BETTER?"
The alternative close was clever the year it was
invented. Now every buyer has heard it from three
reps this month, and the moment they hear a
technique, they stop hearing you.
Ask about their world, not your calendar. "When did
you want this live by?" The date that matters is
theirs.
A buyer who smells a technique starts guarding the
exit.
