2. The first customer was a stranger at a Monterey coffee shop.

Itzler had no list. No leads. No pipeline. He was sitting at a coffee shop and struck up a conversation with the man at the next table.

The conversation drifted to private jets. Itzler mentioned the company. The stranger said, "I'm in the market for a card right now."

The stranger was Josh Kopelman, CEO of Half.com. Marquis Jet's first customer.
