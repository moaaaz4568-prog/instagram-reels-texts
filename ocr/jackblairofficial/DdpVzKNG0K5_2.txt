2. "Where did that figure come from?"

The belief that people dig in when corrected came from a 2010 study run on a small convenience sample of undergraduates, and the trade repeated it for a decade. Wood and Porter tested 52 issues on more than ten thousand people and could not reproduce it once. Before you argue with your buyer's number, find out who gave it to them.

Most wrong numbers are borrowed, not believed.
