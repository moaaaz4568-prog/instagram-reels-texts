5. "I'm not sure this is right for you yet."
Grant's givers who win over time aren't
the pushovers. The lesson for a sales call is
to be straight about fit before the buyer
has to be. Say it early, and say what would
make it right. Nothing on a call is more
persuasive than a rep who sounds willing
to lose the deal, because it's the only proof
you're not hiding something.
The rep willing to lose the deal is the one
they trust with it.
