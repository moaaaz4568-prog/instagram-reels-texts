3. "I have looked at your situation carefully. Here is what I think nobody else has told you yet."

Ghost always knew something the other side did not. That information advantage was the foundation of every deal he ever closed.

This phrase signals that you have done the work the buyer has not seen and that what comes next is exclusive. The buyer stops deep and starts treating you as an intelligence source. That is a completely different power position and one that leads directly to the close.

Know something they do not. Lead with it. Own the conversation from that moment forward.
