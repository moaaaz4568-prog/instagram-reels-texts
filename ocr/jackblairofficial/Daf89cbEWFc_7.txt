7. "There's no clock on this. Take the time to check everything."

Mitnick used urgency as a weapon, because a rushed brain stops verifying. Buyers know that trick. They've been burned by it.

So run the opposite. Remove the clock on purpose. The seller who tells you to slow down and verify is the one whose offer survives verification.

Pressure reads as a trick. Patience reads as proof.
