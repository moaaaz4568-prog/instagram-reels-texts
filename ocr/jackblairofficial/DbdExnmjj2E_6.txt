6. "WHAT DO YOU NEED TO HEAR FROM ME
BEFORE WE TALK TERMS AGAIN?"
Diamond's students learn that words are the
currency. To a buyer who feels dismissed, one
acknowledgment out loud beats any concession in
writing.
Let the buyer dictate the payment, then say it plainly
with nothing tacked on and no pivot back to the deal
in the same breath. Let it stand alone.
An apology with a discount attached is neither.
