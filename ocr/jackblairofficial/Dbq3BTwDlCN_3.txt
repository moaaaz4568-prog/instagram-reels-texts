"SAY IT BACK TO ME. I WANT TO KNOW IF I EXPLAINED IT BADLY."
Shuy's work turns on the gap between what a speaker meant and what a listener recorded. The listener fills every ambiguity with the meaning he already expected.
Make them restate it in their own words. Put the fault on your explanation so restating costs them nothing.
What they cannot repeat, they never agreed to.
