6. "You're not buying a tie. You're buying who you want to be."

"I don't design clothes. I design dreams."
His words. He named the line Polo, not Lauren, because the name sold a world before the tie ever left the box. Ask what your buyer wants to be true about their life a year from now. Sell them that. The product is just the ticket in.

A premium is never for the thing. It's for who it makes you.
