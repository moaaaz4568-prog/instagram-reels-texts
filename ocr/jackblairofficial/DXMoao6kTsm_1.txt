1. "I want to make sure we cover what matters most to you. Can I ask you one question before we go any further?"

A dominant buyer will take the wheel inside the first sixty seconds if you let them.
This phrase takes it first, gently, before they realise it has happened.

It signals that you have a process and that the process serves them.
The buyer feels in control because you asked for their input.
You are in control because you set the direction before they could.

The rep who asks the first question runs the call. Every time.
