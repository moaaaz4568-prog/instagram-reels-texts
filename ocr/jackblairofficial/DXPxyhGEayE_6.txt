6. "I respect that you have a process. My timeline does not move to accommodate it."

Cersei Lannister never let someone else's bureaucracy become her problem.
She held her position and made the other side find a way to meet it.

This phrase signals scarcity and self-respect in a single sentence without ultimatum or aggression.
The buyer who was planning to run out the clock suddenly understands that the clock is yours.
Your time has value. The moment the buyer feels that, the dynamic shifts permanently.

Your timeline is your leverage. Use it like it belongs to you.
