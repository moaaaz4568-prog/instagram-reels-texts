1. "DO YOU SEE YOURSELF FIXING THIS BEFORE THE YEAR ENDS?"
Psychologists call it the mere measurement effect. Vicki Morwitz tracked real households.
The ones asked a single question about their buying plans bought more cars and computers than the ones never asked. Ask it early, then let it work.
People drift toward the future they predict.
