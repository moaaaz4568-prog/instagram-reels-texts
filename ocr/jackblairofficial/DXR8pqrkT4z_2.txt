2. "I am not going to make excuses for what happened. Here is what I am going to do about it."

Buyers who feel let down are not looking for an explanation.
They are looking for evidence that the person in front of them can be trusted again.

This phrase skips the explanation entirely and moves straight to the commitment.
It signals accountability without groveling and confidence without arrogance.
Winston Wolf never explained a problem longer than he needed to be before solving it.

Own it in one sentence. Fix it in the next. The buyer will stay for both.
