6. "I would like to set up one conversation with the full group if you think that would help them feel comfortable."

Most reps treat the multi-stakeholder meeting as a threat.
Tom Hagen treated every room as an opportunity to close the people as nobody else had spoken to yet.

This phrase asks for the meeting without sounding like you need it.
It frames the group call as a service to the buyer, not a move for the rep.
Inside that room you handle every objection at once and you walk out with a decision instead of another follow-up.

Put everyone in the room. Close everyone at once.
