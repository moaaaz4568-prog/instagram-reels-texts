6. LET RIVALS KEEP THE BARGAIN HUNTERS.
With no used market, the only cheap machines left were rivals' new ones. Singer stopped competing on price entirely and let the bottom of the market go.
The trade-in quietly sorted buyers. Serious ones traded up, and the tire kickers went elsewhere.
Losing the wrong customers is a strategy.
