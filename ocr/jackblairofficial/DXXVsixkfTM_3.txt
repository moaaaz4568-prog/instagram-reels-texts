3. "I am not going to make excuses for what did not work. Here is what I am going to do differently."

Roy Kent held himself accountable in public in a way that made every person around him raise their standard. This phrase does the same thing on a call.

It signals maturity, self-awareness and a forward momentum that buyers find genuinely rare in a rep. The buyer who was ready to bring up the previous failure suddenly has nothing to bring up because you already owned it and already moved past it.

Own it before they raise it. The rep who accounts for themselves never has to answer for anyone else.
