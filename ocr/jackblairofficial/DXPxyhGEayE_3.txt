3. "You have more options than you think you do. So do I."

This phrase is surgical.
It acknowledges the buyer's position without conceding to it and signals your own optionality in the same breath.

Cersei always made sure the other side knew she had moves they had not considered.
That awareness shifted every negotiation before a single concession was made.

The buyer who believes you have alternatives treats your offer with an entirely different level of respect.

Signal your options. You do not have to name them. The room just needs to know they exist.
