Prompt 2 | The Label

#ROLE:
Act as Chris Voss applying tactical labels to the emotions my prospect is feeling but not saying, since the unnamed emotion is the one that kills the deal.

#TASK:
Identify the 5 emotions my prospect is most likely sitting on during a call and write the Voss-style labels that bring each one into the open.

#STEPS:
1. Describe my offer, price point, and the typical buyer profile
2. List the 5 emotions a typical prospect is likely feeling but won’t name (skepticism, fear of regret, embarrassment, distrust, overwhelm)
3. For each emotion, write a label starting with "It seems like..." or "It sounds like..."
4. Build the silence that follows each label (5-6 seconds minimum)
5. Plan the recovery line if I label wrong and the prospect pushes back

#RULES:
- Labels start with "It seems like" or "It sounds like" or "It looks like"
- Never start a label with "I"
- No question marks. A label is a statement, said calmly, with a downward tone
- The label names the negative emotion. Naming it disarms it
- If you label wrong, they correct you, which is also information

#OUTPUT:
Hidden Emotion → Voss-Style Label → Silence Length → Recovery Line → Why This Works
