5. "Here is what I know about where you are headed and here is exactly where we fit into that."

Ghost always played the long game while closing the short one.
He understood that the most powerful position in any deal is being aligned with where the other side is going.

This phrase connects your solution to the buyer's future, not their present problem.
They stop evaluating the cost of saying yes and start calculating the cost of arriving at that future without you.
Now you are not selling a product. You are positioning yourself as part of the plan.

Sell the destination. The deal follows the vision.
