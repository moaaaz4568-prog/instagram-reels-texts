7. "Give me this deal and I will show you what I am capable of. You will not offer it to anyone else after."

This is Tommy Shelby Year One in one sentence.
Not arrogance. Total conviction delivered with complete calm by someone who already knows how this ends.

This phrase asks for the chance rather than assume the result and frames the ask as an investment the buyer will not regret. The buyer who hears this and feels the certainty behind it stops thinking about risk and starts thinking about what they might miss.

Conviction without track record is the rarest in sales. It is also the most memorable.

Ask for the chance with complete certainty behind the ask. The buyer who feels the certainty takes the bet.
