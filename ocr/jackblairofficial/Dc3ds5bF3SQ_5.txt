5. "ASK ME ANYTHING. I WILL NOT NEED TO CHECK."

The questions went to the person who had made the thing, and every one of them was answered on the spot. Nothing was deferred to a supervisor or a spec sheet.

Invite the hardest question early and answer it without a pause. Buyers read speed of answer as truth of answer, and they stop hunting for the seam.

A pause before the answer is the sale leaving the room.
