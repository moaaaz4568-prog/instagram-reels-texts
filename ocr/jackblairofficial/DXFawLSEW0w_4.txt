4. STALL DISSOLVER

"My prospect said [paste the stall, for example: let me think about it, send me some info, call me next quarter]. Write me a response that acknowledges what they said, uncovers the real hesitation underneath, and keeps the conversation alive without applying pressure. Under 40 words. Conversational tone. No closing lines. No pitching."

Stalls stop killing momentum. The real objection surfaces and you handle it.
