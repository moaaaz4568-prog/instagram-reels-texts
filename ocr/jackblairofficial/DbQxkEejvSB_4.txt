4. "WHAT WORRIES YOU ABOUT MAKING A CHANGE?"
Johnson's data shows the negative queries always run. You don't get to skip them, only to schedule them.
Asked here, after the gains and the cost of staying, the worries surface smaller, crowded out by everything already on the table. Asked first, they'd have run the meeting. Order is the whole game.
The worry that speaks first speaks loudest.
