6. LET THE PREP DO THE CLOSING.
Duffy never had to push. When the tenth pre-
pared question landed, the president delivered
the closing line himself: I had ten questions for you.
You answered them all.
A prepared rep rarely needs a clever close. The
buyer arrives at yes without being dragged there.
The best close is invisible.
