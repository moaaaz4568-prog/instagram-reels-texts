3. "NOT THE MARKET. NOT YOUR BUDGET. WHAT IN HERE STOPS IT?"

Push once, gently, past the market conditions and the CFO. The real block is usually a fear of being blamed for choosing wrong.

Outside obstacles are excuses. Inside obstacles are solvable.
