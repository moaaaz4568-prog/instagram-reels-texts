3. "Someone in your position doesn't decide this on price. So what do you decide it on?"

Cicero tied every decision to who the listener believed themselves to be. He made saying yes an expression of the person's own character, not a concession to him.

You frame the choice as consistent with the buyer's self-image, then let them name their real criteria. People act to stay who they think they are.

Tie the yes to who they already are.
