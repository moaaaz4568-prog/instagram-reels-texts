4. FEAR AUDIT

"I want to identify what is really holding me back in sales. Here are the situations where I feel most uncomfortable: [list them]. For each one, tell me what the underlying fear is likely to be and give me one reframe I can use before the next call. Format as a simple table. Be honest and direct. Zero guesswork."

You see the pattern. Then you break it before it breaks another deal.
