3. "WHY WOULD I PACE A YEAR I CAN FINISH BY FEBRUARY?"
Two straight years he hit one hundred percent of quota inside the first three weeks of January. Same territory rules, same price book as every rep beside him. He just refused to spread a year's urgency across twelve months.
Front-load the year. The pipeline you build in the first three weeks decides October. Reps who pace themselves are budgeting for their own slowdown.
Quotas measure the company's patience, not your ceiling.
