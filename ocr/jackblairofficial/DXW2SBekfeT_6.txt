6. "Most people who sit where you are sitting come back to this conversation eventually. I would rather have it with you now."

Chuck Bass always framed the inevitable as a courtesy he was extending in the present tense.
This phrase does the same with complete composure and zero desperation.

It tells the buyer that the outcome is not in question only the timing
and that you are offering them the chance to be on the right side of it before it becomes obvious to everyone else. The buyer who was planning to wait suddenly reconsiders the cost of being the last one in the room to move.

Frame the close as a favour. The difficult buyer almost always accepts it on those terms.
