1. "I notice you paused on that point. Tell me more about what is sitting with you there."

Patrick Jane never let a significant moment pass without examining it.
The pause, the slight shift in tone, the answer that was just a little too quick, all of it was data.

This phrase tells the buyer you are paying attention at a level they did not expect.
They feel seen in a way that creates an almost involuntary urge to open up further.
The real objection, the one they were not planning to share, most always hiding in the pause.

Notice what others skip. The close is almost always hiding in the pause.
