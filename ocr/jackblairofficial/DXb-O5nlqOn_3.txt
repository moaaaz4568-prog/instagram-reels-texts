03. Introduction Email

"Write me an introduction email my client [name or job title] can forward to [referral name or job title] on my behalf.

My offer is [one sentence].
The result I delivered for my client is [one sentence].
The reason this referral would benefit from speaking to me is [one sentence].

The email must:
Be written in my client's voice not mine,
be under 80 words,
make the introduction feel like a genuine recommendation not a sales referral,
and end with one clear low friction next step.

Tone: warm, personal, completely authentic.

No pitching. No fluff."

Your client forwards an email that sounds like them not like you.
The referral replies because it came from someone they already trust.
