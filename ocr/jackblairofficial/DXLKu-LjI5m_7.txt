7. "I am going to ask you to make a decision today. Not because of my timeline. Because yours is the one that is actually at risk."

This phrase closes with the buyer's urgency, not yours. It removes every trace of rep desperation and replaces it with genuine concern for their outcome.

Christopher Moltisanti knew that the fastest way to be taken seriously was to make everything about the other person's stakes.

The buyer hears someone thinking about their problem, not their own commission.

That is the close that lands in rooms where nothing else has.

Close for their reason. It is the only close that never sounds like selling.
