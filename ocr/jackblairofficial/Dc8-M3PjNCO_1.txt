1. TRUST THE SYSTEM ON THE BAD DAYS.
Tom Siebel's rule at Oracle: the pipeline does not
care how you feel. Calls got made, stages got
updated, follow-ups went out, whether the morning
was good or not.
Motivation is weather. The system is climate.
Show up to the process, not the mood.
