3. "I want to start this conversation by telling you something I genuinely believe about your situation."

Bombay's greatest coaching moments came when he stopped performing and started meaning it. The players felt the difference immediately and so did the scoreboard.

This phrase strips every layer of sales performance from the opening and replaces it with conviction. The buyer who hears something that sounds nothing like one. Genuine belief delivered without theatre is the rarest and most effective opening available on any call.

Mean the first thing you say. The buyer who feels the difference stays for everything that follows.
