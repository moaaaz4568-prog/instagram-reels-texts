6. He remembered what no one else bothered to write down.

He kept obsessive notes on every client, the kids' names, the worries, the date a policy should be revisited.

Years later he'd call at exactly the right moment, and the client felt known instead of sold. In a business full of strangers, being the one who remembers is an unfair advantage.

Write down what they care about and you'll never run out of reasons to call.
