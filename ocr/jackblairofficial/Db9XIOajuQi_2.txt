2. “THIS ISN’T GOING TO BE HERE FRIDAY. I’M TELLING YOU THAT AS A FACT NOT A PITCH.”
Most reps say “limited time” and it sounds like a script.
This phrase sounds like a warning from someone who actually knows. The difference is delivery. When you state a deadline like weather, calm, factual, inevitable, the buyer stops questioning it and starts reacting to it.
Facts create urgency. Hype creates doubt.
