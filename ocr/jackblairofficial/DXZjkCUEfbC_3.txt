03. Gatekeeper Script

"Write me a script for getting past a gatekeeper when calling [company type] to reach [target job title] about [one sentence on your offer].

The script must:
Sound completely natural and unrehearsed,
avoid triggering the standard gatekeeper deflection,
and give me two different responses if they ask what the call is regarding.

Under 60 words total.

Tone: confident, calm, peer level.

Zero guesswork."

You stop getting blocked before the conversation starts.
You reach the decision-maker on the first call more often than not.
