3. HE ASKED ABOUT THE RECORD, THEN WENT
ONE DEEPER.
In 1952 a young Jimmy Carter proudly reported his
class rank, 59th out of 840 at the Naval Academy.
Rickover did not congratulate him. He asked, did
you do your best. The number was fine. The
question underneath the number is the one that
matters in any room.
The stat is the surface. Ask what is under it.
