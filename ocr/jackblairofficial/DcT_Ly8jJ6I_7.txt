7. “SAY THE QUIET PART FOR THEM.”
Packard wrote the book as a warning. Reps have read it as a map ever since, because the machinery has not changed, only the media carrying it.
The discipline: for every deal, write down the surface reason and the hidden one, and speak to both in the same call. The rep who names the quiet need first is the one the buyer feels understood by.
People call it sold. Buyers call it understood.
