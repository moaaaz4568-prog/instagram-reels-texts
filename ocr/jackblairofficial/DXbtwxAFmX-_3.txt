3. "I want to show you where this space is heading and then I want you to tell me where you want to be in it."

Fury always gave people a map before he asked them to commit to a direction.
This phrase does the same thing and makes the buyer an active participant in their own close.

They stop being sold to and start making a strategic choice about their own future.
The rep who shows the buyer where the world is going and then asks where they want to stand in it has made the close about the buyer's ambition rather than the rep's quota.

Show them the future. Then ask which side of it they want to be on.
