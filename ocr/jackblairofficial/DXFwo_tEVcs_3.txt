3. "What would make this a straightforward yes for you?"

Simple. Direct. Completely disarming. Tom Wambsgans never overcomplicated the path to what he wanted. This phrase cuts through every layer of hesitation and asks the buyer to hand you the close themselves. Most reps spend twenty minutes guessing. This phrase gets the answer in twenty seconds. Ask it early. Use what they tell you.
