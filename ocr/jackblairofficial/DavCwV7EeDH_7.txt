7. "I'm not asking you to change how you work. I'm asking for a seat inside it."

Johnson said it plainly: I don't want to destroy the system, I want to get into it.

Buyers who feel threatened dig in. Buyers offered a low-risk seat at their own table lean forward. Kill the invasion frame before it forms, and the wall you were fighting becomes a door.

Enter as an ally, not an invasion.
