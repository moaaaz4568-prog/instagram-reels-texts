4. "You said no to running it. Would you say yes to owning a piece of it?"

The Starbucks founders refused to run his espresso bars, so he asked them a different question. Starbucks put $150,000 into Il Giornale, the company he built to do it without them. The people who said no to the idea said yes to a share of it. When your buyer refuses the offer, change the shape of the yes: a referral, a later date.

A no to the offer is not a no to you.
