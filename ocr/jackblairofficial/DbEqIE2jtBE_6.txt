6. "At this point, it's only money."
The line auctioneers reach for at the peak,
when two bidders sit one step apart. It
shrinks the final gap against the size of
the want.
When your deal is 5 percent apart at the
end, frame the gap against the outcome,
not against zero.
The last gap is always smaller than the
want.
