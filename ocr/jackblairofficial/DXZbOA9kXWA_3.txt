3. "I am going to show you something that is going to make you question why you have been doing this the hard way."

Tony Stark always made the before look exhausting and the after look inevitable.
That contrast is the emotional engine of every great demo ever delivered.

This phrase sets up the contrast before you create it and the buyer starts feeling the weight of the old way before you have shown them the new one.
By the time you demonstrate the solution they are already relieved.

Make the old way feel heavy before you show them the door out of it.
