6. “WHICH VERSION OF YOU SIGNS THIS?”

His most quoted finding: people buy self-portraits.
The car, the suit, the software stack: each one says I
am this kind of person.

Put identity in the close. The team that catches
problems early. The team that runs a modern
floor. Match the purchase to the person they are
becoming.

A buyer will argue with your price, never with their
mirror.
