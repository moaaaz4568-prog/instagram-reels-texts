6. "I get the sense you have sat through a lot of these conversations. I want this one to feel different."

Neal Caffrey always acknowledged the context he was walking into.
He never pretended the room had no history with people like him.

This phrase names the buyer's fatigue with honesty and signals that you already understand their position. The buyer who was preparing to endure another pitch suddenly feels seen and unexpectedly curious. You acknowledged their experience and immediately separated yourself from every rep who created it.

Name the pattern. Then break it before you expect to.
