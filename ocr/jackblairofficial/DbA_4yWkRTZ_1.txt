In April 2025 the CEO of Shopify sent a memo, there he posted it himself before it could leak.

Tobi Lütke's rule was one sentence. Before asking for more headcount and resources, teams must demonstrate why they cannot get done using AI. It was not a suggestion. He put AI usage into performance reviews and peer reviews, so it on that day, every manager who wanted to hire a person had to stand up and argue that a machine could not do the work.
