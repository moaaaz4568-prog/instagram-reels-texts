05. Re-Engage Script

"Write me a cold call script for re-engaging a prospect who expressed interest [number] weeks or months ago but went cold after [paste what happened].
My offer is [one sentence].

The script must:
Acknowledge the gap without making it awkward, open with something new and relevant to their world today, and propose one specific low-friction next step.

Under 90 words to start.

Tone: confident, natural, no awkwardness about the silence.

Zero guesswork."

You stop avoiding the call because you do not know what to say.
You pick up the phone with a script built for exactly this situation.
