1. "I am going to be honest with you. I have had a difficult run recently and I am coming back stronger because of it."

Gordon Bombay did not pretend the low period had not happened.
He owned it and used it as evidence of something that had survived it.

This phrase is not for the buyer. It is for the rep before the call.
Said internally as a reminder that the difficulty was real and so is the recovery.
The rep who owns his low period stops carrying it into every conversation and starts closing from the other side of it.

Own the difficult period. The rep who survived it has something the rep who never faced it does not.
