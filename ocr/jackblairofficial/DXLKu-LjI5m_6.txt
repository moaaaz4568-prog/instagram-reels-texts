6. "I want this to be right for you. If it is not, I will be the first person to tell you."

The buyer who does not fully trust you yet is looking for one reason to dismiss you.
This phrase removes it before they find it.

It signals integrity in a world where buyers have been burned by reps who overpromised to make quota.
Christopher Mosanti's greatest moments came when he was trusted completely.
That trust was built one honest conversation at a time,
not one polished pitch at a time.

Be the rep who would walk away from the wrong deal.
They will never walk away from you.
