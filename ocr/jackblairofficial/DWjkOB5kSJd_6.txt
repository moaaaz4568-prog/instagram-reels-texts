6. "If I can get the deal
100% to your satisfaction
would you be happy to take it home?
Yes or no?"

The close that demands an answer.

Not aggressive. Direct.

Elliott says most reps are scared
to ask the real question.

This phrase forces a binary response.

And a buyer who says yes to this
is a buyer who just closed themselves.
