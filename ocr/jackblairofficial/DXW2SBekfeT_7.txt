7. "I respect that you want to think about it. I just want to make sure you are thinking about the right thing."

Chuck Bass always redirected the conversation to the terrain that served him without the other realising the redirect had happened.

This phrase keeps the buyer in the conversation while shifting what they are deliberating about. They stop thinking about whether to buy and start thinking about what they might be missing. That shift from decision to insight is where every difficult close becomes possible.

Redirect the deliberation. The buyer who is thinking about the right thing always arrives at yes.
