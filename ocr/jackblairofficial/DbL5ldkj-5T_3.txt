3. "BEFORE I MAKE MY CASE, LET ME MAKE YOURS."
Spence argued the other side's position first, and stronger than they would have.
A room cannot distrust the man who already made its argument. Present the case for no honestly before you present the case for yes.
The objection you raise first can't ambush you later.
