7. He built clients for life, not one-time sales.

Hays noticed that fine clothing stores never remembered his name, his size, or what he had bought last time, and built a company on fixing exactly that.

Tom James sellers knew their clients and came back year after year. One sale is a transaction, a remembered customer is an annuity. The money is in the second order and the tenth.

The first sale is the introduction, not the goal.
