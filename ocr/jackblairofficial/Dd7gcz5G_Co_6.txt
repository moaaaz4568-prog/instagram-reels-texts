Phyllis Vance: the farmer.

Your clients have been with you for years.
They send referrals, they trust you, and
nobody has ever called you pushy.

Where it breaks: you're so careful not to
push that you never ask for more. The
upsell, the referral, the bigger order. You
wait for it to be offered.

Kind isn't the same as quiet. Ask.
