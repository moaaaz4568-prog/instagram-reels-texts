5. "I make money when you say yes. I want to be upfront about that."

Name your own incentive before the buyer suspects it. The moment you say the uncomfortable quiet part out loud, you take it off the table as a weapon they can use against you. Hidden motives get discovered and kill deals. Named motives build the trust that closes them.

Say your incentive out loud before they use it against you.
