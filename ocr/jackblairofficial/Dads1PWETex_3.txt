3. Shrink the decision to one door.

The buyer stalls, overwhelmed by the size of the whole thing.

Overload doesn't kill desire. The shoppers wanted jam. It kills action. Cut the decision down until saying yes is smaller than saying maybe.

Say: "Forget the whole program for a second. The only decision today is whether we fix [X]. Yes or no on that one thing."
