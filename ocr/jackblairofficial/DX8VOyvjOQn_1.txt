1. "If you don't like what's being said,
change the conversation."

The frame control phrase Don ran on
every difficult client.

Most reps argue inside the buyer's frame.
Don rebuilt the room around a different
question entirely.

When the buyer pushed on price, he
pivoted to outcome. When they pushed
on timing, he pivoted to risk.

The conversation the buyer is having is the
one the closer chose for them.
