4. "HERE'S EXACTLY WHAT HAPPENS IN THE NEXT HOUR."
A sinking captain isn't buying a tow, he's buying the end of not knowing. Same with a buyer in trouble.
Skip the pitch and walk the first hour out loud. Who calls who, what's by Friday.
Watch a panicked voice slow down as the plan gets concrete.
In a crisis, the plan is the product.
