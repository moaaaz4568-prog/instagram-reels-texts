7. "I think the real question is not whether this works, It is whether you are ready to be the person who makes this call."

This phrase is Littlfinger at his most precise. It shifts the entire conversation from product evaluation to personal decision-making.

The buyer who was hiding behind process and policy suddenly sees the decision for what it is. A choice about them in this moment, not a spreadsheet exercise. That reframe closes deals that every rational argument in your arsenal could not touch.

Make it personal. Not manipulative. Personal. The close lives in that distinction.
