"IT IS BETTER THAT I SHOULD WAIT FOR THE MONEY THAN THAT YOU SHOULD WAIT FOR THE MACHINE."
McCormick's own words, and the whole invention: payments stretched between harvests, the first installment plan in American business.
Say the modern version out loud on your next stuck deal: the start date moves to now, the payment moves to when the value lands.
Move the payment, not the price.
