4. "WRITE DOWN WHAT YOU THINK I JUST PROMISED."
A con survives on what stays spoken. Nothing said to a mark ever gets written by the mark.
Ask the buyer to put your promise in their own words while you are still on the call. Anything you cannot stand behind in writing was never really an offer, and they will find that gap before you do.
If it can't be written down, it isn't an offer.
