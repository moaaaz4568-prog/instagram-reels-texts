3. "Let me show you what you're really comparing this to, because it isn't the cheaper option."

Bernays controlled what the audience saw the product next to. Change the comparison and you change the decision.

You set the reference point yourself, out loud, instead of letting the buyer measure you against the cheapest thing on the market. Whatever you anchor them to is what they judge everything else from.

Whoever sets the comparison wins it.
