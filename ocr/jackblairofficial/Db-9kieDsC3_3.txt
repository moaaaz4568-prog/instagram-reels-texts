DO YOU WANT TO START WITH THE TUESDAY
BUILD OR THE THURSDAY ONE?
One of his tested points: don't ask if, ask which. He
proved a question that can be answered "no" usually
is.
So he stopped offering yes or no and started offering
two versions of yes. He even ran it at home: not "can
I go out tonight, but "which night do you not mind
me out, Tuesday or Wednesday."
Stop asking if. Ask which. Whatever they pick, you
already won.
