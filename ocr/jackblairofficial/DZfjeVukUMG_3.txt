4. "We cannot be the cheapest, so let me show you what you do get."

Sewell refused to win on price, because someone can always go lower.

He knew there is always a cheaper option. So he competed on everything else, the service, the ease, the trust. Move the buyer off price and onto value, and a small gap stops mattering.

Lose the price war on purpose. Win the rest.
