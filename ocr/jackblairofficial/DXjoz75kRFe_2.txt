2. "There is an opportunity in your business that I do not think anyone has shown you yet. I want to show you what I am seeing."

Logan always saw things in a market before the market saw them itself.
That vision was there before the currency he traded on before he had any other kind.

This phrase positions you as someone who has done the work to find something genuinely worth the buyer's attention. The buyer who was planning to disengage stops and evaluates the claim.
If what follows is real and specific the meeting has already become the most valuable one they took this week.

Find the opportunity they have not seen. Show it with specificity. The buyer who sees it through your eyes trusts your close.
