1. "I have been doing this long enough to know that one call changes everything. This might be it."

A cold streak lies to you.
It tells you the next call will go the same way as the last ten.
This phrase is the antidote. Say it before you dial, not after.
It resets the story you are telling yourself about what is possible today.
The buyer on the other end has no idea what your last week looked like. Neither should you.
Every call is the first call if you decide it is.
