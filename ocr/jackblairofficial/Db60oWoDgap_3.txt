3. "BEFORE YOU DECIDE, TELL ME WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO MAKE SENSE."
When a prospect says no, most reps argue with the no. That is the wrong fight.
This phrase sidesteps the no entirely. It invites the buyer to build their own bridge back to yes. You stop being the salesperson and become the problem solver.
Give them the pen. Let them write the deal.
