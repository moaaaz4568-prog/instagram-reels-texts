7. "I want to talk about what the next twelve months looks like for you because I want to be part of building it."

This phrase converts a closed account into an ongoing partnership with a single sentence.
It signals long-term thinking at a moment when most reps are only thinking about the next renewal date.

Avon Barksdale never thought in single transactions. He thought in Permanence.
This phrase does the same thing inside a sales relationship.
The buyer who maps their future with you in the room has already decided why you stay.

Get into their future. The competitor cannot take ground that you already occupy.
