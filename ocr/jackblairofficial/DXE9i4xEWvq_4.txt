4. "I'm not here to tell you what you need. I just want to understand what you're working with."

Bunk earned trust by never making people feel cornered. This phrase removes every trace of sales pressure from the conversation. The buyer exhales. Their guard comes down. And in the space that opens up, you need to know everything you need to close them. Pressure closes nothing. Understanding closes everything.
