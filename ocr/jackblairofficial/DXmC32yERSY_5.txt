5. "I want to understand what would make this easier for you to approve. Tell me what you need from me."

Gerri always found out what the internal stakeholder needed before she needed them to move.
She never guessed at the approval criteria and she never assumed goodwill was enough to generate momentum.

This phrase converts an internal blocker into a collaborator by making their needs the starting point of the conversation.
The stakeholder who was about to create friction suddenly has a rep who asked what they needed instead of telling them what to do.
That inversion changes every dynamic that was about to slow the deal down.

Ask what they need. The internal stakeholder who feels consulted almost never becomes the obstacle you feared.
