5. "I'll still be the one you call after you sign."
Ballmer did not only run sales. He ran support, the part of the company any a buyer meets after the money has already gone. He knew the second sale is decided by whoever answers the phone in month three. Name that person on your call, out loud, before the buyer thinks to ask.
A deal is rarely lost before the signature. It is lost in month three.
