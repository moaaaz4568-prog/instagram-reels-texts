7. He treats the comeback as more important than the collapse.

Altman made a million at 26, lost every dollar in the crash, and could not get out of bed for eight months before he rebuilt.

He turned the worst year of his life into a nine billion dollar career. His line says it all. It is not what happens to you, it is how you recover from it.

The comeback is the career.
