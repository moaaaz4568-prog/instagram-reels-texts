2. "LET'S LOOK AT THE NUMBER TOGETHER.
NOT THE COST. THE NUMBER."
Frank Luntz tested estate tax against death tax and
watched support for repeal run toward 70 percent.
He even advised lawmakers to hold the press
conference at a tax code conference at the mortuary. Nothing in
mortuary. Nothing in sentiment did.
"Cost" is your buyer's death tax. Say "the number"
and the same figure stops sounding like a loss and
starts sounding like arithmetic.
The buyer isn't afraid of the amount. They're afraid
of the word.
