The Gap

Claude ran the math on what my exact close rate would earn me at 10% commission on $5K to $15K offers.

The gap was $9,400 a month.

That's what I was paying my company every 30 days for the privilege of carrying their pipeline. Voluntarily. With a smile.

The number didn't make me angry. It made me efficient.
