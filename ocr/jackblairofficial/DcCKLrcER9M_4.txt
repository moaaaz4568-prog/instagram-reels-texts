WHAT A MODEL STILL CANNOT DO
AI writes the email, builds the deck, answers the
ticket, and does it politely at three in the morning.
What it cannot do is get a stranger to trust it with a
decision that scares them. A human saying yes to
something big wants a human across the table.
That transfer of trust has a name. It is closing.
