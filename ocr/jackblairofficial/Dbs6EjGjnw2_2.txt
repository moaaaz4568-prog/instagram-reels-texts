"I'LL BE QUICK."
A promise of speed is a confession of weight. It tells the buyer this conversation is safe to half listen to, and they take the deal exactly as seriously as you framed it.
Promise relevance instead. "This is worth two minutes if you own the number this quarter." Let the wrong people leave.
Sprint through it and they learn it wasn't worth walking through.
