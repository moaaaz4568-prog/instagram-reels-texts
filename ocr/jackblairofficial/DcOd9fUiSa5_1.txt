CLAIMS WERE FREE, SO NOBODY BELIEVED HIS

Elisha Otis had invented the safety brake and could barely sell it. Every hoist maker claimed safe. His true claim drowned in a market of identical promises.

Your buyer lives in a market of ids in that market. When says the same words, the truest one sounds exactly like the fakest one.

In a sea of claims, proof is the only accent.
