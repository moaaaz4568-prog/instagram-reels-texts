6. “YOU’LL HEAR FROM ME AT FOUR, EITHER WAY.”
The scariest sound in a crisis is no news. Pope’s clients never sit in: the update arrives when promised, even when the update is nothing yet.
Set an update cadence the moment something breaks, and honor it when there is nothing to report. The call that says maybe they quit beats the gap that says nothing.
On-time bad news builds more trust than late good news.
