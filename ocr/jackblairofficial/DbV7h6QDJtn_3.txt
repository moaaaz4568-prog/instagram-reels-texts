3. "IF YOU RESTART THE SEARCH, I'LL HAND
YOUR NOTES TO WHOEVER YOU PICK."
Kivetz, Urminsky and Zheng tracked coffee cards
and found buyers speed up as the card fills.
Distance to the goal changes behavior, every stamp
more than the last.
Say it and mean it. Handing over the stack makes
the restart cost visible without you arguing it. Most
buyers feel the weight and stay. The ones who leave
were already gone.
The offer to help them leave is what keeps them.
