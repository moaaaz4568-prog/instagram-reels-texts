05. Re-Engage Sequence

"A prospect went warm and then went cold after [describe what happened: a demo, a proposal, a first call or a content download].

Their role is: [job title] at [company type].
My offer is: [one sentence].
The last interaction was: [paste or describe it].

Build me a 3 touch re-engagement sequence across [choose channels] that picks up the thread of the original interest, adds new value or perspective with each touch, and moves toward a conversation without referencing the silence directly.

Under 60 words per touch.

Tone: fresh, relevant, completely unbothered.

No guilt. No fluff."

You stop letting warm leads expire quietly in your CRM.
Every one of them now gets a sequence that picks up the thread before it disappears completely.
