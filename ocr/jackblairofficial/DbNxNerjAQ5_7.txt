7. "I'M SENDING THE AGREEMENT NOW. WE START MONDAY."
When the salvage tug arrives, nobody asks the captain if he's ready, the line goes across because the decision was as already made.
Your close should feel the same. Not a question, a stated next step with a date on it. If the deal is real, motion confirms it. If it isn't, you just found out early.
Stop asking for the yes. Schedule it.
