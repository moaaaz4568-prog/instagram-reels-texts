2. "WHAT'S BEHIND THAT FOR YOU?"
The canvassers were trained to hear the answer
instead of beating it. That's the whole discipline.
Your buyer's position is a fact; the reason under it is
the deal. Ask it flat and let them finish. The real
reason is never the first sentence, so let them get to
the third.
Rebut the position and you never learn the reason.
