2. OFFER A STAKE, NOT A SPONSORSHIP.
Every brand offered Jordan money. Nike offered a shoe with his own name on it and a royalty on every pair sold. A stake, not a fee.
Structure the deal so the buyer visibly wins when it works, inside the terms themselves. Upside beats discounts.
People sign what makes them a partner.
