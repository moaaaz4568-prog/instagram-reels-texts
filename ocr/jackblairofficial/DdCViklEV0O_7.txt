7. "I STILL WORK THE COUNTER. THAT'S WHY I
OWN THE COMPANY."
Decades after the company passed a billion dollars
she was still behind counters touching faces, training
the counter girls, watching which sentence made a
stranger reach for her wallet.
Keep your hands on live buyers no matter your title.
The market whispers at the counter
first, and reps who stop selling stop knowing.
Every empire is a sales floor wearing a nicer suit.
