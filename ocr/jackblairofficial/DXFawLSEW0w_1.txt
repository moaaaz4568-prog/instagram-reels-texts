1. OBJECTION MAPPER

"Act as a senior sales coach. I sell [product or service] to [target buyer]. List the 10 most common objections I will face, ranked from most to least frequent. For each one write one sentence on why the buyer really says it. Format as a numbered list. Be specific to my industry. No generic advice. Just the truth."

You stop being surprised. Every objection becomes one you already prepared for 🚀 CloserCoach.ai
