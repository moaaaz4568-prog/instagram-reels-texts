1. "The next step is the pilot. Let's get it booked."

In Stanley Milgram's 1961 obedience studies, ordinary people kept going because a calm process carried them forward one step at a time. Buyers move the same way. Never end a call at a decision. End it at the next step, stated as fact, calendar open.

A process pulls harder than a pitch.
