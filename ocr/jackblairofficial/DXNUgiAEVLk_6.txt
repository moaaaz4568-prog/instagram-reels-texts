6. "I want to earn this. Tell me what that looks like to you."

Tommy Egan understood that loyalty was not given. It was demonstrated, consistently, until it became undeniable.
This phrase signals that you are not there to take the deal. You are here to deserve it.

It disarms the buyer who was preparing to negotiate from a position of power.
It resets the conversation from transactional to relational in a single sentence.
And the rep who makes a buyer feel worth earning it is the rep they remember long after the call ends.

Ask to earn it. The show up like you meant it.
