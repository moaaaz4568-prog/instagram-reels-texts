4. “Your culture is your brand.”

His thesis after selling LinkExchange to Microsoft and watching the culture collapse.

He believed culture was the ONLY long-term moat. Products get copied. Prices get matched. Culture takes a decade to build and competitors can’t take it. Zappos’s culture WAS the product.

Marketing is what you say. Culture is what you actually do when no one’s watching.
