2. "Here's the one type of person this is wrong for. If that's you, don't buy it."

A flawless pitch triggers suspicion. Nobody believes the thing with zero downsides. When you name a real limitation out loud, the buyer stops hunting for the catch, because you handed it to them. Volunteering the one weakness is what makes the toughen strengths finally land.

Name the flaw yourself and they stop looking for it.
