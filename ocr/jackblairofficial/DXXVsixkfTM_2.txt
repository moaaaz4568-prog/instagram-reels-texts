2. "I have prepared for this conversation more than you probably expect. Let me show you what I mean."

Roy Kent was never the most talented person in the room. He was always the most prepared and the room always felt the difference.

This phrase signals effort before you demonstrate it and effort signals creates an anticipation that makes everything you say next land harder.
The buyer stops assuming this is another routine call and starts paying the kind of attention that closes deals.

Out-prepare everyone in the room. Then tell them you did before you show them you did.
