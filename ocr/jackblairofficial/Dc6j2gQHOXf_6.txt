6. GIVE A LOSS 24 HOURS, NO MORE.
His rule after any defeat: 24 hours to feel it, then the film, then the next opponent. Recruiting losses got the same clock.
Lose a deal, run the autopsy once, take the lesson, close the folder. Reps who replay losses all quarter lose the next three.
Mourn on a timer. Move on schedule.
