6. "I am going to give this everything I have today. Not because I have to. Because this is what I do."

Bombay's final comeback was not motivated by external pressure. It was an internal decision about identity. This phrase reconnects a rep to their professional identity at the exact moment the slump is trying to redefine it.

Said before the call it sets an internal standard that the buyer never sees but always feels.
The rep who gives everything because it is who they are closes at a different level than one who gives everything because they have to.
Identity is always the strongest performance driver available.

Reconnect to who you are before the call. The buyer feels the person who shows up for that reason.
