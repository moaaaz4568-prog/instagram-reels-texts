2. He became the face of his own brand.

Sixty years on, Harvey still records the radio and Tv ads for Harvey Norman himself.

He never hid behind the company name. He put himself out front so customers trusted a person, not a logo. People buy from someone they feel they know. Be the name attached to the product and familiarity does half the selling.

Put your face on it and people trust it.
