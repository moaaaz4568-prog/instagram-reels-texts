7. "IF WE'RE BOTH A LITTLE UNCOMFORTABLE
WITH THIS, WE GOT IT RIGHT."
The oldest test in mediation says a good settlement
leaves both sides slightly unhappy. Piazza's cases
close when each party trades the perfect deal for the
finished one.
Say it out loud at the final number. The last squeeze
of discomfort stops reading as a warning and starts
reading as proof the deal is fair.
Comfort is suspicious. A fair deal pinches.
