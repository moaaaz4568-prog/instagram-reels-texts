1. “CAN I HAVE NINETY SECONDS, AND THEN YOU TELL ME TO STOP?”
Selling takes time from people. Pitching asks for it. The difference is that one of them asks for it. It’s that one of them hands the other person a stop button, and almost nobody ever presses it.
Use it the second before you start. You are not being humble, you are removing the reason to brace.
People give more time to whoever offers to take less.
