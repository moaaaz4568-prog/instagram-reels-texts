5. He turned himself into the logo.

The white suit, the goatee, the string tie,
the title Colonel, all of it was on purpose.

Sanders built a character people trusted
on sight and never forgot, so the brand
could walk into the room a step ahead of
him. In a business anyone could copy, he
made himself the part that couldn't be.

When the product is easy to copy, become
the thing that isn't.
