2. "I have come back to this conversation because I think we left something important unresolved."

Wick always returned with a reason and a direction not just a presence.
This phrase converts a follow-up from a check-in into a purposeful re-entry.

The buyer who was expecting another hollow touch suddenly finds themselves in a conversation with an agenda.
You are not chasing. You are returning to finish something that was started and left incomplete.
That distinction is everything when a deal has gone quiet.

Return with a reason. The rep who comes back with purpose never looks desperate.
