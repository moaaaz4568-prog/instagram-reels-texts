7. "Send me somebody and I'll make it worth your while."

Girard built a paid army. Barbers, bankers, mechanics, anyone who sent him a buyer got cash when the deal closed. Hundreds of people selling for him while he sat at his desk.

He never ate lunch with the other salesmen. While they stood in a circle complaining, his network was out hunting.

Your closed buyers and contacts are an unpaid sales force waiting for an offer. Put a bounty on introductions and watch what shows up.
