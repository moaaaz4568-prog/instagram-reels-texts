7. The state of Oregon offered Bill disability payments his entire life.

He never accepted a check.

He didn't sell because he loved selling. He sold because he refused to be a man who couldn't take care of himself.

The 10-mile walk wasn't the cost of the work. It was the proof he was alive.

The salesperson who works for the dignity of the work outsells the one working for the commission. Every time.
