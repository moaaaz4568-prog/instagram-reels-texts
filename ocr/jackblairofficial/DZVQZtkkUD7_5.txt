5. He turned himself into a teacher.
Savage became a speaker thousands of
agents traveled to hear.
By teaching what he knew, he sharpened
his own thinking and became the name
people trusted before they had ever met
him. The expert who teaches is the one
the room already believes when he walks
in.
Teach what you do and you stop having to
prove you can do it.
