5. The Re-Opener

"My prospect went cold 3 weeks ago
after [specific stage of conversation].
Something has changed since then:
[new thing — oil prices, new law,
industry news, their competitor].
Write me a message that uses this
as a natural reason to reach back
out. Make it feel like I'm doing
them a favor. Not following up."

New reason. New conversation.
Same deal back on the table.
