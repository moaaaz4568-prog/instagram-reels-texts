6. "You've been told that's just how it is. Whoever told you that gave up early."

The Duke had fought the stammer his whole life. For it since childhood, and by the time he found Logue it was treated as permanent. Logue treated it as mechanics, fixed the breathing, and had him delivering clean speeches in Canberra within a year.

Buyers defend problems they've decided are permanent. Challenge the buyer.

Buyers don't defend problems. They defend having given up.
