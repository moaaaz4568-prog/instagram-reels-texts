7. "Don't buy the version you heard from me. Buy the one that survives your questions."

The Vatican's lesson in one line: a yes that was never argued against is worthless, and everyone quietly knows it. Hand the buyer the examiner's chair and let the offer earn the verdict. The deals that survive questioning are the ones that never come back unwound.

An unexamined yes cancels itself later.
