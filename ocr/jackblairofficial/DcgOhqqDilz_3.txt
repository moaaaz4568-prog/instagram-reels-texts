3. THEY SIT THE EXAM OVER AND OVER UNTIL IT STICKS.
The oral exams are called appearances, and students sit them again and again across years, most failing several.
Nobody passes the Knowledge in one sitting and nobody expects to. Every failed appearance maps exactly what to study next. Treat every lost deal as an appearance, not a verdict.
A failed exam is a syllabus for the next one.
