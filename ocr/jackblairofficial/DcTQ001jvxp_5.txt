5. "WE'LL CARRY THE SWITCHING COSTS."
The real lock on any account is never love. It is the pain of moving: retraining, spares, process. He built deals that absorbed the pain.
List the buyer’s true switching costs and eat the ugly ones in the proposal: the migration, the training, the parallel month. The incumbent’s moat is just a bill nobody offered to pay.
Accounts stay for the moat. Pay the toll and they walk across.
