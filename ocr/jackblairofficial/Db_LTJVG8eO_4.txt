I DON'T KNOW YET. LET ME ASK YOU A FEW
MORE THINGS.
Savage refused to recommend anything he said
before he understood the situation, and he said so out loud.
Admitting he did not know made everything he said
afterward credible. The buyer stopped hearing a
salesman and started hearing an advisor.
Saying you don't know yet buys more trust than an
answer you guessed at.
