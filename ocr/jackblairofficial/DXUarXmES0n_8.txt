7. "I think we both already know how this ends, I just want to make sure you are comfortable getting there."

This is the phrase Keyser Soze would deliver in the final moment of any deal.
Certain. Quiet. Completely without pressure and completely without doubt.

The buyer who hears this and feels it is true has nowhere left to go except forward.
The close is not a question shared between two people who both already know now. You simply said it out loud first.

The close that lands softest always hits hardest. Say it like it was never in question.
