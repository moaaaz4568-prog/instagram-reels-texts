Read that again if you sell for a living.

Every habit on that list is a reason a machine can't do your job. It can send the email and log the call. It can't read a room, hold a silence, or hear Friday afternoon in a buyer's voice.

Anything sold from a desk, off a script, on a screen is going to a machine.
