01. Close Architect

"Build me a closing sequence for a [job title] at a [company type] who has been warm for [number] weeks but has not committed yet.
Include a re-engagement opener, a value summary, an urgency frame,
and a closing question that moves toward a decision today.
Format as 4 numbered steps, under 50 words each.
Tone: confident, direct, zero desperation.
No fluff. No pitching."

You stop winging the final conversation. You walk in with a sequence built to land.
