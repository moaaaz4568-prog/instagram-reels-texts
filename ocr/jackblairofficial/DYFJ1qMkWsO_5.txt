5. The brother was the opening.

Donnie Wahlberg was already in New Kids on the Block. After Mark got out he convinced him to try music.

Marky Mark and the Funky Bunch dropped Good Vibrations in 1991. Mark was 19. He had a #1 single, a Calvin Klein deal, and a way out of the neighborhood.

Sometimes the placement that saves you is sitting at your own dinner table.
