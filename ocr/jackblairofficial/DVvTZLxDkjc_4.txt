4. "Attack where he is unprepared. Appear where you are not expected."

Every rep in your market is pitching the same way.

Same opener. Same pitch. Same close attempt.

Sun Tzu called this predictable and said it was how you lose.

The rep who shows up differently — leads with insight instead of pitch, asks instead of tells — is attacking exactly where the buyer isn't ready to defend.
