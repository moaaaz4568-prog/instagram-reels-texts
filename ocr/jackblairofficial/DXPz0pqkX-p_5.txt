5. "I have done this enough times to know what happens when organisations at your stage wait on this."

Experience delivered without arrogance is the most credible thing available on an executive call. Tywin Lannister never needed to prove his track record. The room already felt it.

This phrase invokes pattern recognition without naming names or breaking confidence. The executive hears someone who has seen this situation resolve in both directions and is choosing which direction it usually takes.

Let your experience speak before your solution does. Executives buy track records, not products.
