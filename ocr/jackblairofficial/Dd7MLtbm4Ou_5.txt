5. "Fair enough. Can I ask you one thing before you go?"

Half of the Straight Line is tonality, and this is the reasonable man tone: you agree with the buyer's right to hesitate, so the line stays open. The Stratton scripts used "fair enough" for exactly that reason. Deflect, agree, then come back in with a question. Never fight the objection's words. Agree with the person, not then move.

Agree with the person. Never with the objection.
