EVERYONE GOT THE SAME REPORT
Bertram Forer gave 39 students a personality sketch assembled from a newsstand astrology book. Every copy was identical, presented as a personal reading. The class rated its accuracy 4.26 out of 5. Almost every student was certain a generic paragraph had been written about them alone.
Feeling seen and being seen are different products.
