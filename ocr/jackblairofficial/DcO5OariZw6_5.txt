PRICE AFTER POSSESSION, NEVER BEFORE
The $180,000 conversation happened after the
weekend, not before it. By then she was negotiating
terms of keeping, not deciding on buying.
Sequence is the skill. Let value land in their world
first and price becomes logistics. Lead with price
and price becomes the product.
Price lands soft when the thing already lives with
them.
