7. "What do you need from me right now to make this work?"
Roy Kent always asked the direct question nobody else was willing to ask
because he was more interested in the result than in protecting himself from an uncomfortable answer.
This phrase hands the buyer complete ownership of the final step
and signals that you are prepared to do whatever it takes to meet them there. The buyer who has been waiting for a rep to ask what they actually need almost always tells you exactly what it is.
Ask for what they need. Then go and get it. Every time without exception.
