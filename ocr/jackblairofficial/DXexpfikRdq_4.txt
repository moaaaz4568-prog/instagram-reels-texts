4. "I am not going to tell you things are great if they are not. What I am going to tell you is what I am going to do about it."

Maguire's greatest moments were built on radical honesty delivered with complete accountability. This phrase does the same thing and strips every defensive layer from a conversation that desperately needs none.

The client who was expecting you to minimise the problem gets the opposite. They get acknowledgement and a direction in the same sentence. That combination closes the trust gap faster than any apology or discount ever could.

Acknowledge the problem and commit to the fix in one breath. The client who hears both almost always stays.
