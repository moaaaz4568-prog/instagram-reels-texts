5. THE MEETING ASK
“Write 5 ways to ask for a meeting at the end of a cold call that don’t sound like ‘can I get 15 minutes on your calendar.’ Each must feel like a natural next step, not a hard pivot to selling. Under 15 words. Low pressure. High confidence.”
The ask is the whole point. Don’t mumble it at the end like an afterthought.
