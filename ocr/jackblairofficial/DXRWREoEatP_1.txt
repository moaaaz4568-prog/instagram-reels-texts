1. "I noticed something about how you described your situation and I want to make sure I understood it correctly."

Neal Caffrey made every person he spoke to feel like the most observed person in the room.
Not watched. Noticed. There is a significant difference and buyers feel it immediately.

This phrase signals that you were listening at a level that goes beyond note-taking.
The guarded buyer who was rationing their attention suddenly feels the pull of being genuinely understood. That pull is the fastest rapport builder available on any cold call.

Listen visibly. The buyer who feels heard opens every door you need.
