3. "Can I be straight? The price concern doesn't match the questions you've been asking."

In poker you don't trust the words, you watch whether the bet matches the story. Duke's edge was catching the play that contradicted itself. When a buyer says "too expensive" but every question was about results, the objection is a bluff. Name the mismatch, calmly.

A real objection stays consistent. A bluff argues with itself.
