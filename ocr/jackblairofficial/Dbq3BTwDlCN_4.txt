"I MIGHT BE HEARING AGREEMENT THAT ISN'T THERE."
The most common error in these cases is not lying.
It is a hearer who wanted a yes and found one in
something that was never a yes.
Say it out loud, mid call, while the deal is still cheap
to lose.
The cheapest deal to lose is the one you never had.
