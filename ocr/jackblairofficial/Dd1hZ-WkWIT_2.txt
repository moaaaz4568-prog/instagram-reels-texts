2. "I'd rather have your no today than your maybe for a month."

The students who did not know their grade got a third choice: pay five dollars to decide after the grade came out. Many paid. They spent real money to delay a decision that came out the same way, because a reason attached feels safer than the same decision without one. A month of thinking is that five dollars, paid out of your pipeline. Name the cost of the delay out loud, then offer the fast no as the cheaper option.

Waiting is a purchase. Show them the receipt.
