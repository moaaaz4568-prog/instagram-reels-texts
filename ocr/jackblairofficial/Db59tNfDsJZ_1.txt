1. "WHAT IS IT COSTING YOU TO NOT HAVE THIS RIGHT NOW?"
Most reps talk about the price of yes. Ziglar taught reps to talk about the price of no.
Every day a buyer waits is a day the problem stays.
Make the cost of waiting more visible than the cost of buying and urgency creates itself.
