7. "At the end of this, whatever you decide, you are going to know I gave you everything I had on this call."

Tommy Egan left everything in every room he entered.
No half measures. No hedging. Every conversation at full capacity.

This phrase signals total commitment in the final moments of a call and the buyer feels it. They may not sign today. But they remember the rep who showed up completely. And the rep they remember is always the rep they call back.
