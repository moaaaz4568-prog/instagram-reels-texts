2. "CHEAPER VERSIONS OF THIS EXIST. THEY'RE NOT VERSIONS OF THIS."
Diamonds had rivals, cheaper stones, prettier stones. The campaign never argued specs. It made the diamond the only object that could carry the meaning.
Steal that. When a buyer lists alternatives, agree the cheaper thing exists, then ask what it's supposed to carry. A substitute that can't hold the outcome isn't a substitute. Say so without flinching.
Comparison shopping dies where meaning starts.
