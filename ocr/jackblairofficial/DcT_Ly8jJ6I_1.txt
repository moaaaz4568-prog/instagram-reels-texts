1. “WHAT DOES THIS FIX THAT NOBODY SAYS OUT LOUD?”
Packard’s discovery: postwar researchers stopped asking people what they wanted and started watching why they reached. The real reasons were never on the spec sheet.
Run every deal through the question. The CFO buying reporting software is buying Sunday nights back. Name the quiet job and the spec sheet becomes evidence instead of the argument.
People pay list price for what they cannot admit they need.
