1. "Before we go further, let's drop the titles. This works better between equals."

Lionel Logue took the future King of England as a patient and ran the relationship on level ground. The Duke came to his Harley Street rooms and worked the program like any other patient, because treatment can't reach a man it has to bow to.

With a buyer who outranks you, deference reads as weakness. Level ground is the one thing nobody else in their world offers.

Deference loses the room that respect wins.
