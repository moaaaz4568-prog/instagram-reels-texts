The augment column has one seat that software can't take.

A nervous human, about to move real money, deciding whether to trust another human. Reading the hesitation behind the objection. Making the yes feel safe.

The data says AI eats what can be written down, and that moment can't be written. If you've spent years learning to run it on instinct, you're not holding a skill. You're holding the one asset that dataset says appreciates.
