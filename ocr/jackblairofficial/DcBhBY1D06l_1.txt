THE YES MONEY COULD NOT BUY
1
In 1999 the biggest interview in the world was on the table, and outlets around the world were reportedly offering millions for it.
Walters did not pay for interviews. Walters walked in with nothing to trade except how the story would be told. She won, and 74 million people watched, still the most watched news interview in American television history.
She beat every offer without matching one. Deals are won on outcomes more often than on price.
