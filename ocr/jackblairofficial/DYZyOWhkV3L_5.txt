5. "I love when people say you come from humble beginnings. It means you were poor as hell."

Perry's documented line to Forbes.

He grew up with an abusive father in New Orleans. He dropped out of high school. He worked hotel jobs, used cars, and bill collections. He saved $12,000 from tax returns for years to fund a play that bombed.

He doesn't hide where he came from. He puts it in the marketing.
