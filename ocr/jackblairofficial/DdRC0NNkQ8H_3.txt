3. "You said something last time that stuck with me."

Gagosian went to dinner at Marc Jacobs' apartment in Paris. Days later he called about two paintings he'd noticed on the walls, and the deal closed within three days. Dinner was the research. Write down the one thing your buyer said that mattered most, in their words. Open the next call with it.

Remember what they said and they'll remember you.
