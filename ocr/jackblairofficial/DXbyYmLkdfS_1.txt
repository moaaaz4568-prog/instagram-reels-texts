1. "I want to be direct with you about something. The window on this specific conversation is closing and I think you know that."

Jack Bauer never softened the reality of a deadline to make a room more comfortable.
He named it clearly because clarity was always more useful than comfort.

This phrase signals real urgency without manufactured pressure and buyers feel the difference immediately. The buyer who has been comfortable in the stall suddenly feels the structure and reality of a decision that has an edge. Real edges close deals that false ones never could.

Name the real deadline. The buyer who understands it is real almost always moves before it arrives.
