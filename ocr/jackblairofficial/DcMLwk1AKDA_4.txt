SELL THE FUTURE, NOT THE AUTOPSY
He kept litigating the past, publishing tables of the
dead. The profession dug in harder with every table.
Frame every finding forward: here is what the next
quarter looks like once this is fixed. Skip the exhibit
on who caused the last one.
A buyer will fund a future. They will not prosecute
their past.
