4. "I am not going to pretend I have been doing this for twenty years. What I can tell you is what I have seen work right now."
Buyers respect honesty about experience far more than they respect a performance of it.
This phrase disarms the credibility question before they can use it against you.
It replaces seniority with currency. What works right now is more valuable than what worked a decade ago. The buyer stops measuring your years and starts measuring your relevance.
Christopher Moritsanti never tried to be younger than he was. He made being older the reason to listen.
Recency is a credential. Use it like one.
