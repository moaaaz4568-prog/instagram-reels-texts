6. "SAY IT BACK TO ME. WHAT DOES THIS FIX BY FRIDAY?"
Popeil loaded the rotisserie, flipped the switch,
stepped away, and the studio audience chanted
back at him: set it and forget it. He wrote the line.
The room delivered it. The seller went silent exactly
when the sale spoke.
Plant one phrase at every proof point until the buyer
beats you to it. When they say your line first, stop
selling and paper it.
When they say your line, the show is sold.
When the crowd knows the words, the show is sold.
