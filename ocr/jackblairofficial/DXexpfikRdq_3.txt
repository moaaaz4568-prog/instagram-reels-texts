3. "Help me understand what we would need to look like for you to feel genuinely good about this relationship."

Maguire always asked the question that nobody else in the room was willing to ask.
This phrase finds the specific standard the client needs you to meet and hands you the exact brief for keeping them.

The client who answers honestly gives you a roadmap that no competitor can match
because it was built from what they specifically need from you.
The account saved on those terms is the account that never leaves the same way again.

Ask for the standard. Build to it exactly. The relationship rebuilt on their terms is the one that holds.
