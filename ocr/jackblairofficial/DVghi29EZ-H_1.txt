closed $1.3m in my first year of sales.
the reps next to me were making $60k.

here's the only difference.

1. isolate the real objection.
when prospects say "i need to think about it" most reps explain more. that's the wrong move.

top reps ask one question: "what specifically are you thinking through?"
that question alone closes more deals than any pitch ever will.

2. silence is a weapon.
after you ask for the close — no speaks loses.
most reps can't handle the silence. train yourself to love it.

3. rehearse before it costs you.
i started simulating objection scenarios inside an app called @closercoach.app before every call (app i built so i don't have to burn real leads).
by the time the prospect objected, i'd already answered it 50 times.

most reps improvise. closers train.
