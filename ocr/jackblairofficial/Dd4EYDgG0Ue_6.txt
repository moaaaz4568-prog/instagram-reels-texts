6. "Sell the most this year and there's a prize. Give the most to your church and there's a bigger one."

Philadelphia, 1917. Two hundred agents at the first national convention of her sales force, and the prizes went two ways: the women who had sold the most, and the women who had given the most to charity. She made the yes visible, then made the reason for it visible. Status in a room of peers moved more product than any commission table.

People sell harder for a room that will see it.
