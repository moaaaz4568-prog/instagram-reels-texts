3. "Forget how I'd pitch this to anyone else. Here's what it means for you specifically."

Johnson gave the same ask a hundred different faces, one per person, each built from what that person feared and wanted. Nobody got the generic version.

You rebuild the offer in the buyer's own language and bind stakes. The pitch that sounds mass-produced gets a mass-produced answer.

The generic pitch earns the generic no.
