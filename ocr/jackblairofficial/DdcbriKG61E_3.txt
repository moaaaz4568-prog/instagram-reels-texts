3. "Forget the price for a second. What would make this an easy yes?"

The Mets didn’t win on the headline alone. The deal carries a $75 million signing bonus, no deferred money, a full no-trade clause and an opt-out after five years. Boras sold the shape, not just the size. Ask for the shape before you fight about the number: payment timing, start date, who’s on the call, what happens if it doesn’t work. Half the objections to price are objections to shape.

Buyers argue price when nobody asked about shape.
