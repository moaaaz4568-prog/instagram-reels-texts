3. "I know I am not the name you were expecting on this call. Give me ten minutes and tell me if that still matters."

A slump damages your presence before you open your mouth.
You start apologising for yourself in ways you do not even notice.

This phrase owns the underdog position and converts it into a challenge the buyer wants to take.
Most will give you the ten minutes out the ten minutes of curiosity alone.
Inside those ten minutes you remind yourself and them exactly who you are.

The comeback starts the moment you stop shrinking from the room.
