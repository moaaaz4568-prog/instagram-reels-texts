1. He decided he was a salesman before he was ten.

At six, Jones was selling magazines door to door, and by eight he was running his own Kool-Aid and ice cream operation.

Selling was never a job he fell into as an adult, it was an identity he'd worn his whole life, and identity beats willpower every time. He never had to psych himself up to be what he already was.

When selling is who you are, you never have to force yourself to do it.
