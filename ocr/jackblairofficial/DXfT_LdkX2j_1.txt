1. "I hear you and I want to find a way to make this work. What I cannot do is move on the number."

Vic Mackey always separated what he was willing to do from what he was not
and he communicated that boundary with complete clarity and zero apology.

This phrase signals warmth and firmness simultaneously and the buyer feels both.
The buyer who was using relationship language to soften the price suddenly finds a boundary they cannot move emotionally.
You acknowledged the relationship. You held the number. Both things happened in the same sentence.
Hold the number with warmth. The buyer who cannot move it emotionally almost always accepts it logically.
