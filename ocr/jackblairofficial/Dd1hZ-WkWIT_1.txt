1. "If the budget comes through, is this a yes? And if it doesn't?"

Students were told they had passed a big exam, or failed it, or that the grade was not in yet, then offered a cheap trip to Hawaii. Fifty four percent of the passes booked. Fifty percent of the fails booked. Only seven percent of the thirty two percent of the ones still waiting booked, though they would have gone either way. That is your buyer waiting on the budget. Ask them to decide under each outcome. If yes, the wait was never about the information.

A buyer who says yes both ways was never waiting.
