5. "JUST CHECKING IN"

The three words that tell your buyer you have nothing new to offer.

It signals you're chasing, not leading.

The reps closing the most deals never check in.

They follow up with purpose. With value. With a real reason to get back on the call.
"Just checking in" is the sound of a deal dying quietly.
