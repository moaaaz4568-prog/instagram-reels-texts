3. "What has been tried before and what made it fall short?"

Varys always studied what failed before he designed what would succeed.
A buyer who has been burned before is the most important buyer to understand completely.

This phrase surfaces the history you were never going to be told unprompted. The internal project that collapsed. The previous vendor who overpromised. The decision-maker who took the blame.
All of it is in the answer and all of it tells you exactly how to position your close.

The failure that came before you is the most valuable intelligence on the call. Find it.
