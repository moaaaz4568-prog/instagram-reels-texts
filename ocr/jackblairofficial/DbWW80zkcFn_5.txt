5. "IF YOU TELL ME IT'S DEAD, I STOP CALLING. THAT'S A PROMISE."
The fourth property surprised everyone: clarity beat cunning. Strategies that were hard to read scored worse, because nobody dares cooperate with what they can't predict.
Be boringly predictable. Say what earns what, what ends what, then do precisely that, every time. A buyer who can predict you can afford to trust you, and the one thing nobody trusts is a mystery whose reactions are a mystery.
Mystery is expensive. Predictability compounds. Predictability compounds.
