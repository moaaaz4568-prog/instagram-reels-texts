4. "I'll say the same thing when it's my own side that's wrong."

Across that same study, in no case did a group reject a correction because it landed on someone they already agreed with. People took the hit on their own team as readily as on the other one. So correct your own product out loud, in the same flat voice, before you correct theirs.

The rep who only ever corrects the competitor is the one nobody believes.
