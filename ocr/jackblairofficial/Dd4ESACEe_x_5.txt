5. "So I know I explained it well, tell me how you'll take these at home."

Teach back, from the federal AHRQ toolkit. The nurse doesn't ask "any questions?" She asks the patient to say the plan back in their own words. On one rehabilitation unit it cut thirty day readmissions by 45 percent. The buyer who repeats the plan back in their own words has bought it twice, once from you and once from themselves.

What they can say back, they keep.
