1. "I'M NOT THE SLICKEST REP YOU'LL TALK TO THIS WEEK."

In 1977 Barbara Walters asked her, to her face, if she knew people saw her as a joke. Dolly smiled. She had chosen that reaction years before.

Open under their expectations on purpose. The rep who concedes the rough edges first removes the thing every buyer walks in braced against.

Nobody argues with the rep who already conceded the polish.
