6. "What would you need to see to feel confident moving forward today?"

Most reps try to eliminate doubt. The elite reps name it and hand the buyer a solution.

This phrase tells your prospect you are not afraid of their hesitation. It puts the decision criteria in their hands and their words back in your mouth. Now you are not guessing. You are closing to a target they gave you.

Stop pitching into the dark. Ask for the light switch.
