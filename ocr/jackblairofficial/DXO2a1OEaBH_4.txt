4. "I have heard stronger objections than that one. Here is how I think about it."

Tyron never flinched at an attack he had already survived a version of before.
This phrase signals experience without arrogance and resets the power dynamic in a single sentence.

The buyer who expected their objection to land watches it deflected with calm authority.
They stop probing for weakness and start wondering what else you already know.

Confidence in the face of an objection is the fastest way to make a sceptic reconsider.

Carry your experience like armour. Let the buyer see it holding.
