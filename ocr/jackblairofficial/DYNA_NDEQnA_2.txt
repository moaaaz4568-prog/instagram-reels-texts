2. One old piece of work opens the door years later.

In 1998 Cranston did one X-Files episode called "Drive." The writer was Vince Gilligan.

10 years later AMC didn't want Cranston for Breaking Bad. Gilligan showed them the 1998 tape. They changed their mind.

The work you did when nobody was watching is what the right person uses to fight for you.
