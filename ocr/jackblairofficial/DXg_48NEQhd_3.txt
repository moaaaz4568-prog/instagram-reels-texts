03. Already Have One

"My prospect replied: 'we already use [competitor or solution]. My offer is: [one sentence]. Their role is: [job title] at [company type].

Write me 3 responses that:
Validate their current solution without attacking it,
introduce one specific angle where my offer does something theirs does not,
and end with one question that opens a genuine conversation.

Under 50 words each.

Tone: peer to peer, specific, no desperation.

No pitching. No fluff.

You stop losing warm prospects to the competitor objection.
Every 'we already have one' now has a response worth reading."
