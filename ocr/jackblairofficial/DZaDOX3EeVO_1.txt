1. He treated the cold call as a way to get the meeting, not the sale.

At Xerox, Becker stopped trying to sell copiers over the phone.

He realized the only job of the call was to earn five minutes in person, so he stopped pitching the product and started selling the appointment. One small yes is far easier to get than one big one.

Ask for the meeting, not the money.
