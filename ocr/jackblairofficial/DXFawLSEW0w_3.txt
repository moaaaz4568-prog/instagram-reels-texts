3. PRICE DEFENDER

"My prospect said [paste their exact words about price]. I sell [product] at [price point] and their main alternative is [competitor or doing nothing]. Write me a response that reframes the cost as an investment, acknowledges their concern without folding, and moves the conversation forward. Keep it under 50 words. Make it calm and confident. Zero guesswork."

You stop discounting. The price holds and so does the deal.
