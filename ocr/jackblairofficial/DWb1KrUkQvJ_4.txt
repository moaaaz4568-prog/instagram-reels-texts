4. "It just works."

The three words that ended
every technical objection
before it could form.

Most reps over-explain.
They bury the close in features,
specs, and comparisons.

Jobs stripped everything away
until the value was so obvious
it needed no defense.
Simplicity isn't a design choice.
It's a closing strategy.
