1. "I know you are already talking to someone bigger than us. That is exactly why I asked for this call."

Most reps hide the from the competitor conversation. They hope the buyer never brings it up and panic when they do.

This phrase puts it on the table before the buyer can use it as a weapon. It signals confidence, self-awareness, and a reason to keep listening. The buyer stops comparing and starts wondering what you know that the other side does not.

Name the giant in the room. Then tell them why you are still standing there.
