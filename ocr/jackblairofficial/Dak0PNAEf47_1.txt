1. "Before either of us says a number, let me show you what the market already decided."

Scott Boras never opens a negotiation with an opinion. He opens the binder. Owners can argue with an agent. They can't argue with the market.

Walk your buyer through real third-party numbers before price ever comes up, and the conversation starts on facts instead of feelings.

The first number should never be an opinion.
