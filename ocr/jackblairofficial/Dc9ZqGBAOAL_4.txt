4. DESTROY WHAT YOU TAKE BACK.
The machines Singer took in were not resold. They
were scrapped, every single one, so no cheap
secondhand market could grow underneath the new
ones.
Clark was not buying old machines. He was buying
the absence of a used market.
Sometimes you pay to remove a rival.
