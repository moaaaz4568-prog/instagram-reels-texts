7. He simply never left.

Fakharzadeh wrote his first policy in 1955 and was still working in his nineties.

He outlasted every agent who started alongside him, not by being the most gifted in the room, but by refusing to leave it. Sixty years of compounding relationships built a book no newcomer could ever catch.

Longevity is a strategy almost no one has the patience to use.
