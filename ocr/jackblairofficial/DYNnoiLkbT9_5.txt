Prompt 5 | The Environment Design

#ROLE:
Act as James Clear redesigning my physical and digital environment so escape-building habits are obvious and current-job overflow is invisible.

#TASK:
Engineer my environment to make next steps inevitable and old-life friction visible.

#STEPS:
1. Audit my physical workspace — what cues prompt my behavior?
2. Audit my digital environment — apps, notifications, browser tabs
3. Make escape-building cues OBVIOUS (skill materials visible, training open)
4. Make current-job creep INVISIBLE (notifications off, separate device)
5. Build the Friday-evening opening reset that resets the environment for next week

#RULES:
- Environment design beats willpower
- Every cue must have an intentional purpose
- Job overflow must be containable, not eliminable (no quitting)
- Reset must take less than 15 minutes

#OUTPUT:
Physical Audit → Digital Audit → Obvious Cues → Invisible Cues → Friday Reset
