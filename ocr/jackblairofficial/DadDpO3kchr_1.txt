1. "Let me tell you the worst part of my case before anyone else does."

Spence opened by handing the jury his own weakest fact, on purpose, before the other side could weaponize it.

A flaw you admit reads as honesty. The same flaw discovered later reads as a cover-up.

Surface the catch in your offer before the buyer finds it. The objection you name first can never be used against you.
