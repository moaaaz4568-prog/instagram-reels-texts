2. KNOW EVERY NAME IN THE ROOM.
He walked in knowing who was on every phone,
who wanted the trophy, who hated losing. The gavel
was easy. The homework was the sale.
Ten minutes on who is on the call and what they
lose by passing beats an hour of pitch polish.
Preparation reads as confidence.
