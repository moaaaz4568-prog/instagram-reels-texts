6. "Pick the problem your team hates most. We kill it together before any contract."

The famous stuck truck at Robbers Cave needed every boy from both tribes on one rope. The win belonged to all of them, and the war never recovered from it.

Before any contract, pick one problem you can genuinely kill together in a week, with their hands on the rope too. Then let the shared win argue for you.

A rope pulled together beats a gift handed over.
