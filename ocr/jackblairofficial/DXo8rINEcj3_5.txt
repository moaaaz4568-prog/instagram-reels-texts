05. New Rep Sequence

"Build me a 4 touch cold outreach sequence for [job title] at [company type] in the [industry] space written by someone who is new to selling [product or service] but has a background in [describe transferable experience I do not have].

My offer is: [one sentence].

Each touch must:
Sound confident and credible without claiming experience I do not have,
use a different angle across all four touches,
and build a progressively stronger case for a conversation.

Under 70 words per touch.

Tone: honest, confident, credibly specific without overstating.

Zero guesswork."

You stop winging a sequence that makes your inexperience obvious.
Every touch now sounds like it was written by someone who belongs in this conversation.
