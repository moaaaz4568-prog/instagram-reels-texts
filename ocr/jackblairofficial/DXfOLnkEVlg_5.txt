5. "I am going to give you my honest read
on this based on everything I have seen in
this market. You can take it or leave it."
Clay always delivered his perspective with the quiet authority of
someone who had earned the right to have one.
This phrase signals to the buyer he hears it as
something categorically different from a pitch.
A read from someone with real experience is not an opinion. It is
intelligence.
The buyer who receives it stops evaluating your pitch and starts
considering your counsel.
Counsel from a trusted source closes deals that pitches from
untrusting ones leave permanently open.
Offer the read. The buyer who accepts it has already started
trusting you with the decision.
