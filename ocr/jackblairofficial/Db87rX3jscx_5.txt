5. "WHAT RUNS THE BUSINESS WHEN YOU'RE NOT THERE?"
The question that finds out if you're buying a business or being a job.
If the seller answers 'I do,' the business is the seller, not an asset. Codie's filter: if the owner is required, the price drops 40%.
The business that runs without the owner is the only one worth buying.
