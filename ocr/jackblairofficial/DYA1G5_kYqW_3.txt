3. By 1985 Art had 100,000 agents across America.

He built a private television broadcast system inside the company.

Every week he sat in front of a camera and spoke directly to every agent. Sales numbers. Stories from the field. A pep talk from a guy who looked them in the eye through a screen.

The agent in Idaho felt the same connection to Art as the agent two doors down from headquarters.
