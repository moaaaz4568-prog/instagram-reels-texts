6. "Want me to take three of these off the table?"

Here's the strangest jam detail. Shoppers tasted the same amount at both tables, about 1.5 flavors. Interest was identical. Deciding is what broke.

Your buyer doesn't need more information. They need fewer doors. Ask permission to subtract, and watch relief cross their face when you do.

Selling by subtraction reads as honesty.
