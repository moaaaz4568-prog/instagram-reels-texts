1. "WHEN YOU SHOP THIS AROUND, HERE'S WHAT THEY'LL SAY ABOUT US."
William McGuire spent the Cold War studying why brainwashing worked and how to stop it. His answer: inoculation.
A mind given a weakened dose of an attack builds antibodies against the full one. Your buyer is about to hear every attack your rivals own. Give them the weak dose first, from you, with the antidote attached. Whoever delivers the attack first owns the defense.
