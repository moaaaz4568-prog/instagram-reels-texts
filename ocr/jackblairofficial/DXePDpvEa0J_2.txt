02. Funding Message
"A prospect I want to reach just announced a funding round.
Their company is [company name or type].
Their job title is [job title].
My offer is [one sentence].
Write me 3 outreach messages that reference the funding
announcement
as the reason for reaching out,
connect the funding event directly to a problem
and end with one low friction question that opens a conversation.
Under 70 words each.
No congratulating them on the funding.
Tone: relevant, direct, immediately useful.
No pitching. No fluff.
You stop sending generic messages to funded companies.
Every outreach now sounds like it was written the day the news
broke."
