5. RELAX THE ROOM BEFORE YOU EVER ASK FOR ANYTHING.

Hanna ran his lesson over a long lunch, loose and easy, not in a boardroom. He knew a relaxed buyer says yes and a tense one says let me think.

Drop the rep urgency. Talk like a person. The sale gets easier the second the other side stops bracing for a pitch.

Ease the room first. The yes comes easier.
