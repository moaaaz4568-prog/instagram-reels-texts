7. "I'D RATHER YOU SAY NO NOW THAN WASTE BOTH OUR TIME ON A MAYBE."
This phrase terrifies most reps because it sounds like you are giving up. It is actually the most powerful move on the board.
It signals scarcity, confidence, and respect in one sentence. Buyers who were stalling suddenly decide. Buyers who were out tell you so you can move on.
Either way, you win. The maybe is the only thing that kills pipelines.
