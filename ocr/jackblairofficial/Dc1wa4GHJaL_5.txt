5. "PICTURE YOURSELF A MONTH FROM NOW."
She moved people by relocating them in time. From inside the panic, every choice looks fatal. From a month out, it looks obvious.
Walk the buyer thirty days past the yes and let them describe what they see. Distance shrinks fear faster than discounts do.
Zoom out until the decision looks small.
