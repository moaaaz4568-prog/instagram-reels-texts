7. "WHAT ARE THE LAST TEN SECONDS OF THIS CALL?"
Reps rehearse openers and improvise endings, which is why so many calls die at "so yeah, let me know." The close of the call sets the speed of the deal.
Script the final line before you dial: the ask, the date, who sends what. End it on purpose. Every time.
Calls don't end. They're ended.
