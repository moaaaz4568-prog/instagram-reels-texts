6. "Take the weekend. I'd rather a yes on Monday than a maybe today."

Two years passed between the no and the record. Boras let time work for him instead of against him. The rep who needs the deal today gives it away to the one off the call on purpose. Set the date, name what changes by then, and let the buyer feel that you're not the one in a hurry.

Whoever needs it today pays for it today.
