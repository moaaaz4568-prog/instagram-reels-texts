3. He makes the stakes impossible to
ignore.

First prize a Cadillac. Second prize a set of
steak knives. Third prize you are fired. He
puts it on the board and lets it sit.

No motivation speech, no pep talk. Just a
clear line between winning and the door.
When the cost of not closing is real and
right in front of you, hesitation tends to
disappear fast.

Raise the stakes and watch the excuses
die.
