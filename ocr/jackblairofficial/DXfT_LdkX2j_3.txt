3. "I am not going to apologise for the value I bring. What I will do is show you exactly what that value looks like."

Vic Mackey never apologised for being effective and he never let anyone make him feel like he should.
This phrase does the same thing in a sales conversation without a single defensive word.

The buyer who was using the price as a character attack suddenly finds themself in a value demonstration instead of a negotiation.
You did not defend. You redirected to proof.
Proof is always more persuasive than justification and it never sounds desperate.
Redirect to proof when they attack the price. Proof closes what justification only prolongs.
