1. "Before anything, tell me what the last person promised you."

The families in Hinkley had been told for years their water was fine. Erin Brockovich started with what they'd been told, then let them say what it cost them. A burned buyer can't hear your pitch until the old lie is on the table. Ask for it first.

You can't sell over a scar. Name it first.
