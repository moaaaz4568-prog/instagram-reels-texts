5. "I am going to be straight with you. I did not reach out to everyone in your space. I reached out to you specifically."

Fury always made every recruit feel chosen for a reason that was specific to who they were.
That specificity was not flattery. It was intelligence deployed as positioning.

This phrase signals that the buyer was selected not swept up in a mass campaign.
The buyer who believes they were chosen for a specific reason stops feeling like a prospect and starts feeling like a candidate.
Candidates do not need to be closed. They need to be confirmed.

Make them feel chosen. Then show them the specific reason you came to them first.
