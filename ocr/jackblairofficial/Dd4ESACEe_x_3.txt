3. "Here's what I'm going to do, and here's why."

Explanation is the E. Nurses narrate the next step before they take it, in plain words, because a step you saw coming doesn't hurt the way a surprise does. Reps do the opposite. They hold the price and the process back and call it strategy. Tell the buyer what happens next before it happens, including the part where you ask.

Nobody flinches at a step they saw coming.
