5. "I am not going to push you on this. But I do want to leave you with one thought before we finish."

Soze never forced a conclusion in a room he had already arranged.
He planted the final piece and let the other side arrive at the outcome on their own timeline.

This phrase signals the close without announcing it.
The buyer relaxes the close because the pressure lifted and opens fully to whatever comes next.
What you deliver in that moment lands without resistance because the resistance just stepped aside.

Drop the pressure right before the most important thing you are going to say. The room opens completely.
