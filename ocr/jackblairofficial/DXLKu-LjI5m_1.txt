1. "I know I might not be who you expected on this call. Give me ten minutes and tell me if that still matters."

Senior buyers make a decision about a young rep before the second sentence.
They have been pitched by a hundred versions of you and they are already tired.

This phrase names the dynamic, owns it completely, and converts it into a challenge.
The buyer's curiosity opens the door their scepticism was about to close.
Christopher Moltisanti never waited for the room to decide he was ready. He made the room reconsider.

Own the underdog position. Then dismantle it from the inside.
