1. "You don't have to believe me. Believe the people in your position who already did this."

Edward Bernays never made the claim himself. He put it in the mouth of someone the audience already trusted, a doctor, a neighbor, a name with nothing to sell. A claim from a neutral third party outweighs any pitch from the seller.

You route your value through a real customer and a real result instead of asserting it. Proof from someone with nothing to gain lands where your pitch can't.

The claim someone else makes for you beats any claim you make.
