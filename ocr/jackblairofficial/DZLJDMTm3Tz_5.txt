5. "Look after your people and they'll look after your business."

Branson is famous for putting staff and customers first, convinced that happy people drive everything else.

He treated the relationship as the real asset, never the single transaction. Put the person ahead of the sale and their own. People feel whether you actually care, and they buy from, stay with, and refer the ones who do.

Care for the person and the business takes care of itself.
