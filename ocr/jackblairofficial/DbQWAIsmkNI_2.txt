2. "IS THERE ANYTHING ELSE ABOUT THAT?"
Grove asked it constantly, because first answers are
rehearsed and second answers are real. Buyers
hand you the polished version of every concern.
Ask this, wait, and the unpolished one arrives, the
one with the boss in it, or the last vendor, or the fear.
Nothing you could say retrieves more than this does.
One more beat of patience outsells an hour of
talking.
