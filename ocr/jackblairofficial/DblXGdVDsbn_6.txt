6. "WANT ME TO RUN YOU THROUGH IT AGAIN BEFORE THURSDAY'S MEETING?"
Loftus spent fifty years proving memory is not playback. Every retrieval is a rebuild, and the rebuild grabs whatever material is closest.
So be the closest material. A refresh the day before the meeting means the rebuild runs on your recap instead of on whatever a colleague said at lunch.
Fresh beats accurate. Be both.
