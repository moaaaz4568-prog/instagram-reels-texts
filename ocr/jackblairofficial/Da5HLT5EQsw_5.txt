That moment has a name. It's the close.

It lives in the gap between "I'm interested" and "I'll pay," where money, fear, and ego all arrive at once. Companies have already automated the ad, the email, the booking, and half the demo. The one piece still sitting in a human's hands is the last one. That isn't sentiment. It's just the part nobody has managed to automate yet.
