5. The 1099 Threshold Just Changed.

Old rule, clients had to send you a 1099 for any payment over $600.

New rule, that threshold just jumped to $2,000 indexed to inflation every year.

PayPal, Venmo and Stripe payments only get reported above $20,000 now.

But every dollar is still taxable.
The IRS doesn't forget even when nobody sends you a form.
