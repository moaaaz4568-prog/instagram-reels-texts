04. Breakup Message

"Write me 3 versions of a final re-engagement message for a prospect who has not replied to any of my message about [one sentence on your offer].

Each version must:
Be completely honest about the silence without guilt or pressure, offer one final piece of value or insight, and end by giving them a clear and easy way to either re-engage or close the loop for good.

Under 60 words each.

Tone: composed, respectful, completely unattached to the outcome.

No begging. No fluff."

"You stop chasing deals that are costing you more than they are worth.
The ones worth saving almost always reply to this one."
