3. "I have thought about this and I do not think the terms work for either of us the way they are structured."

Jax Teller always knew when the terms of a deal were going to create problems downstream.
He had seen enough bad agreements to recognise one before the ink dried.

This phrase signals strategic thinking rather than negotiation tactics.
The buyer hears a rep who is thinking about the long-term health of the deal not just the commission on the close.
The deal restructuring on honest terms is always worth more than the deal forced through on the wrong ones.

Say when the terms are wrong before they become a problem neither of you planned for.
