3. The comeback role is the wrong role.

The Singing Detective (2003) bombed.
Gothika (2003) bombed. Kiss Kiss
Bang (2005) bombed.

Downey took every role he could get.
None were the role that would change his
life.

The comeback role isn't supposed to be
the one that makes you. It's the one that
proves you can still be hired.
