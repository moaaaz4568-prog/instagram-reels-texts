Finding 4 | The Wait Tax
Cost: $0

I asked Claude what staying 3 more years
would cost me compared to switching now.

It compounded the gap, factored in raises I
wouldn't get, deducted the high ticket
income I'd never earn.

$612,000.

That's what loyalty to a comp plan that
capped me was worth in real money.

Most reps never run that math. The ones who
do don't stay long.
