2. "What do you think?"
Wintour is known for asking the question
early, then going quiet and letting the
other person fill the silence.
She learns the room before she ever states
her own position. By the time she speaks,
she knows exactly what she is dealing
with.
Ask first, listen hard, and you walk in
knowing. Walk in talking and you never
find out.
