3. "HERE'S THE PART OF THEIR PITCH THAT'S ACTUALLY TRUE."
The inoculation studies used real attacks, weakened, never strawmen, because fake threats build fake immunity. Same on a deal.
Name the rival's genuine strength, the thing they do beat you on, and watch your credibility spike on everything you say after. One honest concession buys ten believed claims.
The rival's best point is safest in your mouth.
