6. "Is it the way I said it, or is it the number itself?"

The fourth study asked whether the thing making people dig in. Across 18 question wording and correction, run on 1,333 people, no backfire showed up anywhere. So when your buyer pushes back, stop rephrasing it. Ask them straight which one it is.
