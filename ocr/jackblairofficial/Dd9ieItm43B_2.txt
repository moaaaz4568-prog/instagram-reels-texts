2. The small yes before the big one.

Once a buyer says something out loud, they act to stay consistent with it. The car lots got a small yes first and let the buyer's own words do the rest. I asked Claude for the ladder of small yeses before the price ever comes up. What came back:

Five rungs for a 20 person accounting firm: the cost of the problem ("fair to say nobody owns IT right now?"), the cost of leaving it, what they've tried, what fixed looks like, and when. The rung most reps skip is what fixed looks like. Skip it and the price lands on nothing.
