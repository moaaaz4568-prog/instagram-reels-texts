6. Don't get attached to any single deal.

Hanna treated every pitch like there were
a hundred more behind it, because there
were. No single client ever had power over
him.

When you are not desperate for this one,
you negotiate from strength on all of
them. Abundance is a posture the buyer
can feel.

One deal can't own you when you know
more are coming.
