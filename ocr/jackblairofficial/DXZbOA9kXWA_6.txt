6. "I am not going to walk you through every feature. I am going to show you the three that will change how you work."

Stark never buried the room in specifications when a demonstration would make the argument faster and better.
This phrase signals editorial intelligence and buyers respond to it with immediate respect.

You are not showing everything. You are showing the right things.
That discipline signals that you understand their world well enough to know what matters inside it.
The buyer who sees exactly what they need sees nothing they need to object to.

Show less. Land more. The focused demo always closes harder than the comprehensive one.
