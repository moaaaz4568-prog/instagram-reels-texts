4. 30 years of "waiting" is the apprenticeship.

Cranston started acting in 1982 at 26. The New Yorker called the next 25 years "scrabbling for guest-star roles on crappy TV shows while making his living in commercials."

When Walter White showed up at 51, he didn't need to learn how to act.

The years that look like waiting are the years that make the breakthrough possible.
