5. "I want to share something I noticed about how you have described this situation because I think it matters."

Littlefinger always used observation as leverage, not in a threatening way, in an illuminating one. This phrase signals that you have been listening at a level the buyer did not expect.

What follows it must be real, specific, and genuinely insightful about their situation.
When it lands correctly the buyer stops feeling managed and starts feeling understood.
A buyer who feels understood does not go looking for a different conversation.

Observe precisely. Reflect accurately. They feel seen.
The close follows the moment they feel seen.
