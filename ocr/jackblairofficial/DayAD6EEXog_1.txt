1. "You never open your mouth till you know what the shot is."

Roma's actual rule, word for word, and he says it in fury after the office manager blurts one helpful-sounding fact to his client, guesses wrong, and detonates the biggest deal on the board. One sentence, spoken blind, costs six thousand 1992 dollars and a Cadillac. Every call has a shot: who this person is, what they're afraid of, what tonight is really about. Find it before you speak. Speaking first is guessing out loud.

Talking before reading is how deals die.
