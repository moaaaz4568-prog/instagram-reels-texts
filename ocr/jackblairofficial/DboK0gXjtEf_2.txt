2. "NOW THE HARDER ONE. WHAT'S ACTUALLY
STOPPING YOU FROM DOING THIS?"
Oettingen's method is called mental contrasting. The
dream alone relaxes you. The dream held against
the obstacle is what produces action.
Follow the vision question immediately with the
obstacle question in the same breath. That pairing
is the whole mechanism, and most reps only ever
ask the first one.
Excitement with an obstacle is a nice
conversation.
