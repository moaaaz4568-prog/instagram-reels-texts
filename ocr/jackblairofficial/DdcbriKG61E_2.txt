2. "You're right to shop this. Who else
are you looking at?"

Boras took Soto to free agency in 2024
and at least five teams bid: the Mets, the
Yankees, the Dodgers, the Red Sox, the
Blue Jays. The record came from the
competition, not from one team's
generosity. Never dodge the competitor.
Name them, then ask what the buyer
would need to see to choose. Your job is to
be the option they measure the others
against.

Competition sets the price. Fear of it only
lowers yours.
