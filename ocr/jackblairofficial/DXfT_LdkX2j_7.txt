7. "I think we can get this done. But it needs to work for both of us or it does not work at all."

Vic Mackey never accepted a deal that only served the other side. This phrase establishes the non-negotiable condition of any close with complete calm and complete certainty.

The buyer who was trying to extract a one-sided concession suddenly has to consider what works for you as well. That consideration is the beginning of a real negotiation rather than a one-sided extraction.

The deal that works for both parties is the only one worth closing and the only one that holds after the signature.

Make mutual benefit the condition. The buyer who accepts it is the buyer worth closing.
