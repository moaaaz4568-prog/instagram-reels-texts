7. "You do not have to decide today. But I want you to know the window for this specific conversation does not stay open indefinitely."

Fury never pressured anyone into the room but he was always clear that the room had a closing time.
This phrase creates real deadlines and real urgency is the only kind that works on serious buyers.

It gives the buyer the making the cost of delay feel genuinely manufactured.
The buyer who needed time suddenly finds that the time they needed has a real edge to it.
Real edges close deals that manufactured ones never could.

Give them the choice. Make sure they understand the window that choice sits inside.
