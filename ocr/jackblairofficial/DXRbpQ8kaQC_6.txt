6. "I am going to make a suggestion and I want you to tell me if it lands correctly."

Patrick Jane never forced a conclusion. He offered one with the calm confidence of someone who already knew it was right.
This phrase signals precision without arrogance and invites the buyer to confirm rather than comply.
The distinction matters. A buyer who confirms your read feels smart.
A buyer who complies with your pitch feels sold to.
One of those buyers refers you to three more. The other one has buyer's remorse by Thursday.
Offer the insight. Let them confirm it. The close that follows is always stickier.
