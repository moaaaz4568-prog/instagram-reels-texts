4. "I have been in rooms far more complicated than this one and the answer was always simpler than anyone expected."

Reddington carried his experience like a coat, worn so naturally it never looked like a credential. Senior buyers close people who have clearly been somewhere and bring worth sharing.

This phrase signals a depth of experience without a single statistic or case study.

It makes the buyer feel that the complexity they were hiding behind is not as formidable as they thought. And a buyer who stops feeling overwhelmed by the decision starts making it.

Let your experience land quietly. The room hears it louder than anything you could announce.
