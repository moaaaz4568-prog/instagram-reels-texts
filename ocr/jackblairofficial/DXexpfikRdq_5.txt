5. "You have been a client I genuinely care about getting right for. I want you to know that has not changed."

Maguire fought for Rod Tidwell because he genuinely believed in him.
That belief was not strategic. It was real and Rod felt it and it changed everything.

This phrase reintroduces the human element to a relationship that has become purely transactional. The client who has been treating the relationship as a vendor arrangement suddenly remembers why they chose you. Genuine care expressed at the right moment is the most powerful retention tool available.

Say it and mean it.

The client who hears something real stops looking for a replacement.
