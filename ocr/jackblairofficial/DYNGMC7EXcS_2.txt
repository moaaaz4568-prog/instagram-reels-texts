2. One person can guarantee your bond.

In 2003 Downey couldn't get cast.
Insurance companies refused to cover
him.

Mel Gibson paid Downey's insurance bond
out of his own pocket so Downey could
star in The Singing Detective.

Downey's words: "He kept a roof over my
head and put food on my table."

One person willing to vouch with their
own money unlocks a door 100
institutions have locked.
