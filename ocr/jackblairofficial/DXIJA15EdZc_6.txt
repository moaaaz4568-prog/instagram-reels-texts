6. "What would make this the easiest decision you made this quarter?"

Most reps ask what it would take to earn the business.
That question puts you beneath the buyer before you finish the sentence.

This phrase is positioned at the same level.
It assumes the decision is coming and only asks what makes it simple.
The buyer answers and hands you the close without realizing they picked up the pen.

Ask from strength. Always from strength.
