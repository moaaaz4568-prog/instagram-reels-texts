The exit was the funding. Itzler didn't retire. He hired a Navy SEAL to live with him for a month. Wrote a book about it. Hit the New York Times bestseller list.

Most people sell a billion-dollar company and disappear. Itzler kept being the kind of guy who'd write 3,000 handwritten letters from his kitchen table.

The work was never about the money. It was about the discipline that built the money.
