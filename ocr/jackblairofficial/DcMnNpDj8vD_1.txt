FORTY YEARS, ONE PRICE

The combo launched at $1.50 in 1985. Four decades of inflation later it is still $1.50, and the company loses money on it on purpose.

It is not a menu item. It is a public promise that never moved, and every member has watched it get kept for decades.

A kept promise compounds faster than a margin.
