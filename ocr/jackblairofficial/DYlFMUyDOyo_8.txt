That carousel was built on direct sales. If you have done it, you know the trap. You recruit your own friends and family to survive. Your income resets to zero every month. And you never actually own the offer you are selling.

Remote high ticket sales fixes all three. The company books the calls, so you stop selling to people you know. You close $5K-$30K offers and keep 10-30% of every deal, so a few closes carry the month. It is done from a laptop.

Start it on the side while you keep your current income. Go full time once the math beats it. What you get on the other side is time freedom, location freedom and an income that scales with your skill. 👉
