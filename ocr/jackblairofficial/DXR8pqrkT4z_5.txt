5. "I am going to be completely transparent with you about where this went wrong and what I am changing."

Transparency in a broken deal is the rarest and most valuable thing a rep can offer.
Most buyers expect spin. Winston Wolf delivered clarity and the room always responded to it.

This phrase converts a trust problem into a trust opportunity by doing the one thing no one expects. Telling the full truth before the buyer has to ask for it.

A buyer who receives honesty they did not request stops looking for the exit.

Full transparency, delivered first, is the fastest way to rebuild a deal that trust broke.
