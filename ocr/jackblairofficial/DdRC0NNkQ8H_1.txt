1. "I've got it until Tuesday."

His actual line. When a collector asked if a piece was for sale, Gagosian would say yes, he had it until Tuesday. No pitch. Just a date. When your buyer says they need to think, give them a real one: the start date, the price hold, the last open spot. Only if it's true.

Open-ended offers get open-ended answers.
