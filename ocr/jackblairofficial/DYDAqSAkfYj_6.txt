6. The check that wasn't the right check.

Sly wrote Rocky in 3.5 days. United Artists offered him $325,000 for the script alone. He had $106 in the bank.

He said no.

He'd watched himself audition for 5 years and never close. The script was going to die without him in it.

A check for the wrong vehicle is just a different version of staying broke.
