4. "That was a yes with a but inside it.
Give me the but."

The conditional yes is on the list because it
lets a person agree and refuse it, in the same
breath. "Love it." "Great fit,
but budget."

The but is the real answer. Everything in
front of it was upholstery. Pull the but into
the open and work on something real.

Everything before the but was manners.
