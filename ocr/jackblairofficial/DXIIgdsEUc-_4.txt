4. URGENCY BUILDER

"I need to create genuine urgency for a prospect who keeps delaying. My product is [describe it] and the real cost of them waiting is [describe the consequence]. Write me 3 urgency statements I can use naturally in conversation. Each under 30 words. Ground them in reality, not my target. No fake deadlines. No fluff."

Urgency lands because it is true. The buyer stops stalling.
