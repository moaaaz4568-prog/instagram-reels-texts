DECISIVE.
Wintour is known for valuing fast, clear decisions
and saying so plainly. She has little patience for
endless deliberation.
She decides, and she moves. The speed itself is a
signal of confidence that the rest of the room follows.
Decide fast and the buyer follows you. Waffle and
you invite them to waffle too.
