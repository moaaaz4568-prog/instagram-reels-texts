5. "What would the other option have to get wrong for you to regret not choosing us?"
This phrase is quietly devastating.
It makes the buyer imagine a future where the competitor fails them, without you saying a single negative word.
They build the risk case themselves.
They name the thing they are most afraid of.
And now you know exactly which gap to fill for the rest of the conversation.
Ask the question that makes the buyer talk themselves into you.
