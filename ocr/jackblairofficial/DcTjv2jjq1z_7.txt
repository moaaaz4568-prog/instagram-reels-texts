7. “THIS DEAL ISN’T DYING. IT’S MID-STORY.”
Fixers exist because powerful people make a mistake a bad chapter for the ending. Pope’s job is holding the frame until the facts catch up.
Deals are the same. Procurement fights, security reviews, budget freezes: every big close has a chapter that looks like the end. The rep who narrates it as a step keeps the buyer walking.
Buyers borrow their confidence from yours. Stock up.
