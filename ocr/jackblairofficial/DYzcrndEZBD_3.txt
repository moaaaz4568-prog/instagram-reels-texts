3. “Interrupt, then give them a reason to stay.”

Blount's structure for a cold call that does not get hung up on.

A cold call is an interruption, and pretending otherwise insults the prospect. Blount owns it fast, then immediately earns the next 30 seconds with something relevant to them. No fake small talk.

Respect their time in the first sentence and they give you the second.
