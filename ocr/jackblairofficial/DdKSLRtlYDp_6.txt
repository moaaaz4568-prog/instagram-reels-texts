6. "Do I participate in F&I, and at what rate?"

The F&I office is the part of the store still growing. If you hand the customer to the box and your part ends there, you sell the half of the deal that is shrinking and someone else gets paid on the half that is not. Ask for the participation rule. Ask if it exists.

The growing half of the deal should have your name on it.
