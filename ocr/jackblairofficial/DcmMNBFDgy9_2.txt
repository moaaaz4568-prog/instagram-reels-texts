2. "ON A SCALE OF 1 TO 10, HOW READY ARE YOU?"
It's an odd question, and that's the point. Normal questions get autopilot answers. An unusual one makes them stop to and actually think.
Now they've put a real number on their own interest, out loud, to you.
Make them rate it. The number pulls the truth out.
