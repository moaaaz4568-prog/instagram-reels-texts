1. "WHAT MADE THIS THE RIGHT CALL WHEN YOU PICKED IT"
In 1990 Marilyn vos Savant answered a game show puzzle in her column: three doors, one the car, stick or switch. She said switch. Ten thousand readers wrote in to call her wrong.
Open by honoring the first pick. People defend a choice harder when it's attacked, so make their original decision smart out loud. A buyer respected for door one will actually look at door two.
Nobody switches while they're busy defending.
