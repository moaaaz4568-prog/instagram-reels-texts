SHE SOLD THEIR OUTCOME, NOT HER ACCESS
Her pitch to reluctant people was never about what
the interview did for her career or her network.
It was the same every time: your story, told whole, to
the biggest audience in America, while you are in
the room to tell it.
People say yes to their own outcome. Pitch that, and
the access takes care of itself.
