6. "I have put together something specific to your situation rather than a standard proposal. Can I walk you through my thinking?"

Generic proposals are how reps signal that the buyer is just a number.
This phrase signals the opposite before they have read a single line.

Nucky Thompson made every person in his orbit feel like the most considered person in the room.
That feeling is not charm. It is strategy delivered with precision.
A buyer who feels special does not go shopping for alternatives.

Personalisation is not a nice touch. It is the close before the close.
