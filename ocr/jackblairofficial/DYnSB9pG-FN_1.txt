1. In 1970 Sadow was hauling two heavy suitcases through an airport.

He watched a worker easily roll a heavy machine across the floor on a wheeled skid.

The idea hit him on the spot. Put wheels on the suitcase. He went home and built a prototype with casters and a strap.

The closer who turns his own frustration into a product owns a market full of people with the same frustration.
