6. "The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting."

The best close doesn't feel like a close.

Sun Tzu's highest principle was winning without force.

In sales that means getting so good at discovery and objection handling that the buyer talks themselves into yes.

You're not pushing. You're guiding.

The rep who masters this stops losing deals to resistance because they never create it.
