3. "WHICH PART OF THE DEMO CUT THE MOST WORK OUT OF YOUR WEEK?"
Loftus showed students films of car crashes.
Smashed pulled speed estimates to 40.5 mph. Hit pulled them to 34. Same crash, different verb, different memory.
Your follow-ups do the same thing. Write cut, killed, erased, whichever strong verb is true. The buyer will re-remember your demo at the intensity of your verb.
Buyers remember the verb, not the feature.
