1. "Before I go any further, help me understand how you like to make decisions like this."

Tom Wambsgans survived every room by figuring out the unwritten rules before anyone told him what they were. This phrase hands the buyer control while giving you the exact information you need to close them. You stop pitching to a type and start selling to a person. Every buyer has a process. Find it before you push.
