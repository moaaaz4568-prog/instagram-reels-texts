5. "I am not going to waste your time with a long pitch. Here is the one thing that matters most."

Desperation makes reps talk too much.
They fill silence with features because silence feels like rejection.

This phrase cuts through everything and signals control you may still be rebuilding inside.
The buyer respects the brevity. The focus lands harder than any full deck ever could.
You do not need to say everything. You need to say the right thing.

Precision looks like confidence even when confidence is still finding its feet.
