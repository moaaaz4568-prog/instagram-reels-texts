5. "I am not going to rush you. I do want to be transparent about what holds at this price and what does not."

Corleone understood that information, released at the right moment, shifts the entire room. This phrase uses transparency as leverage without ever sounding like a threat.

The buyer suddenly understands that waiting has a structural cost, not just a time cost. They are no longer negotiating against you. They are negotiating against the terms themselves. That is a completely different conversation, and one you are already winning.

Control the information. Control the negotiation.
