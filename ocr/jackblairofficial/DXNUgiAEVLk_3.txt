3. "I know we are not the biggest name you are looking at. We are the most committed one."

Tommy Egan never pretended to be something he was not.
He made what he was enough by owning it completely and without apology.

This phrase converts the size objection into a character advantage before the buyer can use it against you.
The biggest name is not always the most invested partner.
And buyers who have been burned by large indifferent vendors know exactly what that costs them.

Own the underdog position. Then make it the reason they choose you.
