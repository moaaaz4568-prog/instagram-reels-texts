5. "If this doesn't close today I completely understand. I just want to make sure you know what changes tomorrow."

Urgency without desperation. Logan Roy applied pressure the way gravity does, quietly, constantly, without ever raising his voice. This phrase creates consequence without threat and lets the truth do the closing for you. The buyer feels the weight of the decision without feeling pushed. That is the difference between pressure and force.
