3. One friend giving you their dinner money is the difference.

Sarah Paulson gave Pedro Pascal her per diem from acting jobs so he could eat through his 30s.

Her own words: "I would give him my per diem so he could have money to feed himself."

One friend kept him in the game.

You don't need a network. You need one person who refuses to let you quit.
