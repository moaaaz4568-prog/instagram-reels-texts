5. "IF THE ANSWER IS NO, SAY NO. I CAN HEAR IT."
Ghosting survives because saying no feels expensive. The posture that revives deals makes no sense. Half your ghosts are not gone. They are avoiding an awkward sentence you can write for them.
Make no cheap to say and yes gets honest.
