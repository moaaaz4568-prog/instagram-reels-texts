3. "I have a way of doing this that I do not compromise on. Here is what that means for you."

Omar never adjusted his code to suit the situation. The code was the constant and everything else adjusted around it. This phrase converts your consistency into a value proposition the buyer can feel before they have experienced it.

The buyer who hears a rep with genuine non-negotiable standards suddenly understands they are dealing with someone different. Different is always more memorable than polished. And memorable reps are always the ones who get called back when the budget opens.

Name your standards. The buyer who feels the consistency of them almost always builds their decision around it.
