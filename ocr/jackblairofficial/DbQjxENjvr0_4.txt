4. "AND HERE'S THE QUESTION THEIR REP HOPES YOU WON'T ASK."
McGuire called the mechanism refutational preemption. You raise the threat and defeat it while it's still small.
On a call this is the sharpest version: hand your buyer the one question that exposes the rival's weak joint. They'll ask it. People never waste ammunition someone trusted them with.
A borrowed question gets asked in every rival meeting.
