1. The desk job that pays the rent is the runway.

After St. Mary's College, Mahershala worked for SportsTicker, a sports data company. Stable. Decent pay. Soul-numbing.

He saved money. Paid down debt. Built a financial runway.

Then he applied to NYU Tisch grad school for acting at 27.

The desk job that pays the rent is what builds the runway for the pivot. The job is the funder, not the identity.
