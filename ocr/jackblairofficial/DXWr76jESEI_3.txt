03. Trigger Finder

"My prospect went cold [number] weeks ago
after [paste last known interaction or objection].

Based on this information about their role and company:
[paste any details you have],

identify 3 potential trigger events that may have happened
in their world since we last spoke
that would make my new offer more relevant right now.

For each trigger write the one re-engagement opener
that references it naturally.

Under 30 words per opener.

Tone: timely, specific, consultative.

No pitching. No fluff!"

You stop reaching out without a reason.
You start showing up at exactly the moment your offer matters most.
