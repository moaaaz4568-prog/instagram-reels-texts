"YOU MIGHT BE RIGHT ABOUT THAT PART."
One of the strongest receptiveness markers in the data is agreeing where you honestly can. Wikipedia editors who wrote this way took fewer personal attacks in disputes.
Find the piece of the pushback that is simply true and say so, with no but attached. The rest of your answer inherits that honesty.
Concede an inch of ground and your next mile gets heard.
