6. "You've seen every pitch there is, so I'm not going to give you one."

Konnikova's most quoted finding is the one reps ignore: the smarter someone is sure they can't be fooled, the harder they fall, because that certainty is the opening. Your most guarded buyer, the one who has heard it all, is not cracked by a slicker pitch. They are disarmed the second you set the pitch down and just talk to them like an adult.

The buyer certain they can't be sold is the one a pitch bounces off.
