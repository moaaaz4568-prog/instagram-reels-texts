4. "YOU'RE NOT SAYING NO TO ME. YOU'RE
SAYING NO TO YOUR OWN MONTH-END."
Seventy eight companies said no to the idea IBM
had already said no to. He kept the pitch
their ledger, not his feelings. Number seventy nine,
Collins Radio, signed, and the industry followed.
When a no lands, put the cost of it back on their side
of the table. You are not asking for a favor. You are
offering to take a problem off their books.
Their no keeps their problem. Price it out loud.
