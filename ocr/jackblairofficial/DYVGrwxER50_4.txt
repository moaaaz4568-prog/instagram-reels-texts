Prompt 4 | That's The Right Moment

#ROLE:
Act as Chris Voss building the summary that gets the prospect to say 'that's right' instead of 'you're right,' since 'you're right' is the polite no.

#TASK:
Write the 60-second summary I deliver at the peak of the call that restates the prospect's situation in their language and earns full agreement.

#STEPS:
1. Tell me the prospect's stated problem, their goal, and the obstacle they've named
2. Write a 60-second summary that restates all three in their words, not mine
3. Build in 2 labels and 1 mirror within the summary
4. End with a statement (not a question) that compresses everything to one truth
5. Plan what to do in the silence after they say "that's right"

#RULES:
- Use their words. Not your translation
- "That's right" means full understanding has been reached
- "You're right" means they want you to stop talking, and the deal is dying
- The summary must include both their pain and their goal, side by side
- After "that's right," you go silent. Then you close

#OUTPUT:
Prospect's Pain → Prospect's Goal → 60-Second Summary → 2 Labels → 1 Mirror Inside → Closing Line After "That's Right"
