2. "You're not buying what this did last year. You're buying what it does for the next five."

Boras prices the future, not the past. His record deals are built on projections of what a player will produce, because last season is already paid for.

Reframe your offer the same way. The cost is today. The output is years.

The past is already priced in. Sell the future.
