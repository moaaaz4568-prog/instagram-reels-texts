5. "I want to tell you something I do not say to everyone I speak with because I do not think everyone can use it."

Tyron Lannister on he was talking to feel chosen for the conversation they were having. That feeling of something exclusive is one of the most powerful closes available.

This phrase signals that what follows is rare, specific, and not for everyone. The buyer leans in because exclusivity triggers attention in a way that no pitch ever does.
What you say next lands ten times harder because you built the anticipation for it.

Make them feel like the insight was meant for them specifically. It will be.
