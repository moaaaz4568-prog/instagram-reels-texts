1. He survived the worst day imaginable and decided his life was a gift to use.

Nightingale was aboard the USS Arizona when it went down at Pearl Harbor, one of a handful of survivors.

He walked away certain he'd been spared for a reason, and spent the rest of his life trying to earn it. That urgency drove everything after.

Once you've seen how fast it can end, you stop wasting the time you've got.
