BEFORE I TELL YOU WHAT IT IS, LET ME TELL YOU WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU BY FRIDAY.
In the Depression, Elmer Wheeler ran a Tested Selling Institute that, by his own count, put 105,000 selling lines in front of 19 million buyers to see which ones actually moved product.
His most famous finding became the most quoted line in sales: don't sell the steak, sell the sizzle. Buyers don't buy the thing. They buy what the thing does to their week.
Nobody buys the steak. They buy the sizzle.
