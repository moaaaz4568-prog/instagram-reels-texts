7. "If I'm ever wrong, you'll hear it from me first."

The corporation hid what it knew until a file clerk dug it out. The antidote is promising the opposite, out loud, and keeping it. Make the bad-news-first contract explicit when you close, then honor it at the first hiccup. That's when the buyer decides you're different.

Deliver the bad news first and own the relationship.
