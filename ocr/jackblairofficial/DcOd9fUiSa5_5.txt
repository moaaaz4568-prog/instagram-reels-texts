SELL AGAINST THE CATEGORY FEAR
People did not doubt elevators could rise. They
doubted the fall. Otis sold against the one fear
freezing the whole category, not against rival hoists.
Name the fear that keeps your buyer in the old way,
out loud, before they raise it. The rep who owns the
fear owns the frame.
The category fear is the real competitor.
