4. Dropping your price in a down economy
is a trap disguised as a solution.

It feels like empathy.
It feels like meeting the buyer where they are.

It isn't.

It tells the buyer their hesitation was valid.
That the product wasn't worth what you asked.
That next time they should push back harder.

You didn't close them. You trained them.
The reps who lose them. You aren't heartless.
They just know confidence closes more than
a discount ever will.
