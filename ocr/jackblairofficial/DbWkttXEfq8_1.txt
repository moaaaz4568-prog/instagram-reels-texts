1. "BEFORE WE TALK NUMBERS, LET'S TALK ABOUT WHAT THIS IS FOR."
In 1947 Frances Gerety, a Philadelphia copywriter, stayed up late on the De Beers account and scribbled four words before bed: a diamond is forever.
The line never mentions carats or cost, and that's the instrument. Open every close above the product. Name the thing that lasts, the outcome, and price quietly becomes a detail instead of the decision.
Nobody haggles over what something means.
