5. "Nostalgia. It's delicate, but potent."

The opening line of his Kodak Carousel pitch. The pitch that made the room cry.

Don sold the slide projector by selling the photographs people had already taken with the family they had already lost.

The buyer paid for the camera. They were buying back time.

The closer who finds the buyer's grief sells the buyer's relief.
