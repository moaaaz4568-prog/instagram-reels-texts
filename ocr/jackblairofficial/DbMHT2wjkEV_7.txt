7. "IF THIS WERE A CON, WHAT WOULD IT LOOK LIKE?"
The boldest line in the set, and the safest one you will ever say.
A con cannot survive being named out loud. Saying it first tells a sharp buyer you are not running one, and it moves the two of you onto the same side of the question they were already quietly asking.
Name the con and you stop looking like one.
