2. "Your mind cannot tell a vivid rehearsal from the real thing."
Maltz had patients and athletes rehearse success in their heads and found the body responded as if it were real.
He proved imagined reps build real skill and calm. Run the call in your mind before it happens, the open, the objections, the close. By the time it is live, your nervous system has already been there.
Win it in your head and you arrive ready.
