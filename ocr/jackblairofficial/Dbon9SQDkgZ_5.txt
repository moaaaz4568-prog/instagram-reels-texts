5. "I'VE SAT WITH FOUR OTHER OWNERS ON THIS STREET. HERE'S WHAT THEY DID."
He worked one small town for fifty years. He was never prospecting strangers. He was the man who had already sat with the neighbours.
Use the density you already have. Name the rooms you have been in nearby, without naming names, and you stop sounding like a cold call.
Depth in one street beats reach across ten.
