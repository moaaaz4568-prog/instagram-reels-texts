1. He is always closing.
Blake walks into a room of broken-down salesmen and lays out the whole job in three words. Always be closing.
Not closing at the end. Closing in the hello, the small talk, the handshake, every second of it. The amateurs treat the close as a moment. He treats the entire conversation as the close, start to finish.
Every word either moves the deal or wastes it.
