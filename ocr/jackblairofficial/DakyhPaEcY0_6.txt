6. "If you had to bet real money this won't work, how much would you actually put down?"

This is Duke's most famous move, the quiet "wanna bet." The second certainty has to be backed with a stake, it drops to an honest number. Ask it lightly, never as a dare. Watch it into a real conversation about the odds.

Certainty shrinks the moment there's money on it.
