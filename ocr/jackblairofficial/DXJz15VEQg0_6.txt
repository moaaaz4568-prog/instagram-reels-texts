6. "I want you to take the time you need. I also want to be honest about what waiting typically costs in situations like yours."

This phrase gives permission and applies consequence in the same breath.
It is generous on the surface and precise underneath.

The buyer feels respected. They also feel the edge of something real. No ultimatum. No pressure. Just a calm, honest view of what the data shows.

Ricky Roma was never the loudest rep in the room. He was always the most certain.

Certainty delivered quietly is the most persuasive force in any negotiation.
