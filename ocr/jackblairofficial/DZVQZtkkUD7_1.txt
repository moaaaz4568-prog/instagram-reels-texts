1. He sold like he was talking to a friend, not a prospect.

Savage refused to use a script or a closing trick.

He spoke to every client the way he'd speak to a friend across his own kitchen table, which quietly disarmed people who'd walked in braced for a pitch. Trust is built in the tone, long before anyone gets to the terms.

Drop the salesman voice and people stop guarding their wallet.
