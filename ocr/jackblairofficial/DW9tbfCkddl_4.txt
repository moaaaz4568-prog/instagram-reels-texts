4. "What's the version of this that would make you feel completely comfortable?"

Not what would make it cheaper. Not what would make it easier.
What would make you feel completely comfortable.

Comfort is emotional, not logical.
This phrase hands the buyer the pen and lets them write the conditions of their own yes.

The close they design is the close they never back out of.
