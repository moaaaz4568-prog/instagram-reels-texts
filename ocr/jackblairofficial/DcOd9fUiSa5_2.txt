STAGE THE FAILURE YOU ARE SELLING AGAINST
At New York's Crystal Palace he rode his platform
up and had the hoisting rope cut before the crowd.
The brake caught him. Legend has him announcing:
all safe, gentlemen.
He did not show the ride working. He staged the
disaster failing. Put the exact fear your buyer carries
on stage and survive it in front of them.
Prove the fear wrong, not the feature right.
