6. He out-sold the company that trained him.

By 1949 the business Beveridge started in a shed was doing $35 million a year, passing Fuller Brush, the giant he'd left.

He'd taken the door-to-door model and improved the one thing that mattered: how the seller got in front of the buyer.

Don't out-work the leader, out-think the part everyone copies.
