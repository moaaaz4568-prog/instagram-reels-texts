2. "CAN I GET YOUR READ BEFORE YOU COMPARE NOTES WITH ANYONE?"
Noise audits force independent judgments before group discussion, because the first voice in the room drags every verdict after it.
Collect each stakeholder’s take one on one before the committee meets. You’ll hear three real opinions instead of one loud one wearing three heads.
Committees echo. Individuals tell you things.
