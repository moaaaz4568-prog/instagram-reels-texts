2. "How sure are you about that, one to ten?"

Duke trained herself to put a number on every belief, and she did it because a number exposes false confidence. A buyer who says "I'm not interested" and then lands on a six just handed you a maybe wearing a no. The distance between their number and ten is the whole conversation.

A real no is a ten. Everything else is an opening.
