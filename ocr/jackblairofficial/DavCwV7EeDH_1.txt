1. "Before we talk business, I did my homework on you. Not your company. You."

In 1947, no major corporation would advertise in a Black magazine. Ebony founder John H. Johnson wanted Zenith, so he studied its chairman and found the man's obsession: he'd trekked toward the North Pole, and his hero was Matthew Henson, the Black explorer who got there with Peary.

Johnson tracked Henson down before the meeting.

Study the person. The company signs later.
