3. "We have a few conversations at a similar stage right now. I want to focus on the one that moves."

This is not a bluff. This is positioning. Logan Roy never sat at a table where he was the only option and let the other side know it. This phrase reintroduces scarcity without aggression and reminds the buyer that your time has value. Scarcity is only a weapon when it sounds like a fact.
