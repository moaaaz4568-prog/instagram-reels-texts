6. "I need to be transparent with you about where this deal is and what happens if we lose it."

Gerri always told the truth to the people who needed it most even when the truth was uncomfortable for everyone in the room.
This phrase does the same thing internally and the effect is immediate.

The internal team that has been treating the deal as a low priority suddenly understands the real cost of that prioritisation.
Transparency about the commercial consequence of internal delay is the most powerful tool available for moving a stuck deal.
The organisation that understands what it stands to lose almost always finds a way to stop losing it.
Tell the truth about it the cost of internal delay. The team that hears it clearly almost always moves.
