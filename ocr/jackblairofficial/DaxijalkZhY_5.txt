5. "You don't need me to be the biggest player in this. You need me to make your side win."

Weeks after being let in, the nation with no leverage signed a secret defensive treaty with two of its conquerors, against the other two. The news alone forced the compromise. Nobody fired a shot, and France was a great power again.
