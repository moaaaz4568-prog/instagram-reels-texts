01. Reply Decoder

"I sent this cold outreach message: [paste your message].
My prospect replied with this: [paste their exact reply].

Tell me:
What their reply is actually signalling beneath the surface,
whether this is a genuine objection or a soft brush off,
and what my best next move is to keep this conversation alive.

Then write me the ideal response under 60 words
that moves toward a booked call without sounding pushy.

Tone: direct, warm, completely unbothered.

No pitching. No fluff."

You stop freezing when a reply lands in your inbox.
You always know exactly what it means and exactly what to say
next.
