1. "BEFORE I SHOW ANYTHING, WHAT ARE YOU ACTUALLY SCORING THIS ON?"
Daniel Kahneman's final book measured expert judgment and found scatter everywhere: underwriters at one firm, pricing the same risk, landed 55% apart. Their executives guessed the gap at 10.
His fix starts here: criteria before options. Get the scorecard stated while it's still abstract, because a scorecard written after the demo is just a mood wearing math.
A verdict without criteria is a coin flip with confidence.
