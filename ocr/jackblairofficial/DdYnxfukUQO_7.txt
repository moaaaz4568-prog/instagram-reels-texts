7. "Now that the ties are working, let me show you the suits."

In 1968 the tie accounts became suit accounts. By 1971 there was a women's line and a store on Rodeo Drive. The buyer who said yes once kept saying yes. The easiest sale you'll ever make is the second one to a buyer whose first one worked. Book it before you leave.

Now look at where all seven started. A drawer in a borrowed showroom, a box of ties, and a guy who could sell.

Sound familiar?
