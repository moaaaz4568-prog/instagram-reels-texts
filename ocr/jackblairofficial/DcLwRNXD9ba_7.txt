TIMING IS A GIFT. AMOUNT IS THE BUSINESS.

The plan never touched the price. The number held
while the calendar bent, and that line is what kept
flexibility from becoming discounting.

Concede calendars generously and amounts never.
The buyer who asked for time was not asking for
money. Do not volunteer it.

Bend the when. Hold the how much.
