4. He hired hungry rookies and trained them his way.

McGrath deliberately brought on inexperienced agents and built his team around his own standards.

He would rather shape raw drive than fix bad habits, so he built a culture from scratch. The right attitude beats a polished CV every time. Bet on hunger and coachability, then train the skill into them.

Hire the hunger, teach the rest.
