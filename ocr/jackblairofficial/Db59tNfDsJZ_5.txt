5. “HERE’S WHAT YOU STAND TO LOSE BY WAITING.”
Ziglar wrote this in 1984. Neuroscience proved him right in 2003.
Buyers are twice as motivated by what they might lose as by what they might gain.
Stop selling the benefit. Start naming the loss. That’s where urgency lives.
