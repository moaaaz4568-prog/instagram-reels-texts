6. "Can you get me an answer by Thursday, even if it's a no?"

The same researchers paid students five dollars to return a questionnaire. Given five days, sixty percent sent it back. Given three weeks, forty two percent. Given no deadline at all, twenty five percent. The more time people had, the less they did. A follow up with a no date is a follow up with no chance. Set a short date, make the no an allowed answer, and put it in both calendars before you hang up.

The more time you give a decision, the less it gets made.
