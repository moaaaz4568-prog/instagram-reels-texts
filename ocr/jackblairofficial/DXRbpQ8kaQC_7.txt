7. "Everything you have told me in this conversation points to the same answer. I think you already see it too."

This is Jane at his most precise and most devastating.
It connects every thread of the conversation into a single inevitable conclusion without a single moment of pressure.

The buyer reviews the call in their mind and realises you are right.
They did say all of those things. The answer was always the same.
You did not close them. You helped them see what the conversation had already decided.

Connect the dots out loud. The buyer who sees the pattern closes themselves every time.
