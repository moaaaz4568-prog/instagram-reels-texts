1. HAND THE QUESTION BACK.
1
A buyer asks, 'Does it come with support?' The amateur answers yes and keeps talking. The champion hands it back: 'Is support the piece that would make this work for you?'
The answer tells you what actually matters. Half the time it closes the deal for you.
A question earns more than a yes.
