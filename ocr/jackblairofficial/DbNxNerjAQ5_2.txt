2. "LET'S GET YOU SAFE FIRST. THE NUMBER
CAN WAIT."
The form's real trick is what it refuses to do. No
salvage price is argued at sea.
The work happens first and the number gets settled
later, in calm water. Copy the sequence when a
buyer is bleeding, prove the value, then price. Solve the emerg-
ency, then negotiate it once they can think. A panicked
negotiation costs whoever panics.
A price argued in a panic is always wrong.
