1. She printed her own authority.

Corcoran had eleven sales and no name.
She averaged their prices, called it The
Corcoran Report and mailed it to the New
York Times. The Times quoted it. I gave
Claude my numbers and asked for the
one page that makes me the expert. What
came back:

One page, three numbers from nine customers: 6.5 hours
saved a week, seven of nine paying late fees before, and a title,
The 2026 Restaurant Payroll Report. Claude's rule: never call it
a survey. Call it a report, and put the biggest number in the title.
