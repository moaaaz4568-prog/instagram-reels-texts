5. "I have done everything I said I would do. Now I need to know where you are."

John Wick operated on a code of complete follow-through and complete accountability. This phrase deploys that code in the close by naming your own delivery before requesting theirs.

The buyer who receives this cannot claim inaction on your side. They are now accountable for the only gap that remains which is their own decision. That accountability shift is the most powerful move available on a deal where you have done everything right.

Demonstrate your delivery. Then name the only thing left undone. It is always their decision.
