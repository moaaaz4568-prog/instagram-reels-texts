7. "This is the only conversation we need to have about this."

Walt was methodical. He never repeated himself.
And he never left a meeting without making it clear that this was the moment.

This phrase creates finality without ultimatum.
It tells the buyer that you are not coming back with a softer version of this.

The rep who treats every call like the only call always gets treated like the only option.
