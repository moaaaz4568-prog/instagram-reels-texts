4. The first time he closed a multi-million dollar deal, he kicked his leg over his head in the lobby.

Other brokers would have shaken hands and walked out professional.

Eklund made the closing a moment the buyer would never forget. The kick became his brand. Then a way buyers remembered him over every other broker on the island.

The biggest signal of confidence is the willingness to be remembered.
