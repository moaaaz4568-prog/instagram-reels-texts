1. "If we were fortunate enough today to find exactly what you're looking for would you be in a position to move forward?"

Hopkins calls this the most important question in selling.

Ask it before the pitch.

If they say yes, the close is already done before it starts.

If they say no you just saved yourself an hour of your life.

Qualify first. Pitch second.
Always.
