4. "I want to make sure that when you bring this to anyone else internally, you have everything you need to feel confident."

This phrase does something most reps never think to do. It arms the buyer after yours, the one that actually decides the deal.

Nucky understood that the person across the table was rarely the only one in the room. The decision lives somewhere else in the organisation and you need an ally who can close it for you. This phrase creates that ally and gives them the ammunition to win internally on your behalf.

The sale you do not see is always the one that decides the one you do.
