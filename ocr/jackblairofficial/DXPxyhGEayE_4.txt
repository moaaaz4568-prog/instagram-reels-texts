4. "I am going to make you one offer. It is a strong one. I would encourage you to take it seriously."

Cersei Lannister never came to a negotiation with a range.
She came with a position and the absolute certainty that it was the right one.

This phrase converts your offer from a starting point into a statement.
The buyer stops treating it as an opening bid and starts treating it as what it is.
One clear, confident offer delivered without flinching closes faster than any negotiation dance ever will.

One offer. Full conviction. Let the room decide if it wants to be difficult.
