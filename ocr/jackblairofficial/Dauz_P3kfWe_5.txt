5. "What would have to be true for this to become a yes?"

The morning Parker turned, he didn't say yes. He named a price. Be in Vegas tomorrow with a million dollars. A wall had just become a door with terms written on it.

Most stalled deals are walls nobody ever asked to price. Ask for the condition, and a vague no turns into a number you can work with.

A no with conditions is a yes with a price.
