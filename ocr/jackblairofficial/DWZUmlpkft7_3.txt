3. What your buyer sounds like
in a frozen economy.

"Let me wait until things settle down."
"Now just isn't the right time."
"Can we revisit this next quarter?"

These aren't objections.

They're a person who is scared
of making the wrong decision
at the worst possible time.

The rep who understands that wins.
The rep who argues with it loses.
