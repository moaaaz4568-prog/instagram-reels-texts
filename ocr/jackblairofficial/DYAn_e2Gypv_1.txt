1. Bill Porter started his day at 5:45 a.m.

It took him two hours just to get dressed. Cerebral palsy made his hands clench and his legs unreliable.

Then he'd bus to west Portland and walk 10 miles. Same route, same houses, five days a week. For 40 years.

The first move every salesperson can make is the one nobody wants to: just be there.
