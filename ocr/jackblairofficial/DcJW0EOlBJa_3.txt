THE BUYER GOT HIM, NOT A DECK

Airline presidents did not negotiate with Boeing. They negotiated with Tex, whose personal style of dealmaking is credited with securing the 737, 747, 757 and 767 programs.

Be the person, not the vendor. At every deal size, buyers commit to whoever shows up personally accountable for the outcome.

People do not shake hands with a company.
