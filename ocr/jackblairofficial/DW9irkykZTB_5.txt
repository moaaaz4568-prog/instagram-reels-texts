5. "I'm not going to respond to that right now."

The most disciplined phrase in this entire list.
And the hardest one for most reps to say out
loud.

When a buyer drops a low offer or a weak
objection,
not responding is the response. It signals you
are unbothered, unmoved, and in no rush.

Rhoades let bad arguments die in the room.
You can too.
