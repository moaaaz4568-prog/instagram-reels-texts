"TO BE HONEST WITH YOU..."
It's a throat clear, and buyers hear it backwards: so
what was the rest? One filler phrase quietly reprices
every sentence that came before it.
Say the uncomfortable thing without the drumroll.
Honesty announced is honesty doubted.
Flagging the honest part prices the rest of it.
