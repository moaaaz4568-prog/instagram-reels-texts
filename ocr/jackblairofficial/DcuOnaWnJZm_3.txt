3. "IF THIS SHOWED UP FREE TOMORROW, WOULD YOU ACTUALLY USE IT?"
People told pollsters they wanted a medium priced statement car. Then 1958 arrived, a recession bit, and America bought small, plain, and cheap. Stated love, absent wallet.
Free strips price out of the answer. A no at free means the problem isn't real. A yes at free means you're only negotiating value now.
If free gets a maybe, the deal was never alive.
