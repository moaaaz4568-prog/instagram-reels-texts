4. "CAN I TELL YOU HOW THE LAST HUNDRED OF
THESE ENDED?"
Forty years and four thousand cases give Piazza
something neither lawyer in the room has: the
pattern. He tests each side's story against how
cases like theirs actually end.
You've watched a hundred buyers stand at this
exact fence. Tell them where the ones who climbed
it landed, and where the ones who stood there
stayed. No push. Just the record.
Precedent moves people who won't be pushed.
