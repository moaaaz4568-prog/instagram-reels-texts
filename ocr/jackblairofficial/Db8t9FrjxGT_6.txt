6. THE POST-CALL DEBRIEF
"I just finished a cold call to a [job title] at [company].
Here's what happened: [paste quick notes]. Tell me
what I did right, what I missed, and one thing to try
differently on the next call. Score my performance 1
to 10. No sugarcoating."
The reps who debrief every call improve faster than
the ones who just dial again.
