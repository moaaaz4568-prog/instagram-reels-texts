3. OCT 26. TWO LETTERS, ONE WEEKEND.
B. He held the Navy back and let the line speak.
Ships stopped dead or turned for home. Then two
letters land. Friday, warm: missiles out for a no-
invasion pledge. Saturday, cold: your Turkey
missiles go too.
State a line once. Then let it work.
A. Answer the second. It is the latest position.
B. Answer the first. Pretend it never arrived.
