7. "Before we book anything, what's the part you're still not sold on?"

Ekman showed concealed emotions don't disappear. They wait. The doubt a buyer swallows on the call is the same doubt that ghosts you next week.

Pull it into the open while you're still in the room. A surfaced objection can be handled. A hidden one just votes no later.

The hidden no always votes. Surface it first.
