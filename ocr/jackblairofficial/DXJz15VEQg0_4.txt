4. "The people I work with who waited always tell me the same thing afterward. I wish I had done this sooner."

This phrase works because it removes you from the pressure entirely.
You are not pushing. You are reporting. The urgency belongs to someone else's story.

The buyer hears a pattern they do not want to become part of.
Nobody wants to be the person who waited and regretted it.
Roma never needed to ask for the close. He just made the alternative feel worse.

A story about someone else's regret is always more powerful than your best argument.
