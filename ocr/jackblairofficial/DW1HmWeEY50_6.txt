6. "You may not like me.
But you'll like what I can do for you."
Pete knew he was not
the most charming person
in the building.
He stopped trying to be liked
and started proving he was useful.
The rep who delivers results
consistently never needs
to be the most likeable person
on the call.
Results earn trust.
Trust closes deals.
