2. "WHAT'S CHANGED SINCE THEN
WASN'T TRUE ON DAY ONE?"
Her critics' mistake was treating the odds as frozen.
The host had opened a door, the information had
changed, and the numbers had already moved from
one in three to two in three.
Same on a call. The original vendor decision was
made with old information. Get the new facts on the
table in their words: the growth, the pricing, what
broke. You're not attacking the choice, you're
updating it.
A decision is only as fresh as its information.
