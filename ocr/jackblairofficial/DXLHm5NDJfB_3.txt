3. "If someone on your side raises a concern I have not addressed, I want to be the first call you make."

Blockers do their best work in rooms you are not in. This phrase puts you back in that room before you have left the building.

It tells your champion that you are available, prepared, and not afraid of the objection they have not told you about yet.
Most reps hand over a proposal and hope for the best.
Tom Hagen never hoped. He positioned.
The deal lives or dies in the conversation after yours. Be in it.
