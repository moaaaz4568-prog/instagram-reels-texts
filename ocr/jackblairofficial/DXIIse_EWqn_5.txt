5. "I want to be straight with you. I have another conversation starting this week in your space."

This phrase introduces consequence without ultimatum.
It is not a bluff. It is a boundary, delivered with calm.

Buyers who were stalling suddenly remember they have a decision to make.
The scarcity is real because your time is real.
Urgency manufactured from outside pressure is weak. Urgency is unbreakable.

The rep who is always available is always last on the list.
