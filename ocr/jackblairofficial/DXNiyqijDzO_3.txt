3. "Before you make any decisions about this, I want to make sure you have heard our side of it."

When a competitor gets into your account the buyer rarely tells you immediately.
This phrase is for the moment you sense something has shifted and you need to re-enter before the door closes.

It is calm, it is confident, and it signals that you expect to be part of the conversation.
Avon Barksdale never let a challenge to his territory go unanswered. Not once.
The rep who asks for the conversation always gets further than the one who waits to be told.

Sense the shift early. Move before it becomes a decision.
