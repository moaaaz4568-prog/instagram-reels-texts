YOU TOLD ME X LAST WEEK. THIS IS THE SAME
YES, ONE SIZE UP.
Two weeks after the sticker, the same billboard
request came back yes. Same
lawn, same eyesore, different person answering the
door.
Tie every ask to the yes before it, out loud. A buyer
who hears their own earlier decision repeated back
agrees with themselves before they agree with you.
The second yes is a rhyme, not a decision.
