5. He partnered with the right person
and trusted him.

Harvey built both Norman Ross and
Harvey Norman with Ian Norman, a fellow
door-to-door salesman he met on the job.

He knew he could not build an empire
alone, so he tied himself to someone
whose strengths covered his gaps. The
right partner multiplies what one person
can do. Surround yourself with people you
trust and it grows faster than you could
push alone.

The right partner doubles your reach.
