1. "I read up on your business before I dialed. Can I tell you what I think is going on?"

Her name is Mary Ann Tighe. At 36 she was an art historian with no business background, and Ed Gordon, a legend of the New York market, turned her down flat. So she wrote a magazine profile of him instead. It ran, and he hired her a month later: anyone who understood his business that well could sell it. Before your next call, learn the buyer's business well enough to say one true thing about it they haven't heard. Open with that.

Buyers don't want to be pitched. They want to be understood first.
