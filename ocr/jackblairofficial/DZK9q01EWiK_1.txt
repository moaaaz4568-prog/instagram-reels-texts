1. He knocked on every door and took every no.

Harvey started selling vacuum cleaners and fridges door-to-door for Goodwins of Newtown, facing rejection all day long.

He learned early that sales is a numbers game won by whoever keeps knocking after everyone else quits. Every shut door taught him something for the next one. Persistence built the foundation talent never could.

Keep knocking and the yeses add up.
