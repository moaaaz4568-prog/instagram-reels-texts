3. "I have done this enough times to know what you are thinking right now. Let me address it."

Pressure makes weak reps hesitate.
It makes elite reps decisive.

This phrase signals experience, confidence, and the ability to read the room accurately.
The buyer feels like they are talking to someone who has seen this play a hundred times.
Because you have. And that certainty is the most closing thing in the room.

When you already know the objection, name it before they can throw it.
