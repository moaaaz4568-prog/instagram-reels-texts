6. "Check in before you move on."
Richardson taught reps to test for
agreement at each step instead of racing
to the end.
A quick "how does that sound so far?"
catches doubt while it's still small and
easy to handle. Skip it and you arrive at the
close carrying objections you never saw.
Confirm as you go.
Check they're with you before you take
the next step.
