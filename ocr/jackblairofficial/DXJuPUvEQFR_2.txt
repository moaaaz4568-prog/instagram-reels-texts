2. "The company you are leaning toward is excellent. Here is the one thing they will not tell you."

This phrase is not an attack. It is a surgical move. It validates the competitor so you do not sound threatened, then pivots to a truth the buyer needs.

Buyers trust reps who acknowledge the competition honestly.

That trust is the exact currency you spend when you deliver what comes next.

One real insight about a gap they had not considered is worth more than any feature comparison sheet ever written.

Respect the competition out loud. Outmanoeuvre them in private.
