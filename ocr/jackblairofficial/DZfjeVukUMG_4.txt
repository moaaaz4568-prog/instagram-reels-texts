5. "Here is what we will do, and here is what we will not promise."

Sewell built trust by underpromising and then overdelivering.

He never oversold to land a deal. He promised a little less than he could do, then beat it. A buyer who gets more than they were told becomes a buyer who never checks a competitor.

Promise less, deliver more, keep them forever.
