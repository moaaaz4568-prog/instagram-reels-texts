"BEFORE WE TALK ABOUT THE MARKET, WHAT WOULD YOU DO IF IT WASN'T A FACTOR?"
Ryan Serhant's first day in real estate was September 15th, 2008, the day Lehman Brothers collapsed. He learned the market objection before he learned anything else, and he never once argued with it.
Take the market off the table for one question. The answer tells you whether you have a buyer or a spectator, and you find out in the first ten minutes instead of the fourth call.
The market is the reason people give, not the reason people have.
