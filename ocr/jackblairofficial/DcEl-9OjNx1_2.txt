THE MARKET MAP

"List the categories of companies that hire remote closers in [my vertical], what their sales cycles look like, and which are growing this year. Rank them by how often the seat leads to a bigger one."

The loudest job posts are rarely the best seats, and the best seats are filled by referral before a post exists.

The good rooms are mapped, not scrolled.
