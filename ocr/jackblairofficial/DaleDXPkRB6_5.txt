5. "I'll stop talking. What's your honest read so far?"

The study buried the hard close. In big deals, aggressive closing didn't just fail, it backfired and raised the buyer's guard. The winners talked less and let the buyer fill the space. When you feel the urge to push, hand them the silence instead. What they say next is the real state of the deal.

On a real deal, the one who talks least holds the most.
