1. "I'm sorry you've had to chase me on this."

Alison Wood Brooks sent a man into a train station on a rainy day to ask 65 strangers to borrow their phone. The version that opened by apologizing for the rain got more yeses.

She calls it the superfluous apology. You apologize for something that was never your fault.

Open on the reschedule. The wait. The four emails you never answered.

You are not admitting fault. You are taking their side.
