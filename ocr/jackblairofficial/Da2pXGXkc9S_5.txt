5. "You keep calling this a small thing. What's it costing to keep calling it small?"

People shrink problems with language, the way jargon shrank nuclear war to a code. Words like "minor" and "later" are anesthetic. Gently take the anesthetic away. When a buyer has to defend why it's small, they usually hear that it isn't.

The words they use to shrink it are the wall.
