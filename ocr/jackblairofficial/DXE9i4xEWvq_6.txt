6. "I want to make sure I've got this right before I say anything else. What I'm hearing is this."

Bunk never assumed. He confirmed. Then he acted. This phrase slows the conversation down at exactly the right moment and shows the buyer that their words matter enough to repeat. When you reflect their problem back accurately, they stop questioning whether you understand them. That trust is the bridge to the close.
