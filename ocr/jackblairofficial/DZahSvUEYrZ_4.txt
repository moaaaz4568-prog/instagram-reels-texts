4. "Answer the reason, not the symptom."

Sobczak hated canned objection rebuttals.

When a prospect pushes back, the words are just the symptom, so firing a memorized comeback misses what they actually meant, while one calm question uncovers the real concern. Diagnose before you respond.

Ask what's behind the objection instead of swinging at the surface.
