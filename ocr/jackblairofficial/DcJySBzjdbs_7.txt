NEVER OUT-PRESSURE A WOBBLE
When a signed buyer wobbles, pushing harder
hands them the outside story that finishes the
cancellation.
Reopen their reasons instead: ask what has
changed against what they told you this fixes. If
nothing changed, the belief restores itself.
You cannot pressure someone into believing. Only
into leaving.
