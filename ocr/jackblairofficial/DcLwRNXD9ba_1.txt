THE OBJECTION WAS A CALENDAR, NOT A NO
A reaper cost more cash than a wheat farmer would
see before the crop came in. Every no was the same
no: not until harvest.
Interrogate your stalls for the ones that are actually
timing. A budget cycle is not a rejection. It is a date.
Half of never just means not yet, priced wrong.
