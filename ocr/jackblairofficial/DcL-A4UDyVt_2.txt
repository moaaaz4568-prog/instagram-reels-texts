THE SCANNER FOUND THE FLIP
In 2004 neuroscientists at Baylor re-ran the challenge inside an fMRI. Blind, brains tracked flavor and leaned Pepsi. Told it was Coke, the memory and identity regions lit up and the preference flipped. The label did not change the story about the drink. It changed the experience of drinking it. The frame is not around the product. It is in it.
