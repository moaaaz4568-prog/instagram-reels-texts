4. "I know this is a hard ask. I am making it anyway because I believe it is the right one."

Roy Kent never avoided the difficult moment because it was difficult.
He ran toward it because running toward difficult things was the only habit that ever produced results.

This phrase signals courage in the close and courage in a rep is the rarest thing a buyer encounters.
The buyer who was expecting you to soften the ask suddenly respects the rep who did not.
Respect closes deals that likability cannot reach.

Make the hard ask. The buyer who respects you for making it almost always gives you the answer.
