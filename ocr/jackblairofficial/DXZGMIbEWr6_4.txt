04. Cross Channel Follow

"My prospect has not replied to [list the touches you have made and on which channels].

My offer is [one sentence].
The last thing I sent was: [paste or describe it].

Write me the next 3 touches across 3 different channels.

Each touch must use a completely new angle they have not heard from me yet
feel appropriate and native to the channel it is sent on
and move toward a reply or a booked call.

Under 50 words each.

Tone: fresh, relevant, completely unbothered by the silence.

No guilt. No fluff."

You stop repeating yourself across channels and calling it a strategy.
Every next touch now gives them a reason they have not had before.
