01. Voice Extractor

"I want to build a personal outreach voice that sounds like me at my best.

Here are 3 examples of communication I have written that I feel good about: [paste 3 emails, messages or LinkedIn posts you wrote].

Analyse these samples and tell me:
The 5 defining characteristics of my natural writing voice,
the words and phrases I use that are distinctly mine,
the tone I default to when I am not overthinking it,
and 3 things I should never do in outreach because they sound nothing like me.

Format as 4 labeled sections.

Tone of analysis: specific, observational, no generic descriptions.

Zero guesswork."

You stop writing messages that sound like they came from a template someone else built.
Every message from here sounds like the best version of you.
