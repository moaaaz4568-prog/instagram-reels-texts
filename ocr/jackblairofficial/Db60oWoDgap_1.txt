1. “HELP ME UNDERSTAND WHAT CHANGED SINCE WE LAST SPOKE.”
Most reps panic when a warm prospect goes cold. They discount, apologise, or disappear entirely.
This phrase does not chase. It commands an answer without sounding desperate. It reopens the door and puts the buyer back in the conversation.
Silence kills deals. This phrase kills silence.
