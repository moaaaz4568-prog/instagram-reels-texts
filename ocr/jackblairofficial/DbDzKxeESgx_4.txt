4. "Let me do something for you first.
Then I'll ask you for one thing."

The Treatment always arrived carrying
something the other man wanted, and
Johnson named the exchange out loud. It
was never a one-way ask.

You put a real concession down before the
close. The buyer feels the trade is fair, and
fairness does the pushing for you.

Give something real first and the ask gets
lighter.
