LOSS DOES THE CLOSING
Once the diamond lived in her house, the frame
flipped. The question stopped being should I buy this
and became can I live without it.
Build that flip into your deals: pilots, trials,
sandboxes, the contract marked up with their name
already in it. Make the walk away cost something.
People pay more to keep than they ever pay to get.
