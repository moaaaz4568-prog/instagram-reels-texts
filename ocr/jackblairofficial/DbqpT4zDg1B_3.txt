"PRICE IS ONE LEVER. WHICH OF THE OTHERS
ACTUALLY MATTER HERE?"
Bargaining in the bazaar ran on many axes at once,
not a single number moving up and down. Price was
one dimension among several being traded.
Put the other levers on the table yourself. Terms,
timing, scope, who you get access to.
A one lever deal only has a winner and a loser.
