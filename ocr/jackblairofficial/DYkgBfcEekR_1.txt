1. "Here is every question you are about to ask me. And here is my answer to each one."

The night before pitching the American Tobacco account, Duffy sat down and wrote out every question the client would ask.

Then he answered each one in advance.

He walked into the meeting and handed them the list. The client realized Duffy understood them better than they understood themselves. He won the account.
