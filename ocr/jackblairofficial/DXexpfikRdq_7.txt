7. "You stuck with me and I want you to know I do not take that lightly."

Maguire never forgot what Rod's loyalty had cost him and what it meant.
That acknowledgement of the client's commitment was the foundation of the relationship that followed.

This phrase is for the client who stayed through a difficult period and deserves to hear that it was noticed.
The client who feels seen for their loyalty does not go looking for alternatives.
Acknowledging what they gave you is the close that keeps the relationship permanent.

Acknowledge the loyalty. The client who hears it recognised almost never withdraws it.
