3. She protects her people so they never turn on her.

Pearson goes to the wall for the people around her, loyal to her, again and again.

She understands that the people you decide whether you rise or fall. By defending hers without flinching, she builds an army that stays through the worst storms. Loyalty earned in private pays off in public.

Guard your people and they guard you.
