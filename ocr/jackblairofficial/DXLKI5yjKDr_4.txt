04. Gap Finder

"Based on this information from my discovery call: [paste notes or key points], identify the 3 biggest gaps between where my prospect is now and where they want to be.
Then write one bridge sentence for each gap that connects their problem to my solution without pitching directly.
Tone: diagnostic, calm, solution-aware.
No pitching.
No fluff."

You stop pitching features. You start closing the distance between their problem and your answer.
