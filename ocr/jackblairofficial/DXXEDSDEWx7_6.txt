6. "I want to make something clear. I would not be in this conversation if I did not believe completely in what I am bringing you."

Burgundy's conviction was never manufactured. At some fundamental level he believed everything he said and that belief helped misplace was the engine of every room he ever commanded.

This phrase transfers that conviction directly into the buyer's evaluation of your credibility.

A rep who says this and means it is impossible to ignore and very difficult to say no to.
Genuine belief in your offer is the one thing in sales that cannot be faked well enough to matter.

Believe it first. The buyer always knows the difference between conviction and performance.
