2. “Never open with anything the customer can answer in one word.”

Friedman on the opening line.

“Can I help you?” gets “no thanks, just looking” every single time. Friedman trained reps to open with something friendly, interesting, and nothing to do with selling, so the customer actually talked back.

A yes-or-no opener buys you a no. Ask something they have to answer.
