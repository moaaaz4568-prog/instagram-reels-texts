1. "Before I pitch a thing, what's the part of this that's actually been a headache?"

Neil Rackham's team studied 35,000 real calls and found the reps who lose open by dumping features. The ones who win open by finding the problem. Features raise price resistance. Problems open the door. Kill the demo reflex and get them describing what's broken first.

Nobody buys the fix until they say the problem out loud.
