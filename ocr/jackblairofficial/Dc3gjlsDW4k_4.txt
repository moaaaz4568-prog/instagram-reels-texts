4. WRITE DOWN EVERY WORD.
4
Hopkins closed with a pen in his hand. When the buyer talked, he wrote. The pad slows the room, makes the buyer feel heard, and hands you their exact words.
The close comes back in their own language.
Nobody argues with their own sentences.
Their words close harder than yours.
