2. “The number one reason for failure in sales is an empty pipe.”
The line that opens his book Fanatical Prospecting.
He studied why reps blow up their careers. It was almost never skill. It was the quiet stretch where they stopped prospecting, coasted on current deals, and woke up to a calendar with nothing on it.
The empty pipe is silent right up until it is fatal.
