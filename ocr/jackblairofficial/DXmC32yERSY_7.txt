7. "I want to close the loop on everything internally so the buyer never feels our process on their end."

Gerri's greatest skill was making complex internal dysfunction invisible to the people outside it.
This phrase sets the standard that the best commercial teams operate to and the best reps enforce internally.

The buyer should never know that legal took three days to review the contract.
They should never feel the internal approval chain or the stakeholder who needed to be managed.
The rep who protects the buyer's experience from internal complexity is the rep who closes the deal and keeps the client.

Shield the buyer from the internal process. The experience they receive is the only one that matters to the close.
