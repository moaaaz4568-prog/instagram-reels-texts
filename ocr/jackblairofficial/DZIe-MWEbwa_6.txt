6. She always keeps one card no one knows about.

Pearson never shows everything she has,
even when she appears cornered.

She enters every fight with something
held in reserve, an option the other side
has not accounted for. That hidden card
turns lost positions into wins. The person
with one more move always controls the
ending.

Never play your last card until it is the last
card.
