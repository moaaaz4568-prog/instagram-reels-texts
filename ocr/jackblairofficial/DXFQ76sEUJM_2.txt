2. "What's the real reason this hasn't moved forward yet?"
McNulty always looked past the official version to find what was actually true. Most reps accept the surface answer and call it an objection. This phrase signals that you are not fooled and that you are safe to be honest with. The buyer stops reciting the script and tells you what is actually blocking the deal.
