2. "IF YOU DID MOVE AHEAD, WHEN WOULD IT START?"
The word if drops their guard. Nothing to resist, so they answer honestly.
But nobody answers a when question without running the movie of doing it, and that rehearsal is what moves behavior. They leave carrying a start date.
Every if quietly auditions a when.
