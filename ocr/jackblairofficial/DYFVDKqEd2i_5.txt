5. The vehicle was hiding in plain sight.

Boxing had been around Mike his whole life. Gyms in his neighborhood. Fights on TV. He'd thrown every day of his life since he could walk.

Nobody had ever connected the skill to the vehicle. Cus did in 10 minutes.

The vehicle that pays your skill is sometimes one room away from where you are. You just need someone to walk you to the door.
