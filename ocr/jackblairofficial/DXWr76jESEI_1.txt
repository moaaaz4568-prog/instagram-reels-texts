01. Revival Opener

"Write me 5 re-engagement opening lines for a prospect
who went cold after [paste last interaction or a prospect
My offer is [one sentence on what you sell].

Each line must:
Open with a completely new angle they have not heard from me
before,
reference something relevant to their world in 2026,
and feel like it was written today, not months ago.

Under 15 words per line.

No checking in. No following up language.

Tone: confident, fresh, completely unbothered by the silence.

No fluff. No guilt."

You stop sounding like the rep who never got the hint.
Every line you have now gives them a real reason to reply.
