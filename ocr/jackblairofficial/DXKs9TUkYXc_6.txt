6. "You might have heard this before. Nobody has shown you this part."

Shelley Levene knew how to find the angle nobody else was working.
When everything else was gone, the angle was still there.

This phrase resets a buyer who thinks they already know what you are selling.
It creates a gap in their certainty and fills it with your strongest card.
One thing they have not seen is worth more than ten things they have.

Find the one thing. Lead with it. Everything else is noise.
