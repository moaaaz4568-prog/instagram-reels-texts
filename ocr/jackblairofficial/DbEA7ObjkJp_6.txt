6. "Most of what we covered doesn't matter. Which one of these is the real reason you'd say no?"

Bernays cut through the surface. People give many small reasons to hide the one that actually governs the decision, and only that one that matters.

You stop chasing every objection and make the buyer name the single one that decides it. Answer that, and the smaller ones fall on their own.

Kill the real objection and the small ones fall with it.
