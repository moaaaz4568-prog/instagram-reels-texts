04. Borrowed Authority

"I am new to this role and do not yet have personal results to reference.

Write me 3 cold outreach messages for [job title] at [company type] in the [industry] space that borrow credibility from:
The company I represent: [paste relevant company proof points],
the results my team or product has produced: [paste any available proof],
and the industry pattern my offer is built around: [describe the broader trend].

Under 80 words each.

Tone: credible through context not personal history, direct, completely confident.

No pitching. No fluff."

You stop waiting to have your own results before you sound credible.
Your company's proof becomes your proof until yours exists to replace it.
