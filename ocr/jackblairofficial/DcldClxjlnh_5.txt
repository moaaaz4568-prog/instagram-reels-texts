5. "IT DOES ONE THING REALLY WELL. WANT TO SEE JUST THAT?"
An impressive demo puts the buyer back on defense. A modest one leaves them room to be impressed on their own, and that version sticks.
Show the one capability that maps to their biggest pain and stop there. Leave the rest for them to find and claim.
Buyers trust the value they find without help.
