7. "Strategy without tactics is the slowest route to victory, Tactics without strategy is the noise before defeat."

Most reps have tactics. Random tactics. Random tips. Things they read. Lines they half remember.

But no system. No daily practice. No way to know if they're actually getting better.

Sun Tzu would call that noise before defeat.

Strategy is knowing exactly what you need to work on.
