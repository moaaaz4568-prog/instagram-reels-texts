5. "What would you have to admit for this to be a yes?"

The move his show was built on. Law ten: you have to name it before you can claim it. Use it when they agree with everything and still won't move. The answer is usually "that I've wasted a year" or "that my guy isn't good enough." Let them say it. Then say nothing.

The admission is the close. The signature is paperwork.
