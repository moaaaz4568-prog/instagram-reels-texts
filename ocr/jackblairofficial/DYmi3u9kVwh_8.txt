If you have read this post all the way to here,
then you understand the value of the skill of
sales and how important it is to keep
improving it.

If you like sales, and you want to learn how
you can build yourself an extra $5K-$15K a
month, putting in 2-3 hours a day on the side,
through remote high ticket sales, there is a
path for it.

You take sales calls that companies book for
you. You close 10-30% of every deal.
Offers run $5K-$30K. Commission only. No
base. Done from a laptop. Start on the side,
then go full time once the math beats your
income, and you get your time and location
back.
