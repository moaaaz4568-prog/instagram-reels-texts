5. "I came from the cotton fields. I was promoted to the washtub. Then I promoted myself."

Chicago, 1912. The National Negro Business League would not put her on the program, so on the last day she stood up from the floor and said it anyway: from the cotton fields to the washtub to the cook kitchen to a factory on her own ground. When nobody introduces you, introduce yourself once, with your record, and sit down.

A credential is what you say when nobody says it for you.
