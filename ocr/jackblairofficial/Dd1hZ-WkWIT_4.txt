4. "Here's the version I wouldn't sell you, so you can see the difference."

When the second player was clearly worse than the Sonny, the walkouts fell to twenty four percent. A comparison the buyer can win makes the decision easy. So put the option you do next to the one you do, and say why it is wrong for them: the smaller tier, the cheaper competitor, the do it yourself route. You are not adding a choice. You are giving the choice a shape they can see.

A choice gets easy next to a worse one.
