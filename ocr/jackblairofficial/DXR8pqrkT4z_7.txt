7. "Here is what I am prepared to do to make sure this never happens again. I need you to tell me if that works."

Winston Wolf always left a room with a system in place, not just a solution to the immediate problem.
This phrase does the same thing and hands the buyer final approval over the resolution.

They feel in control of an outcome that was completely out of their hands.
That feeling of restored agency is what turns a rescued deal into a loyal account.
The buyer who helped design the fix almost never leaves.

Give them the resolution and the ownership of it. Loyalty is built in that moment.
