5. "The people I work with who are in your position tell me the same thing at this stage. Here is what changed their mind."

This phrase borrows credibility from people the buyer already respects.
It says without saying it that others like them, at their level, in their position, moved forward.

You are not asking the buyer to trust your experience.
You are asking them to trust a pattern you have witnessed.
That is a completely different ask and one a senior buyer can say yes to without feeling like they are taking a risk on a young rep.

Let the pattern make the argument. Stand behind it with confidence.
