6. He never stopped calling himself a salesman.

Hays ran a half-billion-dollar empire, and his business card did not say chairman or founder. It said one word, Salesman.

He lived by nothing happens until something is sold, and never pretended to be above the work that made him. The day you think you are too senior to sell is the day you start sliding.

Never get too important to do the thing that pays you.
