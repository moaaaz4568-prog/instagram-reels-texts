4. "If I move on the number, something else in the agreement has to reflect that. What would you like to adjust?"

Michael Corleone never gave anything for free.
Every concession had a cost attached. Every move had a counter.

This phrase keeps the value of your offer intact while appearing completely reasonable.
The buyer wanted a discount. Now they are restructuring a contract instead.
Most will pause, reconsider, and decide the original terms were fine.

Never discount. Trade. There is a significant difference between the two.
