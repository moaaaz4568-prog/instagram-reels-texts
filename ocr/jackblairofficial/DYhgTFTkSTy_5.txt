5. “Make the headline as long as it needs to be.”

His running argument with the clever ad agencies of this era.

His most famous headline was 12 words: “They Laughed When I Sat Down at the Piano. But When I Started to Play!” He hated short, clever headlines that won awards but sold nothing.

Awards sit on shelves. His piano ad ran for 40 years.
