7. For decades, Mehdi never had a single day without a policy sold.

Not a holiday. Not a sick day. Not a funeral.

He didn't believe in streaks. He believed in identity. The man who sold every day became the man who couldn't imagine a day without selling.

By age 80 he was still meeting 15 prospects a day, every day, by choice.

The discipline you choose at the bottom becomes the identity that carries you to the top.
