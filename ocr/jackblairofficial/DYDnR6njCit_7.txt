7. He wrote the number before he earned it.

1985. Broke. 23. Drove up Mulholland Drive at night and parked.

Wrote himself a check for $10 million for "acting services rendered." Dated it Thanksgiving 1995. Folded it into his wallet.

10 years later he was paid exactly that for Dumb and Dumber. He put the original check in his father's casket.
