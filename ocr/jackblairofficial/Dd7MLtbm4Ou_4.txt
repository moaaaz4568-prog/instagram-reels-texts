4. "Does the idea make sense to you?"

The looping question. When an objection lands, the Straight Line doesn't answer it head on. It steps back to the one question that tests logical certainty, then resells the piece that came back short. "I need to think about it" almost never means thinking. It means one of the tens is low. Loop back, raise it, then ask again.

An objection is a smoke screen. Loop, don't argue.
