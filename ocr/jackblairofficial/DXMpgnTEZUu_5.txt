5. "I think you already know this is the right move. What is the one thing still sitting with you?"

This phrase surfaces the final hidden objection with warmth instead of interrogation.
The buyer who was carrying something privately finally has permission to say it out loud.

Nucky Thompson never let an unspoken concern survive long enough to kill a deal.
He created the conditions where people told him the truth before it became a problem.
One honest answer here is worth more than an hour of pitching into silence.

Find the last thing. Solve it. The deal closes itself.
