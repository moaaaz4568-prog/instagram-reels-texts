7. Twenty-two months at Taft
Correctional Institution in California.

He owed $110 million to 1,513 individual
victims. The court required him to pay 50%
of every dollar he ever earned again, for
the rest of his working life.

He shared a cell with comedian Tommy
Chong, who told him to write the book.

The lesson: the bottom isn't the end of the
story. It's the only place quiet enough to
figure out what the next chapter is going
to say.
