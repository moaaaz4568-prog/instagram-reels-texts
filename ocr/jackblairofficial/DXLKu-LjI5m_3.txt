3. "I am going to tell you something the last three reps you spoke to probably did not say."

This phrase creates a gap in the buyer’s certainty before you fill it.
It signals that what comes next is different, specific, and worth hearing.

Christopher Moltisanti always knew that being newer in the room meant he had to say something the room had not heard.
That is not a disadvantage. It is the sharpest edge a hungry rep owns.
The buyer who has heard every pitch is waiting for the one that surprises them. Be that one.

Say the thing nobody else thought to say. That is where attention lives.
