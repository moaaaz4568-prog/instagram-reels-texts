4. "Something shifted in your market this week and I thought of you immediately."

This phrase works because it makes the outreach about them, not you.
The buyer does not feel followed up on. They feel considered.

Tie it to real news, a real shift, a real trigger in their world.
Now you are not a rep with a quota. You are a resource with timing.
That is a completely different conversation.

Relevance is the only follow-up strategy that never gets ignored.
