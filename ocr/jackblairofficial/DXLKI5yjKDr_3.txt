03. Pain Amplifier

"My prospect mentioned this problem during discovery: [paste what they said].
Write 3 follow-up questions that go deeper into the business impact of that problem without sounding like an interrogation.
Each question must make the cost of inaction feel more real than it did before.
Under 20 words each. Tone: empathetic, precise, unhurried.
Zero guesswork."

Surface pain is easy to ignore. Real pain closes deals.
Now you know how to find it.
