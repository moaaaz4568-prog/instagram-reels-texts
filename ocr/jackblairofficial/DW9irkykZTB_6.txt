6. "Before you answer, I want you to be sure."

This phrase does something almost no rep ever does.
It slows the buyer down before they give you a reflex no.

Rhodes never wanted a fast answer. He wanted the right one.
Because a buyer who is sure says yes once and means it.

Slow the close and it sticks. Rush it and it falls apart by Friday.
