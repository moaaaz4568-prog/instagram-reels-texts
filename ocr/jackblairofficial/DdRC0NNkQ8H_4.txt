4. "Honestly, the cheaper option won't get you there."

He told the painter Cecily Brown her work was priced too low for his customers, and pushed her prices up. Serious buyers read a low price as a warning. When yours reaches for the smallest package, hold it against the goal they gave you. If it can't get them there, say so.

A serious buyer hears cheap as risk.
