7. "WHERE ARE YOU ON IT NOW, VERSUS TWENTY MINUTES AGO?"
The canvassers closed every conversation by re-measuring, out loud. Not for their clipboard. For the person.
A position spoken in your own voice sets harder than one you privately drifted into. So make your buyer say where they've landed. Whatever moved, they heard themselves move it, and that's the version they'll repeat tomorrow.
Change said out loud is change that holds.
