3. "WHO HERE IS GOING TO TRY IT THIS WEEK?
HANDS UP."
The discussion ended with one question and a show
of hands. Lewin found that raising a hand in front of
the neighbours did more than any fact in the lecture.
End the meeting with a visible commitment, not a
summary. Ask who is doing what by when, and ask
it where the others can see the answer.
A hand raised in front of the room is worth more than
a yes said to you alone.
