3. ANNOUNCE THE LAST CHANCE OUT LOUD.
Fair warning, he said whenever the hammer got
close. It told the room the door was closing and gave
every buyer a clean moment to act.
A whispered deadline feels like a trick. An
announced one feels like respect. Say plainly when
the window shuts and let the clock work.
Real urgency is spoken, not sprung.
