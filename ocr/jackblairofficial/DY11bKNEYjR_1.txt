1. “Outwork everyone. It is the one edge nobody can take.”

Pacetta's first rule, drilled into every rep he led.

He could not promise his reps the best territory or the lowest price. He could promise that if they outworked the competition, the math eventually broke their way. Effort was the one input fully in their control.

Talent is uneven. Effort is a choice you make every morning.
