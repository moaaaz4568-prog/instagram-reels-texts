04. Decision Mirror

"Write me a closing summary I can deliver verbally to a prospect who has heard my full pitch on [product or service].
The summary must restate their problem in their own language, connect it directly to my solution, and end with one closing question.
Under 80 words total.
Tone: consultative, warm, certain.
No pitching. No fluff."

The buyer hears their own problem solved back to them. The close becomes inevitable.
