5. The soap opera is the apprenticeship.

From 2008 to 2011, Margot did 200+ episodes of the Australian daytime soap Neighbours. Unglamorous. Modest pay. No critical attention.

But every day was a rep. Camera technique. Memorizing pages. Holding a scene under pressure.

The unglamorous gig is the apprenticeship that makes you ready for the real vehicle.
