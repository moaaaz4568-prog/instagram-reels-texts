3. "I might be reading too much into this. When that happens, what does it do to the rest of your week?"

Implication questions. The hardest to ask, and the ones that separated the top performers in large sales. They take a small admission and make the buyer see its size. Follow the problem downstream: time, people, money, the meeting they had to sit through because of it. Let them do the counting. Your job is the next question, not the total.

The cost the buyer says out loud is the only cost that exists.
