THE RIVAL IS BLAME, NOT THE COMPETITOR
The line worked against faster and cheaper rivals
because a mainframe that failed was survivable if it
said IBM and career ending if it said anything else.
Map every deal for the blame channel. Who explains
this purchase if it goes sideways, to whom, in what
meeting. Sell to the scapegoat.
Every big deal has a second buyer: the scapegoat.
