02. Re-Engagement Writer

"Write me 3 re-engagement messages for a prospect who went silent after [paste last interaction or stage they reached].
My offer is [one sentence on what you sell].
Each message must open with a new angle, add a piece of value or new insight,
and end with one low-friction question that is easy to reply to.
Under 60 words each. No checking in. No following up language.
Tone: confident, relevant, not desperate.
Zero guesswork."

Your ghosted deals get a real second chance. Not a follow-up. A reason to reply.
