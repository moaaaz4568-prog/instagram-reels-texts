THE 42 HOUR FUNERAL
In 2011 HBR published an audit of 2,241 US companies. Average response to a fresh web lead: 42 hours. Nearly a quarter never responded at all.
Read that as your competition's obituary and your opening. The bar in most markets is not sharp. It is absent.
You are not losing to better reps. Just faster ones.
