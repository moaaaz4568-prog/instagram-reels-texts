4. "I MIGHT BE WRONG HERE, BUT YOUR CURRENT SETUP LOOKS EXPENSIVE."

The disarmed buyer has no script ready for a hard observation delivered gently. The guard drops for you stays down while it lands.

Underplay the delivery, never the observation. Say the sharpest thing you know in the plainest clothes you own.

Soft package. Hard truth. That is the trade.
