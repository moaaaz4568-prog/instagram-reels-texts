7. "When this conversation is over I want us both to be satisfied with how it ended. Here is how we get there."

Cersei always gave the other side a door that preserved their dignity.
She understood that a buyer who feels defeated does not stay closed.

This phrase brings the negotiation to a close while giving the buyer a version of winning they can live with.
You are not taking on your terms, with enough grace that the relationship survives the deal.
The close that respects the buyer is always the one that holds after the signature.

Win the negotiation. Let them feel good about how it ended. Both things are possible.
