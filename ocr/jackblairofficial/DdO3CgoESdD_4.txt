4. "What's the payoff for staying where you are?"

Law three again: people do what works. Nobody stays in a broken setup for no reason. There's a payoff: comfort, control, not having to explain a change upstairs. Ask it with real curiosity, no trap in your voice. Once the payoff is on the table you can price it against the cost they gave you. Now they're choosing, not stalling.

Every stall has a payoff. Find it or lose to it.
