7. "If I do this right, it won't feel like I sold you anything. That's the point."

Bernays believed the most powerful persuasion is invisible. The moment it looks like selling, resistance climbs, so he removed every visible fingerprint of the pitch.

You strip the pressure, the closing lines, the performance, and just help the buyer see the situation clearly. The less it feels like a sale, the faster it closes.

The best close never feels like one.
