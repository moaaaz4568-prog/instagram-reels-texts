THE LINE BUYERS WROTE THEMSELVES
1
Through the 1970s, purchasing departments repeated one sentence: nobody ever got fired for buying IBM. IBM never printed it. It grew on the buyer's side of the table.
That is the tell. The strongest sales copy in B2B history is not about the product. It is about the buyer surviving their own decision.
Buyers don't buy outcomes. They buy alibis.
