5. "Before you walk, let's list everything you'd be walking away from."

At the brink, Holbrooke's team made two giant posters in block letters: everything the Bosnian delegation had already gained and would lose if the talks failed. On the wall, in writing.

When your buyer wavers late, itemize the stack: the pricing, the terms, the work already done. Make the loss visible.

Nobody reads the cost of walking until you write it down.
