And when something rare becomes the bottleneck, the price explodes.

Closers don't get a salary, they keep a cut of every deal, often 10 to 20 percent of $5,000 to $30,000 offers. The company gladly pays it, because half of a closed deal beats all of a dead one.

That's why remote closers pull $10K to $25K a month. They're not paid for time. They're paid for the one thing nobody else can do.
