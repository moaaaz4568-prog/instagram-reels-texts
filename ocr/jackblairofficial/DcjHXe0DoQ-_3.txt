3. THE CAP TOLD HIM WHAT HE WAS WORTH.
In 1961 IBM put a ceiling on sales commissions. Its best closer suddenly had a number he could not sell past.
He did the math eventually does.
If the room limits the upside, the problem is not the skill, it is the room. Feeling capped is data. Read it.
A capped closer is a resignation letter in progress.
