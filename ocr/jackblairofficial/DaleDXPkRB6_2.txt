2. "What's that actually costing you, past the obvious?"

This is the move that separated the top performers in the whole study. They asked roughly four times more of these than average reps. You make the buyer trace the real cost of the problem until it quietly outgrows your price. They name a small annoyance. You walk it downstream to the hours, the churn, the deals it kills.

A cheap problem gets ignored. Show them the full bill.
