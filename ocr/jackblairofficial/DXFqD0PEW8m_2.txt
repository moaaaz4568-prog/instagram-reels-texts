2. "I've looked at your situation carefully and I genuinely believe this is the right move for you."

The word genuinely only lands when it is true. Kendall at his most powerful was the version who actually believed in what he was saying. This phrase positions you as an advisor who has done the work, not a rep who needs the commission. Conviction is the one thing you cannot fake for long. Mean it or don't say it.
