BEWARE THE BUYER WHO FEELS TOO SEEN
The effect runs both ways. When a prospect says this is exactly what we needed after one call, you may be hearing your own astrology: agreement that fits any vendor.
Test the yes the way you test a lead: make them say what specifically fits, in their words, with their numbers in it.
A yes that fits everyone belongs to no one.
