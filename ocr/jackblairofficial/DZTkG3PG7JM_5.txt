5. He turned his sellers into owners.
Hays handed real ownership to the people
who sold for him, building profit sharing
and employee ownership into the
company.
He knew a seller with a stake outworks a
seller with a wage. By giving his people
the upside, he built a force that treated
the business as theirs, because it was.
Share the win and people fight for it.
People protect what they own, not what
they rent.
