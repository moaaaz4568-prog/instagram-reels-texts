2. "It's not a problem. It's a negotiation."

Goodman treats every dead end as a door he has not opened yet.

Where others see a wall, he sees terms that have not been set. That mindset keeps him calm and creative when everyone else panics. Refuse to accept the first no and there is almost always ways a deal hiding behind it.

Every no is just an opening offer.
