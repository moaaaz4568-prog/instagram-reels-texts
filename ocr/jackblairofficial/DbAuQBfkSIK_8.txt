For four centuries the toughest room in
Europe put a professional skeptic against
every single case, with sainthood on the
line, and only the claims that survived him
became untouchable.

You run that same gauntlet every week:
the skeptical CFO, the procurement
grinder, the committee you never meet.
Surviving scrutiny is the skill, and almost
nobody prices it.

There is an industry that does. Companies
book the sales meetings on your calendar,
you take the call, your case survives the
room, all from a laptop. 👉
