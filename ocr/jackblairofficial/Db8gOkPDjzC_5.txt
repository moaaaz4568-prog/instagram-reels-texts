5. "ASK THEM ONE QUESTION, WHAT HAPPENS WHEN IT BREAKS?"

Every competitor sells the best-case scenario.
This phrase forces the buyer to imagine the worst case with someone else. Support. Accountability.
What happens at 2am on a Thursday when the thing stops working.
You're not attacking the competitor. You're exposing the gap they hoped no one would notice.
