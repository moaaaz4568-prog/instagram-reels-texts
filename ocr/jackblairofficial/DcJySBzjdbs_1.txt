THE $1 GROUP CONVINCED THEMSELVES
In 1959 Festinger and Carlsmith had students do a mind numbing peg turning task, then paid them $1 or $20 to tell the next student it was fascinating.
The $20 group knew exactly why they lied. The $1 group had no excuse, so their minds quietly decided the task really had been interesting. Belief grows where outside justification runs out.
People believe what they cannot otherwise explain doing.
