6. ART'S MOST FAMOUS SPEECH WAS
DELIVERED IN 1987 TO A ROOM OF RELIGIOUS
BROADCASTERS.
The title: "Just Do It."
A man with a pacemaker telling 800 strangers that
the only difference between the people who make it
and the people who don't is the willingness to start
before they feel ready.
He repeated the line throughout: "Just do it. Just do
it. Just do it."
