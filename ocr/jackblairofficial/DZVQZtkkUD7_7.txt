7. He measured success by who he kept, not who he closed.

Savage's clients stayed for decades, and so did the people who worked for him.

He knew a closed sale is a moment but a kept relationship is an annuity, so he poured his energy into the years after the signature instead of chasing the next stranger.

Anyone can win a client, the pros are the ones who never lose one.
