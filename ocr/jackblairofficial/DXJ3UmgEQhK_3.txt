3. "Before I tell you what we do, can I tell you what I noticed about your business?"

Most reps open with a pitch.
The buyer hears it as noise because it is about
the rep, not about them.

This phrase reverses the entire opening
dynamic.
You are not presenting, you are diagnosing,
and that is a completely different power
position.
The buyer leans in because nobody does their
homework anymore.

Walk in knowing their world. Leave knowing you own the next
conversation.
