3. "I hear you on the price. Walk me through what you are comparing us to."

When a buyer says it is too expensive, most reps defend the price immediately.
Defending without understanding is the fastest way to lose the frame entirely.

This phrase buys you intelligence before you spend any position.
Nine times out of ten, the comparison is flawed, incomplete, or based on something entirely different.
Now you are not defending your price. You are correcting their maths.

Never argue about price until you know what price they are actually arguing about.
