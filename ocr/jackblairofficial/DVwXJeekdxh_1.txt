1. Automakers quietly stopped building affordable cars.

A decade ago you could buy a new Jeep for $17,000.

Today Jeep's cheapest model starts at $30,000. The top end hits $65,000.

They didn't raise prices. They eliminated the lower tier entirely.

The average American can no longer afford a new car.

That's your buyer's financial reality right now.
