1. What made you comfortable?

O'Leary doesn't ask what the competitor offered or what they charge. He asks what made you comfortable with them. Comfort is the real reason and the weakest one to defend. I gave Claude my offer and the three competitors I lose to and asked for the question that turns their quote into a comfort blanket. What came back:

Three questions, one survivor: "What did they say that made you comfortable?" On a strip-mall roof the owner answered "they can start Monday." Claude's note: he just told you the real criterion is speed, not price. The follow-up: "And what happens to that start date the first time it rains?"
