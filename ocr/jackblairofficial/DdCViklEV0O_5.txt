5. "IT'S CALLED YOUTH-DEW. THE NAME DOES HALF MY JOB."
She named products like promises. Youth-Dew was a bath oil that doubled as perfume, priced so a woman could buy the indulgence herself instead of waiting for a gift, with the result written into the name.
Put the outcome in the offer's first line. When the name of the thing carries the result, every mention of it re-pitches the buyer.
Products get compared. Promises get bought.
