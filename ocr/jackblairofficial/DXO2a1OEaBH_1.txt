1. "I am not going to argue with you. I am going to show you something and let you decide what it means."

Tyron Lannister never fought a room that was already armed against him. He disarmed it first, then made his move before anyone realised the dynamic had shifted.

This phrase removes confrontation entirely and replaces it with curiosity.
The sceptical buyer stops preparing their next objection and starts evaluating what you are about to show them.
You never entered the fight. You changed the game before it started.

The rep who refuses to argue always has more room to close.
