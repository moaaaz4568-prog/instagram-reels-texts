5. "If this were handled, what would that actually be worth to you?"

This is the need-payoff question. Instead of you listing am proved it is far stronger to make them say the value out loud.

When the buyer describes the upside in their own words, they are no longer being sold. They are selling themselves, and nobody argues with their own conclusion.

Let them say the value. They'll believe it then.
