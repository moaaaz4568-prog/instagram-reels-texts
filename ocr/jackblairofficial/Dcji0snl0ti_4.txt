4. HE BORROWED HIS METHOD FROM BEN FRANKLIN.
A mentor pointed him to Franklin’s autobiography. He copied the system: one skill drilled at a time.
One week on enthusiasm, one on questions, one on remembering names. Rotating deliberate focus beats trying to fix everything at once forever.
Improve one thing a week. Repeat forever.
