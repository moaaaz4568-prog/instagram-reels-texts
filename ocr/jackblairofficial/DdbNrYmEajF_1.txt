1. "You keep ordering the small one. What's it for?"

In 1981 he was running U.S. operations for a Swedish coffee maker company when a small Seattle coffee company kept ordering his plastic cone filters. He flew out to see who was buying. It was Starbucks. Within a year he worked there. Your best lead is already buying from you in a way that doesn't add up. Ask about the odd order before you pitch anything. The order tells you what the buyer hasn't.
