6. "This is hard for about three weeks. Then hard becomes your normal."

Escalante's room ran on ganas. The sign over his blackboard said desire because he knew the work stops feeling like a burden once it becomes identity. His students didn't endure calculus. They adopted it.

Forecast the adaptation, not just the effort. Buyers quit when hard feels permanent. They push through when hard has an expiry date.

Hard is a phase. Quitting makes it permanent.
