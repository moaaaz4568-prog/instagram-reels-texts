02. Ask Architect
"Write me 3 ways to ask an existing client for a referral or introduction to [job title] at a [company type] in the [industry] space.
My relationship with this client is [describe relationship briefly]. The result I have delivered for them is [one sentence on the outcome].
Each ask must:
Feel completely natural and unforced,
reference the result I delivered as the reason for asking,
and make saying yes feel like the easiest thing in the conversation.
Under 60 words each.
Tone: warm, direct, confident without being presumptuous.
No pitching. No fluff.
You stop avoiding the referral ask because you do not know how to phrase it.
Every version you have now feels like a conversation not a request."
