4. "WHAT'S THIS SITUATION ACTUALLY LIKE,
DAY TO DAY?"
Grove's deepest tool was the metaphor question.
People explain what they can't articulate by
comparing it, and the comparison carries the truth.
Herding cats means no authority. Flying blind means
no data. Treading water means no progress.
Whatever picture your buyer reaches for, sell into
the picture. It's more honest than anything on their
requirements list.
Buyers tell the truth in comparisons.
