4. "CAN I TELL YOU WHAT THE CHEAP VERSION OF THIS USUALLY COSTS PEOPLE?"
Washington's commuters saved their dollars that morning and walked past the best free concert they'll ever get. The discount was real. So was what they missed.
Concede the cheaper path exists, then itemize its bill: the redo, the lost quarter, the deal that leaked out the side. Cheap is never free.
Free was the most expensive ticket in the station.
