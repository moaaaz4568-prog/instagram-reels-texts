2. "WHOSE PERMISSION ARE YOU WAITING FOR?"
Her floor was full of killers who still waited to be told they could act. Naming the wait breaks it. Nobody outranks the person deciding.
Buyers stall the same way. Ask it gently and the invisible boss in their head steps into the light.
The wait is the objection. Name it.
