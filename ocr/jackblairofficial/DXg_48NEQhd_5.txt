05. Gone Cold Reviver

"A prospect who replied positively to my outreach has now gone silent.
Our last exchange was: [paste the conversation].
My offer is: [one sentence].

Write me 3 re-engagement messages I can send across [choose: email, LinkedIn or phone] that reference our previous conversation naturally, add one new piece of value or insight they did not have before, and end with one question that makes replying feel easy.

Under 60 words each.

Tone: warm, fresh, completely unbothered by the silence.

No guilt. No fluff."

The warm conversation that went cold just got a real reason to come back.
Not a follow up. A reason.
