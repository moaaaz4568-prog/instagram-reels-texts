6. ASK WHICH, NEVER WHETHER.
'Do you want to move forward?' invites a coin flip.
'Would Tuesday or Thursday suit you better?' offers a choice between two yeses.
Hopkins called it the alternate advance. Two real options, and the buyer keeps the feeling of control the whole way down.
Whether is a trap. Which is a door.
