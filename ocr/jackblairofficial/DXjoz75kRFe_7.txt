7. "This is the deal. I want to close it today. Tell me what we need to do to make that happen."

Logan Roy Year One never waited for the room to offer him the deal. He named it, owned it and moved it to close before anyone else had decided it was time.

This phrase is the close that assumes completion and invites the buyer to participate in the final step. The buyer who was still deliberating suddenly finds themselves in a conversation about how to finish rather than whether to start.

The assumed close delivered with total calm and total conviction closes faster than any soft ask ever written.

Assume the close. Name it. Ask for the final step. The buyer who was ready will always take it.
