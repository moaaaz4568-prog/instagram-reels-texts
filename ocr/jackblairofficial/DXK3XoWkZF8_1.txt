1. "Before I tell you anything about what I do, I want to understand what you are actually dealing with."

Vito Corleone never opened with what you needed, or what he wanted. He opened with what you needed, and the room felt that difference immediately.

This phrase signals that this call is not like every other call the buyer took this week. You are not presenting. You are listening. And the rep who listens in a world full of pitches becomes the only one the buyer remembers.

Ask before you speak. Every time. Without exception.
