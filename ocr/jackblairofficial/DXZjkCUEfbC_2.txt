02. Talk Track Builder

"Build me a cold call talk track for a [job title] selling [product or service] to [ICP] in the [industry] space.

Include:
One attention-grabbing opener under 10 seconds,
one bridge sentence that connects their world to my offer,
two discovery questions that uncover real pain,
and one soft close that proposes a next step.

Format as 5 labeled sections.
Under 30 words per section.

Tone: conversational, direct, peer to peer.

No pitching. No fluff."

Your cold call has a structure that builds toward the close before the prospect realises the conversation has already started.
