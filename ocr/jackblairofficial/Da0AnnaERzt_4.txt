4. "Here's exactly what the next ninety days look like if we do this."

The next stage is the tale. Konnikova found belief is the tale. Konnikova rides not on the pile of evidence but on the coherence of the story the mind builds around it. The con hands you a fake story. You hand them a true, specific one. A vague better future moves no one, with dates and steps, is the thing they replay after the call.

People don't buy the facts. They buy the story the facts add up to.
