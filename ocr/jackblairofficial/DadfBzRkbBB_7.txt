7. "Beauty fades. Dumb is forever."

The title of her second book. Her point:
charm is a depreciating asset. Judgment
isn't.

Sales has its own version. The natural
talker who never learns structure gets
outearned, every single year, by the rep
who studied the craft.

Rapport gets you the conversation. Skill
closes it. Put your hours into the one that
compounds.
