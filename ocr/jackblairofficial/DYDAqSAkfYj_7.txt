7. The deal that paid the skill.

Sly took $35,000 for the script. The lead role. 10% of the profits.

Less money up front than he'd been offered. The most leverage he'd ever held.

Rocky earned $225 million. Won Best Picture. He bought Butkus back for $3,000 the year it came out.

The vehicle that pays the skill what it's worth is worth more than any check that pays you to walk away from it.
