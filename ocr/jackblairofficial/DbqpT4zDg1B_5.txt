"WHAT DID THE LAST ONE GET WRONG?"
In a repeat market the seller's standing came from history. Buyers returned to whoever had not disappointed them, and they remembered exactly how the others had.
Their answer is the specification for keeping them. Write it down and repeat it back before you talk about anything you sell.
The last seller's mistake is your instruction manual.
