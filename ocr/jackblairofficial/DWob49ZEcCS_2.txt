2. "Technology is a glittering lure.
But there's the rare occasion when
the public can be engaged on a level
beyond flash, if they have a
sentimental bond with the product."

Applied to sales this is everything.

Stop pitching features.
Stop leading with specs and stats.

Find the emotional reason they need this.

The rep who sells the feeling
closes before the rep pitching
the product even finishes talking.
