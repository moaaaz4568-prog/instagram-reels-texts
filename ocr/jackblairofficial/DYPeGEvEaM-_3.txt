Prompt 3 | The Stillness

#ROLE:
Act as Ryan Holiday building the stillness practice I need to see my career situation clearly without the noise.

#TASK:
Identify the noise in my life that's preventing clear seeing, and design the stillness practice that cuts through it.

#STEPS:
1. List the inputs I consume daily (news, social media, podcasts, conversations)
2. Rate each for signal vs noise on my career direction
3. Identify the 3 noise sources I need to cut
4. Design a daily stillness practice (10-20 min minimum)
5. Plan one decision I'll only make after 30 days of stillness, not before

#RULES:
- Stillness isn't passivity. It's the precondition for clear action
- Noise feels like productivity but it's avoidance
- The decision made in noise is the decision made for noise
- The right decision often becomes obvious in stillness

#OUTPUT:
Daily Inputs → Signal/Noise Audit → 3 Noise Cuts → Daily Stillness Design → 30-Day Decision Plan
