3. "Shake with your right hand.
But always know what's in your left.

Here is what I'm prepared to offer.
And here is what I need in return."

The reciprocity close Frank used
on everyone he ever negotiated
with.

Generosity is a power move
when you know exactly
what you expect back.

Give something. Ask for something.
Close on the exchange.
