6. "So here is exactly what I'm asking you for."

Spence asked juries for the verdict in plain words. The exact decision. The exact number. No lawyer mumble.

Most reps run a strong call and then blur the ask, hoping the buyer closes themselves. The deal dies in the mumble.

Say the ask in one plain sentence and then stop talking. Clarity at the ask is the whole job.
