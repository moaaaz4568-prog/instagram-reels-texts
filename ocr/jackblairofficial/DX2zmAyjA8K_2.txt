2. THE PATTERN

What it does: surfaces the repeating
mistake across multiple lost calls so it stops
being a one-off.

"Now run that same diagnostic
across these 4
other calls I lost: [PASTE TRANSCRIPTS].
Identify the pattern. What am I doing in every
