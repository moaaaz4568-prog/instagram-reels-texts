7. "No pressure to move today. I've got what I need to point you right."
Scharff's prisoners never felt the extraction happen. He never celebrated, never pressed, never let the moment feel like a win over them.
End calls the same way. Calm, unhurried, in control. The buyer who never feels worked is the one who comes back and signs.
If they never feel sold, they stay sold.
