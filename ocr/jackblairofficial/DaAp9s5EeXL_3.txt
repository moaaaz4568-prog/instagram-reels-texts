3. "And when that happens, what does it cost you down the line?"

This is the implication question, the heart of what Rackham found. Average reps stop at the problem. Closers make the problem grow.

You walk them from a small annoyance to the real, stacking cost of leaving it alone. The pain they into is the pain they will pay to end.

Don't fix a small problem. Reveal the big one under it.
