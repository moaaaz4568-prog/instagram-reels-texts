5. "Here is what I have seen work for people in exactly your situation."

This phrase is three things at once.
It signals experience, it personalises the solution, and it removes the pressure of a pitch entirely.

You are not selling a product. You are sharing a pattern.
The buyer is not being presented to. They are being guided.
Guidance from someone who has seen the problem before is the most valuable thing a trusted advisor offers.

Position yourself as the person who has already solved their problem for someone else.
