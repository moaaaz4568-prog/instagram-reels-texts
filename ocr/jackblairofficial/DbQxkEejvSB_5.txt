5. "WHICH OF THOSE WOULD ACTUALLY STOP
YOU?"
Johnson showed answers compete for the same air,
and worries are no different. Most of what a buyer
lists is noise that was surfaced because you asked.
This question makes them rank their own list, and
rankings are brutal. Watch four objections collapse
into one, and now you're solving a single problem
instead of defending a pitch.
Make the objections compete. Most of them lose.
