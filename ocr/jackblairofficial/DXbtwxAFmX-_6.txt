6. "What I am proposing is not a small commitment. I want you to know I would not be asking if I did not believe you were ready for it."

Fury never recruited anyone by making the mission sound easy.
He made it sound significant and then made the recruit feel significant enough to handle it.

This phrase converts the size of the commitment from an objection into a compliment.
The buyer who was intimidated by the decision suddenly feels honoured to be considered for it.
That emotional shift from intimidation to aspiration is where every big close becomes possible.

Make the commitment feel like an honour. The buyer who accepts it on those terms never looks back.
