1. "I know this is not where either of us wanted to be. Let me tell you exactly how I am going to fix it."

Winston Wolf never wasted time on apology when action was what the room needed.
He assessed, he committed, and he moved before anyone else had finished processing the problem.

This phrase signals to a frustrated buyer that they are now talking to someone different.
Not a rep managing damage. A person with a plan.
The buyer who was about to walk out stops and listens because nobody else offered them a direction.

In a broken deal, a clear plan is worth more than any amount of sorry.
