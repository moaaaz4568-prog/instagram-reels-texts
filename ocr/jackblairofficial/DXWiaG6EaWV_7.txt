7. "I am going to leave you with one thing. Whatever you decide, decide it knowing you had access to the best option available."

This is Plainview's final move, the statement of total conviction delivered so cleanly. It carries it into every other conversation they have about this decision.

It does not pressure. It simply establishes a standard against which every other option will be measured. The buyer who hears this and feels the weight of it does not choose the alternative lightly. They choose you or they spend the rest of the process wondering if they should have.

Plant the standard. Let every competitor be measured against it while you are not in the room.
