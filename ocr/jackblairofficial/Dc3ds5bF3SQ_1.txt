1. "I BUILT THIS BECAUSE THE OLD ONE MADE ME FURIOUS."
Mangano was raising three kids and mopping her floors with a mop that wrung out into her hands. She did not research a market. She fixed the thing that made her angry every Saturday.
Open with the irritation your product exists to end, in one plain sentence, said like it happened to you. Buyers trust irritation over analysis, because irritation is not a pitch.
Nobody sells a fix for a problem they have never felt.
