5. "I have everything ready to move on my side. The only thing left is your decision."

Bauer always made it clear when the obstacle was no longer on his side the operation.
This phrase does the same thing with complete calm and complete accountability.

The buyer who hears this cannot blame process, preparation or the rep for any further delay.
The only remaining deciding variable is their own decision and naming that variable removes every excuse for avoiding it. A buyer who owns the delay almost always resolves it faster than one who can still point at someone else.

Name whose move it is. The accountability shift alone closes deals that weeks of follow-up could not.
