3. "IF THIS WORKS, YOU'LL FORGET THE PRICE.
IF IT DOESN'T, PRICE WAS NEVER THE
PROBLEM."
In 1939 about one American bride in ten got a
diamond. By 1990 it was eight in ten, and not
because diamonds got cheaper.
Time-shift the conversation. Put the buyer twelve
months out and ask which they'd remember, the
invoice or the result. Whoever answers honestly has
just told you what the objection was really about.
Doubt talks about money because it's embarrassed
to talk about itself.
