01. Objection Map

"List the 10 most common objections a [job title] selling [product or service] hears from [ICP] in the [industry] space. For each objection write one sentence on the real fear driving it underneath the surface. Format as a numbered list, one objection then one insight per line. Tone: direct, sharp, no textbook language. No fluff. No guessing."

You stop reacting to what they say. You start responding to what they mean.
