7. “I DON’T NEED YOU TO CHOOSE ME. I NEED YOU TO CHOOSE THE RIGHT. THOSE JUST HAPPEN TO BE THE SAME THING.”
Arrogance with a safety net.
This phrase sounds selfless but it positions you as the only correct answer. The buyer feels respected. The competitor feels irrelevant.
O’Leary never tore down the opposition. He made them invisible by making himself inevitable. That’s displacement.
