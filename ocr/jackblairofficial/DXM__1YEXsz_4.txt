4. "I am not going to let the noise around this deal change what I know is right for you."

Pressure makes most reps bend toward whatever they think the buyer wants to hear. Ghost never let outside pressure contaminate the quality of his decisions.

This phrase signals that your recommendation is based on conviction, not circumstance. The buyer hears someone who believes completely in what they are offering. That belief, delivered without flinching, is the single most persuasive force available on any call.

Conviction under pressure is the close most top reps never make because they neveromer built it.
