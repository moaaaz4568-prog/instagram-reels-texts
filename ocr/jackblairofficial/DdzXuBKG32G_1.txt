1. The ruler.

"Michael Pantalon, a Yale researcher, moved emergency room patients who had already said no by asking them to rate their own readiness out of ten, then asking why they hadn't picked a lower number. I gave Claude my offer for the ruler and asked for the back:

The ask, buried in his own words with his calendar in it, never announcing a scale. A different follow up for a 2, a 5 and an 8, and the line for a zero: "You showed up anyway. What made you keep it?"
