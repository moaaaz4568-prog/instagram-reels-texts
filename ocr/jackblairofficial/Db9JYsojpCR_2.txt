2. "PEOPLE WHO WORRY ABOUT PRICE END UP PAYING TWICE."
This one lands like a verdict.
It reframes cheap as expensive. It makes cutting corners feel reckless. The buyer stops thinking "Can I afford this?" and starts thinking "Can I afford not to?"
Fear of loss always beats the promise of savings.
