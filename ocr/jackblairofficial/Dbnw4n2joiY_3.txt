3. "WHICH PART OF THIS STILL FEELS LIKE A FIGHT TO YOU?"
Mediators never tackle the whole war. Piazza narrows every case to the two or three issues actually in dispute and lets everything else fall away as settled.
Most stalled deals are ninety percent agreed and one hundred percent stuck. Make the buyer point at the live issue, then move everything else into the signed column.
You can't settle a fight you haven't found.
