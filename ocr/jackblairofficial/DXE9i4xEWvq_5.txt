5. "You mentioned something earlier that I think is really important. Can we go back to that?"

Bunk paid attention when everyone else was just waiting to talk. This phrase proves you were listening, elevates something the buyer said, and makes them feel like the smartest person in the room. Buyers buy from reps who hear them. Not reps who perform at them. Go back. It is always worth it.
