5. "Don't take my word for anything. Here's how to check me out."
Mitnick's entire run worked because nobody verified who was on the phone. He proved most people never check.
So flip it. Hand over the references, the receipts, the ways to verify you, unprompted. Scammers dodge scrutiny. Inviting it puts you in a different category on the spot. Frauds never do.
Invite the background check.
