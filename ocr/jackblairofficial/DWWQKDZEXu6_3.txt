3. The reps quietly dominating right now all share one trait.

They stopped trying to overpower the objection.
They learned to outlast it.

When a buyer hesitates, they slow down
while everyone else speeds up.

They ask one question that makes the buyer
articulate their own fear out loud.

The moment a buyer hears their own fear
spoken back to them is when the sale turns.

Most reps never get there.
