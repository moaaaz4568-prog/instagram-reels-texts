6. "Nothing you said earlier locks you in.
Where's your head at today?"

Thompson's rule: when ego is on the line,
people fight facts to save face. A buyer
who said no publicly stays no to protect it.

Hand them a clean exit from their old
answer. Most stalled deals aren't dead.
They're just embarrassed.

Leave them a door to change their mind.
