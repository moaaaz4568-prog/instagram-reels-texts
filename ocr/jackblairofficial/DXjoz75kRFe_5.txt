5. "I am going to ask you for something that most people in my position would not ask for yet. Here is why I think it is the right time."

Logan Roy never waited until he had the conventional right to ask for what he wanted.
He asked from a position of clarity rather than a position of authority and the room gave him what he asked for.

This phrase signals confidence in the ask before the ask has been fully earned.
The buyer who was expecting a soft close gets a direct request that treats them as a decision-maker worth respecting.
The rep who asks clearly for what they want at the right moment almost always gets it.

Ask for what you want directly. The buyer who respects the ask almost always gives it.
