1. "Self-discipline. Do the hard thing whether you feel like it or not."

The trait Iannarino puts at the top of his list.

He argues motivation is unreliable and feelings come and go. Self-discipline is doing the prospecting, the follow-up, the hard call, on the days you do not feel like it. That is most days.

Discipline is just doing it when the motivation did not show up.
