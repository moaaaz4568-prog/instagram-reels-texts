1. "I am not following up. I am coming back with something you need to see."

"Just checking in" is the sound of a rep who has already lost.
It signals desperation before the buyer reads the second word.

This phrase reframes the contact entirely.
You are not chasing. You are returning with value.
The buyer's curiosity opens the door your follow-up never could.

Never check in. Always come back with a reason.
