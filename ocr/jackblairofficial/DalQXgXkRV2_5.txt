5. "Let me give you that back in one sentence."

Harvey wrote in staccato. Short lines, one thought at a time, because a radio listener can't reread. After any explanation, compress it to a single line. That's the line your buyer repeats to their spouse or their boss, and that line closes the deal you're not in the room for.

If they can't repeat it, they can't buy it.
