2. He pitched to the croc brain, not the spreadsheet.

Klaff learned that every message hits the primitive brain first.

That older brain ignores anything boring, fears anything complex, and only wakes up for what's novel and clear, so he leads with simple, vivid stakes instead of data. Logic comes after attention, not before.

Make it clear and intriguing before you make it detailed.
