6. “ASK THEM WHY THEY NEVER OFFERED YOU THIS.”
Every campaign he ran ended the same way: the incumbent scrambling to match terms it could have offered during twenty comfortable years.
The flip’s best weapon is the incumbent’s complacency. Put the question in the buyer’s mouth and let the rival’s scramble make your case for you.
Comfort never sharpened anyone’s pencil.
