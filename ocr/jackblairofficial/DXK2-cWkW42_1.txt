1. "I understand what you are asking for. That is not something I am able to move on."

Most reps hear a demand and immediately start calculating how much to give away. That calculation is visible. The buyer sees it and asks for more.

This phrase closes the door on concession without anger, without apology, without explanation. It is calm. It is final. It is the sound of someone who decided this before the call started. Michael Corleone never negotiated terms he had already settled in his own mind.

Decide your line before the call. Then hold it like it was never in question.
