4. "I want you to understand what you are actually deciding here. This is not a small choice."

Plainview always elevated the stakes of every conversation he was in.
Not through manipulation but through the genuine weight he placed on every commitment he made.

This phrase reframes a routine purchasing decision as the significant choice it actually is.
The buyer who was treating this as a low-stakes vendor selection suddenly feels the real gravity of it.
Elevated stakes demand a more serious decision process and a more serious rep, and you just established which one you are.

Make the decision feel as significant as it actually is. Buyers close harder on things that matter.
