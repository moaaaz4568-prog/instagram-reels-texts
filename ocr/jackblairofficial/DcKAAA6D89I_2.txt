THE MIDDLE WON, AND IT WASN'T CLOSE

The winners were ambiverts, the flexible middle, generating about $151 an hour in revenue against $115 for everyone else. The curve is an inverted U, not a line.

The target is not more energy. It is range: assertive when the moment pays for it, quiet when it does not. Range beats volume. Every quarter, everywhere.
