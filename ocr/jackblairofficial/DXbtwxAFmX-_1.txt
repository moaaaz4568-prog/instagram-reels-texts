1. "I am not here to sell you a product. I am here because I think you are exactly the kind of organisation that should be ahead of what is coming."

Nick Fury never opened a recruitment pitch with a feature list. He opened with a vision and a specific reason why the person in front of him belonged inside it.

This phrase converts a vendor conversation into a partnership conversation before the first objection is raised. The buyer stops evaluating a purchase and starts considering a position.

Those are completely different decisions and the second one closes at a completely different level.

Sell the position first. The product is just the mechanism that gets them there.
