1. "I am not going to waste your time with a presentation. I just want to ask you one question."

Senior buyers have sat through a thousand pitches.
They made their decision about you in the first fifteen seconds.

This phrase tells them you are different before you prove it.
It lowers the shield, creates curiosity, and hands them control they were already taking.
Now they are leaning in instead of looking for an exit.

The rep who skips the deck and asks the right question owns the room.
