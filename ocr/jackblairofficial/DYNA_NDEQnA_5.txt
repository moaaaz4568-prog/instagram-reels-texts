5. The paying job funds the patience to wait.

Cranston played Hal on Malcolm in the Middle from 2000 to 2006. The role wasn't his life's work.

It was the income that let him say no to the wrong roles. He could afford to wait for Walter White because Malcolm had paid for the waiting.

A job that funds your patience buys you the leverage to choose the right vehicle.
