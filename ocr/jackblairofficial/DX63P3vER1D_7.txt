7. She took the empire public on her own terms.

By the time she died in 1919, Walker employed over 40,000 people. She'd built the first beauty conglomerate aimed at Black women in America.

Her funeral procession was attended by tens of thousands.

The closer who serves a market loyally builds an empire the rest of the industry has to acknowledge in death even if they ignored it in life.
