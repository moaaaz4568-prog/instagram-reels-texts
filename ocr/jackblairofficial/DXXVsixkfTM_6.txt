6. "I am not the flashiest option you are going to see this week. I am the one who will still be here doing the work next year."

Roy Kent understood that longevity in any competitive environment belongs to the worker not the talented. This phrase converts the rep who is not the most polished in the market into the most reliable.

Reliability is the close that nobody talks about and everybody needs. The buyer who has been burned by impressive pitches and mediocre deliveries stops wanting the flashiest option and starts wanting the one who will still be there.

Be the rep they can count on for longer than the deal lasts. That is the close that never ends.
