5. "You have mentioned [detail] three times now. That tells me it is the thing that matters most. Am I right?"

Jane catalogued everything and used specific details to demonstrate a quality of attention that felt almost supernatural.
This phrase does the same thing with information the buyer handed you voluntarily.

Reflecting a pattern back to someone signals that you were present in a way that almost no one ever is. The buyer stops and realises they gave themselves away and that you caught it without making them feel exposed. Now they trust you completely because you paid attention when everyone else would have moved on.

Track the repetitions. They are always the map to the close.
