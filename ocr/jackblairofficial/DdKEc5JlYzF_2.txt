2. "What does the seat above mine actually pay for, my closing or my managing?"

Most floors only promote reps into management. That is a different job with a different skill, and it takes the best closer off the phone. Ask which one the next seat rewards. If the only way up is to stop doing the thing you are good at, this floor has no next seat for a closer.

Up should not mean off the phone.
