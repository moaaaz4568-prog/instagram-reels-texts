3. He bet on himself when the banks barely would.

With a net worth around four million, Miller talked six lenders into handing him eight million dollars.

He was buying a team that had lost money almost every year of its life, but he sold them on his conviction the way he sold cars, by believing it first. Belief is the first thing you have to close.

If you are not sold, no one across the table will be either.
