ONE ANCHOR MAKES EVERY PRICE BELIEVABLE

A member who trusts the $1.50 extends that trust to the $999 TV without checking. The hot dog is doing the selling three aisles away.

Build your own anchor: the one term, guarantee or number in your offer that never moves for anyone. Let buyers test you on it.

People check one promise, then believe the rest.
