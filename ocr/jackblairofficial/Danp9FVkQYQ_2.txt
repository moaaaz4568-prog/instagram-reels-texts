2. "Here's what I've got on the line with you."

While asking a country for sacrifice, Iacocca cut his own salary to a dollar a year. The number did more selling than any speech. Show your buyer your actual stake: you're commission only, your name goes on the rollout, your cell number is in the contract. Skin talks.

Nobody believes the pitch of a man with nothing at risk.
