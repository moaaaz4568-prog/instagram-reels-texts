Read those seven back. Not one of them
makes you look good. Every single one
trades a little polish for a lot of truth,
because the impressive rep gets the
rehearsed answers and the human one
gets the real ones.

Here's the mirror. You've probably spent
years being underestimated by rooms
that mistook unpolished for unskilled.
Columbo turned that exact thing into the
deadliest weapon on television.

The instinct to drop your armor and let a
stranger relax cannot be scripted or
automated. It's craft. The only question is
whether the room you use it in prices it
like craft. Same skill. Different room.
