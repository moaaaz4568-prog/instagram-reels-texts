4. "If this lands wrong in writing, it costs us a week. I'd rather say it."
Schroeder and Epley's finding, in plain terms: text strips the thinking out of your message and leaves only the words. Misreadings fill the gap.
Name the risk of writing it and the buyer takes the call. You've made the safe option.
A misread email costs more than a call.
