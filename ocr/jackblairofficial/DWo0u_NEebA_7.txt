7. "Would it be okay if we got the paperwork started so you can begin enjoying this as soon as possible?"

Never ask them to sign.
Never say contract or deal.

Hopkins documented that specific words trigger fear in buyers before they've made the decision.

Paperwork. Investment. Own.
Not contract. Cost. Buy.

The right word at the close is the difference between yes and "let me think about it."
