2. END YOUR CLAIMS WITH A QUESTION.
Tom Hopkins sold one home in his first six months.
Then he spent his last 150 dollars on a three day
closing course and, in his last year selling, moved
365 homes.
His habit afterward: every claim ends in a hook.
'That cuts your commute in half, doesn't it?'
Agreements stack while you talk.
By the close, yes is already a habit.
