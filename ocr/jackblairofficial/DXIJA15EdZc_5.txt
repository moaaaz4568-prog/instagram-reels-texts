5. "I have done my homework on your business. Here is what I think you are actually dealing with."

Roger Sterling never walked into a client meeting unprepared. He knew the room before he entered it, and the room felt that immediately.

This phrase signals that this call is not like every other call they took this week. You are not guessing at their pain. You are naming it. When a buyer hears their own problem described better than they could describe it, the sale is already made.

Preparation is the most underrated closing tool alive.
