2. "I KNOW THEIR HANDICAP, THEIR ALMA MATER, AND THEIR BOSS'S PATIENCE."
The 66 questions cover schools, teams, kids, awards, what the buyer brags about and what the buyer fears at work. Not one question about paper stock. Mackay's rule: the person signs, the company only pays.
Map the signer as a person under pressure. Deals stall on a human's fear long before they stall on your spec sheet.
Companies never buy anything. A person does.
