7. "Before we finish, I want to make sure we have actually solved what you came into this call needing to solve."

This phrase reclaim the end of a call that a dominant buyer tried to run out of time.
It signals that your purpose was never the pitch. It was their outcome.

It resets the energy in the final moments and puts the close back in the right hands.
The buyer who has been in control all suddenly realises you were in control all along.
That realisation is the most powerful moment available on any call.

Own the last word. The close lives there.
