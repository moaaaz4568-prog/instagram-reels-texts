2. "Can I get your advice on something?"

Grant's chapter on advice seeking: ask someone how they'd handle it and they take your side to answer, because they have to see it from where you're standing. On a call, ask the buyer how they'd roll this out if it were they start solving the objection before they've raised it, in their own words, which are the only words they'll believe later.

Ask for advice and they start telling it to themselves.
