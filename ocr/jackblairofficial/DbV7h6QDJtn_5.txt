5. "THERE ARE THREE STEPS LEFT. TWO ARE
MINE."
Kivetz measured it at the register: near the goal,
effort accelerates; far from it, effort stalls. So shrink
the visible remainder of a stuck deal.
Count what's left out loud, then take the bigger
share. A buyer holding one small step no moves. A
buyer staring at a process doesn't.
Whoever shrink the path controls the pace.
