1. Wartime mode is a different operator than peacetime mode.

In peacetime, the rep can build slowly. In wartime, every call carries weight.

The closers who win in slow markets aren't running the playbook from when leads were everywhere. They switched modes the day the market did.

Horowitz wrote that most management books teach the peacetime. Almost none teach the only mode that matters when you're 90 days from broke.
