04. Event Follow Up

"I met or presented to [job title] at [company type]
at [event name or type] and they expressed interest in [describe what
they showed interest in].

My offer is: [one sentence].

Write me 3 follow-up messages I can send in the 48 hours after the
event that:
Reference our specific interaction or their specific interest,
feel like a natural continuation of a real conversation,
and move toward a booked call without feeling like a sales sequence.

Under 70 words each.

Tone: personal, warm, momentum-building.

No pitching. Zero guesswork."

CloserCoach.ai

You stop letting event conversations go cold before the flight home
lands.
Every warm interaction on now has a follow-up with replying to within
48 hours.
