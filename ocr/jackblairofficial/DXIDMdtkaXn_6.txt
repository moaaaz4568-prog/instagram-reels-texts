6. "You do not have to decide today. I just want to make sure you have everything you need if you do."

This is the phrase most reps are too afraid to say because it sounds like letting go. It is actually the tightest close on the list.

It removes pressure without removing momentum. The buyer relaxes. Their guard drops. The real objection surfaces. Now you are solving the actual problem instead of fighting a decoy.

Give them the exit. Most will not take it.
