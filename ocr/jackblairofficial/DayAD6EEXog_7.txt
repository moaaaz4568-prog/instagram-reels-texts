7. "If it's not this, no harm. But it sounded tonight like this mattered to you."

Roma's close doesn't announce itself. The yes arrives as the natural last sentence of a conversation the buyer never wanted to end. No transition into pitch mode, no shift in voice, no moment where the trap becomes visible, because there is no trap. There's a conversation, and at the end of it, a decision.

The smoothest close feels like the conversation ending.
