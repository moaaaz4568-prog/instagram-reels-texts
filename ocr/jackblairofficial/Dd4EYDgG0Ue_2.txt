2. "I'm not here to sell you a jar. I'm here to show you what to do with it."

She called it the Walker System: wash, treat the scalp, comb, repeat, and she taught it in kitchens before she ever had a school. By 1908 the school existed, Lelia College in Pittsburgh, turning customers into trained hair culturists. The jar was the ticket. The method was the sale. Sell the way you'd use it and the product rides along.

People buy the hands, then the jar.
