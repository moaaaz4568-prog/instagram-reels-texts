2. "For a year and a half, nothing worked. It got worse before it got better."

Zak found a story only releases the chemical if it first holds attention, and attention comes from tension. The flat version, same person, no struggle, held nobody and moved nobody. Don't sprint to the happy ending. Sit in the problem long enough that the buyer actually feels it before you resolve it.

No tension, no attention. No attention, no sale.
