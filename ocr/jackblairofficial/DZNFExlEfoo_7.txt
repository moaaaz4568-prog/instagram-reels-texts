7. "The jury stays out till you take your last breath."

Judy says there is no cap on success and the verdict on your life is never final.

At 80 she still works because she refuses to call it done. Carry that into every slump and every no. The deal you lost today is not the verdict. Keep showing up and the next one turns.

It is never over until you stop.
