4. "I am going to ask you something I should have asked earlier. What is the single thing holding this up right now?"

Bauer never wasted time managing symptoms when he could identify the source.
This phrase cuts through every layer of stall and politeness to find the one real thing standing between you and the close.

The buyer who answers honestly gives you the only objection that was ever real.
The buyer who cannot answer reveals that the hesitation was never concrete enough to be a genuine obstacle.
Either answer moves the deal forward faster than another week of follow-up ever could.

Find the single thing. It is always there. And it is always smaller than the delay it created.
