1. “I’M ONLY HAVING THIS CONVERSATION WITH YOU BECAUSE THERE’S STILL A SPOT OPEN.”

This phrase sets the frame before the pitch even starts.

The buyer isn’t being sold to. They’re being let in. Massarelli doesn’t beg people to listen. He makes listening feel like a privilege with an expiration date. Access is the oldest form of urgency. And it never sounds desperate.
