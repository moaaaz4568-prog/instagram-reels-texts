7. "Before you decide, put the phone face down for me."

Robbins controls hands and objects because objects steal focus. The deciding moment has to be clean of competition. You've earned the right to ask for it by then, so ask plainly. Buyers don't resent the frame. They respect the person who cared enough about their decision to protect it.

Own the last minute and you own the decision.
