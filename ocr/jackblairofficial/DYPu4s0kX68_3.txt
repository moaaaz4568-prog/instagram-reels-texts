3. The grad school at 27 isn't the same as grad school at 22.

NYU Tisch usually accepts students in their early 20s. Mahershala arrived at 27 with a sports data career behind him.

He had something the 22-year-olds didn't: real-world reps. He'd already paid rent in a soul-numbing job.

The late starter has urgency. The early starter has time. Urgency closes more skill gaps than time does.
