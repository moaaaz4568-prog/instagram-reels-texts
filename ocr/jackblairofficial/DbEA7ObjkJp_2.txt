2. "Before we talk cost, let me reframe what you're actually buying here."

Bernays sold cigarettes as freedom and bacon as doctor's orders. He never argued a product on its own terms. He changed the category the buyer was judging it in.

You move the frame off the frame where you look expensive and onto the one where you're the obvious answer. The frame settles the verdict before the price ever comes up.

Win the category and the price stops mattering.
