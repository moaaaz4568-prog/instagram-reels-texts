4. "Nobody's built here in ten years. That's the reason to go first, not the reason to wait."

Her first deal that was entirely her own was 4 Times Square, 1996: Condé Nast, the publisher of Vogue, into a new tower on a Times Square no people still avoiding New York had put up in a decade, and it was the first building New York had put up in a decade, and I did it." It started the redevelopment of Times Square. When your buyer says nobody in their industry has done this yet, agree with them. Then show them what being first is worth before everyone else arrives.

Empty isn't a warning. Empty is a head start.
