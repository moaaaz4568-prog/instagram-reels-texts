IF THE OTHER QUOTE IS CHEAPER, ASK THEM
WHAT THEY LEFT OUT.
Zalinsky sold the same parts cheaper by quietly
gutting what was cheaper inside the box.
This line never attacks the competitor. It hands the
buyer a question that does the comparing for you,
and it works because cheaper always costs
something somewhere. Let them go find out what.
Never argue with the cheaper quote. Make it explain
itself.
