4. The early-career roles you didn't get were preparation.

After grad school, Mahershala did small TV roles for years. The 4400. Crossing Jordan. Threat Matrix. Most viewers don't remember any of them.

He didn't book Moonlight at 35. He booked it at 41.

The 10+ years of small roles weren't a failure. They were the prep. By the time the right role came, he was ready to win an Oscar with it.
