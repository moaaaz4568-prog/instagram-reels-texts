1. "Help me help you."

Jerry's client is tanking his own deal, and Jerry stops pitching, stops managing, and says the five most honest words in the movie.

The line works because it flips the roles. The buyer stops being a target you're working and becomes a partner in their own outcome.

When a deal stalls, stop pushing and recruit them. "Help me help you the way here?" Resistance can't survive being a teammate.
