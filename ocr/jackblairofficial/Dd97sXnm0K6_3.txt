3. The reason beats the discount.
The sellers who survive Harrison are the ones who explain the number without flinching. I asked Claude to put my price into three sentences a buyer can repeat to their boss, then tell me where to stop talking. What came back:
Three sentences for a 30 person restaurant group: "$7,200 a year is $20 a day. One missed overtime calculation costs more than that in one lawyer's letter. Your office manager gets eleven hours a week back, and your partners will care about that part." Then: say the number, then stop.
