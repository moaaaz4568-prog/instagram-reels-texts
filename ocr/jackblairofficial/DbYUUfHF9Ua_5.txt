5. "COULD WE RUN IT WITH ONE TEAM AND KEEP YOUR OLD WAY EVERYWHERE ELSE?"
The cart never outlawed the basket. Shoppers kept both, and within weeks his aisles were full of carts anyway. Nobody argued anyone into it.
Shrink the leap. One team, one project, one quarter, old system intact. A buyer who keeps a way back has nothing left to defend.
Reversible decisions don't need courage.
