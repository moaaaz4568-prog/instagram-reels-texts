6. "What about this doesn't work for you?"

Voss used "what" and "how," never "why."
Why feels like an accusation.

These questions make the buyer explain
their real blocker out loud, and the real
blocker is almost never the one they led
with. Now you can solve the true
objection.

Ask what, not why, and the truth comes
out.
