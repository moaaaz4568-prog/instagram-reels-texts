1. "What number were you thinking?"
In July 2022 the Nationals offered 15 years and $440 million. Soto and Boras didn't counter against themselves with a lower ask. They said no and let the other side carry the next number. When a buyer says "what's the best you can do", don't answer with a number. Ask theirs. The first side to move against itself pays for the whole negotiation.
Silence after a number is not rude. It's the price.
