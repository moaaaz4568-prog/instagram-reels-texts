7. "Care about the outcome, but never strain for it."

Maltz found that trying too hard, with too much anxiety, jams the very mechanism that performs.

He taught that tension and desperation choke your timing and your read on a call. Do your work before the call, then relax into it. The calm version of you closes far more than the tight one.

Relaxed beats desperate every time.
