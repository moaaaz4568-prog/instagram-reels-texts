7. "Based on everything you've told me, I think I can genuinely help here. Can I show you how?"

Bunk never oversold. He waited until he had earned the right to make his move. This phrase only works after you have done everything that came before it. But when it lands on the back of real rapport it does not feel like a close. It feels like a natural next step. Earn the right. Then ask.
