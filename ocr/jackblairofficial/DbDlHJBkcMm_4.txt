4. "The thing working against you here isn't the price. Let me show you what actually is."

Cicero never let a room's hostility land on him. He redirected it at the real enemy, the actual problem, so he and the listener ended up on the same side against it.

You move the buyer from you-versus-me to us-versus-the-problem by naming the real obstacle out loud. Shared enemy, shared side.

Give them an enemy that isn't you.
