7. "I think we both know this is the right call. Let me tell you what happens next."

Ron Burgundy never ended a broadcast with a question about whether anyone was still watching. He assumed the close with the certainty of someone for whom doubt was not an option.

This phrase assumes the yes without demanding it and proposes the next step before the buyer has finished deciding.

The buyer who was on the fence suddenly finds themselves in a conversation that has already moved past the fence.
Assumed momentum is always faster than requested permission.

Assume the yes. Move to the next step. The buyer who was ready will follow you there.
