2. "I am not going to tell you something is right for you if I do not believe that it is."

Omar's code was absolute and the people who knew him understood that this word was the only currency he traded in. This phrase signals the same integrity and the buyer who receives it stops treating you like a vendor.

They start treating you like an advisor who can be trusted without verification. The advisor relationship closes at a completely different rate than the vendor relationship because the buyer stops shopping for a second opinion the moment they trust the first one.

Signal the integrity before you need it. The buyer who trusts your word stops looking for reasons not to.
