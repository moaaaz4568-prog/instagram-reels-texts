2. "By the way, when do you plan on
moving?"

Ferry slipped the most important
question in sideways, after the small talk.

The "by the way" makes it feel like an
afterthought, so the prospect answers
honestly instead of guarding the timeline
that tells you everything about how to
work the deal. The throwaway tone is the
technique.

Bury the key question in a casual aside
and let them relax into the truth.
