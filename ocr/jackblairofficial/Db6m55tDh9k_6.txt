6. “EVERY BATTLE IS WON BEFORE IT IS EVER
FOUGHT.”
Gekko lifted the line from Sun Tzu and ran his whole
book on it.
Before the call: the agenda, the decision process,
the likely objections, and your answers, mapped. The
conversation only confirms what the preparation
already built. That is why he never sounded
surprised.
The call is the receipt. The work happened before it.
