7. "NOTHING THEY SAY IN THAT MEETING WILL SURPRISE YOU NOW."
This is what an inoculated deal sounds like at the end. Every attack pre-heard, every number pre-framed, every strength conceded on your terms.
McGuire's subjects didn't win arguments, they became hard to move. That's the close: not a yes squeezed out today, a buyer who walks into rival meetings already immune.
Don't win the argument. Make them immovable.
