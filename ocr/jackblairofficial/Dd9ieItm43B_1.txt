1. He gave before he asked.

Cialdini's first finding undercover: the pros gave something first, and the gift made the ask feel owed. I gave Claudio and asked for the one thing I can hand a buyer in the first five minutes that costs me nothing. What came back:

Three gives, ranked by obligation. The winner: a two minute read of their own setup before any pitch: "Eleven of your people share one admin password. I'd fix that today with the steps?" Then the turn: "Whether you hire us or not. Want me to get the call when that breaks?"
