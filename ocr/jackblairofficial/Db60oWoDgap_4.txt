4. "I'VE HEARD THAT CONCERN BEFORE. THE REPS WHO SAID IT COST THEM THE MOST."
Objections are not the problem. The pattern behind the objection is.
This phrase frames their hesitation as a familiar mistake, not a unique and valid reason to walk. It lands without arrogance because it sounds like experience, not ego.
Every objection has a graveyard. Put theirs in it before they commit.
