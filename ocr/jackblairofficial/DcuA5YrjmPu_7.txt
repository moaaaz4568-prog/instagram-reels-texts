7. "WHEN WE'RE SIGNED, TELL THE STORY AS LOUD AS YOU WANT."
The moment the ink dried, secrecy flipped to spectacle: press conference, the governor, headlines. The same information, released at the right second, but rewrite a kingdom.
Give them the after party: the announcement, the logo slide, the day they get to talk about it. People sign faster when they can see that day.
Quiet until signed. Loud forever after.
