04. Question Arsenal

"Write me 10 discovery questions for a [job title] who is evaluating [product or service] for their [team or company].
Each question must uncover pain, surface consequence, or reveal budget authority.
No yes or no questions. No surface level questions.
Under 20 words each.
Tone: sharp, curious, peer to peer.
No fluff. No textbook language."

Your questions do the selling before your pitch ever starts. The close builds itself.
