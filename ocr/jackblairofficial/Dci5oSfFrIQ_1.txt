1. HE NEVER LEFT THE TOWN EVERYONE ELSE LEFT.

No Manhattan skyline, no famous rolodex. He proved the market is not the city you sell in, it is the depth you sell at. Reps blame the territory. Feldman built an empire inside one.
The goldmine is under the room you are standing in.
