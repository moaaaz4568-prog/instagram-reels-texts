"I'M NOT GOING TO PRETEND WE'RE YOUR ONLY

alternatives. A supplier who acts otherwise gets
scored as a routine one running a bluff.
Concede the field in one sentence. You have just
bought the right to talk about the difference instead
of the discount.
Admit the alternatives exist and you get to discuss
the difference.
