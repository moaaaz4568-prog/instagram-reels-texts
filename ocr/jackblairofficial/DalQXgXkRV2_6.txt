6. "I only sell what I'd put my name on. Here's why I took this one."

Harvey turned sponsors away and said it plainly: he was fiercely loyal to the ones willing to put their money where his mouth was. Advertisers waited in line because his yes meant something. Tell the buyer what you've said no to. Selectivity is the fastest credibility on a call.

A yes only counts from someone who says no.
