4. Bill could barely speak. He could barely walk. His right hand was clenched into a fist that wouldn't open.

He never mentioned any of it at the door.

He'd smile, ring the bell, and ask about their week. Their kids. Their garden.

The cerebral palsy disappeared in the first 30 seconds because he refused to put it on the table.

The buyer doesn't care about your story. They care that you care about theirs.
