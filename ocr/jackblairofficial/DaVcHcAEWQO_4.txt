4. You might be right. Walk me through it.

Grant's own rule is to argue like you are right but listen like you are wrong.

Most reps hear an objection and reach for the counterpunch. The fight makes the objection stronger. Conceding the chance makes them defend it out loud, where it usually falls apart.

The objection they explain to you is one you can actually work with.
