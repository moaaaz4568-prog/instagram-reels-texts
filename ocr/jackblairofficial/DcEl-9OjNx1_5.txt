THE ROLEPLAY
"Interview me for this closing seat. Structured and behavioral: a deal I lost, a discount I refused, a quarter I missed. Push on every vague answer until each claim has a scene attached."
The moment an answer goes abstract, an interviewer stops believing it. Rehearse until the scenes arrive before the adjectives do.
Specifics get hired. Adjectives get thanked for coming in.
