4. Took 1.9% when the industry was
taking 20%.

Standard franchise royalty in 1955 was 20%
of revenue. Kroc took 1.9%, the smallest
cut he could survive on.

The bet was volume. 1.9% of 100 stores
beats 20% of 5.

Closers who play for scale outearn the reps
maximizing the first deal.
