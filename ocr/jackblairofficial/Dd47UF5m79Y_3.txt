3. He found the deadline first.

Most concessions happen in the last 20 percent of the time available, and the side that knows the other side's deadline owns the deal. I pasted my notes on a buyer and asked for their real deadline. What came back:

The renewal on the 31st, 70 percent sure. Three questions that confirm it without asking. How to set mine so the decision lands first. A 48 word follow up that uses it.
