6. "I think we are close but not close enough. I would rather wait until we are completely aligned than push this through and have it fail."

Jax Teller's biggest mistakes came from moving before the alignment was real.
He knew it every time and moved anyway and it cost him everything that mattered.

This phrase signals the patience and the strategic discipline that separate reps who build lasting books of business from those who hit quota once and churn their client base every eighteen months.

The deal closed on complete alignment delivers results. The one forced through on almost-alignment delivers problems.

Wait for the real alignment. The deal closed on it never needs to be resold.
