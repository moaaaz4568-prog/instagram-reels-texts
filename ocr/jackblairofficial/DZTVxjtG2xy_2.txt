2. He turned himself into the brand.

Worthington did not advertise a dealership, he advertised Cal, the cowboy hat, the grin, the name in every jingle.

People did not drive across town for a Ford, they drove to go see Cal. When the seller becomes the reason people show up, you stop competing on price and start competing on trust.

Put your name on it and they buy the person, not the lot.
