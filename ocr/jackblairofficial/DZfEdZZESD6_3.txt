3. He turned his best customers into his salesforce.

McConnell saw that the women buying from him would relate to other women better than he ever could.

So he recruited them to sell, starting with Mrs. Albee, who carried the line door to door and signed up more sellers behind her. The people who love the product make the most natural sellers of it.

Your happiest customer is your next best closer.
