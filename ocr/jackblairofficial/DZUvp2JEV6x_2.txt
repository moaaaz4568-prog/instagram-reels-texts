2. He gave himself a quota of no's, not yes's.

His first weeks were brutal, forty days going out every morning and coming home with nothing.

So he stopped chasing the sale and started counting the rejections instead, treating every no as one paid step toward a yes the math guaranteed was coming. The fear left the moment the word no had a job to do.

Aim for the no's and the yes's stop scaring you.
