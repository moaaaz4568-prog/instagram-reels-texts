1. THE DEATH MOMENT

What it does: pinpoints the exact moment
in a call where the buyer mentally walked
away.

"You are a senior remote high ticket sales
coach who has reviewed 10,000+ recorded
calls. Here is the transcript of a call I lost:
[PASTE TRANSCRIPT]. Find the moment the
buyer's energy shifted from interested to
disengaged. Quote the specific exchange.
Explain what triggered it, what the
buyer was actually responding to, and what I
should have said instead."
