1. "I want to flag this deal internally before it becomes an issue rather than after."

Gerri Kellman always moved information upward before it became a problem downward.
That proactive management of internal dynamics was the thing that kept her in the room when everyone else was being cleared out.

This phrase signals to your own organisation that you are on top of the deal and thinking ahead.
The internal stakeholder who might have become a blocker gets visibility before they have a reason to become defensive.
Deals that surprise internal teams almost always get slowed by them. Deals that include them almost never do.

Flag it early. The internal stakeholder who feels included almost never becomes the reason the deal dies.
