7. "Before we move on, what haven't I asked that I should have?"

The deadliest errors were the ones nobody spoke up about. Leave a clean open door right before the close. The thing they've been sitting on, the one that would've haunted you next week, comes out while you're still in the room to handle it.

The unspoken objection ghosts you. The spoken one closes.
