1. "If this isn't a fit, are you okay telling me no?"

Sandler once made 87 calls in a row and got 87 noes, then built a system around killing the pressure that caused them.

This line hands the buyer permission to say no, which makes them relax and tell you the truth instead of dodging you. Pressure creates resistance, permission dissolves it.

Give them the exit and they stop looking for it.
