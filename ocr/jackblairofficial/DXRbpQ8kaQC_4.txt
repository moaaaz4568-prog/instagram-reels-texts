4. "I think you already know what you want to do. You are just looking for permission to do it."

Jane always saw the decision before the decision-maker did.
This phrase reflects that back to a buyer who has been circling the yes for longer than they realise.

It is not pressure. It is a mirror held up with complete calm and total confidence.
The buyer who hears this and recognises it as true stops deliberating and starts deciding.
You did not push them toward yes. You showed them they were already there.

Show the buyer where they already are. The close is recognition, not persuasion.
