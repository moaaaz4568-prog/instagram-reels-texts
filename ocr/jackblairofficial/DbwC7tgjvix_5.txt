SILENCE. COUNT THREE BEFORE YOU ANSWER ANYTHING.
The enhanced interview's hardest rule isn't a question. Interviewers were trained to stop interrupting, because every interruption cuts off retrieval mid-reach and the detail is gone for good.
The average rep answers a buyer's pause like it's an objection. The pause IS the retrieval. Three full seconds. What comes after them is the sentence they weren't going to say.
Every interruption costs you the sentence that mattered.
