1. The "safe" path had already collapsed.

Jim's father Percy was a working accountant for 17 years. Got laid off in 1978 with no severance.

The family lost the house. Moved into a Volkswagen camper van. Lived in a tent on his sister's lawn for a stretch.

The path your parents called safe is the path that put them in the van.
