6. COUNT THE ACTIVITIES, NOT THE VIBES.

He knew his own math cold. So many conversations
became so many proposals became so many
closes, and he worked the top of that math every
single day.

You cannot control who buys this quarter. You can
control how many people got the chance to.

Volume is a skill when aimed.
