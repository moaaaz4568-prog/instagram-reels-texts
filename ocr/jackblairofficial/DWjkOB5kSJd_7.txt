7. "Earn the right.
You want to ask for somebody's business
you've got to earn the right to ask for it."

Not a phrase for the buyer.
A rule Elliott lives by.

The close only works when
everything before it was done right.

The rapport.
The discovery. The trust.
The questions.

Earn all of it first.
Then the close takes care of itself.
