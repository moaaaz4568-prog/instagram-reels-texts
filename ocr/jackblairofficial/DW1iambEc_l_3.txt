WHY MOST REPS LOSE HERE:

The buyer showed Interest.
The call went well.
The demo landed perfectly.

And then the rep waited.

No follow up with purpose.
No defined next step.
No sequence to fall back on
when the buyer went quiet.

The deal did not die because
the buyer said no.

It died because the rep
had no plan after the yes
started turning into silence.
