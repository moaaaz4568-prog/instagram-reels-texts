2. "We might not be a fit, and I'll tell you straight if we're not."

Konnikova calls the second stage the play: the emotional foundation laid before any offer. The con manufactures it. You earn it, by being visibly willing to lose the sale. Nothing builds real trust faster than a seller who will disqualify you to your face. It is the one thing a con can never actually afford to merit.

The fastest way to be trusted is to risk the sale to earn it.
