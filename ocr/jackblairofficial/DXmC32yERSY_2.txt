2. "I need a decision on this by end of week. Here is what happens to the deal if we do not have one."

Gerri always attached consequences to internal timelines because she understood that without consequences nothing moves. This phrase is the connective tissue that connects the internal urgency close to the external one.

Your internal team stops treating the deal as one of many things on their list. They start treating it as the thing that decides whether a real commercial opportunity survives or disappears. The internal decision that has a clear consequence attached almost always gets made faster than the one that does not.

Attach the consequence to the internal timeline. The team that sees the cost of delay almost always eliminates it.
