6. The objection every rep is about to face.

"Things are too uncertain right now."

Most reps will nod, say they understand, and reschedule.

That reschedule never closes.

The top 1% have a word-for-word response ready that reframes uncertainty as the exact reason to move now instead of later.

When everyone is frozen — the one is frozen — the rep who can make a buyer feel confident enough to decide is the rep who eats.
