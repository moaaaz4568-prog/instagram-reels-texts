"HERE'S WHAT I'M SEEING."
The book replaces commands with description. Not
clean your room. The milk is spilled. Description
hands the conclusion to the listener instead of
forcing it.
Swap you keep pushing this decision for here is
where we are: two calls, one proposal, a start date
three weeks out. Describe the deal and let the buyer
conclude what it needs.
A description invites a decision. An accusation
invites a defence.
