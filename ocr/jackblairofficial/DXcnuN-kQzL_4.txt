4. "The reason most people in your position are not solving this is not budget or timing. It is that nobody has shown them what it actually costs to ignore it."

Baum always traced the real problem back to its actual source rather than accepting the surface explanation. This phrase does the same thing for a buyer who has been using budget or timing as a comfortable reason to stay still.

You are not attacking their excuse. You are elevating the conversation past it.

The buyer who hears their real obstacle named accurately feels seen in a way that moves them faster than any feature demonstration.

Named truth closes what polished pitches leave open.

Name the real reason they have not moved. The buyer who recognises it has nowhere left to hide.
