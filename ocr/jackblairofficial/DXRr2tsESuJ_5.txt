05. Objection Roleplay

"You are a [prospect job title] at a [company type] in [industry].
I sell [one sentence pitch on your offer].
Hit me with your 5 hardest and most realistic objections for 2026,
one at a time, waiting for my response before moving to
the next.
After each of my responses score it out of 10,
tell me exactly what landed and what to sharpen.
Tone: sceptical, busy, not easily impressed.
No easy questions. No holding back."

You get battle-tested before the call that decides your
month. Not during it.
