3. Proof that sounds like her.

"A dentist with two chairs, not a hospital with two hundred." Social proof and authority: people copy people who like them and defer to them. On a call it is one story about someone the buyer recognises as herself. I asked Claude to rewrite my proof that way. What came back:

Three stories under 60 seconds each, blanks left for the real names, each ending on the moment that buyer decided: a failed inspection, a hygienist who quit over the bathrooms, a landlord walk through. Plus one authority line with a number in it and no adjectives.
