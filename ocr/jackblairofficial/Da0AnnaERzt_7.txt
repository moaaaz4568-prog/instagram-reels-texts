7. "After you sign, here's exactly when you'll hear from me next."

Every con ends the same way, the blow-off: take it and vanish before the mark notices. This is the one step you invert completely. The con disappears. Your entire reputation is that you don't. Everything before the sale is trust you promised. Everything after it is trust you proved. Name the next touchpoint out loud, then hit it.

A con's last move is to vanish. Yours is to stay. That's the whole difference.
