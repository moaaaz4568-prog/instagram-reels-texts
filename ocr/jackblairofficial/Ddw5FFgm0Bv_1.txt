1. The envelope that got the meeting.

"Here's the number nobody has shown you." Ben Feldman got a meeting with a billionaire who refused to see him by sending in an envelope with a cheque for the exact estate tax the man's family would owe if he died. The meeting happened that week. I gave Claude a buyer who won't take my call and asked for the envelope. What came back:

It found the number he had never seen sitting next to the other one, then built three things to put in front of him, ranked. The one it said to send first was 43 words and asked for nothing.
