THE TASTE TEST PESI KEPT WINNING 1
In the 1970s Pepsi put unmarked cups in malls
across America. Blind, most people picked Pepsi,
and the ads ran on it for years.
One problem. Coke kept outselling them anyway.
The market refused to agree with its own taste buds.
Products compete blind. Buyers never buy blind.
