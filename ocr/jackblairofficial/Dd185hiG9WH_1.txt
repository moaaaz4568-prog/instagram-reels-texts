1. "I'm not the smartest person on this call, and I don't need to be."

Bill Gates hired him on 11 June 1980 as employee number 30, on $50,000 and a slice of the company, and he could not write a line of code. He was in a building full of people who could. His job was to get things decided. On a technical call, stop competing on expertise with the person who has it. Make the decision easy instead.

Nobody buys from the cleverest person on the call. They buy from the one who makes the decision simple.
