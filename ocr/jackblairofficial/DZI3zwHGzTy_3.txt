3. "Don't say no yet. Hear the whole thing first."

Goodman buys himself room by stopping the early rejection before it hardens.

He knows a fast no is just discomfort, not a real decision. So he gently asks for the full hearing, because once someone commits to a no out loud they defend it. Keep them open long enough to actually consider it.

Hold off the no and you keep the deal alive.
