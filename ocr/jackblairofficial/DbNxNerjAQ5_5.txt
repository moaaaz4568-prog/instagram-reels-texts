5. "YOU'RE FREE TO CALL ANOTHER VENDOR.
SOME ARE GOOD."
Salvage masters name the other boats because the
captain is already thinking about them. Do the same,
plainly, without flinching.
A buyer who chooses you with the alternatives on
the table stays chosen. One who felt
cornered reopens the deal every time something squeaks.
Give the option out loud and let it lose.
A free yes stays closed. A cornered one reopens.
