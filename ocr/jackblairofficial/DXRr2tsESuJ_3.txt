03. Price Defender

"My prospect just pushed back on price by saying: [paste their exact words].
My product costs [price] and the core problem it solves is [one sentence].
Write me a response that holds the price with zero apology, connects the investment directly to the outcome they told me they want, and ends with one question that redirects to value.
Under 60 words.
Tone: grounded, certain, no desperation.
No discounting. No fluff."

You stop giving money away the moment someone pushes.
You close at full price every time.
