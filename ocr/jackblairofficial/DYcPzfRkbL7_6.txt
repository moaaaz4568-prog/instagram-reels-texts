6. "I can do better than this."

Sanders at 66: "I was sixty-six years old. I still had to make a living. I looked at the food that was being served and I said, 'I can do better than this.'"

His chicken wasn't a luxury product. It wasn't a status play. It was just better than what every other roadside restaurant in 1955 America was serving for the same price.

Better at the same price beats fancy every time.
