5. "IF SOMEONE QUOTES YOU HALF MY PRICE, ASK WHAT'S NOT IN IT."
Inoculation works before exposure, never after, that was McGuire's whole point.
Once a lowball lands, you're arguing uphill against a number they already like. So plant the defense while you're still trusted. Now every cheap quote arrives pre-framed as a question mark instead of a bargain, and you didn't have to say one bad word.
Cheap quotes only win when they arrive unannounced.
