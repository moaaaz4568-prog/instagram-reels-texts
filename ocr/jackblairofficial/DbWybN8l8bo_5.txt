5. "WHAT PROOF WOULD MAKE SWITCHING FEEL SAFER THAN STAYING?"
Vos Savant finally moved the public not with another proof but with an experiment anyone could run: three paper cups, a penny, four hundred trials at any table.
Copy that move. Ask what evidence would settle it, then hand over the cups: the pilot, the reference call, the trial month. Proof a buyer collects with their own hands outsells any proof you present.
Nobody argues with their own kitchen table.
