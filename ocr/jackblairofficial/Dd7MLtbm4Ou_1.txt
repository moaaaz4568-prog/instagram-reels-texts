1. "I'll be quick, I know you're busy, and I'm not calling to sell you anything today."

The Straight Line opens on four seconds. By Belfort's rule the buyer decides in that window whether you're sharp, enthusiastic, and an expert, and nothing you say later beats that first read. So the opener does one job: it takes the pressure off and hands you the floor. No apology, no "is this a bad time", no permission asked. Say why you're calling in one line, then ask a question before the whole decide to hang up.

The first four seconds are the line audition.
