3. "I want to make sure the right people are in the room for this conversation before it goes any further."

Gerri never let decisions be made by the wrong people when the right ones were available.
She mapped the power structure before she moved anything through it.

This phrase surfaces the real decision-making authority inside your own organisation before the deal gets trapped in the wrong layer.
The rep who knows who actually signs off and puts them in the room early never loses a deal to an internal approval chain.
Internal deals die in the gap between the buyer and the stakeholder nobody briefed.
The rep who closed almost never loses the deal.
The rep who briefed the deal to the wrong one.

Map the internal power structure. The rep who knows the right person early almost never loses the deal.
