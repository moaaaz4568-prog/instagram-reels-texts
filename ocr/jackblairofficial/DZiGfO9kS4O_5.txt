5. "Earn the right to ask."
Richardson taught that hard questions
only get honest answers once you've built
trust.
You can't interrogate a stranger. Open
with warmth, show you're useful, and the
buyer gives you permission to ask the
deeper questions. Credibility comes
before the probing, never after.
Build the trust first, then the real
questions get answered.
