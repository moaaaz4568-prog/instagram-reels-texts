5. "I'm doing a survey of the homes for sale by owner in the area."

Ferry approached for-sale-by-owners as a researcher, not a closer.

Leading with a survey lowers the wall a FSBO throws up at every agent, and the questions that follow surface exactly why they are struggling to sell alone. Curiosity opens doors.

Open as someone gathering information, then let the answers make your case.
