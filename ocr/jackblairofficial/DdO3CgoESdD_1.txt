1. "How's that working for you?"

Dr. Phil's signature question, and his third life law in five words: people do what works. If the buyer is still doing it the old way, some part of it is paying him. Ask it flat, no edge, right after he describes the current setup, then wait. A buyer who has to grade his own status quo out loud stops defending it.

Make them grade the old way before you pitch the new one.
