3. "I am going to be straightforward with you because I think you deserve that from me."

Trust is not built through polish. It is built through honesty delivered at the right moment.
This phrase positions you as someone who operates differently from everyone every other rep they have met.

It signals respect. And in a world where buyers have been burned by reps who overpromised and underdelivered, character is the rarest and most valuable thing you can put on the table.

Say what you mean. Mean what you say. Build the relationship nobody else has the patience to build.
