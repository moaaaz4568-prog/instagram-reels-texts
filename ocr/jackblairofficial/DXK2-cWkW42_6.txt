6. "We have worked hard to get to this point. I would hate to see us lose it over something we can solve right now."

This phrase protects the deal without protecting the ego.
It reminds the buyer how much ground you have already covered together.
Sunk cost is real and it is ethical to name it when it is true. The buyer who remembers every conversation, every meeting, every version of this negotiation does not want to start over any more than you do.

Remind them what walking away actually costs. Do it with care. Do it once.
