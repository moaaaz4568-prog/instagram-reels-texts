6. HE KEPT SELLING WHEN THE ANSWER WAS
NO.
Feldman treated a no as the start of the file, not the
end. Some buyers took him years.
He kept notes, kept visiting, kept adding one more
reason. The buyer's situation changes even when
their answer has not. Patience with a system beats
charm without one.
A no is a snapshot. The file stays open.
