2. Two weeks of silence before he wrote a single line.

Schwartz was reading the buyer's mail.
Hand-sorted into stacks. Annotated. Re-
read at 6 AM with coffee.

By the time he opened a Word doc, he
was already inside their head. The first
sentence wasn't writing. It was repeating
something the buyer had been thinking
for years.
