6. "I am not the biggest name in this space yet. I am the most committed one. Here is what that means for you."

Logan Roy built everything on commitment that outran resources every single time.
This phrase converts the absence of an established name into a competitive advantage. The established name can never replicate.

The buyer who was comparing you to larger competitors suddenly has a reason to reconsider the comparison. Size means history. Commitment means future. And the buyer who needs a partner for the future always values the second more.

Make commitment the credential. The buyer who understands its value chooses it over size every time.
