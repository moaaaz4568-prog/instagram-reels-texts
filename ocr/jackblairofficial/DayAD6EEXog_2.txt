2. "Forget business for a second. What do you actually want out of the next five years?"

The famous restaurant scene runs long, and almost none of it is about real estate. Roma talks life, regret, fear, what a man's allowed to want. The lonely stranger across the table leans in, opens up, starts confessing. The property appears only at the very end, almost as an afterthought. Meanwhile, across town, the always-be-closing guy is screaming at salesmen. He closes nothing in the entire film.

The best pitch doesn't sound like one.
