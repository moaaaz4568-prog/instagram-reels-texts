1. "I'M NOT HERE TO PITCH YOU. I'M HERE TO SEE IF THIS FITS."
Frank Luntz runs dial sessions where a room of people twist dials from 0 to 100 while someone talks. The needle drops the instant they feel sold to, before they could tell you why.
Your buyer's needle drops at "let me show you." Open with a fit check instead and the wall never goes up. You're not selling yet. You're sorting.
