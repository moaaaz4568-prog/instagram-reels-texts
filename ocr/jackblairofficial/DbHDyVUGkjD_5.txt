5. "From here on it's our timeline. What's our first deadline?"

Sherif's boys stopped saying us and them only after they had pulled together. Language followed the shared work, then locked it in.

Run the swap yourself and watch what happens: your rollout becomes our rollout, my product becomes our plan. Buyers hear whose side you're on in your pronouns.
