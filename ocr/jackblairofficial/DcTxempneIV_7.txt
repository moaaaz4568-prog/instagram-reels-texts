7. “THANK YOU FOR TRUSTING ME WITH
SOMEONE YOU KNOW.”
In the data the referred customer’s loyalty edge
never faded: the loyalty kept leaving 18 percent less often
across the whole window. One introduction
compounds for years.
Close the loop like it matters that much. Tell the
referrer what has happened, thank them in writing, and
watch the second name arrive without an ask. The
channel, once opened, feeds itself.
One earned introduction outlives a hundred cold
lists.
