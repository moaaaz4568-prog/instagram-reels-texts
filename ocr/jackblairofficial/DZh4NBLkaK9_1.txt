1. "You'll have to do better than that."

Dawson called this the vise, or the calm line
you say to almost any number the other
side puts on the table.

You don't argue or justify. You just hand
the pressure back and go quiet. More
often than people expect, the other side
moves against themselves to fill the
silence.

Say it, stop talking, and let them negotiate
against themselves.
