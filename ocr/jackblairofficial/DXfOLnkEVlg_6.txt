6. "I know what good looks like in this space because I have seen enough bad to know the difference completely."

Clay Morrow's authority was always implicit in his history. This phrase makes it understand that what follows is not a sales opinion but a practitioner's judgement. Practitioner's judgement closes at a completely different rate than vendor enthusiasm because it carries a different kind of weight. The rep who has seen enough bad to know good is the only rep worth listening to about what good looks like.

Invoke the earned perspective. The buyer who feels the weight of it stops comparing you to anyone else.
