1. "WHAT CHANGED IN THEIR WORLD SINCE WE LAST TALKED?"

Deals move when something in the buyer's world moves. New boss, new quarter, new fire. Most reps dial with three-week-old context and open the call already behind.

Sixty seconds. Their site, their LinkedIn, your last note. You're not researching, you're catching up. One changed fact rewrites your whole opener.

Stale context sounds exactly like not caring.
