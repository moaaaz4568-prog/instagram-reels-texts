1. "We built this for people who do the job themselves, not for the ones with a sales floor."

In the study, pitches that said "to build this" and "accessible to the" got funded. They named the thing was for and who it was not for, and the backers who fit stepped forward. The researchers filed it under social identity: people give to a version of themselves. On a call, that is the first sentence after hello. Name the buyer's tribe before you name a in the buyer's words before you name a feature, and say who it is not for out loud.

Nobody buys a product. They buy a version of themselves.
