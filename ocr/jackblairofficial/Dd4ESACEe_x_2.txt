2. "This will take about ten minutes."
Duration is the D in the same script, and
the reason is anxiety. A patient who knows
how long the needle takes stops bracing.
A buyer who knows the call is fifteen
minutes and the decision comes at the
end stops looking for the exit. Say the
length, say when the ask comes, then
keep to it.
A known length is easier to say yes to than
an open one.
