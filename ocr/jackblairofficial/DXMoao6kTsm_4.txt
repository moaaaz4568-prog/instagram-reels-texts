4. "I want to give you a straight answer on that. Give me thirty seconds to make sure it is the right one."

Strong buyers respect strength. They have no patience for fumbling.
This phrase buys you the pause you need while projecting complete composure.

It signals that you think before you speak and that what you say next is worth waiting for.
Al Swearingen never rushed an answer under pressure. He made the pressure wait for him.
That pause is not hesitation. In the right hands it is authority.

Slow down on purpose. The room will wait for someone who seems certain.
