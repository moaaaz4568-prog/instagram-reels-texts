2. "That concern makes sense. Here is what I have seen happen when people wait."

Most reps panic at hesitation and start discounting. That is the moment you hand the buyer your commission.

This phrase validates without agreeing. It redirects the energy toward consequence, not combat. The buyer stops debating price and starts thinking about cost.

Make them afraid of the no. Not the yes.
