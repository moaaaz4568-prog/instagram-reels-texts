6. "IF THEIR DEAL WAS THAT GOOD YOU WOULDN'T STILL BE ON THIS CALL WITH ME."

This is the line that ends the comparison.
The buyer is still talking to you. That means something is missing from the other offer. This phrase names that truth without making them admit it.

It's not confrontation. It's a mirror. And mirrors close deals that pitches can't.
