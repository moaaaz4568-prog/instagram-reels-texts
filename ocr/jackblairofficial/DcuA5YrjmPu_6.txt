6. "WHO ELSE ON YOUR SIDE NEEDS TO KNOW, AND WHEN?"
Fifty one separate sellers never compared notes.
The buying ran quiet and sequenced, so no two
landowners could price against each other.
Sequence your stakeholders on purpose: who hears
the number first, who hears it last, what each needs
before the next call. Order is leverage.
Sequence is strategy.
