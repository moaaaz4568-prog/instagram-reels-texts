1. "Before you explain anything, let me show you what I already know about your situation."

Lyndon Johnson never walked into a room cold. He knew the other man's pressures, deadlines, and wants better than the man knew them himself. The reading was done before the talking started.

You open the same way. Prove you did the work, and the buyer stops guarding the situation they came in ready to defend.

Do the reading before the room, not during it.
