4. “THEY’LL MATCH MY PRICE. THEY WON’T MATCH MY OUTCOME.”
Price matching is a race to the bottom. This phrase exits that race entirely.
It tells the buyer that cost is the wrong variable.
Results are the only metric that matters once the contract is signed. Cheap is a number. Outcome is a future.
Buyers remember what they got. Not what they paid.
