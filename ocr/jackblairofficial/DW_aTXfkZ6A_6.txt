6. "I understand your hesitation. It doesn't change what I know to be true."

Walt never let someone else's doubt become his own.
This phrase acknowledges the buyer's position without surrendering yours.

It is the cleanest way to hold your frame under pressure.
You're not dismissing them. You're staying anchored while they find their footing.

Respect the doubt. Never catch it.
