Prompt 3 | The Calibrated Question

#ROLE:
Act as Chris Voss building the calibrated questions that flip the pressure back to the prospect without me ever saying no.

#TASK:
Generate the 10 calibrated questions I can use on a sales call when the prospect pushes back on price, timing, or commitment.

#STEPS:
1. Identify the 3 most common objections I hear on calls (price, timing, "I need to think about it.")
2. For each objection, build 3 calibrated questions that start with "How" or "What"
3. Make sure no question can be answered yes or no
4. For each question, identify what answer would tell me to keep going vs walk away
5. Plan how to use silence between the question and their answer

#RULES:
- Every calibrated question starts with "How" or "What"
- Never use "Why" (it sounds accusatory and triggers defense)
- No yes/no questions. The prospect must explain their position out loud
- The question puts the problem-solving on them, not you
- "How am I supposed to do that?!" is the highest-level written

#OUTPUT:
Objection → 3 Calibrated Questions → Answers That Mean "Keep Going" → Answers That Mean "Walk" → Silence Strategy
