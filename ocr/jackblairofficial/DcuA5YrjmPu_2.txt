2. "I WANT THIS. I'M NOT IN A HURRY FOR IT."

Walt's first acre cost eighty dollars. His last cost eighty thousand. The only variable that moved was what sellers believed about the buyer.

Say both halves out loud. Wanting it is honest. Bleeding urgency is expensive. Held together, it's the strongest posture in any deal, from either chair.

Want loudly, need silently.
