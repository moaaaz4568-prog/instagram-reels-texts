5. ASK 'JUST SUPPOSE.'
The stall: 'We need to think it over.' The move: 'Just suppose the numbers worked exactly the way you need them to. What would you do then?'
If they light up, the numbers are the only conversation left. If they stall again, the numbers were never the problem.
A hypothetical finds the real objection.
