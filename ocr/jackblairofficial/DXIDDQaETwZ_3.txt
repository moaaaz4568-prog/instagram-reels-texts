3. "Before you decide, tell me what
would have to be true for this to make
sense."

When a prospect says no, most reps argue
with the no.
That is the wrong fight.

This phrase sidesteps the no entirely.
It invites the buyer to build their own bridge
back to yes.
You stop being the salesperson and become
the problem solver.

Give them the pen. Let them write the deal.
