3. He sold on volume, not on every last dollar.

While other dealers squeezed every sale for all it was worth, Worthington moved a gazillion cars, as he put it.

He made his fortune on sheer number and would rather win the deal and the next ten referrals than win an argument over a few hundred dollars. Price it to move and let volume carry you.

A fair deal closed beats a perfect deal lost.
