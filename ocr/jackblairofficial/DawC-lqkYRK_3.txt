3. "Everyone in your world tells you yes. That's not what you're paying me for."

A king lives inside a wall of agreement. Logue's value was being the one room where rank bought nothing, because the stammer didn't care about the crown.

Your biggest buyers are drowning in flattery from every rep chasing them. The one voice that won't perform for them becomes the only voice they actually trust.

The yes-men are free. The truth is what costs.
