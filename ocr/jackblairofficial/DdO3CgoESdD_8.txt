Here's what nobody on your floor says out loud.

It doesn't matter how good you are at this if you still have to ask permission to take a Friday off.

You can read a stranger in ten seconds. You can't pick which mornings you work. You close the deal. Someone else prices it.

There's a version of sales where that's over. The calls are booked before you sit down. You take them from home, or from wherever your family is that week. You sell something you believe in and you keep a cut of every deal you close. Nobody signs your time off. Nobody decides what you're worth but you.
