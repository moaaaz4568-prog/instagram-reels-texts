2. "Know your enemy and know yourself and you need not fear a hundred battles."

Your enemy on every call is the objection you didn't see coming.

The reps who lose deals aren't losing because the buyer wasn't ready.

They're losing because they didn't know what was coming and had nothing ready when it arrived.

Know every objection your buyer uses. Know your answer to each one cold. That's the whole game.
