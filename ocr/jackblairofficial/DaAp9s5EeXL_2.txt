2. "Where does that break down for you?"

This is the problem question. Rackham's research showed top performers spend the early call hunting for the crack, not presenting the fix.

A buyer who has not named a problem out loud has no reason to buy. The moment they say where it hurts, you stop being a salesperson and start being the person who might fix it.

No admitted problem, no sale.
