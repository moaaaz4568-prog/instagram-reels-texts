5. "Set the target clearly and let your mind steer to it."
Maltz named his idea after the Greek word for a helmsman steering a ship to port.
He believed the mind homes in on a clear goal the way a missile finds its mark. Vague wants get vague results. Define exactly what you are after and your focus and instincts quietly start aiming there.
A clear target pulls you toward it.
