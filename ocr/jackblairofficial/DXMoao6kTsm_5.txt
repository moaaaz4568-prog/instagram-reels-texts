5. "I want to be straight with you. The way this works best is if I walk you through it in order. Can I do that?"

Most reps abandon their process the moment a buyer pushes.
They skip ahead, lose context, and close on a foundation that was never built properly.

This phrase asks for permission to keep your structure intact while making it sound like a courtesy.
The buyer says yes because you asked respectfully.
The close lands because the foundation was never compromised.

Your process is your power. Protect it every single call.
