7. He got sacked from the company he
built, then started over bigger.

When Norman Ross was sold to Alan
Bond's group, Harvey was fired from his
own creation. At 43, he started Harvey
Norman.

Most would have called it the end. He
treated it as a clean page and built
something far bigger than what he lost.
When it all falls apart, the only move that
matters is what you build next.

The comeback is bigger than the fall.
