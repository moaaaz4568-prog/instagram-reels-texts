5. On a scale of 1 to 10, how ready are you?

Then the move Grant highlights from motivational interviewing. Whatever number they say, ask why they did not pick a lower one.

Now they are listing their own reasons to move. People are terrible at being argued into things and great at persuading themselves.

Your job on a close is not to make the case. It is to get them making it.
