4. "Whatever you decide today, you'll also leave with the plan on paper."

The authors' own example of a funded phrase was "also receive two". Backers were promised a second thing on top of the first, and they gave. The researchers filed it under reciprocity, and the gift was named before the money, not the audit. On a call, the gift is the thing the buyer keeps even if the answer is no: the written plan, the number you worked out for them. Say it, and say it as a certainty, not a favour.

Give the second thing before they've bought the first.
