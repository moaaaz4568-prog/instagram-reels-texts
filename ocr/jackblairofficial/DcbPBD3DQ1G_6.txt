6. "BEFORE I ANSWER, TELL ME WHAT'S BEHIND
THAT QUESTION."
The empathic read on an objection: the words are
the surface, and the worry underneath decides. The
study's great closers answered the worry.
Buy one beat before every rebuttal. The question
behind the question is usually cheaper to solve than
the one they asked.
Answer the worry and the objection files itself.
