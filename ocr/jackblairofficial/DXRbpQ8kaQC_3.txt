3. "You said yes but something in how you said it told me you are not quite there yet. What is the real hesitation?"
Patrick Jane trusted what he observed over what he was told. Most reps trust what they are told and miss everything they are being shown.
This phrase gives you permission to look past the stated answer and into the real one.
Buyers who say yes almost always confirm the observation when it is made with confidence. The yes you close on must be real. This phrase makes sure it is.
Never close on a yes that does not sound like one. Find the real answer first.
