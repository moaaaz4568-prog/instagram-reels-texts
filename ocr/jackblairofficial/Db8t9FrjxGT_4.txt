4. THE VOICEMAIL THAT EARNS A CALLBACK
"Write 3 voicemails for a [job title] who didn't pick up.
Each under 14 seconds. Lead with a problem they're
likely facing, not any. End with
just my number. No 'I'll try again later.' Make them
curious enough to call back."
Most reps leave voicemails nobody replays. Leave
one they write down.
