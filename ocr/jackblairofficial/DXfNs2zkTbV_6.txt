6. "I understand why you would choose the safer option. I am here to show you why the better one is worth the difference."

Tommy always acknowledged the case for the safer choice before making the case for himself. This phrase signals that you understand the buyer's hesitation completely and have come prepared to address it specifically.

The buyer who was leaning toward the safer choice stops feeling like they need to defend that instinct. You validated it. Now you can redirect from it. The buyer who feels understood stops defending their position and starts evaluating yours.

Validate the safe choice before you make the case against it. The buyer who feels heard opens to being redirected.
