6. The supporting role wins the Oscar before the lead does.

Mahershala didn't win Best Actor. He won Best Supporting Actor. Twice.

The supporting role demands precision. Less screen time. Less margin for error. Every scene has to land.

If you can win the supporting role of someone else's bigger play, you've built the skill the lead is built on. The supporting reps train the leading career.
