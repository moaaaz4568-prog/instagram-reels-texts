01. Prospect Profiler

"Build me a full pre-call research brief on a [job title] at [company name] in the [industry] space.
Include their likely priorities in 2026, the pressures their role carries,
3 business problems my [product or service] solves for someone in their position,
and 2 conversation openers that reference their world specifically.
Format as 4 sections, bullet points, under 20 words per point.
Tone: strategic, specific, no assumptions.
Zero guesswork."

You stop walking into calls cold. You arrive already knowing what matters to them.
