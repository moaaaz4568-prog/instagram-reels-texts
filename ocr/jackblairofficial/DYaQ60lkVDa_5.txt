5. "I never left the station empty-handed."

Huang's documented work ethic from his Denny's days.

As a 15-year-old busboy he made it a rule: every trip back to the kitchen, his hands had to be full. He carried out one tray, came back with the next. He never wasted a single trip.

He carried that habit into Nvidia for the next 40 years.
