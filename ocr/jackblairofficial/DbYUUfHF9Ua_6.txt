6. "CAN YOU GIVE IT TWO WEEKS BEFORE YOU JUDGE HOW IT FEELS?"
In 1937 nobody would touch the cart. Three years later it was on the cover of the Saturday Evening Post, and factories reportedly had a seven-year waiting list.
Tell the buyer the awkward phase has a length, then point past it. Discomfort with a deadline is tolerable. Discomfort with no end date is a reason to quit.
Strangeness dies on a schedule. Advantages don't.
