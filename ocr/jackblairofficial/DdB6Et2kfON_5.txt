5. "WHAT'S THE OPTION NOBODY IN THIS ROOM
HAS PRICED YET?"
Bard won partly on an argument Harvard admitted
they never considered. The champions had
rehearsed every standard attack. The upset came
from off the map.
Every stalled deal has an unpriced option: a phased
start, a different owner, a smaller yes. Name the one
nobody brought.
The winning option is usually the one nobody
argued.
