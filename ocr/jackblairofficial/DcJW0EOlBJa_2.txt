HALF A BILLION, ONE ROOM
When TWA wavered on the 767, Boeing sent
Boullioun to their Manhattan headquarters. He left
with an order for ten aircraft, about 500 million
dollars, and options for ten more.
Stakes that size do not change the craft. They
concentrate it: fewer meetings, realer promises, a
seller the buyer can actually reach.
The bigger the number, the smaller the circle it
trusts.
