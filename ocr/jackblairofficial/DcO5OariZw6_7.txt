ASK FOR THE DECISION THEY ALREADY MADE
By every account of the sale, the Monday
conversation was terms and payment schedule, not
persuasion. The decision had already
happened in
her mirror.
Stop reclosing buyers who are already sold. When
the trial ends, skip the recap pitch and ask one thing:
how do you want to set this up.
Collect decisions. Do not reopen them.
