CONSISTENCY IS A STORY BUYERS TELL FOR
YOU
Nobody shares a flyer about margins. Members tell
each other about the hot dog, for free, forty years
running.
Design one thing about how you sell that is so
reliably identical that clients quote it: the response
time, the recap format, the no-surprises invoice.
What never changes is what gets repeated.
