5. "WHICH THREE THINGS AM I NOT GOING TO
BRING UP?"
The feature dump is a nerves problem, not a sales
problem. Everything you add past the buyer's actual
issue dilutes the one thing that closes.
Cut the pitch to what this buyer, on this day, needs
to hear. Park the rest. If they want the tour, they'll
ask for the tour.
What you leave out is also a pitch.
