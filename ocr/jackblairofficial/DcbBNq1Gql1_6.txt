6. "YOU BUILT THIS. I JUST PRICED IT."
Ross's work shows the value of an offer travels with
its author. Handing the buyer authorship of the final
terms is not modesty. It is pricing strategy.
When you present the final version, walk through
every line they touched and credit it back to them.
Your number rides in on their structure.
Credit is the cheapest concession there is.
