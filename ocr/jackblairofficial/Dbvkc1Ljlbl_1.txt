"I'M GOING TO EXPLAIN THIS ONCE, THEN WE MOVE ON."
Nigel Owens settled one thing before anything else: who was refereeing. His best known line, to a player who started making the calls for him, was that he wasn't sure they had met, but he was the referee on that field, not the player.
Say your piece once, cleanly, then move. Explaining it twice tells the buyer the first version was negotiable.
Repeat yourself and you reopen the question.
