3. NEVER LET THEM FEEL HOW MUCH YOU NEED
THE SALE.
The second a buyer smells desperation, the power
flips and the deal is gone. Hanna never looked
like he needed anything.
Need repels. Calm detachment pulls. Act like the
deal closing or not changes nothing for you, and
watch how fast they want in.
The less you need it, the more they want it.
