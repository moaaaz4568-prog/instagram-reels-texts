5. The first check isn't the real check.

Downey was paid $500,000 for Iron Man in 2008. By Avengers: Endgame in 2019 he was reportedly paid $55 to $75 million for one film.

Over 100x in 11 years.

The first paycheck after a comeback isn't supposed to be the big one. It's the proof that makes the big one possible.
