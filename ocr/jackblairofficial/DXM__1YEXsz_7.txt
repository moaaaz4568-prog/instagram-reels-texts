7. "I think we both know what the right decision is here. Let me show you what moving forward looks like."

Ghost never asked for permission to proceed when the outcome was already clear to him. He moved with the quiet certainty of someone who had already decided how this ends.

This phrase assumes the close with grace and confidence rather than aggression. The buyer who has been on a well-built call hears this and recognises it as the natural next step. You are not pushing them toward a decision. You are walking alongside them toward a decision they already made.

Build the call correctly and the close is never a moment. It is just what happens.
