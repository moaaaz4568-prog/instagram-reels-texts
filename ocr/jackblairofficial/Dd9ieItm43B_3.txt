3. Let other people do the convincing.

Social proof and authority: people copy people who like them and defer to people who know. I asked my proof to Claude to rebuild stories a buyer recognises as himself, plus one authority line that sounds like a fact. What came back:

Three stories under 60 seconds, each ending at the moment they decided: the firm that lost a week in tax season, the partner who paid a ransom, the office manager being the help desk. The authority line: "We run IT for 31 accounting firms in Ohio."
