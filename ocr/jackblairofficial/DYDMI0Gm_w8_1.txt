1. The goal was breaking him to reach it.

Rock had wanted the NFL since he was 14. By 23 he'd been cut from Calgary in the CFL, evicted from his apartment, and was sleeping in his car in Tampa.

Wanting it harder didn't make it work. Wanting it harder made him smaller.

A goal that breaks you to chase it is the wrong goal even if you reach it.
