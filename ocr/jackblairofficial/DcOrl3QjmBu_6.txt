SPEED WITHOUT A POINT IS JUST PANIC

Fast and empty still loses. The one hour call has one job: hold the moment. Confirm the trigger, book the real conversation behind.

Keep it under four minutes. You are not closing on this call. You are freezing the buyer's moment in place until the close can happen.

The first call's product is the second call.
