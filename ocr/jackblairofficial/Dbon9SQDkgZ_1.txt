1. "BEFORE I SHOW YOU ANYTHING, TELL ME WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU DO NOTHING."

Ask it before a single feature. A buyer who has just described their own problem has started the selling for you.
Nobody buys a fix until they can see the break.
