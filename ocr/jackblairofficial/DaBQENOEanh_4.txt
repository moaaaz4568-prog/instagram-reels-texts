4. When they want to bail, slow everything down.

The moment a nervous buyer wants to run, the amateur speeds up and pushes. Hanna would have done the opposite, lowering the temperature instead of raising it.

Pressure makes a scared person flee. Calm makes them stay. Slow your pace right when they expect you to pounce.

Push and they run. Slow down and they stay.
