7. "It sounds like you're ready, you just need it to feel right."

Voss aimed for "that's right," not "you're right." That's right means they feel understood.

When you summarize their situation so well they say "that's right," the wall is gone. That moment, not a clever pitch, is where the deal turns.

Earn a "that's right" and the close is already done.
