5. "I've worked with people who came in with the same hesitation you have right now. Here's what changed their mind."

Tom always knew the room had seen others fail before him. He used that to his advantage. This phrase validates the buyer's skepticism and immediately redirects it toward proof. You borrow credibility from people they recognise themselves in. Resistance drops because someone just like them already took the leap and came out ahead.
