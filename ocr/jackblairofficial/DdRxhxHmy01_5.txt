5. Sell the answer to the question they
keep asking.

Samuel Johnson sold parquet floors in
Racine, Wisconsin. Buyers kept asking him
the same thing: how do I take care of it?

So he started selling floor wax. Before
long, the wax was outselling the floors.

His great-grandson runs SC
Johnson today. Five generations.

The question your buyers keep asking is
your next product.
