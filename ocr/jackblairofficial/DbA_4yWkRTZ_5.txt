That moment has a name. It is the close.
It lives in the gap between I'm interested
and I'll pay, where money, fear and ego all
arrive at one. Look at what sits in front of
it. The ad. The email. The booking. The
follow-up. The CRM note. The first
qualifying question. One of those is a
rule, and a rule is exactly what a machine
learns first. The last one is a room
somebody has to hold.
