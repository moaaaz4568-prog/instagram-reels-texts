1. He sold the feeling, not the machine.

The Carousel pitch never once describes the projector. Draper sold what it would let people feel again. I gave Claude my offer and ask what my buyer is really paying for. What came back:

Ten feelings ranked by the night before they buy, protection first. For the top three, the moment in the buyer's own words and the question that gets them to tell it, like "Who's the thermostat cop in your house? Tell me about the last fight over it."
