6. "HERE IS THE WRONG WAY TO PICTURE IT. A
NEW SYSTEM. HERE IS THE RIGHT WAY. THE
SAME TUESDAY, WITH ONE STEP MISSING."
The study's control groups proved the point. The
strange phrasing with no reframe sold nothing extra.
The reframe with no strange phrasing sold nothing
extra. Only the pair moved the number.
Name the picture the buyer already has, call it the
wrong one, and replace it with a smaller, more
familiar one. Removal sells better than addition.
Buyers say yes to less of what they already do.
