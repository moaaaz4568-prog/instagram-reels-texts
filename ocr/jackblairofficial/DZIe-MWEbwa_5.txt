5. She lets them underestimate her, then springs the trap.

Pearson is comfortable being doubted in a room full of people who think they are smarter.

Being underestimated is an advantage she refuses to waste. She lets opponents get comfortable and sloppy, gathers what she needs, and closes the door before they realize it was open. Surprise beats strength.

Let them feel safe right up until they are not.
