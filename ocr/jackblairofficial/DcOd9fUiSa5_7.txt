RAISE THE STAKES UNTIL DOUBT LOOKS SILLY
A quieter man would have published test data. Otis bet his body in public because the size of a stake is the size of the belief it buys.
Escalate your guarantees past where rivals will follow: the pilot free if it misses, the audit published either way. Doubt dies where stakes live.
Certainty is priced in what you risk.
