5. "I am going to follow up until I get an answer because this deal is worth that to both of us."

Roy Kent never stopped competing because the scoreboard was uncomfortable.
He competed harder when the scoreboard was uncomfortable because that was the only way it ever changed.

This phrase signals persistence with purpose and buyers respond to purpose in a way they never respond to pressure. You are not following up because you need the deal.
You are following up because you believe in the outcome enough to keep showing up for it.

Persist with purpose. The buyer who sees the purpose stops seeing the follow-up as noise.
