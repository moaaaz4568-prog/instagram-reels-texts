7. "I want to leave you with one thought that I suspect is going to stay with you after this call ends."

Reddington always made sure the last thing he said was the thing that echoed longest.
He understood that the decision is rarely made during the call. It is made in the silence after it.

This phrase signals that what comes next is worth carrying and gives the buyer permission to keep thinking about it. The thought you leave them with is the one that works while you are not in the room.
Plant it with precision. The best closes are the ones that land after you have already gone.
Own the silence after the call. That is where the decision actually lives.
