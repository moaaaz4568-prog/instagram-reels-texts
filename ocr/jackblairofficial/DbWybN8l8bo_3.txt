3. "IF YOU WERE CHOOSING FRESH TODAY,
WOULD YOU PICK THE SAME?"
This is the whole puzzle in one question. Staying is
a choice, re-made every day, and vos Savant's math
showed sticking was the bet most people got wrong.
Ask it and go silent. A yes tells you the deal isn't real
yet. A pause tells you the buyer just heard
themselves re-choose out of habit, and habit is the
only thing you're actually up against.
The incumbent's best salesman is momentum.
