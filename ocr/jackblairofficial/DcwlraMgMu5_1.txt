1. "BEFORE YOU COMPARE US TO ANYONE, LET ME DO IT FOR YOU."
Twenty one POWs chose their captors over home in 1953. William McGrue spent the panic asking why minds fold under pressure. His answer, inoculation theory: a belief survives attack only if it has rehearsed one.
Run the comparison before the competitor can. A buyer who hears the attack from you first builds the defense with you in the room.
The first version of the attack wins.
