1. "Walk me through how it works today."

Rackham's observers sat in on 35,000 calls over 12 years. The background questions, what he called situation questions, did nothing for success. Buyers found them tedious, and the reps who asked the most of them were the least experienced. Ask one. Get the picture in the buyer's words, and stop before you ask a second. Everything you need from here comes from the problem, not the setup.

Background is for you. The buyer already knows it.
