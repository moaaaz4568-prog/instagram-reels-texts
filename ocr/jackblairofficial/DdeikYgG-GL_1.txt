1. "Before I ask you anything, what made you take this call?"

Gong's 519,000-call discovery study found the winners spread their questions across the whole call. The average rep front-loads them, and the first five minutes turn into a form. Open with the one question that hands the buyer the wheel. Whatever they say first is the agenda. Leave your list alone until they've finished the sentence.

A buyer who sets the agenda answers everything else.
