7. "I will think about what you have told me and come back to you with something specific. Give me until tomorrow."

This phrase signals that you are still working on their behalf after the call ends.
It is not a delay. It is a demonstration of care that almost no rep in the market offers.

The buyer feels the weight of being genuinely considered.
They stop comparing you to the competition because the competition does not do this.
Vito Corleone never rushed an answer. He gave the right one, and people waited for it willingly.

Take the time to come back with something real. That is what turns a sale into a relationship.
