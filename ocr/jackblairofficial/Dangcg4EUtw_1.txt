1. "Let's stop trading emails. One room, one afternoon, everyone who matters."

Richard Holbrooke called it lock-up diplomacy: three warring presidents housed steps from each other at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base until the deal was done. He knew they'd never agree at a distance.

Neither will your stakeholders. Deals scattered across email threads die of exposure. Assemble the decision.

A deal that lives in an inbox dies in an inbox.
