1. The neighborhood was writing the story.

Mark grew up in Dorchester, Boston in the late 70s. The 8th of 9 kids. Father drove a delivery truck. Family on welfare for stretches.

Every kid on his block had the same likely outcome: drop out, get in trouble, work whatever jobs would have you.

Without a different room to walk into, the room you grew up in writes the rest of the script.
