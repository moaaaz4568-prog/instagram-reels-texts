4. "Start with the one store. If it moves, we talk about the flagship."

His ties didn't start on Lexington Avenue. Bloomingdale's put them in the Meadows branch out in Queen the Fresh sold, and then the flagship followed. He took the small yes and let the result make the big one. When a buyer won't sign for the whole thing, shrink the first step until they can, and put the review date on the calendar before you leave.

A small yes that works beats a big maybe.
