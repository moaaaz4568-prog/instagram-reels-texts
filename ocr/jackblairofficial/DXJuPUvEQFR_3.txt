3. "Can I show you how the last three clients who chose them ended up calling us instead?"

Ted Chaough never wasted time talking about himself when the work could talk for him.
This phrase does the same thing without a single claim you have to prove.

It invites the buyer into a story that already has an ending.
They are not hearing a pitch. They are hearing a pattern.
And patterns are the most persuasive thing a rep can put in front of a sceptical buyer.

Let your history make the argument. You just have to introduce it.
