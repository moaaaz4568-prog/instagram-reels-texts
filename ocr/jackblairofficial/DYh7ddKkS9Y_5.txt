5. “You’re one funnel away.”

The thesis of his One Funnel Away
Challenge.

He frames every product with this: “You’re
one funnel away from solving this
problem.” Pinpoint the single barrier. Sell
the solution to just that one thing. The
prospect’s brain finishes the sentence for
him.

Sell the gap. Not the entire universe of
problems.
