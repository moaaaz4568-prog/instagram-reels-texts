1. "I am going to be honest with you about where I think this is going and what I think we should do about it."

Roy Kent never dressed up a hard truth in comfortable language to make a room feel better.
He said the difficult thing because saying it was the only way to fix it.

This phrase signals a level of honesty that disarms every defensive buyer instantly.
The buyer who was expecting a pitch gets a straight assessment instead of polished presentations from someone who clearly means them are worth more than any polished presentation.

Say the hard thing first. The buyer who hears it will trust everything that comes after.
