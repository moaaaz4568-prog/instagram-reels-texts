Notice what nobody on those beds regretted: being good with people. That was never the problem. The problem was the room that skill was chained to, the floor, the territory, the schedule that ate the evenings.

The skill itself travels. Getting a stranger to trust you with a big decision works the same on a call from your kitchen floor at 8pm then as it does on a showroom floor on a Saturday.

Same ability. Different room. Completely different life. Math.
