3. "I am not going to pretend the investment is small. It is not. Neither is what you are solving."

Buyers test you on price to see if you believe what you are selling.
If you flinch, you fail the test before the sentence is over.

This phrase owns the number with no apology. It pairs the price with the problem and makes both feel equal. Now the buyer is not comparing you to cheap, they are comparing bearing cheap to the cost of standing still.

Say the number like it owes you nothing.
