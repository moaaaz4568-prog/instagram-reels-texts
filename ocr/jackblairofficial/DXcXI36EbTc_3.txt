3. "I am going to keep showing up until we either close this or you tell me directly it is not happening."

John Wick never operated in ambiguity when clarity was available.
This phrase removes every comfortable middle ground the stalling buyer has been hiding in.

The buyer who has been avoiding a decision suddenly understands that the avoidance has an endpoint.
They either engage or they close the door and either outcome moves you forward faster than the silence you were living in.
Clarity is always more valuable than a comfortable maybe.
