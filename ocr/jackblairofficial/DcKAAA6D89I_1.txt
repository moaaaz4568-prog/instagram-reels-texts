THE MYTH DIED IN THE DATA
In 2013 Psychological Science published Adam Grant’s study of 340 call centre reps and three months of real revenue. Extreme extraverts sold no more than extreme introverts.
If you were ever told you are too quiet for this job, the biggest myth in sales just left the room.
Loud was never the skill. It was the costume.
