"SHOW ME EXACTLY HOW YOU DO IT TODAY."
Feidner made buyers play before she prescribed.
Skill, taste, the gap between what they could do and
what they wanted, all of it surfaced in minutes of
playing.
Descriptions lie. What you watch them do cannot.
Get the buyer to walk you through the current way
live and the real problem introduces itself.
What they show you beats what they tell you.
