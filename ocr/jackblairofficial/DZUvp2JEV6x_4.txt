4. He met people face to face every working day for sixty years.

Even in his eighties, Fakharzadeh still booked in-person meetings every single day.

He believed insurance was sold heart to heart, never over paper, and that the sheer volume of real human contact was the one input no rival could fake or shortcut.

The calendar full of real conversations beats the one full of good intentions.
