2. "Let me back up. That came out wrong."

Gottman's biggest finding isn't about avoiding conflict. Couples heavy on destructive patterns still thrived when their repair attempts landed, 84% of them. When you feel the call turn cold, name it and reset on the spot. The repair itself builds more trust than a clean call ever would.

Deals die from unrepaired moments, not big blowups.
