6. "WHO ELSE NEEDS TO BE SURE BEFORE
YOU'D MOVE?"
The letters that stung came from PhDs, a thousand
of them, wrong together and confident together.
Groups don't get smarter when they agree, they get
louder.
So find every voice on the committee before the
vote, one at a time. A room hears its loudest doubter
first. Reach each person while their opinion is still
their own and the room never forms against you.
Win the votes before the meeting votes.
