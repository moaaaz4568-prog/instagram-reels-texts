4. You need one director fighting for you.

In 2007 Jon Favreau wanted Downey for Iron Man. Marvel didn't.

Avi Arad and the Marvel executive team thought Downey was too high-risk.

Favreau fought until Marvel agreed. They paid Downey $500,000 for the first movie.

The room that doesn't want you can be changed by one person willing to put their reputation on the line.
