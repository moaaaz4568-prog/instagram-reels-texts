7. "I'M NOT ASKING YOU FOR A DECISION. I'M ASKING FOR A GREEN LIGHT."
More than 2,000 surveys, focus groups, and dial sessions across two dozen countries showed Luntz the same recoil: people freeze at words that sound final. Decision is one of them.
Your buyer isn't stalling on the offer. They're stalling on the finality you packed into one word. Hand them a green light instead.
Decisions feel like endings. Green lights feel like motion.
