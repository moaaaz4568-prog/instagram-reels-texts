Here's the part that should hit. Ray Kroc wasn't a young hotshot. He was 52, tired, selling machines out of his car, decent at it, never rich. He'd been doing it for thirty years.

He didn't learn a new skill. He found a better vehicle for the one he already had. Same salesman, same hustle, pointed at something that could finally scale.

If you've been good at this for years and the money never matched the skill, that was never the problem. The vehicle was.
