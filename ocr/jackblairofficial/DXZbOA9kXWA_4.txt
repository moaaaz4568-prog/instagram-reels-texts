4. "I am going to let you drive this part. I want you to see how it feels rather than just watch me use it."

Stark always understood that the person controlling the technology became its most committed advocate.
The buyer who touches your solution owns it emotionally before they own it contractually.

This phrase converts a passive demo into an active experience and active experiences close at a completely different rate.
The buyer who drives the demo stops evaluating and starts experiencing.
An experienced solution is infinitely harder to walk away from than a presented one.

Put them in the seat. The buyer who experiences it themselves has already started closing.
