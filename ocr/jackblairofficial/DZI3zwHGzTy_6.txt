6. "The story you tell is the deal you get."
Goodman wins by controlling how the
situation is framed before anyone else
can.
He understood that whoever sets the
narrative sets the outcome. He tells the
version of events that points straight at
the result he wants. Frame the situation
first, in your words, and you have already
half-closed it.
Set the story and you set the ending.
