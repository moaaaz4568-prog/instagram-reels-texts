4. He heard no a thousand times and kept driving.

By his own telling, Sanders was turned away more than a thousand times before one owner said yes.

He slept in his car between stops and treated each rejection as a note, not a verdict, adjusting the pitch and the recipe at every door. The market wasn't saying never, it was saying not yet, not like that.

A no is information, right up until you let it be the ending.
