"SAY MORE ABOUT THE PART THAT WORRIES YOU."
Receptive language invites elaboration instead of racing to rebut. In the study, government executives rated receptive counterparts better advisors and teammates, mid disagreement.
When the objection is vague, do not answer the vague version. Ask for the worry underneath it and answer that one. It is usually smaller.
The stated objection is a container. The real one is inside.
