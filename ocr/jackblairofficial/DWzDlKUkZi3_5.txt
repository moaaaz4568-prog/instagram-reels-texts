5. "What about this
is most important to you?"

The calibrated question that
gives the buyer the wheel
while you steer the direction.

Voss never asked yes or no questions.
Only what and how.
Every answer the buyer gives
is a map to the close.
The rep who asks this
and stays quiet long enough
to hear the full answer
wins every time.
