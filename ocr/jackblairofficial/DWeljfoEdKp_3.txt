3. "The only question you need to answer
right now is — does this make sense
for you or not?"

Strip every call down to a single
binary decision.

Most reps let calls drift into endless
back and forth because they never ask
the real question directly.

This phrase forces a real answer.
And a real answer is always better
than a stall that goes nowhere.
