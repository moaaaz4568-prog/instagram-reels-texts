4. "YOU CAN HAVE THE PRICE RIGHT NOW IF YOU WANT IT. I'D WAIT."
Popeil held the number until the bird was carved. Not a trick, a sequence. A price named early gets judged against nothing. A price named late gets judged against everything they just watched work. When they ask early, offer it straight, then ask for two minutes of proof first. Most buyers take the trade, and now the number lands on evidence. A naked number always looks expensive.
