5. "WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THIS TO BE WORTH IT TO YOU?"
Now they build the case instead of you. Every reason they say out loud is a reason they already believe, which is more than any reason of yours will ever be.
Use it when you feel yourself about to list features. Ask this instead and stop talking.
Your reasons convince you. Theirs convince them.
