3. "I'd love to, but I can't."

Dawson taught reps to lean on a limit
instead of their own stubbornness, a
policy, a budget, a rule.

It's far harder to push against a wall than
against a person. Friendly on the outside,
immovable underneath, you hold the line
without ever picking a fight.

Blame the rule, not yourself, and the
pressure slides off.
