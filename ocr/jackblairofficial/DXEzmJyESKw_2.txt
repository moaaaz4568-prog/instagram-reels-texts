2. "That's an interesting position. Let me think about whether that works for us."

Most reps respond to every counter offer instantly. That urgency is the tell. Logan never rushed a response in his life because he knew that whoever speaks first after a number loses. This phrase buys you silence, signals strength, and makes the buyer question on whether they pushed too hard. Pause. Then answer.
