4. "There's no clock on this. The car will still be here."
Manufactured deadlines are the oldest tool on the lot, and his record year ran without one. Invented urgency reads as pure desperation.
Kill the fake clock. If a real deadline exists, show it. If it doesn't, saying so buys the trust a countdown never did.
Fake urgency spends trust fastest.
