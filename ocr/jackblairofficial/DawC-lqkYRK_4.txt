4. "Your title isn't on this call. Your problem is."

The most powerful man in the Empire stood at an open window in a rented consulting room, doing breathing drills, because rank has no purchase on a real problem.

When a big-title buyer big-times you, calmly separate who they are from what they're dealing with. They took this call because the problem doesn't answer to them.

Problems don't read business cards.
