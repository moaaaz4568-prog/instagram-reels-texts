4. "HOW'D THAT ONE TURN OUT?"
This is where the durability came from. A feeling someone re-lives sticks; an argument they nodded at is gone by Friday.
So don't rush past the story. Let them remember the fear before that change, and the payoff after. You're not building rapport. You're loading their own evidence.
The feeling lasts three months. The argument doesn't.
