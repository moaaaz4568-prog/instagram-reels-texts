5. "When you say too much, what are you comparing it to?"

Dawson called the vague squeeze "you'll have to do better than that." Reps panic and discount against nothing.

Never drop price to a fog. Make them tell you what "too much" is measured against. Half the time there's no real number behind it, and the objection quietly dissolves.

Never discount a vague objection.
