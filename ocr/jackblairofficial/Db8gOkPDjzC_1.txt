1. "YOU SHOULD ABSOLUTELY TALK TO THEM.
THEN CALL ME BACK."
This phrase is a grenade disguised as generosity.
Most reps panic as when a buyer mentions a
competitor. O'Leary would encourage it. Because
confidence is the most disruptive force in a
negotiation.
When you tell someone to go look at the competition
you're betting they'll come back. And that bet closes
harder than any discount ever could.
