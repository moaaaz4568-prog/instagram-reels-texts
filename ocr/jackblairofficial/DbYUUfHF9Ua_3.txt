3. "CAN I TELL YOU ABOUT SOMEONE WHO WAS IN YOUR EXACT SPOT SIX MONTHS AGO?"
So Goldman hired actors, men and women of every age, to push carts through his aisles posing as shoppers. Forty years later on CBS, asked if they were shills, he agreed: exactly what it was.
Your actors are real. Keep three customer stories loaded, matched by size, industry and fear, and tell the one that mirrors the buyer.
One matching story beats a hundred impressive ones.
