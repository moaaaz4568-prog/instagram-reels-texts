SHE CLOSED BOTH SIDES OF A WAR
In November 1977 she got Anwar Sadat and Menachem Begin, enemies for decades, into the first joint interview either man ever gave.
She asked each man separately, in person, within days, and framed the sit down as history rather than television.
When there are two decision makers, close them one at a time and give them one reason bigger than both.
