2. "I want to tell you a story about a company exactly like yours that almost made this mistake."

Reddington understood that stories bypasses every defence a sceptical buyer has ever built.
Logic is evaluated. Stories are experienced. And experienced things close deals that arguments never could.

This phrase wraps your value proposition inside a narrative the buyer lives through rather than judges.
By the time the story ends they have already felt the consequence of not acting.
The close was never a pitch. It was a story that ended with only one sensible conclusion.

Tell the story that makes the decision feel inevitable. Then stop talking.
