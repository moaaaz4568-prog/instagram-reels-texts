1. Can I get your advice on something?
Grant's research on influence found
advice-seeking is one of the most
persuasive moves there is, especially with
someone who outranks you.
Asking for advice turns a gatekeeper into
a stakeholder. The moment they advise
you, they are invested in you being right.
Use it on the skeptical decision maker. You
are borrowing their status, not lowering
yours.
