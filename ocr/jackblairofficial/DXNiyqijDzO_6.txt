6. "There is something I want to bring to you before someone else does."

Intelligence and timing are the two things that separate reps who hold accounts from those who lose them.
This phrase signals that you are ahead of the market on something that affects their business.

It positions you as a resource, not a vendor, and resources do not get replaced at renewal.
Avon Barksdale always moved first. Always had the information before the other side knew it existed.
The rep who brings the insight first owns the relationship that it follows it.

Move first. The account belongs to whoever shows up with the right information at the right time.
