1. "IT LOOKS LIKE PRIORITIES SHIFTED OVER THERE. DID THEY?"
Reps chase ghosts with warmer and warmer check-ins, and every rests with warmer and warmer check-ins, and every rep knows the pattern by heart. The dealmaker posture does the opposite. It names the silence on message one.
No recap, no pitch, no apology for writing. One observation, one question, send. You are not reminding them you exist. You are asking what changed.
Silence is an answer waiting for permission.
