Bernays researched the desire under the desire. People asked for one thing and wanted another, and he sold to the real want.

You look past the surface objection to the actual driver, the fear of a bad call, the pressure from mom above, the last vendor who burned them, and you speak to that. The stated reason is almost never the real one.
