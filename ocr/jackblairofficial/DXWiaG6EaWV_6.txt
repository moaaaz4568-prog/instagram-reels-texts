6. "I do not need you to take my word for it. Here is what the outcome looks like. You can decide what that means."

Plainview built his reputation on results so visible that persuasion became unnecessary.
He showed and let the showing do every piece of work the argument would have done worse.

This phrase removes you from the position of advocate and places you in the position of evidence provider.
The buyer stops evaluating your pitch and starts evaluating your proof.
Proof closes at a level no pitch has ever reached.

Show the outcome. Step back. Let it close the deal without your help.
