4. He treated reading as a sales weapon.

Jones built a personal library and
preached that leaders are readers.

Every book handed him a new way to
understand a buyer, frame a value, or
dismantle an objection, while his
competitors winged it on instinct. The
seller who keeps learning always has one
more angle than the one who doesn't.

Read more than your competition and
you'll always have an answer they don't.
