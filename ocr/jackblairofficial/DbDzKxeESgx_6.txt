6. "I'd rather you hear the cost of doing nothing from me than find it out later."

The Treatment always carried a quiet sense of what standing still would cost. Never a threat. Just the price of no, made visible next to the price of yes.

You name the cost of the status quo out loud, calmly. Now the buyer stops comparing you to zero and starts comparing you to waiting.

Make staying put the expensive option.
