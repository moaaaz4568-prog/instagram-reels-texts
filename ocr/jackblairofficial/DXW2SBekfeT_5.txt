5. "I am going to leave this with you. When you are ready to move forward you know where I am."

Chuck Bass never waited in anyone's lobby.
He made the other side come to him and they always did because he made leaving feel more expensive than staying.

This phrase is the most powerful close available for the buyer who needs to feel in control of the timeline.
You remove yourself from the pressure entirely and suddenly the buyer feels the absence of it.
The buyer who was resisting the push almost always moves the moment the push disappears.

Release the deal. The buyer who feels no pressure almost always applies it to themselves.
