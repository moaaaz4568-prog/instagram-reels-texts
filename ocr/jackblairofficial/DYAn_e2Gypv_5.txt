5. The first time Bill knocked on a new door, he'd usually get a no.

He didn't argue. Thanked them, handed them a brochure, put them on his route for the next month.

Then the next month. And the next.

By the sixth knock, the no was usually a yes. Sometimes the eighth.

The door that closes today opens by month six if you keep showing up.
