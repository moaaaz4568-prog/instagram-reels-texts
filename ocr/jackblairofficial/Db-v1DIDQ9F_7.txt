SILENCE.
Wintour's most powerful tool is not a phrase at all. It
is the pause. The unreadable expression behind
dark glasses.
She lets silence sit until the other person reveals
their real position. She never rushes to fill it.
Be comfortable in silence. Fill every gap
and you give the room away.
