5. "I want to show you the numbers on this because I think when you see them the decision becomes a lot simpler."

Baum always brought the data when the room was operating on assumption and comfortable fiction. This phrase signals that what follows is not opinion but evidence and evidence changes rooms that arguments cannot move.

The buyer who was deliberating on instinct suddenly has a concrete basis for the decision they were stalled on. Numbers remove the subjectivity from a close and subjectivity is where delay lives. The rep who brings the right data at the right moment closes deals that were stalled purely on uncertainty.

Bring the numbers. The buyer who sees the evidence almost always stops deliberating and starts deciding.
