2. "BE SUSPICIOUS OF ANYTHING I HAND YOU FOR FREE."
The ugly half of the finding: a concession nobody asked for reads as a tell. The buyer's brain asks what you know that they don't.
Say it before they think it. Naming the suspicion out loud drains it, and it tells them the next thing you give was pulled out of you, not planted.
Nobody trusts a gift from across the table.
