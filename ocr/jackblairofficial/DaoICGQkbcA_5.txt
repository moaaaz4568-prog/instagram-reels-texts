5. "Can I ask you something blunt? Because guessing wastes your time more than asking does."

Hard questions get denied at the door when they arrive alone. Ride the door with them and they get permission instead. The because tells the buyer the bluntness serves them, not you, and the question that would have stalled the call becomes the one that moves it.

The because is the passport that gets hard questions across the border.
