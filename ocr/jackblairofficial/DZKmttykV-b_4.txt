4. "Give a new self-image 21 days to set."

Maltz observed it took his patients about 21 days to adjust to a new face, and the same for a new mental image.

He warned that people quit a new identity in days, long before it takes hold. Commit to seeing yourself as a closer for three steady weeks before you judge it. The image needs time to harden into instinct.

Hold the new picture long enough to keep it.
