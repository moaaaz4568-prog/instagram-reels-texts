1. He started over at the age he was
supposed to retire.

At 65, his restaurant bankrupted by a new
interstate that rerouted his customers,
Sanders cashed a $105 social security
check and decided it was a starting line,
not a finish.

He had a recipe and a station wagon, so
he hit the road to sell the one asset failure
couldn't repossess, his skill.

It's never too late to start the one thing
you were always good at.
