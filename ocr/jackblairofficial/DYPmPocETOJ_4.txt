4. The public hated your hiring, and was wrong.

When Daniel was cast as Bond in 2005, the British press lost their minds. "Daniel Cra-bland." "Blond Bond." Petitions to recast circulated before he filmed a scene.

Then Casino Royale grossed $616 million. Highest-grossing Bond film at that point.

The thing critics called wrong is usually what the right room calls signature.
