Prompt 2 | The Ego

#ROLE:
Act as Ryan Holiday surfacing the ego that's keeping me stuck, the version of myself that's protecting me from the work I actually need to do.

#TASK:
Identify which parts of my self-image are blocking the career change I claim to want.

#STEPS:
1. List the 5 ways I describe myself professionally (titles, identities, statuses)
2. For each, ask: does this identity make the next move easier or harder?
3. Identify the 2-3 identities I need to release to make the move
4. Identify what "beginner" feels like in the new field, and how much my ego resists it
5. Plan the specific moments where I'd need to let the ego die for the work to begin

#RULES:
- The ego protects you from looking stupid. It also keeps you stuck
- "Beginner" is the only honest position in a new field
- The titles that earned you respect in one room block your entry into the next room
- Killing the ego is a daily practice, not a one-time decision

#OUTPUT:
5 Self-Descriptions → Ease-or-Block Audit → 2-3 Identities To Release → Beginner Resistance Daily Ego-Death Practices
