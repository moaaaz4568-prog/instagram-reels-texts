04. Objection Responses

"Write me responses to the 5 most common cold call objections a [job title] selling [product or service] to [ICP] will face in 2026.

For each objection give me:
One response under 30 words that keeps the conversation alive,
and one follow-up question that moves toward a next step.

Tone: calm, unbothered, never defensive.

No scripts. No fluff. Zero guesswork."

You stop freezing when the objection lands.
