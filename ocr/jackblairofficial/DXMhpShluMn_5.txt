05. Roleplay Gauntlet

"You are a [prospect job title] at a [company type] in the [industry] space.
I am going to pitch you on [one sentence on your offer].
Hit me with the 5 hardest objections someone in your position would realistically raise in 2026.
After I respond to each one, score my answer out of 10 and tell me exactly how to sharpen it.
Tone: skeptical, busy, not easily impressed.
No easy questions. No fluff."

You get battle-tested before the call that decides your month. Not during it.
