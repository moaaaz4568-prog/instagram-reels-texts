"TELL ME THE PART YOU'RE NOT SOLD ON YET."
His transcripts show people rarely refuse outright.
They go quiet on the one piece they reject, and the
silence gets read as consent for the whole thing.
Name that you expect one, then wait. The gap is
where the real objection lives.
Silence on one point is not agreement on all of them.
