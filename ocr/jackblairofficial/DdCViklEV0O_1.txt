1. "THE FIRST JAR IS ON ME. YOU'LL BE BACK FOR THE SECOND."
In 1946 Saks gave her an eight hundred dollar order, her first real shelf. She mailed to the store's preferred customers a card in gold lettering promising a free gift with any purchase. The stock sold out in two days.
Lead with a real piece of the product, not a discount. A free taste creates a customer. A discount creates a negotiation.
What you give away sells what you charge for.
