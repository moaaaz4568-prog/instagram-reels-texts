7. "Who sat in this seat before me, and where did they go?"

The desk knows. If the last three people in your seat went to other stores, the seat is the problem and the store already knows it. If they went to F&I, the desk, or a better plan across the street, that is the map. Ask for names.

The seat has a history. Read it before you give it another year.
