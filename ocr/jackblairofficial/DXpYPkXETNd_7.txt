7. "I am going to do exactly what I said I would do. You have my word on that."

This is Omar Little's entire philosophy delivered in a single sentence.
No conditions. No qualifications. No fine print. Just a commitment made by someone whose word has always been enough.

The buyer who receives this from a rep who then delivers on it exactly as promised stops treating the relationship as transactional and starts treating it as something worth protecting.
The relationship worth protecting is always the one that renews, refers, and returns.

Give your word. Keep it exactly. The buyer who experiences that once never needs to be closed again.
