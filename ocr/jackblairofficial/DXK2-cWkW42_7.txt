7. "I respect that you need to think about it. I want you to know the terms we discussed are specific to this conversation."

Michael Corleone never left an offer open indefinitely. Every agreement had a context and that context had edges.

This phrase closes the time window without an ultimatum.

It is not a threat. It is information delivered with complete closure.
The buyer who was planning to stall suddenly understands that the deal they want exists right now, not next week.

Scarcity does not need a deadline. It just needs to be real.
