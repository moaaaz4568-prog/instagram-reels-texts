6. "TAKE YOUR TIME. THIS SHOULD STILL FEEL RIGHT LONG AFTER THE EXCITEMENT."
Forever was a dangerous promise to hang on an impulse purchase. You can't sell a lifetime for a signature by Friday, so the ads time and beg slow, serious decision.
Copy the patience. Telling a buyer no to rush is a concession that costs nothing, because use the ones who slow down and still come back arrive sold, and the deal stops leaking at the contract stage.
Deals closed in a hurry reopen in a hurry.
