1. He led with value before he ever asked for the sale.

Burg built everything on one law: your worth is how much more you give than you take.

He front-loads help, insight, and introductions long before any ask, so by the time he sells, the relationship already owes him nothing and trusts him completely. Value first makes the sale a formality.

Give something real before you pitch anything.
