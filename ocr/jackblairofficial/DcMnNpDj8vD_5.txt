LOSE WHERE THE BUYER IS WATCHING

The food court loses so the membership renews.
The visible loss is the cheapest trust the company
buys.

Give your version away where it shows: the audit
before the contract, the fix that was not billable, the
answer that cost you a deal. Visibly.

Trust is bought where the buyer can see the receipt.
