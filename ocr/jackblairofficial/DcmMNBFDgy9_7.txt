7. "WHAT'S ONE SMALL STEP YOU COULD TAKE?"
Not "are you ready to sign." Pantalon ends on the smallest safe move, because a giant ask triggers the flinch and a tiny one gets a yes.
And the first small yes is the hardest one to walk back.
End on the smallest step, not the biggest ask.
