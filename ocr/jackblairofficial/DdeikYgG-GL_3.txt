3. "What have you already tried?"

The same discovery data tied deal outcomes to one kind of question: the ones about the buyer's own problems, goals and what's in the way. Not the ones about your product. This gets the whole history in one shot: what failed, what it cost, who chose it. Let them tell it as a story. Don't interrupt it with your fix.

Every failed fix is a reason to buy yours.
