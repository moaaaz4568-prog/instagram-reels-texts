1. "Before I say a word, look at my hair. Two years ago it was falling out in my hands."

Denver, 1905. A laundress with $1.50, a jar of hair grower she mixed herself, and a scalp that had been going bald. She sold door to door and the proof came first: her own head, before and after, later printed in every ad she ran. Nobody had to believe a promise. They had to look. Open with the result you can show, not the product you want to describe.

Proof you can see beats a promise they have to trust.
