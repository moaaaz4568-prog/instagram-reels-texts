7. "I know I can do this. I have done it before and I remember exactly how."

This is Bombay stepping back onto the ice for the first time after everything felt apart.
Not certainty borrowed from the future but certainty borrowed from the past.

This phrase activates the rep's existing record of success at the exact moment doubt is loudest.
The slump makes reps forget that they have closed before, that buyers have said yes before and that they know how to make it happen.
This phrase is the reminder that breaks the pattern and breaks it completely.

Remember what you already know. The slump convinces you that you forgot. You never did.
