3. He made the deal easy to say yes to.

Harvey built his retail empire on stripping out the friction that stops a buyer, like easy finance and no-deposit offers.

He understood most sales die on the hurdle between wanting and affording. So he cleared the path and let people say yes today. Clear the obstacles out of the buyer's way and a maybe becomes a sale far more often.
