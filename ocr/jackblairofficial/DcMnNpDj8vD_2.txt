THE FOUNDER'S ONE RULE
When the CEO said the price had to rise, founder
Jim Sinegal answered with the most famous
sentence in retail: if you raise the effing hot dog, I
will kill you. Figure it out.
Notice what he protected. Not a product line. The
one signal that tells every customer the whole
warehouse can be trusted on price.
Guard the promise that vouches for the rest.
