6. "THE BEST DEAL IN THE ROOM IS THE ONE THAT MAKES YOU MONEY."

Price resistance is never about money. It's about uncertainty.

This phrase kills uncertainty by redirecting focus to return on investment. The buyer stops seeing a cost and starts seeing a bet they'd be stupid not to make.

Expensive is an opinion. ROI is math.
