5. "THAT MACHINE SITS DARK ALL NIGHT.
WANT IT TO EARN ITS KEEP?"
EDS's first business bought the midnight hours on
other companies' idle computers and sold that time
to clients who needed work done. He turned a cost
nobody was watching into somebody's inventory.
Find what the buyer already owns that sits unused.
Budget, machine hours, a clause in an old contract.
Deals close faster when half the resources are
already in the room.
The easiest sale is the one they already paid for.
