2. "WANT THE WHOLE THING IN ONE SENTENCE
BEFORE WE GO DEEP?"
The tappers failed because they could hear the tune
while they tapped. The curse of knowledge: once
you know a thing, you cannot imagine not knowing
it.
Lead with the one-sentence version a stranger could
repeat. Depth that arrives before clarity just sounds
like tapping on wood.
Clarity first. Depth is a privilege you earn.
