2. See why the cap exists and you stop blaming yourself for it.

A commission plan is built so the house keeps the upside. You get a percentage. They keep the deal, the client, the renewal, and the book of business you built.

Work twice as hard and you nudge the number. The structure will not let you move it far.

You are not underperforming. You are performing perfectly inside a box.
