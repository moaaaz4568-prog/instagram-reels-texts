5. Two years before a building existed, he was already selling it.

Met with the architect in the empty lot. Walked the developer through who the buyer would be. Built a buyer list from sketches and renderings before the foundation was poured.

By the time the building opened, half the units were already under contract.

The closer who can sell the future before it's built doesn't compete with anyone selling the present.
