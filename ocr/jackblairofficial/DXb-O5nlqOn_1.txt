01. Referral Spotter

"I have the following existing clients:
[paste client names, industries or job titles].

For each client identify:
The 3 most likely types of people in their network
who would benefit from [one sentence on your offer],
why they are likely to know these people specifically,
and one conversation starter I can use with my client
to naturally bring up an introduction.

Format as a client breakdown.

Tone: strategic, specific, no generic suggestions.

Zero guesswork."

You stop guessing
who your clients know.
You start walking in to every referral conversation already knowing
who to ask for.
