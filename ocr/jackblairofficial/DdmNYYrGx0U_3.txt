3. A choice, not a discount.

"Pay it in full. Pay it monthly. Or keep the money and keep the seat. Which of those three is yours?" When the buyer pushes, Harvey doesn't defend. He lays out the paths and lets them pick. I asked for my ten most common objections that came back:

"I need to think about it." "Think about it here with the real question named, or alone for a week and run the demo next Tuesday. Which one?" Nine more, ranked, plus the two where the right move is to walk.
