THEY SAY:
"It's a little out of my budget."

WHAT THEY MEAN:
I don't see enough value to justify the number.

HOUSE SAYS:
"Budget is rarely the actual issue. If I cut the price in half right now, would you move forward today? If the answer isn't an immediate yes, we both know this isn't about money."

Price is the most honest-sounding lie in sales.
