2. He chased momentum over the perfect start.

McGrath struggled for months before his first sale on Norfolk Street in Paddington, and says that one deal saved his career.

He did not wait to feel ready. He kept moving until one win led to the next. Momentum, not talent, carried him. When you are stuck, stop waiting for the perfect moment and put the first one on the board.

One sale breaks the slump. Go get it.
