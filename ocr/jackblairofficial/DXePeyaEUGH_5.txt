5. "I am not the same rep I was six months ago. Let me show you what that means for you."

Bombay's comeback was not a return to who he was. It was an arrival at who he had become.
That distinction is everything for a rep rebuilding after a difficult period.

This phrase signals growth without explaining the difficulty that produced it.
The buyer who hears it gets curiosity rather than a history lesson.
And curiosity about what changed is always more powerful than sympathy for what was hard.

Signal the growth without explaining the fall. The buyer who sees the evolution closes toward it.
