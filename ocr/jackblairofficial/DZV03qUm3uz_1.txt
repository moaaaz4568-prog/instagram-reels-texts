1. He started over at 52 with his savings and a shed.

After two decades climbing to VP of sales at Fuller Brush, Beveridge walked away and, at 52, opened his own company in a rented tobacco shed in the middle of the Depression.

Everyone his age was protecting what they had. He bet it on what he knew.

The skill that got you here is the one asset worth betting on again.
