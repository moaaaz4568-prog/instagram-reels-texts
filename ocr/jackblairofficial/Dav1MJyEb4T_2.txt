2. "If easy worked, you wouldn't be on this call."

Garfield High had already tried easy. Low expectations, watered-down classes, a school that nearly lost its accreditation. Escalante's offer was the opposite of comfortable, dawn sessions and Saturdays and summer school, and the hard version is the one that finally moved the numbers.

When a buyer flinches at the effort, don't soften it. Point at the easy attempt already behind them.

Easy already had its chance.
