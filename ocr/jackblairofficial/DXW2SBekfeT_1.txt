1. "I want to be straightforward with you. This is not for everyone and I am not here to convince you it is."

Chuck Bass never chased anyone who needed convincing. He made scarcity the opening position and let the buyer's ego do the rest.

This phrase signals that you have standards for who you work with and the buyer in front of you has not automatically qualified.

The entitled buyer who has dismissed every other rep suddenly finds themselves in the unfamiliar position of needing to qualify for you.
