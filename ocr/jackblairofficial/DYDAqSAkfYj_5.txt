5. The fight he watched changed everything.

March 24, 1975. Sly watched Chuck Wepner, a no-name club fighter from New Jersey, last 15 rounds with Muhammad Ali on TV.

Wepner had the skill. He'd had it for years. He just didn't have the vehicle.

That night gave him 15 minutes of being unbeatable.

The skill is universal. The vehicle decides if anyone ever sees it.
