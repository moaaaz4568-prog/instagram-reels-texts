7. "You don't need more information.
You need the one answer you came for.
Ask me it."

Late-stage stalls almost never come from
missing facts. They come from one
unasked question the buyer's been
circling all call. More information just gives
it more places to hide.

Say this calmly at the stall and wait. What
comes out is the close.

The last question in the room is the deal.
