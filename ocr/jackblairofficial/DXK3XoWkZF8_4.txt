4. "I want to make sure whatever we do together is actually right for you. If it is not, I will tell you."

Vito Corleone's power came from the fact that his word was worth something.
People trusted him because he had never given them a reason not to.

This phrase plants that same seed in a single sentence. The buyer hears that you are not here to sell. You are here to solve.
That posture makes every recommendation you make from that point forward land with the weight of advice, not pitch.

The rep who would walk away from the wrong deal is the rep every buyer wants to call back.
