5. "I would rather have a difficult conversation with you now than lose the right to have any conversation later."

This is the phrase for the account that is slipping and both sides know it.
Most reps avoid the tension and lose the account to the silence they created.

Avon Barksdale faced every challenge to his territory directly because he understood that avoidance is surrender.
This phrase opens the hardest conversation with the most disarming honesty available.
The buyer who hears this almost always stays in the room long enough to give you a real chance.

Say the hard thing first. It is always cheaper than losing the account.
