7. "I am going to stop here and ask you something. Based on what you have seen so far what is your honest reaction?"

Stark always wanted real-time data on how the room was receiving what he was showing. He never waited until the end to find out he had lost them in the middle.

This phrase mid-demo is the most underused in any rep's toolkit.
The buyer who answers honestly gives you the objection while you still have the screen in front of you to address it. Now you are not handling an objection after the demo. You are solving it inside it.

Check the room mid-demo. The objection caught early is the only one you can close against in real time.
