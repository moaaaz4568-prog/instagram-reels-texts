THE BEST LINE WAS NEVER AN AD.
It was the buyer’s survival plan, said out loud.
For twenty years the most feared sentence in B2B
sold blame insurance, not machines, and it still runs
every committee you pitch. Software can flood a
market with claims. It cannot read which person at
the table is scared for their job, say the risk out loud
without flinching, and hand them the alibi that lets
the deal move. Reps who can sell safety in the room
set the price of it.
No product fulfillment. No cold calling, all from a
laptop. 👇
