2. “Let’s look at what staying where you are is already costing you.”

Kahneman showed that losing something hurts about twice as much as gaining the same thing feels good. Loss does the heavy lifting.

Frame the decision around what they keep bleeding by doing nothing, not just what they’d gain. The pain of the leak moves people.

Make the cost of staying louder than the cost of moving.
