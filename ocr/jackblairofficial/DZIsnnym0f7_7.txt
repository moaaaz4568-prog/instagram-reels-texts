7. He wants it more than anyone in the building.
The whole speech is built on one thing.
Blake simply wants it more than the men
sitting in front of him.
Skill can be matched. Scripts can be
copied. Raw, relentless want is the one
edge most people quietly give up on. The
closer who wants the outcome more than
the prospect, and more than the
competition, usually walks out with it.
Outwant the room and you win it.
