6. "I can work with that.
Here's what I need in return."

The reciprocity close
Stringer used on everyone
he ever negotiated with.

Never give without asking.
Never ask without giving.

The rep who makes concessions
with nothing attached
trains the buyer to keep pushing.

Give something. Get something.
Close on the exchange.
