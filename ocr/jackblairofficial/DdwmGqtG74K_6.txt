6. "I've decided I want to do this with you. Can we start?"

In 2002 she was vice chairman of her firm and believed her career had peaked. So she cold called the CEO of CBRE, the biggest real estate firm in the world, and asked him for a job. She has run its New York region ever since. That's the whole ask, and she made it without a warm-up. When you've earned the close, say it that plainly. Skip the slide, the soft landing and the "does that make sense." Tell the buyer you want them, and ask.

The direct ask only feels awkward to the people who never make it.
