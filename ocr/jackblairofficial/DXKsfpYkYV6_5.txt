5. "You can think it over. I just want you to know that thinking it over is also a decision."

Every rep has lost a deal to the phrase "I need to think about it."
Most respond by saying "of course, take your time." That is how the deal dies.

This phrase respects their process and names its consequence in the same breath.
No pressure. No ultimatum. Just clarity that inaction is still a choice.
The buyer who understands that rarely chooses to keep losing quietly.

Name the cost of stillness. Then let them decide.
