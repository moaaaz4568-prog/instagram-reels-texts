3. "Forget the other one. If it were only this, would you take it?"

A second study put a Sony player on a one day sale at $99. Sixty six percent of people bought it. Then the researchers added a top of the line Aiwa at $159 next to it. Sony sales fell to twenty seven percent, and forty six percent walked out with nothing. Two good options did not double the sales. They produced a no. When a buyer is stuck between your two packages, take one off the table and recommend the other by name.

Two good options is how a buyer buys nothing.
