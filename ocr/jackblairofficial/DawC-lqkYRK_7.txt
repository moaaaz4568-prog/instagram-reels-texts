7. "I can't want this more than you do."

Logue's method only worked if the Duke King did the daily hour himself. The King owned the work, and within a year he was writing to Logue from a ship, almost bragging that the old fear didn't worry him anymore.

When a buyer wants the outcome without the engagement, name the ownership plainly. It turns spectators into participants, or disqualifies them before they cost you a quarter.

A buyer who owns the work keeps the result.
