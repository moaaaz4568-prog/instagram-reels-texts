BEING RIGHT WAS NEVER THE JOB
He was committed to an asylum in 1865 and dead in
two weeks at 47, from an infection, the very thing he
had proven how to stop. The idea won in decades
later, after Pasteur.
The lesson is not that good ideas lose. It is that
ideas do not sell themselves, ever, at any level of
proof. Somebody has to sell them.
Being right is the entry fee. Selling it is the job.
