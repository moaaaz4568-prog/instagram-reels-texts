2. He went door to door to sell people on
the idea of a bank.

Immigrants in 1904 kept their money
under the mattress. They did not trust
banks, and banks did not want them.

Giannini personally walked the
neighborhoods, knocking on doors,
explaining what a savings account was
and why it would help their family.

The closer who goes to the buyer beats
the rep who waits for the buyer to walk in.
