1. "THAT ONE'S ON ME, START TO FINISH."
1
Roy Lewicki tested apologies on 755 people at Ohio State and one component buried the other five: acknowledgment of responsibility. Not regret. Not explanation. Ownership.
So when the demo breaks or the deadline slips, say it before they've decided whose fault it was. First sentence of the call. The longer ownership waits, the more it looks negotiated.
Ownership is the apology. Everything else is delivery.
