7. "THIS GOES AS ONE PIECE OR IT DOESN'T GO.
HERE'S WHY THAT PROTECTS YOU."

Split apart, every piece of a package went back to
being shoppable piece of a package and every deal went
back to committee. The refusal was the product.

Hold that line once and your next three buyers hear
about it. Break it once and your next three once and every future call starts
with a request for future pieces.

What you unbundle once stays unbundled.
