1. "I want to make sure this is right for both of us before we go any further."

The moment you show need, the buyer owns the negotiation. Logan Roy never needed anything from anyone, at least not visibly. This phrase signals that you are evaluating them as much as they are evaluating you. The power shifts before they even realise it happened. Equal footing is still footing you control.
