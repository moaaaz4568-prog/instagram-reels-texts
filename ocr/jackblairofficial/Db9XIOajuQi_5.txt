5. “I’M NOT GOING TO HOLD THIS AND HOPE YOU GET BACK TO ME. THAT’S NOT HOW I WORK.”
Massarelli doesn’t wait. This phrase makes that philosophy the buyer’s problem.
It tells them the train is leaving and you’re not the conductor, you’re a passenger too. The urgency isn’t manufactured. It’s structural.
You’re busy. Your calendar is full. Waiting isn’t something you offer. That’s not pressure. That’s respect for your own time.
