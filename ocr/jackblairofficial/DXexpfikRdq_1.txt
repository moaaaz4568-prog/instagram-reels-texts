1. "I want to have a different kind of conversation with you today. Not about the contract. About whether we are actually working for you."

Jerry Maguire's entire arc was about choosing the relationship over the transaction.
That choice made he him the only rep his client trusted when it mattered most.

This phrase signals to a drifting client that this call is not a retention exercise dressed up as a check-in.
The client who was preparing to leave suddenly finds themselves in a conversation worth having.
You are not fighting to keep the account. You are fighting to deserve it.

Choose the relationship over the renewal. The client who sees that distinction almost always stays.
