KILL THE PIVOT
The flip fires hardest at the turn, the moment warm
chat becomes pitch. The paper calls it a change of
meaning: everything before the turn gets reread as
setup.
Stop building rapport as a runway. Open with the
business, and let the human part happen inside the
work, where it cannot be reread as a move.
Warmth before the ask gets audited. Warmth inside
the work does not.
