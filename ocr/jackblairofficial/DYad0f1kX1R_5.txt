5. "Give the customer more for their money."

Master P's documented production principle.

Most rap albums had 15 tracks. He put 20 on every No Limit album. He gave fans Marvel-comic-book-style covers, branded merchandise, video games, and clothing. He cross-promoted every artist on the label inside every cover.

He earned the second purchase by overdelivering on the first.
