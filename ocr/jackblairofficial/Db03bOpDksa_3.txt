NOTHING EXPIRES AT MIDNIGHT. WHAT RUNS OUT IS ROOM ON THE CALENDAR.
Worchel’s sharper finding came from the same experiment: cookies that BECAME scarce, ten falling to two in front of the rater, beat cookies that started scarce. The drop itself does the persuading.
So narrate depletion as it happens. Two spots became one this morning is arithmetic, not a tactic, and it lands because you didn’t have to dress it up.
Real scarcity survives a fact check.
