2. "You have probably seen every version of what I am about to show you. Tell me what none of them got right."

This phrase does something most reps never attempt.
It makes the buyer the expert before they say a single thing about your product.

They feel respected. They feel heard before they have spoken.
And what they tell you next is the exact roadmap to your close.
You did not pitch. You listened your way into position.

Let the buyer hand you the key. Then open the door.
