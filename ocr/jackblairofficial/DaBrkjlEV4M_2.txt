2. Talk so plainly a kid could follow.
Iacocca's rule was simple. Say it in plain
words or you don't really understand it
yourself, and a confused buyer never buys.
Jargon is where deals go to die. The
clearer you make the decision, the easier it
is for them to say yes.
Make it simple and the yes gets simple
too.
