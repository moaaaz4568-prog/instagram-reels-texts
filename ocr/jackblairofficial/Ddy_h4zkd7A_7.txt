7. "I'm trusting you'll see the value in this."

That one predicted failure. So did "not been able", "hope to get" and "even a dollar", which the authors said reads as grovelling for money. The pitches that asked to be trusted, hedged, or begged, lost. The funded ones never asked for trust. They gave the reason trust was unnecessary: a date, a number, a name. Cut every "trust me", "honestly" and "hopefully" from your calls, and put the fact you were protecting in its place.

Asking for trust is admitting you haven't earned it.

Now look at where all seven came from. A web page, a stranger, and a pitch with the right words in it.

Sound familiar?
