2. “I’m not sure we can help yet. Mind if I ask a few questions?”

Sandler taught reps to drop the eager pitch and take the posture of a doctor, not a salesman.

Admitting you might not be able to help flips the dynamic, now the buyer is the one trying to qualify for you. Chasing repels, mild detachment attracts.

The second you stop needing the sale, they start wanting it.
