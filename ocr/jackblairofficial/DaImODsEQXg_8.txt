Here's the uncomfortable part. You've heard every one of these. On real calls, from real people, probably this week. And you talked right past most of them.

Not because you're bad. Because nobody trains you to hear the yes, they train you to pitch louder. The best closers aren't the smoothest talkers. They're the ones who shut up at the exact right second and let the buyer land.
