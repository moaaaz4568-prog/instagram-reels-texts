7. "Between the two, which one would you keep?"

Asked which parent should get custody, sixty four percent picked the parent with the bigger positives and the bigger flaws. Asked which should be denied custody, most picked the same parent. Call, "which would you drop" sends the buyer hunting for flaws, and yours option, which they can see. Ask which they would keep, and they argue for the stronger option, which is yours.

The question you ask decides which facts they weigh.

Now look at every buyer you have marked "thinking about it". Not one is waiting for information.

Sound familiar?
