DISCOVERY IS THE ANTIDOTE
Forer's students believed because nobody asked
them a single question. The sketch only had to feel
right, never be right.
Real discovery produces sentences no astrology
can fake: their words, their numbers,
read back with dates attached.
What you learned beats what you guessed, every
time.
