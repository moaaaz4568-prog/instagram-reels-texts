7. "Let us get this done today."
Umansky built his name as the man who
actually closes.
When a buyer is ready, he does not let the
moment cool overnight. He names the
next step plainly and moves, because a
deal left to drift is a deal that dies. Your
certainty becomes their certainty.
When they are ready, close. Do not admire
the deal, do it.
