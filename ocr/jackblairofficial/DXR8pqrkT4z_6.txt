6. "I am not asking you to forget what happened. I am asking you to give me the chance to make it right."

Winston Wolf understood that redemption ask, not a general apology. This phrase makes the ask precise and frames it as an opportunity for the buyer, not a favour to you.

The buyer who grants it has already decided to stay in the conversation. That decision is the close beginning to rebuild itself. You are not back where you started. You are somewhere the other rep never reached.

Make the specific ask. The buyer who grants it has already chosen you again.
