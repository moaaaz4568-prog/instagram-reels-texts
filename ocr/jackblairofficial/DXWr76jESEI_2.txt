02. Ghost Breaker

"Write me 3 re-engagement messages for a prospect who has not replied to my last [number] attempts.

Last interaction: [paste what was said or what stage they reached].
My offer: [one sentence].

Each message must:
Open with a new angle or fresh insight,
add one piece of value they did not have before,
and end with one question so easy to answer
they would feel odd not replying.

Under 60 words each.

No referencing previous messages.

Tone: warm, direct, zero desperation.

Zero guesswork."

The prospect who ghosted you just got a reason to come back.
Not a follow-up. An actual reason.
