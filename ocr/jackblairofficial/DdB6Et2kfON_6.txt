6. "ARGUE ME OUT OF IT. IF YOU CAN'T, THAT'S YOUR ANSWER."

Debaters win by inviting the counterattack, because an argument that survives contact is the only kind a judge trusts. Barn trained on their own weakest points first.

Hand the buyer the floor to kill your proposal. Objections you invited arrive smaller than objections you dodged.

An offer that survives their best shot closes itself.
