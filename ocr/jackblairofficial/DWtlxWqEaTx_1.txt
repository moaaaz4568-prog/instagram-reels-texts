1. "I could show you the best solution on the market today. Are you in a position to move forward if it makes sense for you?"

Tracy calls this the demonstration close, and it works from the very first sentence.

The conversation is no longer about whether they listen to you.

It's about whether they're ready to buy from you if you hold up your end.

You controlled the frame before you said a single thing about your product.
