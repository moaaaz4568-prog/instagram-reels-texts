"IF WE'RE INTERCHANGEABLE WITH YOUR OTHER QUOTES, TAKE THE CHEAPEST ONE."
Peter Kraljic's model grades every purchase on two axes, how much it affects profit and how risky it is to source. Land in the easy to replace corner and grinding your price is literally the buyer's job.
Say it early and mean it. Then find out whether they actually believe you are interchangeable, because that is the only question on the table.
You cannot out-argue being replaceable. You can only stop being it.
