6. "Before I close out your file, something's been bugging me about your situation."

The reopener for a buyer who's gone dark. "Closing the file" threatens to end a loop they secretly wanted open, and the unfinished thing that's "bugging you" hands them a brand new one. Two tensions in one sentence. Ghosts answer it.

Nobody lets you close a loop they wanted open.
