5. "What happened the last time you brought them something like this?"

Assimilation: the message bends toward what the listener already believes. Your pitch won't be heard fresh, it'll be heard through the last thing that burned them. Find out what that was, because that's the ghost your offer is actually negotiating with tonight.

They won't hear your pitch. They'll hear it through the last one.
