3. "Tell me exactly what would need to happen for you to feel good about moving forward."

A deal that is falling apart usually has one specific wound underneath the frustration.
Most reps try to heal everything at once and fix nothing completely.

This phrase finds the one thing with surgical precision and gives the buyer complete ownership of the solution. They stop being angry and start being specific.
And specific is always something you can work with.

Find the one thing. Fix it completely. Everything else follows.
