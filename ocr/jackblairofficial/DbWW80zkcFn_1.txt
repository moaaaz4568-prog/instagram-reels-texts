1. "HERE'S EVERYTHING UP FRONT. I'M NOT HOLDING CARDS."
Robert Axelrod invited game theorists to submit strategies for a repeated dilemma: cooperate or betray, round after round. Anatol Rapoport sent the simplest entry. Cooperate first, then mirror.
It won both tournaments. Rule one is nice: never betray first. On the one hand, that's real numbers and real limits up front, then mirroring whatever comes back, because round one teaches the buyer the rules of you.
Trust is a strategy, not a personality.
