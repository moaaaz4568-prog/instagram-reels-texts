7. "I am going to walk away from this one. And I want you to know that door stays open if the situation changes."

This is Jax Teller's mode in a single sentence. Walk away with dignity, with honesty and with the door left open for the version of this that works.

The buyer who watches a rep walk away cleanly trusts that rep completely when they return. Because a rep who walks away from the wrong deal for the right reasons is the rarest thing in any market. And rare things get called back.

Walk away clean. The buyer who respects the exit calls you back for the deal that was always worth doing.
