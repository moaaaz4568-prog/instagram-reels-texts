THE ROOM THAT SAID NO
1
March 1959, New York Toy Fair. Ruth Handler showed Barbie to the men who decided what America's toy shelves carried.
The reception was cold. The line she kept hearing: mothers will never buy their daughters a doll like this. Orders came in thin.
A gatekeeper's no is a bet about their shelf. It is not a fact about your buyer.
