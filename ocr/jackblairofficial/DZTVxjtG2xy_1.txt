1. He made himself impossible to ignore.

In car-crazy 1950s California, every dealer was screaming on TV at once.

Worthington blanketed the airwaves so completely that one night's tape from a single station held more than seven minutes of just his ads. Attention is the first sale, and he made sure nobody could forget him.

You cannot close someone who has never heard of you.
