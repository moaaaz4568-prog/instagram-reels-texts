3. "I'd rather undersell this and have you be surprised than oversell it and let you down."

Aronson's work showed people trust the person who doesn't oversell. Round your claims down, not up. Promise a little less than you know you can deliver, then beat it. The rep who underpromises and overdelivers builds the kind of trust no closing script can fake.

Underpromise on purpose. Let the result do the bragging.
