04. Referral Follow Up

"My client agreed to refer me to [job title] at [company name]. The introduction was made [number] days ago and I have not heard from the referral yet.

Write me 3 follow-up messages I can send directly to the referral that acknowledge the introduction, add one piece of genuine value related to their world, and move toward a conversation without being pushy.

Under 60 words each.

Tone: warm, confident, completely unbothered by the silence.

No pitching. No fluff. Zero guesswork."

You stop letting warm introductions go cold because you did not know what to say next.
Every referral now gets a follow-up worth replying to.
