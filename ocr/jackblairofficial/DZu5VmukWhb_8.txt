Strip away the mansions and Flagg's edge
is simple. He knows more than the other
side, stays calm, and holds his ground
until the deal comes to him.

That is closing, and right now it is the
most valuable skill in sales. AI is wiping out
every sales job that runs on a script. The
one thing it cannot do is earn a real
buyer's trust and carry a negotiation to
yes. That part works remote: companies
book the calls, you take them from a
laptop, and you keep a cut of every deal
you close.

No floor, no commute, no manager
deciding your worth. If you have ever held
your nerve in a negotiation and won, you
already do this.
