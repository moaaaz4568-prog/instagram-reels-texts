4. "THIS ISN'T A NEW CONVERSATION. IT'S MINUTE FORTY OF THE LAST ONE."
The stamps in the study were physical, inked, impossible to miss. Progress out of sight stops pulling. A cold gap hides the card.
So open every call with the card face up. Recap what's decided, name what's left, and the meeting starts on stamp one. The last six instead of stamp one. The last four go faster, that part is measured.
A deal you can't see is a deal at zero.
