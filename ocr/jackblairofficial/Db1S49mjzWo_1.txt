CAN I GET YOUR TAKE ON ONE THING? THIRTY SECONDS.

In 1966 two psychologists asked homeowners to plant a huge ugly DRIVE CAREFULLY billboard on their front lawns. Cold, fewer than one in five said yes.

The reps who die at the big ask opened with it. Start with an ask so small that yes costs nothing: an opinion, a preference, the next five minutes.

Nobody installs a billboard for a stranger.
