6. "Here's what I'd do if I were you, and I could be off."

Same principle, aimed at the close. Give the recommendation, then hand back the right to disagree. Grant's point is that the tentative version lands harder than the confident one when the listener is deciding whether to trust you, and every buyer is. Say what you'd do in their seat, name the one thing that would change your mind, and wait.

Tell them what to do like someone who'll still be there tomorrow.
