2. "I understand what you are asking for. That is not where we are going to land on this."

Most reps soften a no until it sounds like a maybe. Cersei understands that a soft no invites a harder push and a harder push costs you the deal.

This phrase closes the door on a concession without anger, without apology, without a gap they can negotiate into. The buyer hears complete finality delivered with complete composure. And composure in the face of pressure is the most powerful signal of leverage available on any call.

Say no like you mean it. The room will adjust to the certainty.
