1. "Full honesty, there's one thing this doesn't do well. Let me tell you what it is."

Psychologist Elliot Aronson ran a study where a clearly talented person spilled coffee on himself, and people rated him more likable for it. The catch: it only worked because he'd already proven he was competent. Show your skill first, then admit one real flaw. A small, honest weakness makes everything else you said believable.

Competence first, then one honest flaw. That order sells.
