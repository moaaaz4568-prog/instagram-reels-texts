04. Wrong Person

"My prospect replied that they are not the right person and suggested I contact [name or department] instead.

My offer is: [one sentence].
Write me:
A thank you response that keeps the relationship warm,
one sentence asking for a warm introduction to the right person,
and a follow-up message I can send to the new contact
that references who referred me and why.

Under 40 words per message.

Tone: gracious, direct, professional.

Zero guesswork."

You stop letting referrals inside a company go cold.
Every wrong person reply now opens the door to the right one.
