7. "You're not buying from a company.
You're buying from me."

Iacocca put his own face in the ads of a
doubted brand, and when a scandal hit,
went on camera with "Did we screw up?
You bet. We're human." A company can be
distrusted forever. A named human
standing behind the promise is much
harder to dismiss. Be the name, not the
shield.

A brand can hide. A name on the line
can't.
