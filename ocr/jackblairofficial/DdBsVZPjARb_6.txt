6. "SILENCE USUALLY MEANS NO OR NOT YET. WHICH ONE IS THIS?"

The binary is the mechanism. Every message in the sequence collapses to two doors, and either door beats the fog.

Never ask ghosts open questions. Give them a two-word reply that resolves the thread. Not yet comes with a date. No comes with your time back.

Named silence breaks. Unnamed silence calcifies.
