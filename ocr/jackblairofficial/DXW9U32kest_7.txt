7. "No."

Ron Swanson’s most powerful word was also his shortest. The ability to say no to a bad deal, a bad concession, or a bad buyer is the most underrated skill in sales.

This phrase is for the moment a buyer tries to restructure your offer into something that does not work for you. One word. No apology. No explanation unless they ask for one.

The buyer who expected negotiation suddenly recalibrates the entire conversation around someone who has standards.

Say no when no is the right answer. The buyer who respects it was always worth closing.
