6. "I'd rather get this right than get it this week."

Talleyrand's most quoted advice to young diplomats, as it has come down through two centuries: above all, not to do too much zeal. At Vienna he let the autumn burn while the victors exhausted themselves on each other, and the deadlock came to him.

Eagerness is the one card a losing position can't afford. The rep sprinting to close by Friday hands over the only leverage the buyer needed.

Zeal is expensive. Patience gets paid.
