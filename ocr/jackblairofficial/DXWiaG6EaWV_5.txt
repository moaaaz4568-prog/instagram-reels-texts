5. "I have competitors. I know what they are offering. Here is why you are talking to me last."

Plainview never pretended the competition did not exist. He acknowledged it and immediately converted it into evidence of his own confidence.

This phrase signals that you are aware of every other option the buyer is considering and you are unbothered by all of them. That unbothered quality, delivered without arrogance, is the most powerful competitive differentiator available and it costs nothing to deploy.

Name the competition. Then make your confidence in yourself the reason they stay.
