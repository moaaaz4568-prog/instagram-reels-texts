AN ANSWER, NOT A CALLBACK

In Manhattan he improved Boeing's terms on the spot. No recess to check with the office. The authority was in the room, and TWA could feel it.

Know your walkaway before you walk in, and carry the authority to act inside it. Deals cool during callbacks, and buyers hear the checking as doubt. Momentum dies on hold.
