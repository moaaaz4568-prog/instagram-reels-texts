6. "Most people in your position make the decision they can explain. The best ones make the decision they know is right."

Keyser Soze operated in the space between what people would admit and what they actually knew. This phrase reaches into that same space and speaks directly to the buyer's instinct over their logic.

The buyer who has been hiding behind process and approval suddenly feels the weight of what they actually believe. They stop justifying the delay and start owning the decision. You did not close them. You gave them permission to close themselves.

Speak to what they know, not what they can defend. The close lives in that gap.
