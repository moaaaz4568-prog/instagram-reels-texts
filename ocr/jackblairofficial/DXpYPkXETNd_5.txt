5. "I am going to be straight with you even if it is not what you were hoping to hear."

Omar's honesty was never comfortable but it was always respected.
He told people the truth because the truth was the only thing worth saying and everyone knew it.

This phrase signals that what follows is real and unfiltered and the buyer prepares to receive something valuable.
The buyer who has been managed with comfortable half-truths by every other rep suddenly has access to someone who will just say it.
Straight talk from a trusted source closes deals that polished presentations leave permanently warm.

Say the straight thing. The buyer who hears it almost always trusts everything that follows it.
