1. "This call isn't about me. It's about where you're trying to get."

Donald Miller's whole framework rests on one line: the customer is the hero, and you're the guide. Brands that cast themselves as the hero lose. The second you stop selling yourself and start mapping their goal, the buyer leans in, because now the story is about them.

Make the buyer the hero. You're the guide.
