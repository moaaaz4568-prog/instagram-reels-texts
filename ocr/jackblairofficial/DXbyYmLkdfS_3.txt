3. "Every day this does not move forward is a day the problem is still costing you something. I want to make sure you know exactly what that is."

Bauer calculated the cost of inaction in real time and made everyone around him feel it with the same precision. This phrase converts delay from a neutral position into an active and measurable cost.

The buyer who was treating the stall as a pause suddenly sees it as a choice with a price tag.
You did not create pressure. You revealed a cost that was already accumulating before you named it.
That distinction is everything when a buyer is deciding whether to feel pushed or informed.

Reveal the cost of the delay. The buyer who calculates it themselves closes without being asked.
