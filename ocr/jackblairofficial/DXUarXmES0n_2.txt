2. "That is more useful than you realise. Tell me more about that."

Soze collected information with complete patience and never showed his hand until the moment was exactly right. This phrase signals mild interest while extracting intelligence the buyer did not plan to give.

The buyer thinks they are filling silence. You are building your entire close from what they hand you. Every detail they add narrows the gap between where they are and where you need them to be. By the time you make your move, the deal is already decided.

The rep who says less always knows more. Use it accordingly.
