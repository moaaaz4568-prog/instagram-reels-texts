7. You grade every server, cashier and cab driver on the job.

Not rudely. Professionally. You noticed the upsell, the eye contact, the half second they let the silence sit before the total. You tipped the good ones extra and you thought about the bad ones on the drive home. Once you can see the skill, you see it everywhere.

Now look at the list again. Every one of those is a habit, and a habit you cannot switch off is a skill nobody can take away from you.

Sound familiar?
