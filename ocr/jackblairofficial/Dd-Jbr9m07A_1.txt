1. "I'd rather lose this sale than put you in the wrong car."

Reda's record year was built walking buyers away from wrong cars. A salesman arguing against his own commission is remembered for a decade.

Say it the moment the fit turns doubtful, not after they push back. Losing the wrong deal on purpose is why the right ones close fast.

A wrong sale costs more than a lost one.
