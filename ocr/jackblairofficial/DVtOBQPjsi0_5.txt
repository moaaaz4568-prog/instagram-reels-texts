Prompt 5 — The Roleplay Setup

"Act as a skeptical [buyer] who is interested but keeps stalling. I'm going to try to close you. Push back the way a real buyer would. After 10 exchanges tell me exactly what I did wrong and what lost the deal."

This is where most reps stop.

They read the prompt. They think about running it. And then they never actually do it.

They get on a live call and freeze on the exact objection they already knew was coming.
