1. "WHAT DOES THIS ACTUALLY GET YOU? SAY IT ONCE, THEN WE'RE DONE WITH IT."
Gabriele Oettingen tracked dieters, job hunters and patients recovering from surgery. The ones who fantasized hardest about the result lost the least weight and got the fewest offers.
Let the buyer name the win one time and move on. Every extra minute in the dream drains the effort they would need to actually chase it.
A dream rehearsed too long gets spent.
