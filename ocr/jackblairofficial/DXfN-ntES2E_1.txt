1. "I want to be honest with you. I am not sure this is the right fit and I would rather say that now than waste both our time."

Jax Teller always knew when a deal was pulling him in a direction that was going to cost him more than it gave.
The reps who close earn that lesson the same way he did.

This phrase signals a quality of integrity that buyers almost never receive from a rep.
The buyer who hears it stops treating you like a vendor and starts treating you like someone worth trusting.
And the rep who is willing to say this almost always gets called back when the timing is actually right.

Say it when you mean it. The buyer who hears it never forgets the rep who was honest enough to say it.
