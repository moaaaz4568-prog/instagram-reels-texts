3. "What would make me the wrong call here?"

McGraw prepped witnesses for a living before television, and the witness who admits the weak point first is the one the jury trusts on the strong one. Say it when they get defensive. Invite the doubt, then ask what would fix it. Pressure drops, and the real objection walks out on its own.

Concede the doubt first and the trust comes with it.
