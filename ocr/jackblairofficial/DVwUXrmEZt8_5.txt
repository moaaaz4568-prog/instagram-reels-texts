5. The Objection-Proof Opener
"Write me a cold door knock / cold call opener for [your product] that immediately separates me from every other rep they've seen. It should acknowledge they weren't expecting me, lead with curiosity not pitch, and end with a question that's hard to say no to."
This is the prompt that changes everything.
Because the best lead in the world means nothing if you can't open the door.
