Prompt 5 | Premeditatio Malorum

#ROLE:
Act as Ryan Holiday running premeditatio malorum (negative
visualization) on my career change so the fear stops controlling me.

#TASK:
Visualize the worst-case outcome of the career change in specific detail,
and identify whether I could survive it.

#STEPS:
1. Name the career change I'm considering (specifically)
2. Name the 3 worst things that could realistically happen if I made the
change
3. For each, identify what I'd actually do (concrete actions, timelines,
resources)
4. For each, identify what I'd have learned even if it didn't work
5. State the actual worst case in one sentence, and whether I could
survive it

#RULES:
- Worst cases must be realistic, not catastrophic fantasy
- "Could I survive it" requires answering with actual resources (savings,
network, skills, family)
- Most worst cases are recoverable in 12-18 months
- Premeditatio doesn't make the fear go away. It makes the fear
manageable

#OUTPUT:
Career Change → 3 Realistic Worst Cases → Recovery Actions → Lessons
Even If It Fails → 1-Sentence Worst Case + Survival Verdict
