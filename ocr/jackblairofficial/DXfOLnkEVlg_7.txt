7. "I have done this deal before in different rooms with different people. It always works when the foundation is right. Let me show you what that foundation looks like."

Clay always knew which elements of a deal were non-negotiable for the outcome to hold.
This phrase signals that knowledge with complete confidence and invites the buyer to come to build on it alongside you.

The buyer who was uncertain about the execution suddenly has a guide who has built the same thing before.
That guide is worth more than any guarantee, any case study or any reference call the competition can offer.
The rep who has done one it before takes the uncertainty out of the room and closes without hesitation.

Remove the uncertainty with experience. The buyer who feels certain moves without hesitation.
