6. "WHAT WOULD HAVE TO CHANGE FOR THIS
TO BE A YES?"
Even in the best conversations, most people didn't
move, and the method treats that as information.
A real condition beats a polite no every time. When
they name one, the conversation stops being
persuasion and becomes logistics. Go satisfy the
condition. Come back. The door they left open is still
open.
Ask for the condition and the no becomes a task.
