5. "Next call. Now, while it still stings."

The special force at MetLife, the reps who failed the screen, outsold the pessimists by 21% in year one. By Seligman's account the pessimists also quit at twice the rate. The difference was persistence after the no, not talent before it. Dial inside ten minutes. The last call hardens with every minute you give it, and the next buyer can hear it in your voice.

Rejection has a half-life. Beat it to the phone.
