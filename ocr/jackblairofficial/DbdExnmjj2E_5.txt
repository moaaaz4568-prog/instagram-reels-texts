5. "IF WE SET THE NUMBERS ASIDE, WHAT STILL BOTHERS YOU?"
Google made Diamond its chief negotiation instructor, and twelve thousand Googlers later, the core move is unchanged: split the emotion from the economics, then settle the emotion first.
Most objections travel with a passenger. Ask this and the passenger steps out: the slight from week two, the demo that ran long, the email that went unanswered.
Price is where hurt feelings go to hide.
