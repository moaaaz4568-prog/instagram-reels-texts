1. "I am going to tell you something about your industry that most people in it have not noticed yet."

Reddington always opened with something the room did not have before he arrived.
That is the only opening worth making on a call where attention is the scarcest resource available.

This phrase creates an immediate information gap the buyer needs to close.
They were not planning to engage fully. Now they cannot afford not to.
You have not pitched a product. You have made yourself the most interesting person on their calendar today. Everything else is just noise competing for the same crowded space.

Open with insight.
