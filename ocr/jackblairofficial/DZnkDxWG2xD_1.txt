1. "What would you do if you weren't afraid of getting it wrong?"

Wendy never argued someone out of their hesitation. She asked the question that walked them around it.

The buyer isn't stuck on your offer, they're stuck on the fear of making the wrong call in front of someone. Move the fear and the decision was never actually hard.

Stop answering objections. Start asking the question underneath them.
