4. "I want to make sure what I am promising you is something I can actually deliver. Give me a moment to confirm."

Omar never made a commitment he was not certain he could keep.
That discipline was the reason his word meant more than anyone else's in every room he entered.

This phrase signals the kind of careful commitment that buyers almost never receive from a rep.
The buyer who watches you pause before promising stops expecting the overpromise that has burned them before. That absence of the overpromise is the most credible thing a rep can offer in a market full of them.

Pause before you promise. The buyer who watches you do it trusts what comes after completely.
