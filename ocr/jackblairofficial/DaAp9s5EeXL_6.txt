6. "Just so I'm clear, what are you hoping this changes?"
Rackham found weak reps assume they know what the buyer wants. Closers confirm it, out loud, before they ever present.
This locks the target before you pitch, so your offer lands as the exact answer to the thing they just named. You are no longer guessing. You are reflecting their own words back as the solution.
Confirm the target before you take the shot.
