1. "I want to be upfront with you about how I operate because I think it saves us both time."

Omar Little always made his code visible before anyone had a chance to test it.
That visibility was not vulnerability. It was the most efficient trust-building move available.

This phrase signals to the buyer that you are someone who operates consistently and transparently.
The buyer who receives this in the first sixty seconds of a call stops evaluating whether you can be trusted and starts listening to what you are actually saying.
Trust established very close is built on.

Make your operating principles visible before the buyer has to discover them. It closes faster than anything else.
