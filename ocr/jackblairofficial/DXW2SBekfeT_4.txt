4. "I think you already know this is the right move. You are just not used to someone telling you directly."

Chuck Bass always says the thing nobody else in the room was willing to say.
That directness was never rude. It was simply a level of honesty the entitled buyer had never received.

This phrase cuts through the performance of deliberation and names what is actually happening.
The buyer who has been stalling behind the theatre of considering their options suddenly has nowhere left to hide from the decision they already made.

Say the direct thing. The buyer who has never heard it always responds to it.
