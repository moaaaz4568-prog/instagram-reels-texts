3. He managed his own self-talk.

Alongside high goals, Hays preached good self-talk, because the conversation in a seller's head shows up in the room.

A rep who has already talked himself into the no carries it on his face. He coached himself the way he coached his people, on purpose, every day. You hear your own pitch before the buyer ever does.
