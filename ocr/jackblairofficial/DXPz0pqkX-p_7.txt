7. "I think we have covered everything that needed covering. Here is what I propose we do next."

Tywin Lannister ended every meeting with a decision already made.
He never left a room open, never allowed ambiguity to survive the final minute.

This phrase closes the conversation with the quiet authority of someone who always knew how this would end.
No asking. No checking. Just a calm, certain proposal for the next step.
The executive who has been in a well-run meeting recognises the natural conclusion it is.

End every meeting with a direction. Ambiguity is where deals go to disappear.
