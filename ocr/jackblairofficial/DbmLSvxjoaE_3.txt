3. "STOP CALLING THIS THE DEAL. FROM HERE ON IT'S YOUR PLAN."
School vouchers kept losing the room, so Luntz renamed them opportunity scholarships. Same policy, same dollar, and the resistance thinned. People weren't rejecting the thing. They were rejecting the label.
"The deal" is a label your buyer braces against. Hand them "your plan" and the thing on the table stops being yours to be theirs to protect.
Deals get negotiated. Plans get followed.
