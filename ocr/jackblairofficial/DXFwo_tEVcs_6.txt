6. "I can tell this isn't the right moment to push. So I won't. What would make the timing better?"

Tom Wambsgans knew when to advance and when to hold. Most reps push hardest at the exact moment the buyer needs space. This phrase does the opposite. It removes pressure, earns respect, and uncovers the real blocker in one move. Knowing when not to close is what separates a rep from a closer.
