5. "I'll stop. What do you think?"
Brooks and Michael Yeomans named this
trap in 2025. Boomerasking.
You ask them a question, then answer it
yourself before they can. It feels like
curiosity. It lands as a monologue with a
question mark stuck on it.
The second you catch yourself answering
your own question, cut the sentence and
hand it back.
A question you answer yourself was never
a question.
