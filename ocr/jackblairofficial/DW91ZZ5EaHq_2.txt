3. The Cold Opener
"Write me 5 cold outreach messages to
send to [job title] at [company type]
about [product or service]. Each one
under 50 words. Lead with their
problem, not my product. One message
per pain point. Conversational tone, like
a text from someone who knows what
they are doing. Zero guesswork."
Five openers. Five angles. One reply is
all it takes.
