2. "AND WHO IS GOING TO RUN THIS THING AFTER THE TRUCK LEAVES?"
His billion dollar insight was one awkward question. Companies were buying computers they could not operate. IBM sold the box and left. Perot offered to stay and run it, and IBM turned the idea down.
Ask the question your product creates and answer it before the buyer does. The gap after the sale is where the next deal lives.
The sale ends at the invoice. The customer starts there.
