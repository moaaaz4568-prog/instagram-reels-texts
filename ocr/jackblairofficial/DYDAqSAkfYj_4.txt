4. Selling the dog was the bottom.

Sly stood outside a liquor store with Butkus on a leash. The bullmastiff had been with him through every audition.

A guy walked up. Offered $50. Sly took it because he had no other option.

He's called it the lowest moment of his life.

When doing everything right keeps leading to the worst day, the path was never going to work.
