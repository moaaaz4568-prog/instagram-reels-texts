6. "Here's what that your mornings look like once this is handled."

Zaltman found abstract benefits barely register. Lived, sensory detail is what the deciding part of the brain actually processes.

So drop the feature list. Give them the 7am with no fire to put out. The inbox that stopped growing. The wing. Make the after feel like a memory.
