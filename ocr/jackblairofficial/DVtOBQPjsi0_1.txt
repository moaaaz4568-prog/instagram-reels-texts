Prompt 1 — The Objection Mapper

"I sell [product] to [buyer]. Write me the 10 most common objections I'll hear and a word-for-word response to each. Make each response feel like something a real person would actually say. No scripts. No corporate language."

Now you know exactly what's coming before you get on a single call.

The next step is drilling them until the responses are automatic.
