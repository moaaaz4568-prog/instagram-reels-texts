4. "When do you plan on interviewing
the right agent to sell your home?"
On an expired listing, Ferry never begged
for the appointment.
The word "interviewing" hands the seller
control and quietly assumes they will hire
someone good, which positions him as
that someone without a hard pitch.
Assume the sale, don't chase it.
Frame yourself as the obvious next choice,
not another hopeful caller.
