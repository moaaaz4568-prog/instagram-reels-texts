01. Trigger Finder

"I sell [one sentence on your offer] to [ICP].

Identify the 10 most powerful buying trigger events that would make a [Job title] at [company type] in the [industry] space more likely to need my offer right now.

For each trigger include:
What the event signals about their current situation,
why it creates urgency or relevance for my offer specifically,
and one outreach angle I can use to reference it naturally.

Format as a numbered list.

Tone: strategic, specific, no generic observations.

Zero guesswork."

You stop reaching out without a reason.
You start showing up at exactly the moment your offer matters most to them.
