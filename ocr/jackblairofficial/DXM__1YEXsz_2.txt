2. "I want to be straight with you about why I think this conversation matters for your business right now."

Ghost never wasted time on small talk when something real was on the table. He signalled immediate importance and the room adjusted its attention to match.

This phrase sets the tone for a conversation that operates at a different level than the buyer expected. It tells them within the first thirty seconds that you came prepared and that what follows is worth their full focus.

The buyer who was half present suddenly becomes wholly present.

Open at the level you intend to close at. Never lower.
