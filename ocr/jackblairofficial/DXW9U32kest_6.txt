6. "I have said everything I have to say. The rest is your decision."

Ron Swanson understood that the hardest part of any close is knowing when to stop talking.
Most reps talk themselves out of deals they had already won by filling the silence after their best point.

This phrase marks the end of your pitch with complete finality and puts the full weight of the decision where it belongs.
The buyer who has been half-listening suddenly has nothing left to wait for.
The silence that follows is not awkward. It is the close working.

Know when you are done. Then stop. The silence you are brave enough to hold.
