2. "I'm not here to sell you anything. I'm here to show you something."

The phrase that lowers every defensive wall in the first 30 seconds.

The moment a buyer hears "sell" their guard goes up.

The moment they hear "show" their curiosity goes up instead.

You haven't changed what you're doing. You've changed how it lands.
