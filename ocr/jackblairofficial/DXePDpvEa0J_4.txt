04. News Hook Builder

"Here is a piece of recent news about my prospect's company:
[paste news article, press release or announcement].

My offer is [one sentence].
Their job title is [job title].

Write me 3 outreach messages that:
Use this specific news as the hook,
connect what the news signals about their current priorities
directly to the problem my offer solves,
and end with one question that makes a reply feel natural.

Under 70 words each.

Tone: informed, specific, completely relevant to their world today.

No pitching. No fluff."

You stop reaching out without context.
Every message now proves you were paying attention before you
asked for their time.
