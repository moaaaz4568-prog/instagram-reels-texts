6. "I have had more complex conversations than this one and found a way through every single one."

When a deal gets complicated most reps signal stress and the buyer loses confidence immediately. Ghost radiated certainty in the most uncertain moments because he had earned it through experience.

This phrase does not boast. It reassures. It tells the buyer that complexity is not a problem for someone who has navigated worse. The buyer stops worrying about the deal and starts trusting the person managing it.

Calm in complexity is a credential. Use it like one.
