5. "YOUR LAST VENDOR WAS PROBABLY RIGHT ABOUT ONE THING. THE PROBLEM IS REAL. THEY WERE WRONG ABOUT THE FIX."
Conceding the other side is a disruption because buyers expect the opposite. In Knowles's model the window that opens after a disruption stays open for seconds, not minutes.
Give the concession first, plainly, and follow it in the same breath with the frame you want to stand. Never leave the gap open long enough for the buyer to fill it.
Agree first and you get to say what the agreement means.
