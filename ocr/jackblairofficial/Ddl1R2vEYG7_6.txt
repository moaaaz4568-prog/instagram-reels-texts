6. "I'm not going to tell you the other guy is wrong."

In Boise in 1907 Darrow defended Big Bill Haywood on a murder charge and never once tried to make the prosecution look dishonest. He made their case look thin instead, and Haywood walked out acquitted. When your buyer names a competitor, do not run them down. Name the one thing that setup cannot do.

Go after the case, never the man. The room is watching how you treat him.
