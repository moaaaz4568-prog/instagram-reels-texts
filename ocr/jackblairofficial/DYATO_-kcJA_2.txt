2. Other salespeople knocked on doors.

Brownie didn't. She'd find one woman in a neighborhood, ask her to host 8 of her friends, and bring food.

The hostess sold for her. The friends sold each other. By the end of the night every woman in the room had bought at least one bowl, and three of them wanted to host the next party.

One closer. Eight buyers. Three referrals.
