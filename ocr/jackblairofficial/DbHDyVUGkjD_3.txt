3. "Let's not do another meeting. Let's fix one thing together this week."

Peace at Robbers Cave started with a broken water tank both tribes had to fix together. Not a speech. A shared task.

Swap your next status meeting for a working session on something real. An hour spent solving beside them rewires how they read everything you say after it.

Meetings make tribes. Missions make teams.
