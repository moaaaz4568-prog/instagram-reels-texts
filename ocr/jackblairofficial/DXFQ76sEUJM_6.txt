6. "I get the sense something shifted since we last spoke. What changed?"

McNulty read rooms for a living. He noticed the thing nobody said out loud. This phrase shows the buyer you are paying attention and creates a safe space for the real conversation to begin. Something always changes between calls. The rep who asks about it gets the truth. The rep who ignores it loses the deal quietly.
