3. Hear the real objection under the fake one.

"I need to think about it" is never the real reason. A person feels what's underneath it. Software takes it at face value and loses the deal.

The closer hears the money, the fear, or the doubt hiding behind the polite no, and answers that instead.

The real objection is unspoken. You have to feel for it.
