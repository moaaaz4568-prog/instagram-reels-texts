4. "What would change for you if this problem was gone in thirty days?"

When a buyer stalls, most reps push harder on features.
That is the wrong direction entirely.

This phrase pulls them forward instead of pushing from behind.
It makes them paint the picture of life after yes.
By the time they finish answering, they have already sold themselves.

Let the buyer close the deal. You just asked the right question.
