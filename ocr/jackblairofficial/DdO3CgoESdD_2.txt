2. "What's it costing you to keep it?"
Law four: you cannot change what you do
not acknowledge. His whole show ran on
making people put a number on the thing
they'd been calling fine. Don't supply the
cost. Ask for it in their words: hours, lost
deals, the hire they can't make. Whatever
they say becomes the price of doing
nothing, and the cost of they said it, not you.
The cost they name is the only cost they'll
believe.
