4. Here is what changed while you were
working the floor.

Companies automated almost everything
around the sale. The ad finds the buyer.
The funnel books the call. Software runs
the demo.

The one piece no machine can do is the
part you are already best at: closing a real
human in real time.

So the most valuable seat in selling is now
the one with your exact skill in it, and
almost no ceiling on top.
