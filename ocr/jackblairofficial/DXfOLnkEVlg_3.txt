3. "I want to tell you what I have seen happen when companies in your position delay this decision. The pattern is always the same."

Clay understood that the most persuasive thing a seasoned operator can offer is a pattern observed over time.
This phrase creates urgency from experience rather than manufactured pressure and buyers feel the difference immediately.

The buyer who was comfortable in their delay suddenly sees themselves inside a familiar story with a known ending.
You did not threaten them. You showed them something you have watched happen to people exactly like them.
Urgency born from observed pattern is always more credible than urgency born from a rep's quota.

Show the pattern. The buyer who recognises themselves inside it stops delaying.
