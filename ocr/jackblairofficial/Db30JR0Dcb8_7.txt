7. COUNT YOUR PICKS.
Five for five: you have flipped a buyer who loved the
other guy, and you know it. Three or four: your
instincts are better than your training. Two or less:
you are still selling the product. Most reps are.
The five rules: win the person they listen to. Sell
their future, not your product. Change the currency.
Make the bet part of the pitch. Build the offer only
you can make.
