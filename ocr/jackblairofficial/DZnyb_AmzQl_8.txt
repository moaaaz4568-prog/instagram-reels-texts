Here's what makes Marty worth studying.

He was never the most powerful person in the room, usually the least. He won by staying calm when everything said panic and by being the one who could think clearly while everyone else lost it. That exact nerve is the rarest, highest-paid skill in sales right now.

Companies are generating more interested buyers than they can call, so they hand the calls to closers and pay 10 to 30% of every deal, all from a laptop. AI already took the work that ran on a script. The one thing the work that ran it cannot do is sit with a nervous human in a high-stakes moment and steady them into a yes. If you've ever stayed calm while everyone around you came apart, that is the whole job.
