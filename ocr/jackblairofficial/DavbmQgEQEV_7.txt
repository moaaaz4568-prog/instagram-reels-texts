7. "They served you well. We'll take it from here."

Grant's soldiers were ordered not to taunt the men stacking their arms. Same rule when you beat an incumbent vendor.

That vendor was your buyer's last decision, and every insult you aim at them lands on the person who chose them. Respect the loser and the buyer stops defending the past.

Insult their old vendor, insult their judgment.
