"HOW WIDE ARE YOU LOOKING RIGHT NOW?"

Clifford Geertz found buyers in that market faced one real choice, spread themselves across many sellers or go further with one. Which one they picked decided everything that followed.

Ask it early and take the answer flat. Wide means they are still gathering. Deep means the decision already narrowed and you are inside it.

Breadth means they are still looking. Depth means they are deciding.
