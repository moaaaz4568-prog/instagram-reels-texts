4. He out-worked every dealer on the lot.

Worthington was still shooting as many as four commercials a day well into his eighties.

Through the 1960s he was the top-selling Dodge dealer in the entire country, not because he was the slickest, but because he refused to be out-worked. Effort, not talent, is what gets the sale.

The hardest worker in the room usually goes home with the deal.
