2. He plays the man.

"Nothing you've told me is about learning to sell. It's about who gets handed the close." Harvey doesn't argue the deal. He reads the person across the table. I pasted my notes from the first call and asked Claude to read the buyer his way. What came back:

A read of the buyer in six lines: what he wants, what he's protecting, what he isn't saying, what he does if nothing changes, the one sentence that speaks to it, and the one thing not to bring up. Then the next three questions, in order.
