1. "Can I give you the current number on that?"

Wood and Porter showed 3,127 people eight real public statements that were wrong, with the speakers split evenly between the two parties. Every correction moved the average person toward the fact, including the ones that cut against their own side. Say the number plainly and once. Do not build a case around it first.

Nobody has ever been argued into a fact. They just get told it.
