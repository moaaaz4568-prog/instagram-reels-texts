2. "This is one of the strongest conversations I have had this week and we are just getting started."

Ron Burgundy made every person in his orbit feel like they were part of something significant.
That feeling is not flattery. It is energy and energy is contagious on a sales call.

This phrase lifts the temperature of the conversation before the pitch begins. The buyer who was half present suddenly feels the pull of a rep who is fully alive in the room.
Energy at the right moment converts a routine call into the one the buyer remembers.

Show up with more energy than the room expects. The buyer will match it without knowing why.
