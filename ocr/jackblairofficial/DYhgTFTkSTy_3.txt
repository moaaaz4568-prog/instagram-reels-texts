3. "Use specific words. Avoid abstractions."

Headline rule #3 in Tested Advertising Methods.

"Saves you $200" beats "saves you money" every time. Vague claims sound like sales pitches. Specific numbers sound like reporting. Caples ran the tests. The data never budged.

He kept this rule for 60 years and never lost on it.
