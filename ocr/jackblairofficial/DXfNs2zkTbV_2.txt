2. "I know you do not know me yet. By the end of this conversation I think that will have changed."

Tommy always named the dynamic before anyone else could use it against him.
This phrase converted the unknown rep's greatest vulnerability into the opening statement of a compelling pitch.

The buyer who was about to dismiss you on the basis of unfamiliarity suddenly has a reason to stay curious.
You have not defended your lack of reputation. You have made overcoming it the subject of the next twenty minutes.
That reframe alone is worth every second it buys you.

Name the vulnerability first. The buyer who hears you acknowledge it stops using it as a reason to disengage.
