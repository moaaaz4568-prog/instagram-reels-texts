2. THE 8-SECOND OPENER
“Write 7 cold call openers for reaching a [job title]
about [pain point]. Each must be under 8 seconds
when spoken. No ‘how are you.’ No ‘did I catch you
at a bad time.’ Start with something that earns the
next 15 seconds. Tone: human, not scripted.”
Eight seconds to earn thirty more. That’s cold
calling. Everything else is noise.
