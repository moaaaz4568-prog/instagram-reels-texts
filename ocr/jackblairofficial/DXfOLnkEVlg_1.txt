1. "I have been in this business long enough to know exactly what you are about to run into. Let me show you how to get ahead of it."

Clay Morrow's greatest asset was never ambition. It was the accumulated intelligence of having seen everything before. This phrase converts years of experience into cross from you.

The buyer who was evaluating you alongside a competitor suddenly only has a reason to choose differently. Experience that names the problem before it arrives is worth more than any feature comparison sheet ever written. The rep who has already solved what the buyer is about to face closes before the buyer finishes asking why they should choose them.

Name what is coming before they see it. That is the close experience makes possible and youth never can.
