3. "WHAT DID YOU DECIDE THE LAST TIME
SOMETHING LIKE THIS CROSSED YOUR DESK?"
Kahneman found comparisons beat ratings: experts
scatter when they judge a case cold, and steady
when they set it beside one already decided.
Anchor the decision to their own precedent instead
of today's weather. What they approved before, and
why, is a steadier judge than the room they're sitting
in. Make the past version of your buyer vote too.
Precedent doesn't have moods.
