6. TURN THE OBJECTION INTO THE AD.
The league banned the black and red shoe. Nike paid every fine and ran ads about the ban. The forbidden shoe sold out everywhere.
When a buyer raises the scary thing, do not shrink it. Own it so hard it becomes the reason you are different.
What they ban, they advertise.
