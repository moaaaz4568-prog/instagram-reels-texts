2. His reasons, in his words.

"The forty grand is not an objection."
Pantalon's rule is that the buyer's reasons beat mine every time, even weak ones, because he said them. I pasted the notes from a first call and asked Claude to find every reason the buyer said himself. What came back:

Six reasons, five in his words and one in his behaviour: he said ten minutes and stayed twenty. Two of my lines thrown out, because "yeah, probably" is politeness. Then a question he can only answer by adding a reason.
