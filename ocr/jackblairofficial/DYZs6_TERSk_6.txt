6. "The people you hire are the company."

He kept 5 of the original 55 laid-off Kraft workers. They're still with him.

In 2016 he gave 10% of Chobani's shares to all 2,000 employees, worth roughly $500,000 per worker. Today 30% of his workforce is immigrants or refugees, with 19 languages spoken at the factories.

He bet on the people Kraft left behind.
