03. Price Defender

"Write me a response to a prospect who says [paste their exact price objection].
My product costs [price] and solves [one sentence on the core problem it fixes].
The response must hold the price with zero apology,
redirect to value not cost, and end with a question that keeps them talking.
Under 50 words. Tone: grounded, direct, no desperation.
No pitching. No fluff."

You stop discounting the moment they push. You start closing from strength.
