3. The contracts ran longer than anyone else thought to write them.

Standard industry deal in 1962: 1 year.
Perot pitched 5-year contracts at fixed prices. Locked in revenue while everyone else fought for renewals.

Customers liked the predictability. Perot liked the runaway.

Reps chase monthly. Closers structure deals that compound. The ones who sell long pay long.
