5. "I'm not going to add anything to the offer. Tell me what's missing from it."

Students were offered $1.50 or a nice pen instead. Seventy five percent took the pen. Offer the same pen or a second pen, and more than half kept the cash. Adding a sweetener made people keep their money. Reps do this to stalled deals every week: a bonus, a discount, an extra month, and the stall gets deeper, an extra closer. Stop adding. Ask what is missing, and fix that one thing.

Every bonus you add is another reason to wait.
