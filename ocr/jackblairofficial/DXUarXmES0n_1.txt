1. "I am going to let you lead this conversation. I just want to make sure I understand everything correctly before I say anything."

Keyser Soze let every room underestimate him because underestimation is the most useful advantage available. The buyer who believes they are leading the call reveals everything you need while guarding nothing.

This phrase hands the buyer the wheel and the illusion of control simultaneously.
They open up, they share, they expose the real objection and the real desire in the same breath.
You have said almost nothing and learned everything.

Give the buyer the pen. Let them write the roadmap to their own yes.
