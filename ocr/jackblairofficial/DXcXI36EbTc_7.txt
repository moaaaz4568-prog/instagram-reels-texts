7. "I am going to ask you one more time and then I am going to respect whatever you decide."

This is John Wick's final move and the most precise close available on a deal at the edge.
It signals absolute respect for the buyer's autonomy while making the finality of the moment completely clear.

The buyer who has been stalling suddenly feels the last window opening and closing in the same sentence. Most will move. The ones who do not give you the clean end you needed to stop carrying a dead deal. Either outcome is better than the one you had before you asked.

Ask one final time with complete composure. Then mean it when you say you will respect the answer.
