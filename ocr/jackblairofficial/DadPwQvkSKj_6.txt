6. Never end a call on a vibe.

"Great call, let's keep in touch!" That deal is already dead.

The researchers split call endings into advances and continuations. Continuations feel friendly and go nowhere. Top closers left every call with a commitment the buyer had to act on.

Say: "Before we hang up, what's the next step you're taking on your side, and by when?"
