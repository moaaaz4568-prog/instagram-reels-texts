THE DISCOUNT THAT UNSOLD THE DEAL
A large unearned discount is the cleanest outside
justification there is. The buyer's story becomes the
price, not the product.
If you concede anything, attach it to something they
did: a scope cut, a case study, a reference call.
Never let cheap be the reason they signed.
A buyer who signed for the discount stays until the
discount ends.
