6. "THAT'S A WEIRD OFFER. WALK ME THROUGH IT."
A town in Saskatchewan offered a house for a role in a movie. The strangest offer in the chain turned out to be the best one in it. He took the meeting.
The offer that sounds wrong is information: it's the buyer showing you their private math. Never flinch at weird. Ask for the walkthrough and read what they value.
Weird offers are value maps.
