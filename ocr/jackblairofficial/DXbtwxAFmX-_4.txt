4. "I have had this conversation with a lot of organisations. The ones who hesitated are now trying to catch up."

Nick Fury understood that the fear of being left behind is a more powerful motivator than the promise of getting ahead. This phrase uses social proof at the strategic level rather than the testimonial level.

The buyer who was comfortable in their current position suddenly sees a landscape that has been moving without them.

They stop asking whether this is a good idea and start asking how far behind they already are.

That question closes deals that no feature comparison ever could.

Show them the ones who waited. Then let them decide which story they want to be in.
