2. "I want this to work for both of us. Here is what I can do, and here is where I need to stay firm."

Corleone always gave something small to protect something large.
That is not weakness. That is architecture.

This phrase signals goodwill while drawing a boundary the buyer can feel but cannot move. You are not stonewalling. You are structuring. The buyer feels heard and respected, and the number stays exactly where you put it.

Give on the things that cost you nothing. Hold on the things that cost you everything.
