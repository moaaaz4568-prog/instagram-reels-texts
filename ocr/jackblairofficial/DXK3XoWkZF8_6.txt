6. "I am not going anywhere. If something changes or a question comes up, I want you to call me directly."

Most reps disappear after the close and reappear at renewal.
That is not a relationship. That is a transaction with a gap in the middle.

This phrase commits to presence and makes the buyer feel held, not handled.
Vito Corleone's relationships lasted decades because people knew he would still be there.
That certainty is the single most powerful reason a buyer never looks for another rep.

Relationships are not closed. They are maintained.
Every single day.
