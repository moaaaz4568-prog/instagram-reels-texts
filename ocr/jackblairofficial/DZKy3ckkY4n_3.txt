3. He set out to fix an industry everyone distrusted.

McGrath saw that agents had a poor reputation and built his entire approach around changing it.

Instead of competing on price or pressure, he competed on trust, in a field starved for it. Being the honest, professional option in a skeptical market made him the obvious choice. Stand out by being the one people actually believe.

Trust is the rarest edge in a crowded market.
