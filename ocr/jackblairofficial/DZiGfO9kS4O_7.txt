7. "What questions do you have for
me?"

Richardson closed by opening the floor,
inviting the buyer to surface whatever was
still unsaid.

The questions they ask tell you exactly
what stands between them and yes.
Answer those and there's nothing left to
block the deal. Their last questions are
your closing checklist.

Let them ask, and they'll hand you the
path to yes.
