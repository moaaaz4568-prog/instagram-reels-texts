4. "YOU'RE NOT COMMITTING TO FOREVER
HERE. YOU'RE PICKING A START DATE."
In 2002 Luntz advised saying climate change
instead of global warming because it sounded less
frightening. In 2019 he sat before the Senate and
recanted, asking them to stop using something he
wrote 18 years ago.
"Forever" is your buyer's frightening word. Swap it
for a start date and you've turned a life sentence into
a Tuesday.
Forever is a debate. A date is a diary entry.
