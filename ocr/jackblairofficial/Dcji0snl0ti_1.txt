1. HE GOT CUT FOR THE WAY HE CARRIED HIMSELF.
Frank Bettger was released by his first ball club in Johnstown. The manager's reason: he played like he didn't care.
Not his arm, not his bat. His energy. Buyers read the same signal in the first minute of every call. Most reps lose the room before the pitch starts.
The room reads your energy before your words.
