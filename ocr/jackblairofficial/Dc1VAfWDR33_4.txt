4. "PICTURE IT ALREADY WORKING FOR YOU.
NOW IMAGINE HANDING IT BACK."
Kahneman found people value a thing far more once
they feel it's theirs. The endowment effect.
Get them to mentally own the result before they buy
it, and walking away starts to feel like a loss they
won't take.
Let them own it
in their head first.
