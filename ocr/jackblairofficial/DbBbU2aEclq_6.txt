6. "At this point the budget has a personality."

Levity is one of the four moves in Brooks's conversation model. Levity is not comedy.

It is a light touch on a heavy moment, and it does two things at once. It lifts your status and it tells the room you are not frightened of the problem.

Save it for the fourth delay. Never the first.

Fear is quiet. Nobody jokes about a deal they need.
