5. "You're right, this isn't cheap to fix. What has living with it cost so far?"

The top reps in the research built the size of the problem before they even named a price. A price is only ever compared to something. A price is only ever compared to nothing. Get the buyer's number before you say yours. If they can't put one on it, help them build it: hours, months, deals lost. Then your price has something to stand next to.

Your price only looks big next to nothing.
