7. "What's holding you back
from moving forward today?"

The question Bettger drilled
until it became automatic.

Not pushy. Direct.

Most reps are afraid to ask
the real question.

This one cuts through every stall,
every hesitation, every "let me think."

And the answer tells you exactly
what you need to close the deal.
