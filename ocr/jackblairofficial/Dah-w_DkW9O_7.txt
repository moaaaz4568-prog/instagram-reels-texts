7. "I really shouldn't do this, but for you I'll make it work if we lock it today."

Dawson proved a concession given too easily is worth nothing. One that feels earned and hard-won is what actually closes.

Never hand over the discount like it's free. Make it an exception you're stretching for, tied to a decision now. They feel like they won, and winners sign.

An easy concession is worthless. Make them earn it.
