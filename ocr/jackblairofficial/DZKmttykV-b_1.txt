1. "You will never out-sell the picture you hold of yourself."

Maltz fixed patients' faces and watched many stay miserable, because the real scar was on the inside.

He realized self-image sets the limit on everything, including what you can close. If you see yourself as average, your numbers obey that picture. Raise the internal image first and your results climb to meet it.

Change the picture and the numbers follow.
