3. “Take full accountability. No excuses, no blaming the lead.”

Iannarino on the habit that separates pros.

He has zero patience for reps who blame bad leads, the territory, or the market. The pro owns the lead, good or bad. Accountability is uncomfortable, and it is the only way you actually improve.

The moment you blame the lead, you stop getting better.
