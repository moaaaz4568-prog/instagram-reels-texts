1. "Before we start I want to be completely present for this conversation. Give me one second."

Ghost never walked into a high-stakes room carrying the weight of the last one.
He compartmentalised with surgical precision and the room always felt the difference.

This phrase is not for the buyer. It is for you.
Said internally before every call that matters, it draws a line between what just happened and what is about to.
The buyer gets your full capacity. Not the leftover of a bad morning.

Presence is a decision. Make it before you dial.
