7. "You didn't ask for this one. Look at it anyway."

It was her idea to move The New York Times. The paper hadn't gone looking for a 52 story tower on Eighth Avenue; she brought them the option nobody was pitching, and it became a highlight of her career. The buyer's best deal is often the one they hadn't imagined until someone put it in front of them, and nobody else is pitching it. Bring one thing to every call the buyer didn't ask for.

Now look at where all seven started. A woman of 36 with no contacts, no deals for a year and a half, in a business that told her she didn't belong.

Sound familiar?
