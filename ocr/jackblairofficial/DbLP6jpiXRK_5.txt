5. "IS THIS A THIS-YEAR PROBLEM OR A
SOMEDAY PROBLEM?"
A prediction in disguise. You are asking them to
forecast their own move.
This year means the clock is real and you both plan
around it. Someday means you stop chasing and set
a date to recheck. Vague is the only enemy.
Someday is a no with better manners.
