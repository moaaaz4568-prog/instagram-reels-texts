6. He sells certainty before he sells anything else.

Blake is the most certain man in a room full of doubt, and the doubt bends toward him.

He never asks the room to believe in the product first. He makes them believe in the conviction itself. Walk in sure, talk sure, close sure, and the buyer borrows the certainty you carry instead of trusting their own hesitation.

Conviction closes before the pitch does.
