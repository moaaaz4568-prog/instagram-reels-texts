1. "I AM GOING TO STOP TALKING NOW. TELL ME WHAT YOU WOULD ACTUALLY DO WITH THIS."
Lewin's lecture groups got the full case for organ meats, delivered well. Three percent of them served it. The discussion groups heard the same facts and then talked. Thirty-two percent served it.
Make the pitch short and then hand the room the problem. A buyer who was told a conclusion owns it. A buyer who argued the conclusion rents it. A buyer who was told a conclusion rents it.
People defend the decisions they made. They forget the ones they were given.
