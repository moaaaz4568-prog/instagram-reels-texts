4. "Serve the audience the industry ignores."

Perry's documented strategy on his audience.

Hollywood considered Black church-going Southern audiences "uncommercial." He toured the "chitlin' circuit" of Black theaters for years, learning exactly what made them laugh, cry, and recommend the show to their family.

By 2005 his stage plays had grossed $100M in tickets and $30M in merchandise.
