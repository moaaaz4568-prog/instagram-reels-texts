4. "That is a fair point. Here is my answer."

Ron Swanson never took five sentences to respond to a question that deserved one.
Brevity in the face of an objection signals confidence in a way that length never can.

This phrase acknowledges the objection without validating its power over the conversation and delivers the response with the calm certainty of someone who has the answer before the question was finished.
The buyer who expected a long defensive response gets a short confident one and confidence always closes harder than explanation.

Answer in one sentence when one sentence is enough. It almost always is.
