6. “THE PEOPLE WHO WAITED ON THIS LAST MONTH ARE THE ONES ASKING ME IF IT’S STILL AVAILABLE. IT’S NOT.”
A story. A consequence. A warning. All in three lines.
This phrase doesn’t threaten. It reports. The buyer hears what happened to someone just like them who did exactly what they’re about to do.
Real social proof isn’t a testimonial. It’s a cautionary tale told at the right moment.
