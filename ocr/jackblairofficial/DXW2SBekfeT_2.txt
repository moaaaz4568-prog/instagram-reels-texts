2. "I have spoken to others at your level. The ones who moved quickly were right to."

Chuck Bass always made the people around him feel the weight of being left behind.
Not through pressure but through the quiet suggestion that the smart money had already moved.

This phrase creates social proof at the exact peer level the difficult buyer respects and nobody below it.
The buyer who would ignore any statistic you quoted suddenly cares deeply about what people exactly like them already decided.

Peer pressure at the right altitude closes rooms that logic never could.
