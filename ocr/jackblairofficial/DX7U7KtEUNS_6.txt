6. Every objection he heard went into a notebook.

Perot learned the technique selling encyclopedias door to door before IBM. The notebook came with him to EDS.

By year 3 there wasn't an objection a Fortune 500 CFO could raise that hadn't already been answered 200 times in writing.

The closer who's heard the objection 500 times destroys it in 30 seconds. The rep hearing it for the first time loses the deal.
