3. "Most people in your position say exactly what you just said. The ones who moved forward said something different the second time."

Reddington never let a predictable response settle in a room he was running.
He redirected with precision and a touch of pattern disruption that made the other side reconsider immediately.

This phrase signals that the objection or hesitation is a familiar script, not a unique position.
The buyer who priced themselves on independent thinking suddenly wants to know what the second response sounds like.
That curiosity is the door and you are already standing on the other side of it.

Disrupt the pattern. The buyer who is thinking differently is the buyer who is ready to move.
