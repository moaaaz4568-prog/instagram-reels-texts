6. "Stop reliving the deal you already lost."

Maltz saw that emotional scars outlast physical ones, and that people carry old failures like fresh wounds.

He taught that replaying a past loss programs your mind to expect another. The lost call is gone, but rehearsing it drags it into the next one. Let it go so it stops walking into the room with you.

Drop the old scar and the next call opens.
