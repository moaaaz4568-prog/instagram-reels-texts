6. "One suggestion before we lock in, and I'll only make it once."

The fries ask works because saying no costs nothing. No pressure, no repeat, no guilt. That's why nobody resents it after seventy years.

Make your attach ask once, make declining painless, and the yes rate goes up because the guard never comes up.
