6. He read the buyer and the moment
better than anyone.

Harvey is known for blunt, sharp instincts
about what people will buy and exactly
when.

Sixty years of selling taught him to feel
where the market and the buyer were
heading. He trusted that read and moved
fast while others hesitated. Pay close
attention to what people actually want,
then act before the moment passes.
