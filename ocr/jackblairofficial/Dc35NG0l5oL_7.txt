7. "CAN I CHECK BACK IN A WEEK AND HEAR
HOW IT WENT?"
Lewin's numbers came from a follow-up, not from
the meeting. He came back and asked who had
actually served the meat. Without the check, the
show of hands would have been theatre.
Book the follow-up inside the meeting, with a date,
and frame it as hearing the result rather than
chasing the order. The follow-up is what turns a
raised hand into a habit.
A commitment with a date attached is a plan.
Without one it is a mood.
