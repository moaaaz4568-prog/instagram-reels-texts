01. Warm Re-Entry

"Write me 5 outreach messages for prospects who have already shown interest in my offer by [describe the action: attended a webinar, downloaded content, visited the pricing page, requested a demo, engaged with an ad].
My offer is: [one sentence].
Their job title is: [job title] at [company type].

Each message must:
Reference their specific action as the reason for reaching out,
feel completely natural and not like a triggered email sequence,
and end with one question that opens a real conversation.

Under 70 words each.

Tone: warm, specific.

No pitching. No fluff.

You stop treating warm leads like cold ones.
Every message now picks up exactly where their interest left off.
