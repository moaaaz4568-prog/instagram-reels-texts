4. “YOU’D BE THE ONE HOLDING THE CONTROLS.”
Sense of power ranks high on the eight. He watched it sell cars, boats and boardroom contracts alike.
Hand the buyer levers, not features: what they can override, launch, or shut off themselves. A buyer who feels in command stops negotiating for safety.
Nobody resists a control panel built for them.
