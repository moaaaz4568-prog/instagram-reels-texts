1. Remote Sales (High-Ticket Closing)
Companies book sales calls and pay someone a cut of every deal they close. Offers run $5,000 to $30,000 and you keep 10 to 30%. A lot of these calls are evenings and weekends, so you can do it after a 9-5.
Where to find them:
- Closing-specific job boards
- Referrals from other reps
- Training-and-placement programs
