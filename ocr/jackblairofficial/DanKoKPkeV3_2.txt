2. "Here's the version you could walk out with today."

The manual's Present step is specific: present a solution the customer can take home today. Not someday, not after a committee. Today.

Frame your offer in immediate-ownership terms: what starts this week, what they'd have running by Friday. Distance is where deals go to die.

Sell the version they can hold today, not the one they'll consider someday.
