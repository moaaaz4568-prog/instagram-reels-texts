1. "Tell me what's worrying you about this."

The phrase that disarms the objection before the buyer fires it.

Sugarman called this trigger Objection Raising. Most reps wait for the buyer to push back. Sugarman taught reps to bring it up first.

The buyer who hears their own concern named out loud stops bracing.

The objection cycle dies the second the rep stops avoiding it.
