6. 4 networks said no before AMC said yes.

Gilligan pitched Breaking Bad to HBO, Showtime, FX, and TNT before AMC. All four passed.

AMC in 2008 was a basic cable network nobody took seriously. The show that won 16 Emmys was rejected by half of premium cable first.

The vehicle you need might be the one nobody else has noticed yet.
