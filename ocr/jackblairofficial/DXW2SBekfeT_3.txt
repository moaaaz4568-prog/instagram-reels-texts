3. "I am not going to oversell this. You are either the right fit for it or you are not."

Chuck Bass treated exclusivity as the highest form of value and he was right every single time he did it.

This phrase removes every trace of salesmanship from the conversation and replaces it with a filter the buyer suddenly wants to pass through.
The buyer who was about to object stops and starts wondering which side of the filter they are on.

Turn the close into a qualification. The buyer who wants to qualify never needs to be closed.
