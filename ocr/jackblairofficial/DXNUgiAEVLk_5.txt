5. "I am not going to oversell this. Here is exactly what you get and here is exactly what I expect from us both."

Tommy Egan's loyalty was always reciprocal and he made that clear from the start. No overpromising. Just a straight line No illusions. No what he offered and what he expected back.

This phrase builds trust faster than any polished pitch because it sounds like a partnership being formed in real time between two adults who respect each other. Buyers who have been oversold before respond to this like oxygen in a room that ran out of it.

Underpromise with confidence. It closes more deals than enthusiasm ever will.
