3. "Don't pee on my leg and tell me it's raining."

Her father's line. She liked it enough to make it the title of her first book.
Translation: I know a smokescreen when I'm standing in one.

Every rep knows the rain. "Send me some info." "We're definitely doing this next quarter." The use instead of the truth.

You don't say Judy's version on a call. You say yours: "Can I be straight with you? That sounds like a polite no. If it is, I'd rather know now." Buyers respect the rep who calls the weather.
