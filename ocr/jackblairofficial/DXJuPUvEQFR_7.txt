7. "At the end of this I want you to choose whoever is right for you. Let me just make sure you have the full picture."

Ted Chaough never begged for the account.
He made himself impossible to ignore and then let
the work close the room.

This phrase removes all pressure from the buyer and
all desperation from you.
It positions you as the advisor in the room, not the rep
competing for a signature.
Buyers move toward confidence. This phrase wears it
without saying the word.

The rep who needs the deal the least always
wins it.
