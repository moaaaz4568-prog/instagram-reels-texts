3. "WHAT DID WE IGNORE IN THE FIRST MONTH?"
Failures aren't sudden. They're ignored slowly, and the premortem gives that a past tense like an accusation. Asked straight, this question sounds like an accusation. Asked inside a story about a failure that never happened, it's easy. Your buyer lists their real worries and never once has to call them objections. An objection dressed as history stops feeling like a fight.
