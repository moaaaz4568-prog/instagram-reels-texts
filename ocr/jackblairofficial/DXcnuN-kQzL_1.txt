1. "I am going to tell you something that the last rep who sat here probably did not say."

Mark Baum's entire career was built on the willingness to say the thing the room was deliberately avoiding. That willingness was not recklessness. It was the most sophisticated competitive advantage available.

This phrase signals that this conversation is going to be different before you have proven it. The buyer who has been through a hundred polished pitches sits up for a rep who opens with that kind of directness. What you say next must be real, specific, and worth the setup.

Open with the promise of honesty. Then deliver something nobody else had the courage to bring.
