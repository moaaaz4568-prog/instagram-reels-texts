7. "I'm not asking you to trust me yet. I'm just asking for the chance to earn it."

This is the most disarming phrase in a cold rep's arsenal. It removes all pressure and replaces it with something every buyer responds to: fairness. Jimmy McGill built an empire on the back of this exact energy. You are not selling. You are auditioning. And you are confident enough to say so out loud.
