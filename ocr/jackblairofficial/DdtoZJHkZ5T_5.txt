5. You hear "let me think about it" at dinner and reach for a pen.

Somebody at the table says it about a holiday, a car, a restaurant for next weekend, and your whole body goes into a discovery call. What specifically do you want to think about. What would need to be true. Your family has learned not to say it anywhere near you.

The pen is a reflex now. Dinner was never safe.
