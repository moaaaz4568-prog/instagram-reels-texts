1. "BEFORE I PUT ANYTHING ON THE TABLE, WHAT WOULD YOU PUT ON IT?"
Lee Ross at Stanford named it reactive devaluation: the identical offer dropped from 90 percent approval to 44 the moment people heard it came from the other side.
So stop being the author. Open by asking for their version. A term is already worth more to them than the same term you volunteer.
The offer they write is the offer they defend.
