10. A trust protects the money you
already made. It does nothing for how
the money comes in.

For most families, the income
stops the day one employer decides it
should.

The families I see stay in control have
one thing in common: someone in the
house has a skill that pays wherever
they are.

For me that skill is selling. Specifically,
helping companies turn interested
prospects into customers, remotely.
