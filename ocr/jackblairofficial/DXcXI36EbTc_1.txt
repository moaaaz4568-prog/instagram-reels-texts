1. "I want to be upfront with you. I do not give up on deals I believe in and I believe in this one."

John Wick never abandoned a mission because it got difficult.
He recalibrated, he recovered and he kept moving because stopping was never an option he entertained.

This phrase signals a quality of commitment that buyers almost never experience from a rep.
The buyer who was expecting you to fade after the second no suddenly understands who they are dealing with.
That understanding changes the entire dynamic of every conversation that follows.

Signal the commitment early. The buyer who knows you will not disappear treats the relationship differently.
