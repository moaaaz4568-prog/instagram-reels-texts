2. "TELL ME YOUR SIDE OF THIS LIKE I'M NOT
THE ONE SELLING IT."
In mediation the stories change the moment sides
stop performing for an opponent. Piazza hears
versions of a case that never surfaced in two years
of lawyers.
Ask for the version they'd give a stranger with no
stake in the answer. A buyer who isn't being sold
stops defending, and the real story walks out on its
own.
Unsaid objections don't die. They wait.
