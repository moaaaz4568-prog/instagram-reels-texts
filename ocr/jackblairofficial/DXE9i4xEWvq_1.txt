1. "Before we get into anything, I just want to say I appreciate you making the time."

Bunk never rushed a conversation that needed to breathe. This phrase costs nothing and signals everything. It tells the buyer they are not just another call in your queue. Respect lands before the pitch does. And the buyer who feels respected before you ask for anything is already halfway to yes.
