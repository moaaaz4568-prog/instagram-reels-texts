6. "Stack the value until
the price feels small."

Hormozi's core teaching on
making price irrelevant.

Don't present one thing.
Present everything.

Every outcome. Every guarantee. Every bonus.
Every fee. Every result.

By the time the price lands
the buyer has already decided
the value is worth three times more.

Price isn't the problem.
Perceived value is.
