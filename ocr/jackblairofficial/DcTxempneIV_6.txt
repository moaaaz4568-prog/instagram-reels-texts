6. “YOU DON’T OWE ME THIS. THE WORK WAS THE DEAL.”
The bank paid 25 euros per introduction. Not a commission, a thank-you. The signal mattered more than the money; this is appreciated, never owed.
Kill the invoice energy before it forms. Say the work was the deal, the referral is a favor, and favors are optional. Optional is what makes people generous.
Nobody refers a debt collector.
