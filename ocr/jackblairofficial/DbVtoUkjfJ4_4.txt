4. "I CAN'T MOVE THE PRICE. I CAN MOVE WHAT YOU GET."
One-price shops still close hard deals, they just never bend the number. Neither should you.
Keep a list of things you can flex that aren't dollars: scope, speed, access, terms. Every concession from that list feels generous. A price cut just tells the buyer the first number was a lie.
Flex the package, never the number.
