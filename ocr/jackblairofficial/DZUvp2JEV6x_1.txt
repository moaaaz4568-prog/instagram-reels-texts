1. He treated the accent everyone pitied as his edge.

Fakharzadeh arrived from Iran with broken English certain his accent would cost him every sale.

It did the opposite. Prospects slowed down, listened harder, and remembered the foreign agent who explained everything twice and never rushed them. The thing he was ashamed of made him impossible to forget.

The flaw you apologize for is often the reason they remember you.
