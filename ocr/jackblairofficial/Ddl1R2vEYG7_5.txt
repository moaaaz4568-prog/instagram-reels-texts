5. "What would you have done, standing in that house?"

In 1926 Darrow defended Henry Sweet, a Black man who fired from a Detroit house surrounded by a white mob, and he asked an all white jury to stand inside that house for a few minutes. They acquitted him. When your buyer is on the fence, stop listing features and put them inside the Monday after they say yes.

Nobody buys a description. They buy a place they can stand.
