5. “I’M GOING TO BE STRAIGHT WITH YOU
BECAUSE I RESPECT YOUR TIME.”
Buyers are waiting for you to lie to them. They have
been burned before and they are armed.
This phrase disarms them before they can raise the
shield. It signals that what comes next is different
from every other rep they have heard. Honesty,
delivered first, is the sharpest sales tool alive.
The rep who earns trust in the first sixty seconds
owns the rest of the call.
