2. “WHAT DID THEY PROMISE YOU THAT MADE YOU COMFORTABLE?”
Not “what are they offering?” Not “what’s their price?” What made you comfortable.
This phrase reframes the competitor’s entire pitch as a comfort blanket. Comfortable is safe. Safe is average. Average doesn’t grow revenue.
One question. And suddenly their offer feels like settling.
