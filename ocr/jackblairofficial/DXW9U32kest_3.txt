3. "I have told you what I know. What else do you need from me to make a decision?"

Ron Swanson never repeated himself. He said the thing once, clearly, and then waited.
Repetition is what reps do when they do not trust the strength of what they already said.

This phrase signals complete confidence in everything that came before it.
It puts the next move entirely in the buyer's hands and signals that you are not going to fill the silence with more pitch.
The buyer who has been waiting for you to stop talking finally has the space to decide.

Trust what you already said. The buyer who needed more information would have asked for it.
