05. Trigger Sequence

"Build me a 4 touch outreach sequence
for a prospect who just experienced this trigger event:
[describe the trigger event: funding, new hire, expansion, job change or news].

My offer is [one sentence].
Their job title is [job title] at [company type].

For each touch include:
The channel, the core angle connected to the trigger,
how it builds on the previous touch,
and the suggested timing.

Each touch must feel fresh and use a different angle.
No repeating the same point across messages.

Tone: timely, varied, never repetitive.
No fluff. Zero guesswork."

You stop sending one triggered message and calling it a strategy.
You have a four touch triggered message and call sequence that builds urgency with every send.
