7. "I think we are ready to move forward. Let me tell you what the next step looks like."

Nucky Thompson never asked for permission to proceed when the outcome was already clear. He simply moved, with grace, with certainty, and with the full expectation of agreement.

This phrase assumes the close without demanding it. It is the most natural next sentence after a conversation that has been built correctly from the first word.

The buyer who has been guided here never a moment. It is just what happens next.

Build the conversation right and the close is next.
