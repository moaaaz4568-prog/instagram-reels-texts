4. "With all due respect, I think you've been looking at this the wrong way."

Walt corrected people who had more power than him without flinching.
This phrase does the same thing inside a sales call without burning the relationship.

It positions you as the expert in the room, not the vendor at the door.
Buyers don't want to be sold to. They want to be guided by someone who actually knows more than they do.

Be that person out loud and they will follow you to the close.
