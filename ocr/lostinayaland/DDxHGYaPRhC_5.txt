MY WHOLE WORLD FELL APART WHEN MY PARENTS FORCED ME TO STUDY ARCHITECTURE AND MOVE TO GERMANY. THOSE THREE YEARS WERE SOME OF THE HARDEST OF MY LIFE. WHILE I WAS THERE, I TRIED TRANSFERRING TO FILM SCHOOL IN THE USA, ONLY TO BE REJECTED BECAUSE THEY DIDN'T OFFER SCHOLARSHIPS TO NON-CITIZENS. I FELT LIKE I WAS RUNNING IN THE WRONG DIRECTION, COMPLETELY LOST.
