Ask high-level questions
Is this video part of a larger campaign? If so, what are the supporting assets and messaging? Where does this sit in the funnel - top of funnel awareness or bottom of funnel conversion? Are they running paid ads?

Don't be scared to ask these questions. It shows you understand the bigger picture beyond just delivering a beautiful video. Connect with their marketing team and position yourself as someone who gets strategy, not just production.
