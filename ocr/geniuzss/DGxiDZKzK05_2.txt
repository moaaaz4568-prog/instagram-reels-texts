Physical & Health Transformation

• Drink 3 Liters of Water – Stay hydrated for better focus and energy.

• Exercise Daily – Move your body, even if it’s just a walk or stretching.

• Fix Your Sleep Schedule – Get 7-8 hours of quality rest.

• Take Cold Showers – Boost discipline and mental toughness.

• Eat Whole, Unprocessed Foods – Fuel your body, not destroy it.

• Cut Out Sugar & Junk Food – Watch how fast your energy levels rise.
