Being alone is a part of life, it's a part of adulting.
Your friends and mates cannot stay with you for the
rest of your life. Life moves pretty fast and everyone
is trying to run faster so that they aren't left behind.
The simple truth of life is: people leave, for good or
bad and life goes on. Some people will leave for a
better job opportunity while there will be times when
you will have to leave people for career growth. And
then there will be times when people will get bored
of you and they will find another toy to play with.
That's how it is.
Can you do something about it? Probably not.
But you can do one thing and that is stop feeling
sorry for yourself. Stop thinking that there is something
wrong with you that needs to be fixed. Stop trying to
figure out what you can change in yourself or how
you can change in others.
