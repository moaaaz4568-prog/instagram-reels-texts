doing nothing or the art of being idle is somewhat
similar to me. Our world is full of cheap entertainment.
As soon as we feel bored, we put on Netflix or watch a
bunch of people dancing on YouTube or doing weird
acts on social media. All in all, entertainment is at
our fingertips and we access it at any time. But that
is making us weak and numb.

We wear the word ‘busy’ as a badge of honour.
If we are not working, we are focusing on personal
development, travelling, being productive, starting
businesses, learning, growing, creating, etc. And not
being busy feels scary. For some people it means that
they have to meet themselves face to face, for others,
it means not feeling worthy. Some can’t handle not
being perfect and useful. When we have free time, it
just doesn’t feel right.
