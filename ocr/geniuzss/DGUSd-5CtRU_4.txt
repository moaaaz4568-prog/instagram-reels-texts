Our lives have become a collection of others’
thoughts and judgements. The fear of judgement is
rooted so deep within us that before anyone else,
we start judging ourselves. David Foster Wallace says,
‘You’ll stop worrying what others think about you when
you realise how seldom they do.’
It’s quite ironic, but your biggest enemy and critic
is no one else but YOU. Before anyone else, you know
what you are up to, and before anyone can, you pass
judgements on your next move based on ‘what you
think people will think of you’. Look around you, no
one’s judgements are louder than your own in your life.
