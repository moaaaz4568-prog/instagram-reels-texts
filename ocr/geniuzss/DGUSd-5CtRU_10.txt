THE ART OF BEING ALONE

And that is the reason we call the time we spend with ourselves ‘lonely’.

Loneliness is not when you don’t have people around. Loneliness occurs when you cannot find yourself inside you. The moment you feel the loss of your real self, loneliness makes a home inside you. That’s the worst kind of loneliness where you might be surrounded by people yet you feel lonely. It’s like you cannot feel yourself there anymore. And do you know what’s even more ironic?

It starts with you trying to become someone you are not because you wanted to fit in. Like someone you hate being with. Tell me something, how can anyone accept a person who doesn’t accept themselves? How would anyone love to spend time with a person who hates spending time with themselves?
