THE CONTENT FORMATS
I WOULD USE
FOR DIFFERENT PILLARS:
05
MYTH VS. FACT CAROUSELS
Side-by-side correction of a common belief.
Strong for PCOS and Fertility, where misinformation is everywhere online.
06
“RATE THIS” OR REACTION REELS
React to a trending claim or viral video clip.
Good for Sex, Pain & Intimacy and PCOS, topics where bad advice spreads fast and correcting it publicly builds trust.
07
CHECKLIST CAROUSELS
“By age” or “by trimester” style breakdowns.
Best for Pregnancy & Postpartum and Preventive-style content within Fertility.
08
TEXT-ON-SCREEN TALKING HEAD
No script, just the doctor speaking directly to camera with key words on screen. Works well for Perimenopause & Menopause, feels like a conversation, not a lecture.
