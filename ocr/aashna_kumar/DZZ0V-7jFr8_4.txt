PROMPTS THAT IDENTIFY RELATABLE MOMENTS

• What are 25 everyday moments people in [industry] experience that would make great relatable content?

• What are the small frustrations clients experience when dealing with [industry]? Include frustrations they rarely talk about publicly.

• What are the awkward, funny, or unexpected situations professionals in [industry] deal with regularly?

• What are moments that make someone in [industry] internally say, "You've got to be kidding me"? Turn each into a content idea.
