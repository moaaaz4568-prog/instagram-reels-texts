THE CONTENT FORMATS
I WOULD USE
FOR DIFFERENT PILLARS:
01
Q&A REELS
Answer a real patient question on camera.
Works for every pillar, especially
Sex, Pain & Intimacy, where people ask
in DMs but never comment.
02
STORYTELLING REELS
An anonymized patient journey, told
start to finish. Best for Fertility & Conception
and Perimenopause & Menopause use, topics
with an emotional arc people relate to.
03
DOCTOR POV REELS
"Things I tell every patient about X."
Fast, personal, and works across all 5,
especially PCOS & Hormonal Health, where
people want it explained simply,
doctor-to-camera.
04
EDUCATIONAL CAROUSELS
Swipe-through lessons, highest save rate
of any format. Best for Pregnancy &
Postpartum checklists and Pericyn &
menopause
symptom breakdowns, where people want
to save and reread.
