For Building Strong Content Pillars
Prompt:
Build 4-5 content pillars for my brand that balance education, storytelling, and promotion. My niche is [add your niche]. For each pillar, list 3-5 subtopics, explain the emotional or practical value it brings to [target audience], and describe the best content formats (e.g., carousel, video, quote post). Make the pillars consistent enough to build authority but flexible enough to stay creative.
High-Converting CTAs
Prompt:
Write 15 creative, natural CTAs I can use in posts or videos about [topic]. Include CTAs that drive engagement ('Save this for later,' 'Tag someone who needs this') and ones that guide people toward a product or offer ('DM me for [offer],' 'Check out my [resource] if this helped you'). Keep them friendly, action-oriented, and aligned with how [audience type] actually speaks online.
