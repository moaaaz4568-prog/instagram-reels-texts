CONTENT PILLAR #4: ✨
"TRY SAYING THIS INSTEAD"
CONTENT IDEAS:
How to set a boundary
How to ask for reassurance
How to pause an argument
How to say no politely
How to express a need clearly
Phrases That Help
I need some time to think.
Can we talk about this later?
I feel overwhelmed right now.
I appreciate you, but I need to say no.
What I need is...
WHY IT WORKS:
People often know how they feel but cannot find the right words. Giving them a simple script makes the content immediately useful.
PRACTICAL. RELATABLE. SHAREABLE.
