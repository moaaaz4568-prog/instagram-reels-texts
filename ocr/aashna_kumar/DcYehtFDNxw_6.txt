REEL FORMATS
THAT WORK
FOR PSYCHOLOGISTS

1
POV REEL:
Shows a relatable thought, behaviour
or emotional situation.

2
EXPLAINER REEL:
Answers one common psychology
question in under 30 seconds.

3
PSYCHOLOGIST REACTS:
Adds professional context
to viral
relationship or mental-health advice.

4
“TRY SAYING THIS” REEL:
Gives viewers simple words for
boundaries and difficult con-
versations.

5
MYTH-BUSTING REEL:
Corrects a common belief
without
using complicated terminology.

EACH REEL SHOULD FOCUS ON ONE PROBLEM,
ONE EXPLANATION OR ONE PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY.
