CONTENT PILLAR #4 - ✨
TRANSFORMATION
STORIES

NOT:
"Another successful
acne treatment."

INSTEAD:
"She had tried 7 products,
but one daily habit kept
triggering her acne."

Then explain what changed.

WHY IT GROWS:

People remember the struggle
and the lesson, not just the result.

Always use patient consent
and avoid guaranteeing outcomes.

Be honest, transparent and
education-focused.
