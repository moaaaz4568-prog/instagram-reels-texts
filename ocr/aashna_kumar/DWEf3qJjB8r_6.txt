5. CAPTION THAT SELLS PROMPT
Write an Instagram caption that sells my digital product [product name] without sounding pushy. Start with a scroll-stopping hook. Then share a short personal story of why I created it. Then list 3 clear outcomes the buyer will get. Add one line of social proof (real or relatable). End with a soft CTA to comment [keyword]. Keep it under 300 words.
