Brand Strategy

01 — Define your offer, audience, and competitors. Map your three key differentiators: your unique method, specific results with numbers, and niche you serve.

02 — Act as a market research expert. Create a detailed ICP profile: top 10 problems, exact language they use, current solutions, what is failing, and their emotional state.

03 — Build a 3-part language library for my ICP: pain vocabulary, aspirational language, and self-framing. Keep phrases raw and specific to how they actually speak.

04 — Write a 70-90 word first-person positioning statement: who you help, the outcome you deliver with a metric, how you do it, and why you.

05 — Help me develop three to five content pillars. Each anchored to a specific belief, distinct enough that no post sits in two pillars, connected to a business outcome.

06 — Analyse my writing samples and build a voice profile: sentence structure, vocabulary, tone, openings and closings. Turn this into clear guidelines so another writer could replicate my style.
