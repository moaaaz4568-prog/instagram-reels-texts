Video Scripts & VSLs
37 — Write a 60-second talking head script: start with a strong hook, deliver one clear insight, and close with a simple CTA or thought. Keep it natural and jargon-free.
38 — Write a 3-5 minute talking head script between 450-750 words: include a hook, problem setup, key teaching points with clear examples, and a clear close with a CTA.
39 — Write five VSL opening hooks under 75 words each: start with a specific situation, build curiosity, and hint at the promise without revealing the solution.
40 — Write a 2-minute VSL problem section around 500 words: describe your audience’s situation in specific detail so they understood, without lecturing or telling them what to do.
41 — Write a 2-minute VSL solution section: explain how your offer works, what makes it different, and why it produces results. Keep the mechanism clear and avoid vague claims.
42 — Write a VSL social proof section: three 60-second spoken mini case studies, each opening with the result, then covering the starting point, actions taken, and outcome.
