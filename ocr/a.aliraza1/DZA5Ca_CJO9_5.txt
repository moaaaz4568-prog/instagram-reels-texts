Lead Magnet
Creation

25 — Generate 10 lead magnet
ideas: include a title, format, and
one sentence on why your audience
would use it immediately. Focus on
quick tangible results.

26 — Write 10 lead magnet titles
using varied formats: numbers,
outcomes, problems, with and
without format labels. Keep them
specific. Then choose the top 3 and
explain why.

27 — Create a detailed outline for
my resource: include an intro,
structured sections with clear
headings and key points, and a
closing that directs the reader to a
next step.

28 — Write a lead magnet
introduction under 200 words: speak
directly to the reader’s situation,
establish credibility, and clearly
explain what they will get and how
to use it.

29 — Write a lead magnet section
under 300 words: cover one key
point, include a concrete example,
and ensure the reader can take one
clear specific action immediately.

30 — Write a lead magnet closing
under 200 words: acknowledge
progress, show what is now
possible, and introduce your paid
offer as a natural next step, not a
hard pitch.
