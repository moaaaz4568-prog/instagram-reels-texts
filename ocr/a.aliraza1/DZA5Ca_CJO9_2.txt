Offer Positioning
07 — Write 10 sales page headlines under 15 words each. Lead with a clear outcome, timeframe, and audience. Pick the top 3 and explain why they will perform best.
08 — Write a value proposition built on three key outcomes. For each: state a specific result with a metric, explain its impact in 2-3 sentences, include one concrete example.
09 — Write a problem section under 200 words describing your audience’s current situation and frustrations in their own words. Make it specific and relatable, not lecturing.
10 — Write a this-is-for-you-if section with five specific sentence-length statements describing your ideal client’s situation, beliefs, and past attempts.
11 — Write a this-is-not-for-you-if section with four specific statements explaining who will not benefit and why. Focus on mindsets. Keep the tone direct but respectful.
12 — Write a 70-90 word first-person positioning statement: who you help, the outcome you deliver with a metric, how you do it, and why you. Make it instantly recognisable.
