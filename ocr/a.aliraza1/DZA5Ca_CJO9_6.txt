Email Sequences
31 — Write a first welcome email under 220 words: include the download link, one line on how to use it, and a brief credibility-based intro. Avoid Welcome or Congratulations.
32 — Write a second welcome email under 250 words: share a specific result as a short story, highlight one actionable lesson, and tease the next email. No selling.
33 — Write a third welcome email between 220-280 words: share one genuinely useful insight with no selling, build trust through value, and close with a line showing you understand the reader.
34 — Write a fourth welcome email under 260 words: introduce your paid offer as a natural next step, include 1-2 lines of proof, keep the tone soft, and add a clear CTA.
35 — Write a 240-300 word client case study email: open with the result, share the before and what you did, and close with a reflective line and soft CTA. Keep it story-driven.
36 — Write a 220-270 word email addressing a key objection: acknowledge it honestly, respond with a specific example or reframe, and close with a soft CTA.
