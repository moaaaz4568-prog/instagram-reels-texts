Organic marketing is your testing ground. Paid ads is how you amplify what’s working organically. You need both.

Don’t get stuck in “either or” thinking. It’s not organic OR paid. It’s organic AND paid. If you want to grow quickly, combine them.

Anyone saying “organic is the way” or “paid ads are the way” is trying to sell you something, so they’re heavily incentivized to make you believe that “their way” is “the way”
