Because our work is so intimately tied to who we are and what we love, it's hard to go even a day without being engaged in it.

You might say, "Well if I enjoy what I do, why does it matter?"

For one, most creators go through hidden struggles and certainly don't enjoy every aspect of what they do.

But even if you did, it's still not healthy to be in a place where you couldn't take time off if you needed it.

Because life happens...
