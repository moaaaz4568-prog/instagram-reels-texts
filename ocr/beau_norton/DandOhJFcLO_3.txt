There will always be more work to do. There is no destination. Put down your work for the day. It’ll be there for you tomorrow.

As an entrepreneur there’s this feeling of needing to get to the finish line. So you’ll be tempted to work more than you should.

But when you realize the finish line is an illusion, you’ll see that what matters isn’t working longer or harder. What matters is simply staying consistent and doing a little bit each day to move yourself forward.
