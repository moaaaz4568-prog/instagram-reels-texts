My Exact Workflow
What I do before every single build.

BEFORE YOU START
01 Identify your build type
App, landing page, component, or dashboard. The type tells you which site to open.

FIND REFERENCE
02 Go to the right site
Use this carousel as your cheat sheet. Match site to build type. Takes 30 seconds.

COLLECT INPUT
03 Screenshot 3 references
Pick 3 examples closest to your vision. Do this BEFORE opening Cursor or Claude.

GIVE CONTEXT
04 Drop into your AI tool
Attach screenshots. Say: match this design style. AI now has visual context, not just words.

FINAL CHECK
05 Ship and compare
Compare result to reference. Iterate once. The improvement will surprise you every time.
