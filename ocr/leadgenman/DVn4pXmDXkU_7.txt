Small Language Models

Compact, efficient models optimized for speed and specific tasks.

How it processes information:
• Same transformer architecture as LLMs
• Fewer parameters (typically under 10B)
• Often distilled from larger models
• Fine-tuned for narrow use cases

Why it works:
SLMs trade broad knowledge for efficiency, making them fast enough to run on-device or at scale.

Think of it as:
A specialist that knows one thing really well instead of knowing a little about everything.
