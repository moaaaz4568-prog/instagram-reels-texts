Large Language Models
A super-autocomplete engine that reasons using attention.
How it processes information:
• Breaks text into tiny pieces (tokens)
• Looks at all relevant tokens at once (self-attention = "focus where needed")
• Builds meaning layer by layer
• Predicts the next token
Why it works:
LLMs don't understand words, they understand relationships between pieces of text.
Think of it as:
A very smart "next-word predictor" that learned logic by reading the entire internet.
