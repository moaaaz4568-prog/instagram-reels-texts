7. Mistral Large 2
The Open-Source European

Best for: Developers, researchers, and privacy-focused organisations.

Strengths:
• Fully open-source, meaning you can run it on your own servers for security.
• Strong performance in generating code across over 80 languages and solving mathematical problems.
• Fast, transparent, and ideal for sensitive data.

Keep in mind: Not yet as fluent in creative or emotional writing as Claude or ChatGPT.

Use it when you care about privacy, independence, and running AI your own way.
