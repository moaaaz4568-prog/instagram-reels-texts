Week 1
Pick the idea & build the MVP with Claude

This week is about picking one tiny idea and shipping a working version with Claude Code.

Your checklist for week 1:

week 1:
🌶️ Pick one tiny app idea & write a one-page PRD (what it does, who it helps, what you charge)
🌶️ Paste the PRD into Claude Code
Let it scaffold Next.js + Supabase auth + Stripe checkout
🌶️ Push to GitHub and deploy on Vercel
Ugly is fine, working is the goal

By the end of this week:
You have a LIVE app on a real domain.
