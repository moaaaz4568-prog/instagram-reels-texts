Stop
A rule that tells the AI to stop writing when it sees a certain word or symbol.
Value Range : Any text or symbol.
Use Case
• Stop AI text generation at a period (".") to produce single concise sentences.
• Use a custom stop word or tag (e.g., "END") to control structured API responses and avoid unwanted text.
