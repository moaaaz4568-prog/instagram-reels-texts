Week 4
Make it properly scalable

Now that paying users are using it, lock down the infra so nothing breaks at 2am.
OST solo founders skip steps and pay for it later.
Don't.

Your checklist for week 4:
Add Sentry for error tracking & PostHog for analytics
So you actually see what users do and where things break
Lock down Supabase RLS, env vars, and Stripe webhooks
(the boring stuff that saves you from getting hacked)
Set up Resend for transactional emails & Trigger.dev for background jobs
So the app runs itself while you sleep
