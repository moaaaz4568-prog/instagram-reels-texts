Blue
1997
IBM's Deep Blue beat world champion Garry Kasparov at chess. It was the first time a machine outplayed a human at something we thought required pure intelligence.

Big Tech went all in. Google, Facebook and Microsoft started hiring every AI researcher they could find. The modern AI race officially started here.

2012
AlexNet
AlexNet crushed the ImageNet competition using GPUs and deep neural networks. This kicked off the deep learning era and changed AI forever.
