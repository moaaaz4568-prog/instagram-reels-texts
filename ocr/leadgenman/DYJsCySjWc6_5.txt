GANs
Ian Goodfellow invented Generative Adversarial Networks. Two neural nets competing against each other could now create realistic images, voices and video. The era of generative AI officially started here.
2014
AlphaGo
DeepMind's AlphaGo beat world champion Lee Sedol at Go, a game with more possible moves than atoms in the universe. AI was no longer just memorizing patterns. It was learning strategy.
2016
Voice AI
Voice assistants like Siri, Alexa and Google Assistant exploded into millions of homes. AI quietly moved from research labs into everyday life.
