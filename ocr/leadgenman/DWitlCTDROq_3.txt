Summary
vercel-react-native-skills provides practical, production-ready guidance and recipes for building performant React Native and Expo mobile apps. Use it when creating reusable components, optimizing long lists with FlatList/SectionList virtualization and getLayout, minimizing re-renders with useMemo/useCallback and PureComponent, and implementing smooth animations with Animated and Gesture Handler. It also covers Expo-specific (managed vs. bare) workflow, EAS Build, and testing configs.
