AI Terminology
Key AI Terms Explained →
AI – The science of making machines think, learn, and act like humans.
ML – Teaches machines to learn from data.
NN – Brain-inspired systems that spot patterns.
DL – Multi-layered learning for complex tasks.
Transformers – Models that understand context in data.
GenAI – AI that creates, not just analyzes.
LLM – AI trained on massive amounts of text.
GPT – A language model that generates text.
GPT-5.3 – The newest, smartest GPT version.
ChatGPT – Your everyday AI assistant for ideas and writing.
