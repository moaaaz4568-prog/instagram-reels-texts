Max Token
Sets the maximum length of the answer the AI
can give. If the number is small, answer is small
too.
Value Range : 1 to Infinity.
Use Case
• Limit chatbot responses to concise answers,
ensuring clear and focused conversations.
• Control length when summarizing long documents,
avoiding truncated or incomplete outputs.
