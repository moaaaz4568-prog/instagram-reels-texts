3. THE BEST QUALITY
Media quality
Use less mobile data
Using less mobile data may affect your experience on Instagram. For example, photos and videos may take longer to load.
Upload at highest quality
Always upload the highest-quality photos and videos, even if uploading takes longer. When this is off, we'll automatically adjust upload quality to fit network conditions.
Disable HDR video playback
HDR (high dynamic range) video offers greater quality in areas of extreme brightness or darkness. As a result, HDR content will appear brighter than standard content.
