Eurostat Structural Business Statistics - Primary official dataset (government/EU)	Provided baseline number of small and mid-sized enterprises (e.g., firms with 10-249 employees) by country to size the total addressable market.	Latest available data: 2021 (released 2023).	National statistical bureaus (e.g., DESTATIS for Germany, INSEE for France) collect similar business counts; definitions of SME size bands and update frequency may vary slightly by country. OECD enterprise statistics can also serve this purpose, though coverage and year might differ.
UK Business Population Estimates (Department for Business & Trade) - Primary official stats (government/UK)	Supplied the number of UK businesses by size (e.g., 5.47 million small and 35,900 medium enterprises) in 2022 to contextualize the UK market sizing (post-Brexit).	Data 2022 (published Oct 2023).	ONS Business Counts (inter-Departmental Business Register) provide similar UK enterprise figures; minor methodological differences (e.g., includes some additional small firms); both sources closely align, with negligible discrepancies in totals.
