Category 4:
Automation

181. Automate Sales Forecasting
Design an automation that pulls data from [CRM AND OTHER SOURCES], applies [DESCRIBE FORECASTING MODEL], generates weekly forecast reports, and flags deals needing attention.

182. Content Moderation Pipeline
Design an automation that receives content support tickets, categorizes them, assigns priority, routes to the right team, and tracks resolution time against SLAs.

183. Automate IT Ticket Routing
Design an automation that receives IT support tickets, categorizes them, assigns priority, routes to the right team, and tracks resolution time against SLAs.

184. Subscription Management System
Design automations for managing [DESCRIBE SUBSCRIPTION BUSINESS]. Handle signups, upgrades, downgrades, failed payments, renewals, and cancellations automatically.

185. Market Research Collection
Design an automation that monitors intelligence on [TOPIC OF INTEREST], filters results for relevance, deduplicates, and delivers a curated daily briefing.

186. Build an A/B Testing Automation
Design a system that sets up A/B tests for [DESCRIBE WHAT IS BEING TESTED], monitors results, determines statistical significance, implements winners, and documents learnings.

187. Automate Expense Management
Design an automation that processes expense submissions, validates against [DESCRIBE POLICY], gathers approver approval, rejects non-compliant expenses, and exports to payroll.

188. Build a User Lifecycle Automation
Design automated workflows for each stage of the user lifecycle for [DESCRIBE PRODUCT/PROCESS]. Identify at-risk users, trigger re-engagement, and win-back.

189. Quality Assurance Testing
Design an automated QA pipeline for [DESCRIBE APPLICATION]. What tests run on each commit? What runs nightly, and what blocks deployment if it fails?

190. Data Quality Monitoring System
Design an automation that monitors [DESCRIBE DATA SOURCES] for quality issues, alerts when data is missing or anomalous, and generates data quality scorecards.

191. Automate Partner Reporting
Design an automation that compiles [DESCRIBE PARTNER PROGRAM] performance data into partner-specific reports, and distributes them on a defined schedule.

192. Customer Communication
Design an automation that triggers personalized communications to customers based on [DESCRIBE BEHAVIORS OR EVENTS]. What messages are sent, when, and which channels?

193. Product Analytics Reporting
Design an automation that pulls product usage data from [DESCRIBE SOURCES], calculates key metrics, generates a weekly product health report, and distributes it to stakeholders.

194. Build a Security Scanning Pipeline
Design an automated security scanning program. [DESCRIBE WHAT IS BEING SCANNED]. What tools run, what findings are critical versus informational, and how are remediations tracked?

195. Automate Vendor Management
Design automations for vendor management including onboarding, contract renewals, performance reviews, invoice processing, and off-boarding.
