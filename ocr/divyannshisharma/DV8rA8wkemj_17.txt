Overview
Since its release in late 2024, Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP) has redefined how developers build and connect AI
tools, data, and tools. All Agents with MCP is the first comprehensive guide to this rapidly emerging standard, helping
engineers unlock its full potential with hands-on projects. Whether you're developing agentic workflows, bridging tools across
platforms, or creating robust multi-agent systems, this book walks you through every layer of MCP—from protocol structure to
agent implementation.
Author Kyle Stratis provides the practical expertise needed to build fully functional MCP servers, clients, and more. Unlike high-
level or fragmented documentation, this book gives you a deep, systems-level understanding of MCP's capabilities—and
limitations. With its flexible, modular approach, MCP continues to gain traction across the generative AI community; this
ensures you're ready to build with it confidently and effectively.
