Agent Teams provide visual dashboards that show how multiple AI agents collaborate on the same project or codebase. Each agent can handle a different part of the task while working together in a coordinated system. This allows complex work to be divided efficiently among several agents. As a result, development becomes faster, more organized, and easier to manage.
