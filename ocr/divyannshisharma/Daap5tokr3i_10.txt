11 HOW TO USE LOOPS
How to use
loops.
>_ /loop 15 minutes + goal
A loop lets Fable 5 run a task over and over automatically
without you touching it.
The format is simple: /loop [time interval] + goal
Example: "/loop every 15 minutes, check if my build is
passing and notify me if it fails."
To stop it: /loop stop [loop name]
This is one of the most powerful things you can do with Fable 5.
Set a loop and let it run while you do something else.
