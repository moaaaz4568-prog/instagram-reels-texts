2 Manually Verify Accounts

• Before scraping, Verify each account:
  - Check the username on X.
  - Confirm: [Image: flowchart]
  - Follower count (within range)
  - Post engagement (avg. 10K views/post)
  - Relevance to your audience

• Example:
  - @stevejobsfan – Co-founder of Apple, ~600K followers, solid engagement [green checkmark]
  - @drewhouston – Dropbox founder, ~208K followers [green checkmark]

Repeat this for 5–10 quality accounts. You’ll now have your seed list.
