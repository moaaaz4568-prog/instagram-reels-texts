Get campaigns details

1. get_campaign_details → pulls campaign config: name, emoji, timezone, attached sequence, sender setup, labels. Use case: understand how the campaign is wired before judging performance.
2. get_campaigns_reports → pulls 65+ lifetime metrics in one shot - leads total, in sequence, sent, opened, clicked, replied, interested, bounced, unsubscribed + sender breakdown. Use case: instant scorecard.
3. get_campaign_sequences → pulls every step's actual content: subject lines, body copy, channels (email/LinkedIn/SMS/WhatsApp), waiting times, conditions, A/B variants. Use case: audit the copy and the sequence logic. - returned 404 here (sequence not accessible / draft state)
4. get_campaigns_stats → pulls the time-filterable detailed funnel: leadMetrics (launched → reached → opened → interacted → answered → interested), messageMetrics with per-channel breakdown (email vs LinkedIn vs SMS vs WhatsApp), and per-step performance. Use case: deep performance analysis, period-over-period.
