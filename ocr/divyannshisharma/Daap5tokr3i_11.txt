A few important warnings before you start:
It runs longer than you expect. Hard tasks at high effort settings can run for many minutes. This is normal.
It can go beyond what you asked for. Fable is proactive by design. Use checkpoints to keep it in bounds.
Your old prompts may hurt you. Prompts you built for earlier Claude models can actually produce worse results on Fable 5. Start fresh.
It costs more. This is the most powerful and most expensive model Anthropic has built. Use effort levels wisely.
If it stops early — just say "go ahead and do it end to end. "That's enough to get it moving again.
