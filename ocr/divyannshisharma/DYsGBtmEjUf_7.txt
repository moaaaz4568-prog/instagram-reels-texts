Most people ask Claude to help them with ideas.
That means Claude builds on what you say,
adds to it, expands it.
You get agreement and elaboration.
Claude to attack it.
That is useful sometimes. But it is not how you stress-test an idea.
Before committing to any plan, decision, or piece of writing, ask
Not critique it. Attack it. The distinction matters.
Here is my plan: [describe your plan]
Your job is to destroy it. Find every assumption I'm making
that could be wrong.
Find every way this could fail. Argue the opposite position as hard as you can.
Do not be polite. Do not fail. Add qualifications. Just attack.
After that, steel man my position. Build the strongest possible case for why I'm right.
Then tell me what you actually think.
