Money
Ray Dalio in his 1987 book (principles: Life and Work) talks about the creation of credit. In the 16th century (referring to Mediaeval times), his point is that it allowed for more efficient movement of capital and allocation of capital.

In a similar way, AI is also focused on allocation of computation power — the more efficiently this can be done, the more productive gains can be achieved. In fact, Microsoft is one of the champions of this, as shown in the link: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/ai-computation-power/

At the same time, using «refining gap» analysis, we find that there’s a disconnect between the topics of money creation and these new and potential uses of AI between GPTs and USA.
