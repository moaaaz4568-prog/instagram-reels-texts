What is ‘prompt engineering’ anyway?
The practice of systematically improving prompts for LLM applications – through testing, evaluation, analysis, and optimization of prompts.

Skills involved:
- Programming in natural language
- Clear, unambiguous, precise writing
- Conceptual engineering
- Creating experiments with a scientific mindset
- Product thinking – what is the ideal model behavior for your product?
- Testing
- Understanding the LLMs
- Aggregating and understanding failure modes + thinking of ways to fix them
- Making LLMs scale to a wide range of inputs
- Modeling LLMs as a probabilistic system
