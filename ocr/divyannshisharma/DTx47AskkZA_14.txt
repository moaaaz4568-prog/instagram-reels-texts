This balance is maintained through several key strategies:
1. Utilizing Iterative, Short-Term Goal Setting (The Anchor)
Instead of setting rigid, long-term goals that can quickly become outdated, successful entrepreneurs use frameworks that allow for flexibility and adaptation.
