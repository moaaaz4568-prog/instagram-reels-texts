How to organise information in your prompt
>> how to organize
information in your prompts
- Use delimiters to clearly distinguish
various parts of the prompt
- Delimiters can be anything like """,
''' , < >, <tag> </tag>, :
- Example:
Summarize the text delimited by
""""" into a few sentences.
"""
<text to summarize>
</text to summarize>
"""
- Organize your prompt into logical
sections using section headers
- Example:
Summarize the following document
delimited by """" into a few sentences.
"""
<Document>
</Document>
"""
