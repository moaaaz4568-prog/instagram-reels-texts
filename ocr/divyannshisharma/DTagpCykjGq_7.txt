Prioritizing Flash's Utility

The "best" way to use it depends on whether you are an everyday user or a developer.
The "Flash" series of Gemini models (e.g., Gemini 1.5 Flash) is designed to optimize for volume efficiency, and massive context handling, and faster than the "Pro" or "Ultra" models, but with a (smaller) token count.
