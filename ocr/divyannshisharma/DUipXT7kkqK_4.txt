Locations and precedence
Skills (OpenClaw)
Skills are loaded from three places:
1. Bundled skills: shipped with the install (npm package or local skills: `~/.openclaw/skills
2. Managed skills: `<workspace>/skills
3. Workspace skills: `<workspace>/skills
If a skill name conflicts, precedence is:
`~/.openclaw/skills` (highest) > `~/.openclaw/skills` > bundled (lowest)
