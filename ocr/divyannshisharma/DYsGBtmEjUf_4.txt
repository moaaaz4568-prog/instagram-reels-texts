Claude is not a search engine
Most people use Claude the way they use Google. They type a question and wait for an answer. That is the lowest-value way to use it.
It is a thinking partner.
• Claude is not a retrieval tool.
• It doesn’t just pull information, it reasons, synthesizes, argues, and on context.
• The moment you treat it like a search engine, you cut its usefulness by 80 percent.
