File Organization Automations

#31 Downloads Folder Organizer Weekly Claude sorts your Downloads folder, renames files consistently, and deletes anything older than 60 days. Clean workspace without you touching it.

#32 Receipt Processor Monthly Claude scans your receipts folder, extracts date, vendor, amount, and category from each one, and creates a categorized spreadsheet with totals.

#33 Document Renamer Claude batch-renames files in any folder following your naming rules. Client files get the client prefix. Invoices get the date. Screenshots get descriptions.

#34 PDF Merger Combine multiple PDF files into one document with proper ordering. Useful for proposals, portfolios, and report packages.

#35 Knowledge Base Updater After every project Claude takes your raw notes and integrates the key insights into your organized knowledge base. Tagged, linked, and categorized.

#36 Invoice Generator Give Claude client name, project details, and amount. It generates a professional PDF invoice, saves it, and drafts the email to send with it attached.

#37 Backup Verifier Weekly Claude checks that your critical files have been backed up and flags anything that seems off.
