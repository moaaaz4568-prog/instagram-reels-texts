every conversation

Stop repeating
the same stuff!
It wastes tokens
and time.

If you’re pasting the same background
information into every new chat,
you are wasting tokens every single
time
and training yourself into a habit
that costs you more as Claude
usage scales.

DON’T DO THIS!
✗ Wastes tokens
✗ Costs more
✗ Trains you into

This is exactly what Projects and Custom Instructions are for.
Put your context in one place.
Let Claude read it automatically at the start of every session.
Never paste your background again.

If you are not using Projects yet

anything else in this article.
