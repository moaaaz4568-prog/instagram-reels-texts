What you get
- Manual entry form (date, expense/income toggle, amount, merchant, category, note)
- Four charts: monthly income vs. expenses, category donut, running balance line, top 10 merchants
- Month filter in the header drives the category + merchant charts and tile totals
- Recent transactions table with delete
- Export/Import JSON for backups
- Data persists in browser localStorage

Seeded with ~2 months of sample data so the charts aren't empty on first open. To clear it: hit Export first
if you want a backup, then in the browser console run localStorage.removeItem('finance.v1') and reload -
or just delete the sample rows one by one.
