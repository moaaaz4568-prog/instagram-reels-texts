Extended Thinking
Most Claude users have never turned this on. Extended Thinking is a mode where the Claude reasons through a problem step by step before giving you an answer, instead of going straight to the output.
For simple tasks, you don’t need it.
For complex decisions, analysis, or any question where you want Claude to actually think rather than pattern-match, turn it on.
In Claude, click the brain icon or add this prompt:
Think through this carefully before responding.
Work through the problem step by step, show your reasoning, identify where you’re uncertain, then give me your conclusion.
The difference in output quality on hard questions is significant.
