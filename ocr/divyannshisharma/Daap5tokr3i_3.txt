04 IT WILL ASK YOU QUESTIONS FIRST

It will ask you
questions first.

Before Fable starts a big task
it will ask you a series of
clarifying questions.

This is not a bug.
This is by design.

It does this because it needs to
fully understand the job before it runs
autonomously for a long time.
The more clearly you answer its questions
upfront, the better the final
result will be.

Don’t skip this step.
It saves you
hours later.

Better questions
upfront, better
results later.
