05 TIP 1: MATCH YOUR EFFORT LEVEL TO THE TASK
RT LEVEL TO THE TASK
Tip 1: Match your effort level to the task.
Not about using the highest. It's about using the right one.
Fable 5 has different effort settings. Think of it like a volume dial.
You don't blast the volume for a quick question. You do for your hardest problem.
Here's the simple guide:
• Low or medium — quick questions, simple rewrites, casual back and forth
• High — use this as your default for most things
• Xhigh — your hardest problems, complex builds, anything where the quality really matters
• Ultracode — full autonomous mode for big multi-step projects
Using Xhigh for everything wastes money and time. Match the level to the job.
