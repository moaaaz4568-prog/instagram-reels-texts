The CLAUDE.md Hierarchy
There are four layers that merge
1. Managed Policy (org-wide): IT deployed. Cannot be overridden. Organization-wide rules.
2. ~/.claude/CLAUDE.md (global): Your personal preferences across all projects. Not version controlled.
3. /CLAUDE.md (project root): Team instructions. Committed to git. Everyone who clones the repo gets these.
4. CLAUDE.local.md (personal project context overrides): Your personal tweaks for this specific project. Automatically gitignored.
