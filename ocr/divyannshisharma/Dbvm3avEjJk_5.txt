06 TIP 2: TELL IT WHY, NOT JUST WHAT
Tip 2: Tell it WHY,
not just what.
This is the biggest shift you need to make.

Old way
Prompt
Write a blog post about AI agents.

Fable 5 way
Prompt
Role:
You are a senior content strategist and writer
who specializes in AI and automation.
Task:
Write a blog post about AI agents.
Context:
- Target audience: technical professionals
- Goal: explain what AI agents are, real-world
use cases, and why they matter
- Tone: clear, insightful, and actionable
- Include examples and a strong conclusion
Format:
- 1000-1200 words
- Use markdown with headings, bullets,
and examples
- Include an SEO-friendly title and meta
description
Constraints:
- No fluff or repetition
- Cite credible sources where relevant

Old way: "Write me a landing page."
Fable 5 way: "I'm building a landing page for a fitness app targeting busy
moms. They need to sign up for a free trial. Write the hero section copy."
Fable 5 needs the full picture to do its best work. Give it the context and it
will figure out the right approach on its own.
Use this formula every time: "I'm working on [the bigger task] for [who it's
for]. They need [what the result enables]. With that in mind: [your actual
request]."
