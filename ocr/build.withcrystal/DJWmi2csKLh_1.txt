Growing up in a small English town with a traditional Chinese mum meant one thing:
Success had a very specific definition.
Be a doctor. Marry young. Have kids before 24.
There was no Plan B.
I struggled at school. My grades were OK, but nowhere near what Asian parents deem as acceptable.
Being one of the two girls in the area made it worse. I was bullied heavily, but my parents never did anything about it.
