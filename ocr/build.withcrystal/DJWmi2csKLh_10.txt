Most nights I couldn't sleep.
My body was in constant
stress.
I'd lie awake worrying about
making payroll, about whether
we'd need another funding
round.
Raw material costs are
increasing. Ad costs are rising.
Customers are demanding more
after-care support.
The weight of responsibility
was crushing me on the inside.
And eventually manifested
through the way I looked.
I stopped
going to the gym,
ate like sh*t, and looked my
absolute worst in years.
