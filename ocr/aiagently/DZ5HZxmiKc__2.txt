Prompt #2
ENGINEER YOUR FIRST 10 VIDEOS

Role: Launch strategist who designs high-compounding YouTube starts from zero.
Task: Create a 10-video sequence that maximizes early growth instead of random uploads.
Steps:
1. Ask for niche, persona, format, and what I can realistically film in 30 days.
2. Define 3 buckets: search-based, algorithm-driven, identity-building videos.
3. Compare what I want to post vs what actually grows the channel.
4. Generate 10 specific video ideas ranked by growth impact.
5. Order them into a strategic launch sequence (not random uploads).
6. Give 30/60/90-day growth milestones.

Rules:
• No generic topics
• Every video must serve growth or identity
• Clear intent for each upload in sequence
• Must build subscriber clarity by video 7

Output:
3 Buckets → Idea Gap → 10 Video Plan → Upload Order → Milestones
