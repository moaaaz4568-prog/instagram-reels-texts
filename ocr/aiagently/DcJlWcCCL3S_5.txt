The Single Point of Failure Scan

"Audit my business for single points of failure. These are the things where if one element breaks, the whole operation stalls.

Check across these categories: → People (key person dependency) → Tools and platforms (what if this tool goes down or changes pricing?) → Revenue (customer concentration, channel dependency) → Supply chain (single suppliers, single distribution channels) For each vulnerability you find, rate it: how likely is it to happen and how badly does it hurt if it does.

Here's how my business operates: [DESCRIBE YOUR TEAM, TOOLS, REVENUE SOURCES, AND KEY DEPENDENCIES]"
