Prompt #2
Customer Churn Betrayal
Simulation
“You are a real customer who used my product or
service for 6 months, initially loved it, but has now
decided to cancel.
Write me the raw, honest breakup message explaining
exactly why you’re leaving. Include what gradually
frustrated you, what finally pushed you over the edge,
and where you’re going instead.
Be emotionally raw and direct — write it like you would
text a close friend, not a polite support ticket.
Here is what my product does and who it serves:
[DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT AND TARGET CUSTOMER]”
