4. The Caption Writer

Write 20 Instagram captions for [TOPIC/BRAND].
For each:
- Attraction (first line that stops scrolling)
- Body (2-4 paragraphs)
- Hashtags (10-15 mixed size)
- Call to action
Formats to include:
- Educational posts (value)
- Personal stories (relatable)
- How-to tutorials (useful)
- Opinion pieces (provocative)
- Product/service promos (selling)
Write like a human. Not a brand.
