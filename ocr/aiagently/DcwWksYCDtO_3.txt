Phone Number ID

123456789012345

EABsbCSzXXXXXXXXXXXX

3
Verify Token

webhook requests.
my-verify-token-123

These keys let your app
talk to WhatsApp.
.env.local
1 WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID=123456789012345
2 WHATSAPP_ACCESS_TOKEN=EABsbCSzXXXXXXXXXXXX
XXXXXXXXXXXX
3 WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN=my-verify-token-123
