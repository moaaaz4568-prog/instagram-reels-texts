Look at Your Competitors

In 2025, over 42% of small businesses in the U.S. are testing or using AI to handle calls (source: SMBTech & Forbes).

Barbers in London, Dubai, and LA are already using Voice AI agents to:

✅ Book appointments 24/7
✅ Text reminders (cut no-shows by 20–30%)
✅ Upsell services (“Want to add beard trim?”)

The truth:
you’re not losing clients because of bad service
you’re losing them because no one picked up.
