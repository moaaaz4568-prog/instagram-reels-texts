Step 7
Give Your Agent Your Business Knowledge
Create a system prompt file inside your project and give your agent everything it needs to know.

What is a system prompt?
It's a file that tells your agent who it is, what to do, and how to behave.

Your Project
system-prompt.txt
src
config
data
logs

What to include:
Business details
Name, type, tagline, short description
Services & pricing
What you offer and how much it costs
Opening hours
When you're available
Location
Address and area coverage
Policies & FAQs
Rules, terms, common questions
Brand voice
Tone, style, and how you want to sound
Booking rules
How to schedule, confirm, and follow up

The best part?
Update the system prompt anytime without rebuilding your entire application.

Prompt anytime your entire application.

Your business knowledge changes. Your agent adapts.
