Prompt #1
Competitor Game
“You are my most intelligent and aggressive competitor. I will describe my business model, pricing structure, and target customers in detail.
Your job: Create the complete strategy you would use to take my customers away, weaken my market position, or make my entire offer obsolete within the next 12 months.
Be extremely specific. List out the exact moves you would make and provide a realistic timeline for execution.
Here is my business:
[DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS MODEL, PRICING, TARGET CUSTOMER, AND KEY DIFFERENTIATOR]”
