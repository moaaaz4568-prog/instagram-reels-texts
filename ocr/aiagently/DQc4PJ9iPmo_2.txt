The Real Reason It Happens

Your receptionist isn’t the problem.
The system is.

Receptionists can’t:
– Answer 3 calls at once
– Work after hours
– Follow up with every no-show

So patients hit voicemail → call the next clinic
→ book there. And you lose – quietly.

Not because you don’t have demand…
because you don’t have capacity.
