6 months after I turned 18 I found myself sitting
with my brother and mom beside me at the
oncology centre.
It was a Friday afternoon
when the doctor came in and told us
"I'm sorry, but there's nothing else we can do"
It's been 9 years since my father passed away and to this day
whenever I hear anything close to that phrase my eyes water up
