The trade off
The only way I could sustain myself in Cyprus was to perform music at hotels.
Trading my love and passion for music that has been my only true saviour for income.
I had to put on a mask, pretending that everything was going great while playing songs I love to people who gave literally 0 f*cks about it.
I would spend all my driving hours listening to podcasts and audiobooks. I distinctively remember listening to a Jim Rohn seminar saying
“If you want things to change, you have to change. If you will change, everything will change for you.”
