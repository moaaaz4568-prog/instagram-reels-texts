EVERY YOUTUBE INTRO
NEEDS THESE 5 THINGS:
copy this and you'll be set.
1. Context (with click confirmation) - what is the topic or
what will the video be about? This can be framed as a bold
statement or a matter of fact contextual line
2. Stakes/Relevance - why will this video help the viewer?
Specifically, a reminder of what painpoint this is solving
and why that painpoint is important. The more painful this
pain point, the better
3. Contrast (Common Take + Contrarian Belief) - what most
people think vs what I think (for how to solve the stated
pain)
4. Proof - why should you trust me?
5. Plan - how am I going to walk through the approach or
the different things we'll cover?
