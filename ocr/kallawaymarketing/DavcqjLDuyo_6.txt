Hero's Journey Story Structure

1 INTRO
establishes the hero and the problem

2 HIGHLIGHT PAIN
highlights the pain points from the problem

3 EXPLAIN FAILED SOLUTIONS
explains the failed solutions / attempts they tried to solve the problem

4 INTRODUCE SOLUTION
introduce the solution to this problem

5 EXPLAIN SUCCESS
explain the success you've seen because of the use of the solution

6 CTA
optional call to action
