Write a 60-second talking head script:
- start with a strong hook
- deliver one clear insight
- close with a simple CTA or thought

Keep it natural, jargon-free, and include a note on tone.

Write a 3-5 minute talking head script (450-750 words):
- include a hook
- problem setup
- key teaching points with clear examples
- and a clear close with a CTA or final thought

Keep it natural and conversational.
