Offer Positioning & Sales Copy

Write 10 sales page headlines:
- each under 15 words
- leading with a clear outcome, timeframe, or result (with metric/timeframe)
Avoid vague adjectives and questions.
Then pick the top 3 and briefly explain why they’ll perform best.

Write a value proposition built on three key outcomes.
For each:
- state a specific outcome (with metric/timeframe)
- explain its impact in 2–3 sentences
- include one concrete example or recognisable scenario
Sonnet 4.7

Write a problem section (under 200 words) describing your audience’s current situation and frustrations in their own words.
Make it specific and relatable so they feel deeply understood, not lectured.
Sonnet 4.7

Write a “this is for you if” section with five specific, sentence-length statements describing your ideal client’s:
- situation
- beliefs
- past attempts
Make each feel precise enough that the right reader instantly recognises themselves.
Sonnet 4.7

Write a 70–90 word, first-person positioning statement:
- who you help (specific situation)
- the outcome you deliver (with metric/timeframe)
- how you do it (method)
- why you (proof)
Make it clear, specific, and instantly recognisable to your ideal client.
Sonnet 4.7

Write a “this is not for you if” section with four specific, sentence-length statements explaining who won’t benefit and why.
Focus on mindsets or situations, and keep the tone direct but respectful.
