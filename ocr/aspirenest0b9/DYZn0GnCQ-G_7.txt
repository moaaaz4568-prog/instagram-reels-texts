Webinar & Event Promotion

Write a webinar registration page (under 450 words).
Include:
- headline
- clear audience
- outcome-focused content
- host intro
- clear CTA with date and time

Write the registration confirmation email for my webinar: [title] on [date and time].
- Confirm their spot.
- Tell them exactly what to expect on the call in two to three sentences.
- Give them one thing to do or think about before they join.
- Close with a line that makes them look forward to the session.

Write a 24-hour webinar reminder email (under 180 words):
- restate key outcomes
- include the join link
- suggest one way to prepare
- add three subject lines designed to maximise opens

Write a one-hour webinar reminder email (under 100 words):
- confirm the time
- include the join link
- restate one key reason they signed up
Include three subject lines.

Write a 160–210 word LinkedIn post promoting your live training:
- open with the problem
- outline what attendees will gain
- include date/time
- close with a clear registration CTA

Write a three-minute webinare opening script:
- introduce your credibility through specific results
- define who it’s for and what they’ll gain
- give one instruction to get the most from it
Keep it natural and conversational.
