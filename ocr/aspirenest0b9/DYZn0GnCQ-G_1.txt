Brand Positioning

Act as a market research expert.
For my defined audience, create a detailed profile:
- top 10 problems, exact language they use, current solutions, what's failing, and their emotional state.
Keep it specific to this stage, there should be no generic founder insights.

Using the audience analysis, build a 3-part language library for my ICP:
1. Pain vocabulary: how they describe problems.
2. Aspirational language: how they define success.
3. Self-framing: how they describe their expertise.
Keep phrases raw, specific, and true to how they actually speak.

Help me develop three to five content pillars.
Each pillar must be anchored to a specific belief or observation rather than a broad topic.
Each pillar must be distinct enough that a post from one could not plausibly sit within another.
Each pillar must connect to the business outcomes my audience cares about.

Analyse my writing samples and build a voice profile:
- sentence structure
- tone
- vocabulary
- openings/closings or stories
- use of examples
Then turn this into clear second-person guidelines so another writer can replicate my style accurately.

Write a 70–90 word, first-person positioning statement:
- who you help (specific situation)
- the outcome you deliver (with metric/timeframe)
- how you do it (method)
- why you (proof)
Make it clear, specific, and instantly recognizable to your ideal client.

Define your offer, audience, and competitors.
Then map key differentiators:
1. Your unique method.
2. Specific results achieved in numbers.
3. The niche you serve.
For each, write 2–3 clear, specific sentences, no vague claims.
