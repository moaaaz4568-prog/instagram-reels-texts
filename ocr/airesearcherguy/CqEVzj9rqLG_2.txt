Bard Experiment
There are a few possible explanations
for why the boat might suddenly be
empty, even though it has not sunk.
• It is possible that the people on
the boat jumped overboard,
perhaps because they were in
danger or because they wanted
to swim to shore.
• It is also possible that the boat
was attacked by pirates or other
criminals, who took the people
aboard captive or killed them.
• Finally, it is also possible that the
boat was simply abandoned by
its crew, perhaps because they
were lost or because they had
run out of fuel.
WRONG!
