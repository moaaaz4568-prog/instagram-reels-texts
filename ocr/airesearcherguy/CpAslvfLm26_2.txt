Behavioral Economics
Behavioral economics is the study of the effects of irrationality and emotion on consumer behavior. It looks at the impact of psychological biases on both decision making and customer outcomes.

Subliminal Advertising
Subliminal advertising attempts to influence consumer behavior by providing sensory messages that fall below the conscious level of awareness. Typically, this includes a message that is flashed for a brief period of time, or words presented unrecognizably.

By understanding the psychological principles behind consumer decision making, businesses can use this knowledge to inform their marketing strategies, understand their customers, and gain their loyalty.
