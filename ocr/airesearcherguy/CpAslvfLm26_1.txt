Emotion
Emotion is also essential to understanding consumer psychology. How people feel, or are made to feel, directly impacts their decisions. To truly engage in successful marketing, businesses must create campaigns that speak to the emotions of their target audience.

Beliefs, Biases, and Cognitive Dissonance
Humans make decisions based off of their deeply-rooted beliefs and biases. Our beliefs and perceptions of the world directly affect our actions and how we process incoming stimuli. Cognitive dissonance is the concept that examines how conflicting thoughts and beliefs can lead to psychological tension and drive ambivalent decisions.

Heuristics and Mental Shortcuts
Heuristics refer to mental shortcuts used to make decisions quickly and help reduce effort. They are based on prior knowledge and experience that people use every day to make decisions.
