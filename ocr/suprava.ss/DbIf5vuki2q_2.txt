Step 1: Get your first 10 sales by hand.
Written by you, signed by you.
Ten letters
Pick ten companies you'd genuinely want to work with. Look up their actual office address, not a PO box.
Write each one a page. Mention the specific thing you noticed about their business, not the flattering version of it.
Sign it in pen, put a stamp on it, drop it in the mail.
Most people have never received a letter at work in their life. That's the entire reason this works.
Takes a Saturday afternoon.
You'll hear back from people who never once opened your emails.
