Strategy to build lead list
The lead list
a lot of companies look similar. three filters decide who actually goes in.
everyone who looks similar
matches the ICP
right title, verified email
has a reason to care
your list
small on purpose
collect for every prospect:
first_name | company | job_title | work_email
website | linkedin_url | trigger_or_research_note
Alex | XYZ | CEO | alex@xyz.com
xyz.com | linkedin.com/in/alex | shipped v2, no posts yet
before you add anyone, four questions:
does this company match my ICP?
is this the right decision maker?
is the email address valid?
why this offer fits them?
can I say this?
not mostly yes? they do not go in.
The better the list, the less the copy has to work
