2. Most people are
watching with the sound off
If your captions are just plain text sitting on
screen, you're losing the majority of your
audience before they even hear what you're
saying.
Good captions aren't subtitles.
They guide the eyes.
Movement, emphasis, timing, animation...
that's what keeps someone watching instead
of scrolling past.
I stopped leaving mine on default and
started animating them properly.
Takes seconds. Changes retention every
time.
