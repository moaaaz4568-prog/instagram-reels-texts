Storytelling Hooks

1. I really hope I'm not the first person that tell you this but...
2. I love [-] but [boring action] kind of sucks, so here are [X] things I do to make [boring action] suck a lot less.
3. I [did action] every day for [X] days. This is what happened.
4. If I woke up [in [undesirable state] tomorrow and wanted to [get result] [in time], here's exactly what I'd do.
5. Here's how you turn [small number] into [bigger number].
6. It took me [X] years to master [a skill], but I'm going to teach you the [X] most powerful lessons in the next [X] seconds.
7. I've spent [X] hours testing different methods. Don't waste your time. Here's the [X] that actually worked.
8. I've [listened to/read/watched] over [X] [content types] on [topic]. Most were useless, but these changed how I think forever.
