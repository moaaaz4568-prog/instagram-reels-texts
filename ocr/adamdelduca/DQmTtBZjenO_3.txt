4. Gather visuals efficiently
You’ll need footage but you don’t have
to film it.
Use these sources:
• Pexels & Pixabay (free stock
footage)
• Storyblocks / Artgrid (paid B-roll)
• Manufacturer videos (press footage
= public use)
• Third-party footage
Keep all clips organized by category:
“interiors,” “buttons,” “maintenance,” etc.
