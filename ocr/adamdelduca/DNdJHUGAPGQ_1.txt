• CTR (Click-Through Rate) – % of viewers who click after seeing your thumbnail/title.
• AVD (Average View Duration) – Average time viewers watch your video.
• Watch Time – Total minutes people have watched your videos.
• Impressions – Times your thumbnail is shown to viewers.
• Impression CTR – % of impressions that turned into views.
• Audience Retention – % of a video watched on average.
• Traffic Sources – Where views are coming from (search, suggested, etc).
• Unique Viewers – Estimated number of individual users who viewed.
• Returning Viewers – Viewers who come back to your channel.
• Real-Time Analytics – Live stats on how a video is performing.
