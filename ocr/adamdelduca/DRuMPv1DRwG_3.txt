3. Create a Repeatable Format
Faceless channels thrive on consistency. Pick one style:
• Animations (Videoscribe, Vyond)
• Stock footage compilations (Pexels, Storyblocks)
• Slide-based explainers (Canva, PowerPoint + voiceover)
• AI-enhanced storytelling (SubscribrAI for scripts, ElevenLabs for voiceovers).
