• Thumbnail – Custom image representing the video.
• B-Roll – Supplemental footage used to cover cuts or add context.
• Voiceover – Narration recorded separately from on-screen footage.
• Script – Planned outline or word-for-word video content.
• Editing Software – Tools like Premiere Pro, Final Cut, CapCut, etc.
• Render – The final process of exporting your edited video.
• Frame Rate – How many frames per second your video plays (e.g., 24fps).
• Resolution – Video quality (e.g., 1080p, 4K).
• Aspect Ratio – Width to height ratio (e.g., 16:9).
• LUTs – Presets to color grade your videos.
