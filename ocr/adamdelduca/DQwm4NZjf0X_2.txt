2. You don’t need to show your face

Most professionals don’t want to be on camera. The good news? You don’t have to.

Faceless channels let you stay private while building public leverage.
You can:

• Narrate with your voice (or AI)
• Use stock footage and B-roll
• Automate editing and posting

All you need is an idea and a voice.
