McDonald's Isn't Actually a Fast Food Company?
Opening Hook
It’s a well-known fact that McDonald’s is one of the most recognizable
brands in the world. But did you know that it’s not actually a fast food
company? That’s right, McDonald’s is a real estate company. Yes, you
heard that right. McDonald’s makes most of its money from real estate.
How does that work? Well, McDonald’s buys or leases the land where
its restaurants are located. It then leases that land to the franchisees
who operate the restaurants. This means that McDonald’s makes money
from rent, regardless of how well the restaurants are doing.
The Real Reason
The real reason why McDonald’s is a real estate company is because it
allows the company to have a stable source of income. Fast food
industries are known for their high turnover rates and competitive
markets. By focusing on real estate, McDonald’s is able to mitigate some
of the risks associated with the fast food industry.
Conclusion
So, the next time you go to McDonald’s, remember that you’re not just
getting a burger and fries, you’re also supporting a real estate empire.
