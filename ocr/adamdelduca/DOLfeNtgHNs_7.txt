• Content ID – Tool to detect copyright-protected content.
• Strike System – YouTube’s penalty system for policy violations.
• YouTube Studio – Dashboard for managing your channel.
• Copyright Claim – When another party claims ownership of your content.
• Ad Suitability – Determines if content is advertiser-friendly.
• Limited Ads – Videos that can only show a restricted set of ads due to content.
• Revenue Share – The percentage split between YouTube and creators (55/45 for ads).
• Estimated Monetized Playbacks – Number of video views that showed ads.
• Copyright Strike – A penalty for using copyrighted material without permission.
