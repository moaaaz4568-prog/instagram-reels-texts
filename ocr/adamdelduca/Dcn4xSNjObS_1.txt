I am thinking about starting a dividend investing faceless YouTube channel for people over fifty. Give me every reason why I should and should not start that channel.

Here's a full rundown, organized so you can weigh them against each other.

Reasons to start:
• The format itself is good.
• Faceless removes the biggest barrier for most people — no camera anxiety, no need to look "YouTube-ready," and you can eventually outsource voiceover/editing to scale.
• Low production cost: stock footage, screen recordings of charts/portfolios, text-to-speech or a simple voiceover, and a template-based editing style.

Reasons to stop:
• It’s a saturated niche.
• You’re competing with established channels.
• It’s hard to stand out without a unique angle or personality.
• Monetization can be slow to pick up.
• You need consistency and patience — results take time.
