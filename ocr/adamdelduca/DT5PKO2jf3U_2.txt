McDonald's Isn't Actually a Fast Food Company?
Opening Scene
McDonald's is the biggest fast food chain in the world. But is it really a fast food company?
The Ray Kroc Revelation
In 1954, Ray Kroc met the McDonald brothers, Maurice and Richard, in San Bernardino, California. They had already built a successful burger stand. Ray Kroc saw potential in their system and decided to franchise it.
The Ray Kroc Deal
Ray Kroc didn't just buy the McDonald's name; he bought the entire system. He realized that the real money wasn't in the burgers, but in the real estate. He started buying the land where McDonald's restaurants were built.
The Real Estate Mogul
Ray Kroc formed a company called Frame Company, which bought the land and leased it to McDonald's. This meant McDonald's was paying rent to Ray Kroc's company.
The Expansion
Ray Kroc franchised McDonald's across the United States and eventually worldwide. He used the real estate model to fund the expansion.
Conclusion
So, is McDonald's a fast food company? Or is it a real estate company that happens to sell burgers? The answer might surprise you.
