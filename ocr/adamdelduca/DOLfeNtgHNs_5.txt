• SEO (Search Engine Optimization) – Optimizing content to appear in search.
• Tags – Keywords added to help YouTube categorize your video.
• Description – Text below the video to explain and add keywords/links.
• Hashtags – Keywords preceded by “#” to help with discovery.
• End Screen – Interactive elements at the end of videos.
• Cards – Pop-ups that link to other videos or playlists.
• Community Tab – Feed for posting text, images, and polls.
• Playlists – Organized collections of related videos.
• Suggested Videos – Recommendations YouTube makes to viewers.
• YouTube Shorts – Vertical videos under 60 seconds.
