- Niche – The focused topic area of your channel.
- Target Audience – The type of viewer you’re aiming to attract.
- Value Proposition – The core reason viewers should watch you.
- Hook – The attention-grabber at the beginning of a video.
- Pacing – The speed and rhythm of your content delivery.
- Call to Action (CTA) – Encouraging viewers to like, subscribe, comment, etc.
- Content Pillars – Core themes your videos consistently revolve around.
- Series Format – A repeatable content style across multiple videos.
- Trendjacking – Leveraging trending topics to gain views.
- Batch Filming – Creating multiple videos in one session.
