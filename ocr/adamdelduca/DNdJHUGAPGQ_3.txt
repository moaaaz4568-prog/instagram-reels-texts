• AdSense – Google’s program that pays you for ad views/clicks.
• RPM (Revenue per Mille) – How much you earn per 1,000 views.
• CPM (Cost per Mille) – What advertisers pay per 1,000 ad views.
• Super Chat – Donations during live streams.
• Channel Memberships – Monthly paid subscriptions for fans.

• Sponsorships – Brands paying for product integration or mentions.

• Merch Shelf – Selling branded products through YouTube.
