McDonald's Isn't Actually a Fast Food Company?
Opening Scene
McDonald's is the biggest fast food chain in the world, with over 38,000 locations in more than 100 countries. But did you know that McDonald's isn't actually a fast food company?
The Real Business Model
McDonald's main source of revenue is not from selling burgers and fries, but from real estate. The company owns the land and buildings where its franchises operate, and then rents them out to franchisees.
Franchise Model
McDonald's franchisees are responsible for the day-to-day operations of their restaurants, including hiring staff, managing inventory, and ensuring food quality. In return, they pay McDonald's a percentage of their sales as rent and royalties.
Real Estate Empire
McDonald's real estate portfolio is valued at billions of dollars. The company strategically locations its restaurants in high-traffic areas, ensuring consistent customer flow for its franchisees.
Conclusion
So, while McDonald's may seem like a fast food company, it's really a real estate company that happens to sell burgers. This unique business model has contributed to its immense success and global presence.
