6. Kill/keep
decisions.

Use explicit thresholds so humans
do not keep bad campaigns alive emotionally.

Metric	Keep	Kill
Reply Rate
(unique replies / sent)	≥ 5%	< 2%
Positive Rate
(positive / total replies)	≥ 35%	< 20%
PCPL
(cost per positive lead)	≤ $25	> $50
Bounce Rate
(hard + soft bounces)	≤ 5%	> 8%
Domain Health
(deliverability status)	Healthy
(green)	At Risk / Poor
(yellow / red)
