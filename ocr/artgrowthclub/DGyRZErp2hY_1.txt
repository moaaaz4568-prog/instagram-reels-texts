Improving how reels are ranked and recommended

We’ve been working hard to improve how reels are ranked and recommended on Instagram. Our goal is simple: serve the right reels to the right people at the right time. In April 2024, we announced initial updates to how we recommend reels, giving creators of all sizes a way to break through and be seen by new audiences. We’re excited to share that these changes are now fully launched, and the reels you’re recommended today are more relevant, personalized, and timely. For creators, this means you can grow more quickly as people discover you through your reels.

Personally I think it’s great for emerging artists!
